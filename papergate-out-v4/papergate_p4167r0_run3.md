Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides a narrow foundation for its standardization case: it demonstrates implementation activity and acknowledges an alternative design, but leaves most of the burden—especially who benefits and why only the standard can solve the problem—largely unargued. The core motivational claim appears only as a technical observation repeated without elaboration into affected users, standardization necessity, or interoperability impact.

- The strongest support is the linked prototype on Compiler Explorer, which offers concrete implementation experience and experimentation.
- The paper also clearly situates itself against P2822R2, distinguishing its use of existing annotation syntax as an alternative design direction.
- The thinnest support lies in the repeated claimed rationale about ADL associated entities, which never expands into a demonstrated need for standardization, an affected audience, or evidence that a library solution cannot suffice.
