Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides only a slim basis for its own standardization, chiefly by tying the proposed wording to an earlier design decision and explaining its departure from prior trait-based specification. The case is thinnest around the people and systems affected, how the change coordinates with existing practice, and why a library-level solution is impossible.

- The strongest support comes from the account of prior art, which connects the omission to the earlier structural-type change in P3778R0.
- The paper asserts a consistency fix relative to P2830R10, but does not substantiate why that consistency requires standardization rather than an editorial correction.
- The paper never identifies who is affected by the current wording or what real-world code, compilers, or users are impacted.
- Most glaringly, there is no implementation experience offered to show that the proposed allowance is unambiguous and already supported in practice.
