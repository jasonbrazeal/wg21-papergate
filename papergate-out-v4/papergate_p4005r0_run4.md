Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a partial case for its own standardization, with prior art and alternatives addressed clearly but most other required showings resting on assertion rather than evidence. The thinnest areas are the absence of any argument for why a library cannot meet the need and the lack of demonstrated implementation experience with the full proposal.

- The paper’s strongest support is its discussion of prior art and how the proposed facility differs from and follows existing design guidance.
- The rationale for why the feature matters and why it belongs in the standard leans on a single general claim about safety guarantees, with little concrete development.
- The paper does not attempt to establish why a library solution would be insufficient.
- The most glaring omission is the admitted lack of implementation experience with the full proposal as described.
