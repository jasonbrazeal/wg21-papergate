Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a partial case for standardization, with concrete implementation evidence and some engagement with alternatives, but much of its rationale rests on asserted benefits rather than demonstrated need or affected users. The thinnest support appears where the proposal should show who would use the feature, why only a standard library solution suffices, and how it would interoperate across the ecosystem.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with a working prototype and references to divergent existing container internals across major standard libraries.
- The paper also establishes some prior art and alternative designs, acknowledging consistency concerns while arguing for its chosen API shape.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who is affected by the proposed change or what concrete user problems motivate it, leaving the central justification largely unsupported.
