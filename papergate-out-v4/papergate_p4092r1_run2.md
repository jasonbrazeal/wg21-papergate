Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers credible evidence of implementation and prior art, but its case for standardization rests heavily on assertions about the bridge’s general applicability and necessity that are not substantiated in the text. The thinnest support concerns why the standard must adopt this facility and who beyond the authors is actually affected.

- The strongest support is the demonstrated implementation experience, including a complete Appendix and compiled output against two independent implementations.
- Prior art and alternatives are also well established through references to P4003R3, P2300R10, and the discussion of complementary domains.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution will not suffice, since the claimed avoidance of specific mechanisms is mentioned but not argued with enough context or counterfactual analysis.
- Most glaringly, the paper merely claims rather than demonstrates who is affected and why the feature matters beyond the synthetic bridge example provided.
