Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers concrete support for its motivation, its lineage from prior proposals, and the existence of an implementation, but it leaves several important parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the lack of evidence about who actually needs this feature, why existing library mechanisms cannot address it, and how it will coordinate with existing practice.

- The strongest support is the working implementation in a Clang fork, which gives the design at least some practical grounding.
- The paper clearly situates itself in an established line of prior proposals and explains what new direction it takes.
- The rationale for why a library solution will not do is essentially absent, leaving a central standardization question unanswered.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, so the urgency and breadth of the need remain unclear.
