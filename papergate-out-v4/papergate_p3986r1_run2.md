Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper leans heavily on references to P3425 and prior committee discussion, but it does not independently build the case for why this wording strategy itself needs to be standardized. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, interoperability, or any implementation experience.

- The paper offers some support by citing a LEWG decision and identifying a wording obstacle that has slowed P3425, though it mostly asserts rather than demonstrates the significance of that obstacle.
- The discussion of why the problem matters gestures at receiver synthesis and operation state location, but stops short of establishing the practical stakes for users or implementers.
- The paper provides no evidence of implementation experience with the proposed wording approach.
- The most glaring omission is any treatment of who is affected or why this cannot be handled outside the standard.
