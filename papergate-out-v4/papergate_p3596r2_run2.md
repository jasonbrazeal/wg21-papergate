Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some useful context for its organizational choices and shows that the proposed annex wording has been developed in a real drafting branch, but it leaves large parts of the standardization case unstated, especially around who would use this catalog and why it must live in the standard itself. The strongest support is for the existence of prior efforts and a concrete drafting artifact; the thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, interoperability, or why a non-standard reference document would be insufficient.

- The paper establishes that similar cataloging efforts and organizational groupings already exist, and that the proposed annex has been developed collaboratively as a branch of the working draft.
- The paper claims, though does not fully establish, that the catalog addresses a real need by making undefined and IFNDR behavior more visible and by providing stable names for future work.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the lack of such an annex or what practical problem they currently face.
- The paper does not address why this material needs to be in the C++ standard rather than in a separately maintained reference or library-like resource.
