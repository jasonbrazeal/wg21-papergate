Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is quite thin: its substantive points are largely asserted rather than developed, and several essential parts of the case are simply absent. The strongest material concerns the existence of C23 `_BitInt` and its implementation in GCC and Clang, but even those facts are not tied into a sustained argument for why C++ should act.

- The most concrete support is the mention of C23 `_BitInt` and its implementation in GCC and Clang, which at least gestures toward existing practice.
- The paper does not develop who would use the feature or why the C++ ecosystem would benefit from adopting it.
- It offers no meaningful discussion of alternatives, interoperability, why a library cannot address the need, or why standardization is required.
- Most glaringly, the paper gives no reason grounded in standard C++ practice or portability for taking up the feature.
