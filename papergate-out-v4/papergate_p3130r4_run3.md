Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper relies heavily on analogy to the STL and references to a companion proposal, but it presents those claims without the surrounding evidence that would show who benefits, what alternatives were seriously considered, or how the proposed design would interact with existing practice. The thinnest parts are the absence of an affected-user discussion and the lack of demonstrated implementation experience for several of the functions central to the proposal’s claims.

- The strongest support is the repeated appeal to STL-like generic algorithm reuse as the motivating goal, tying the proposal to an established and familiar design philosophy.
- The paper gestures toward customization for external adjacency list structures, but it leaves the interoperability and coordination case largely asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of who is affected, leaving the need for standardization disconnected from actual users, projects, or ecosystems.
