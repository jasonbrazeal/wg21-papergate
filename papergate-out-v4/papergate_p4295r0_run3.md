Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with the strongest material concerning prior interface designs and the existence of a partial implementation. The case is thinnest where it needs to show who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The proposal does establish that the single-ended concurrent queue interface has prior art dating back to N3353, and that a partial implementation is available for examination.
- The paper claims, though without sufficient supporting reasoning, that bounded concurrent queues matter and that an end-view helper would improve code structure and interoperability with P0260.
- The paper does not establish who would be affected by the proposed feature or why this capability specifically requires standardization rather than an ordinary library.
- The paper offers no discussion of why a library implementation would be inadequate, leaving a central part of the standardization rationale unaddressed.
