Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a clear account of why assignable capture lambdas would be useful and shows familiarity with the relevant prior work, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case more asserted than demonstrated. The strongest support concerns motivation and prior art, while the thinnest areas are the lack of evidence about implementation experience and the absence of a concrete affected-user picture.

- The paper’s motivation is well grounded in the limits of existing lambda behavior and its interaction with parallel range algorithms.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is substantive, particularly in distinguishing this narrower proposal from the broader P3960R0.
- The claims about why a library workaround is insufficient and why this belongs in the standard are present but lack the supporting detail needed to make them persuasive.
- The most glaring omission is the total absence of implementation experience or evidence about who specifically is affected by the current restriction.
