Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some grounding for its motivation and shows awareness of the prior discussion and alternatives, but it leaves several key parts of the standardization case largely unsupported. The thinnest areas are the arguments for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and why the standard itself is the right venue.

- The paper most clearly establishes that the proposal responds to real concerns raised against `affine_on` and points toward a revised design.
- It gives a fair account of prior art and alternatives, including the discontinued P3718R0 and the earlier `continues_on` approach.
- It only claims, without substantiating, who would be affected and how the feature would coordinate with existing or fallible schedulers.
- The paper does not establish why standardization is necessary or why a library solution would be insufficient.
