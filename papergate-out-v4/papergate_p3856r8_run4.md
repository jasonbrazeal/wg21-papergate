Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gives a clear and repeated account of why the ability to query whether a type is structural matters, but much of the surrounding case is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support is in the areas that would connect the feature to users, the standard, and existing practice.

- The strongest part of the paper is its explanation that structural types are already used throughout the language and library while no user-facing query exists for them.
- The paper gestures at implementation experience and a sample implementation, but does not establish that experience beyond a brief mention.
- The argument that reflection-based alternatives are insufficient, and that this therefore belongs in the standard rather than a library, is claimed but never developed.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, why the standard is the right vehicle, or how the proposal coordinates with existing rules and facilities.
