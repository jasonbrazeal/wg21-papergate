Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, mostly resting on repeated assertions that saturating operations are common and that Intel has implemented them. Several sections provide claims without supporting detail, and the case for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library is essentially absent.

- The strongest support is the repeated statement that Intel has implemented all three functions in its reference implementation and used them in software products.
- The paper gestures at prior art by citing P0543R3, but does not develop the discussion of alternatives or what was learned from that proposal.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of why a library solution would not suffice, leaving the central standardization question unaddressed.
