Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

This paper offers only a narrow basis for standardization: it can point to prior discussion and to some existing implementations, but it does not convincingly show who would be affected, why a library type would be insufficient, or what the standard specifically needs to provide. The strongest material is about the existence of similar types in the wild; the thinnest is the almost complete absence of a demonstrated need for the feature to be in the standard rather than in a library.

- The paper does show that related work exists, including LEWG’s prior engagement with `zstring_view` and multiple independent implementations under that name.
- The paper gestures at why the contract matters and why the standard might be the right venue, but it leaves those claims undeveloped rather than establishing them.
- The paper provides almost no account of who is affected by the absence of a standardized type or why ordinary library approaches cannot meet that need.
