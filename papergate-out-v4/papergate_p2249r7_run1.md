Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers solid grounding in the problem and in the landscape of existing practice, but it has not fully made the case that standardization is the necessary or appropriate remedy. The support is thinnest around coordination with related library components and around why users cannot address the gap adequately through their own code.

- The strongest support comes from the documented precedent that the standard library already moved toward heterogeneous comparisons in nearby components, making this a natural follow-on.
- The paper also demonstrates real implementation experience through a working prototype on GCC.
- A notable weakness is the repeated but unsubstantiated claim that the mixed-comparison need occurs commonly in practice, without concrete examples of affected code or user reports.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of coordination or interoperability with other standardization efforts and existing library facilities.
