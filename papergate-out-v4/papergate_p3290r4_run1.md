Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers solid support for the motivating problem and for its account of existing alternatives, but its case thins considerably when it comes to showing who is concretely affected, why the standard is the right layer, and how the proposed pieces coordinate without creating new inconsistencies. The absence of any argument that the functionality cannot be provided as an ordinary library is the most striking gap.

- The strongest support establishes a clear need to bring legacy assertion facilities into a centralized contract-violation handling strategy.
- The paper adequately demonstrates awareness of prior facilities and alternative names, and reports implementation work in libc++ and libstdc++.
- The claim of implementation experience is weakened by the admission that the work has not yet been made publicly available or upstreamed.
- The paper gives no argument at all for why a library solution would be insufficient.
