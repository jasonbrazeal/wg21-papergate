Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers meaningful support for the broad concern that standardization is outpacing implementation capacity, but it provides almost no evidence that the specific mechanism it proposes belongs in the standard. The strongest material concerns the general problem, while the case for this particular solution remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper establishes why the topic matters by connecting real implementation lag and limited conformance capacity to a need for resource realism.
- The only evidence about who is affected is the attendance count for a meeting, which does not show whose work or conformance burden would change.
- The paper offers no established prior art, alternatives, standardization rationale, coordination analysis, or argument against a non-standard solution.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of implementation experience for the proposed feature itself.
