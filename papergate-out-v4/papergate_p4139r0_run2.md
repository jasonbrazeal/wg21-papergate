Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a fragment of the case needed to justify standardization, resting almost entirely on a named, unsettled concern about `get()` and a brief mention of one rejected alternative. Its support is thinnest where a standards document should be strongest: there is nothing about who would be affected, what prior practice or implementation experience shows, why the standard rather than a library is the right venue, or how this would coordinate with existing interfaces.

- The clearest, though still tentative, support is the claim that the proposed facility has obvious utility and would introduce a novel, potentially failing `get()` into the library.
- The paper mentions prior art and an alternative name, but only as a naming discussion, not as an exploration of existing practice or design precedent.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any evidence of implementation experience or analysis of why existing library mechanisms cannot provide the facility.
