Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is uneven: it can point to accepted prior work and a concrete change in the working draft, but it leaves most of the basic case—who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, why libraries cannot fill the need, and whether anyone has tried—essentially unargued. The strongest material concerns alternatives and precedent, while the weakest concerns the existence of a problem that requires committee action at all.

- The paper’s most solid support is the established prior art, since it builds directly on P3950 and an accepted change to the C++29 working draft.
- The paper gestures at why the issue matters and at coordination with `std::execution::task`, but those points are only claimed rather than demonstrated.
- Most notably, the paper does not establish who is affected, why the standard should address this rather than a library, or any implementation experience to ground the proposal.
