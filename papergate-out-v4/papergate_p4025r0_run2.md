Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper gives a solid starting point by pointing to an existing implementation and naming a real gap in C++’s current data-handling story, but it does not build much further than that. The case for standardization is thinnest where it matters most: showing why a standard rather than a library is needed, and showing how the proposed facility would actually interoperate with the Python-centric workflows it identifies.

- The strongest support comes from the reference to an existing DataFrame library, which provides concrete prior art and implementation experience.
- The claim that AI workflows default to Python/Pandas because C++ lacks a native DataFrame is credited as establishing why the problem matters.
- The need for a standard, as opposed to a library, is asserted through fears of fragmentation but not actually demonstrated.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument that a library cannot solve the problem.
