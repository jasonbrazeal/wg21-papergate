Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some support for its core motivation by tying the proposed comparison to existing ordering facilities and established metaprogramming needs, but it leaves most of the practical and procedural case for standardization unsubstantiated. The thinnest areas are coordination with related proposals, implementation experience, and demonstration that the feature cannot be adequately supplied as a library.

- The clearest support is for why the feature matters, since the paper connects direct comparison of `meta::info` to canonical ordering and constant template arguments in a way the assessment credits as established.
- The paper invokes prior work such as `type_order` and a motivating `type_set` example, but only as claims rather than demonstrated precedent or explored alternatives.
- The case for standardization itself, the affected audience, and the insufficiency of a library solution are asserted without being backed by evidence.
- There is no established coordination with related standardization efforts or implementation experience, leaving the proposal’s path to integration essentially unaddressed.
