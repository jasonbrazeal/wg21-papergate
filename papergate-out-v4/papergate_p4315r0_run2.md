Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper gestures at the role profiles could play in constraining dangerous constructs and supporting safety guarantees, but it does not develop those points into a case for standardizing this particular proposal. The support is thinnest around the practical questions: whose code would change, what alternatives exist, how implementations would coordinate, and why anything beyond a library is needed.

- The strongest support lies in the general observation that profiles can attach runtime checks and safety guarantees to active code.
- The discussion of disallowing unavoidable or tool-enabling constructs hints at prior art and alternatives, but remains undeveloped.
- The paper leaves entirely unaddressed who would be affected and whether the proposed facility actually belongs in the standard.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of implementation experience, which leaves the standardization need almost wholly unsupported.
