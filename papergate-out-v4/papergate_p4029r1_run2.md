Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper does establish why its domain—low-latency, zero-overhead systems—cares about the problem, but most of the remaining case for standardization is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any identified affected constituency, any argument that a library cannot meet the need, and any implementation experience beyond a general observation about existing networking patterns.

- The paper most clearly establishes the performance and latency constraints that motivate interest in this work from the SG14 perspective.
- It gestures at prior efforts and interoperability considerations, but does not develop them into a supporting case.
- The paper offers no evidence about who specifically is affected by the lack of standardization.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why an implementation outside the standard would be insufficient.
