Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers some concrete evidence about existing library behavior, but it leaves much of the case for standardization unstated or asserted rather than shown. The thinnest support is in explaining who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution cannot suffice.

- The strongest point is the implementation experience, since both libstdc++ and libc++ are shown to abandon state in a way the standard does not clearly support.
- The paper makes claims about prior art and alternatives, but does not establish how those efforts or options bear on this proposal.
- The discussion of coordination and interoperability only asserts implementation divergence without showing how widespread or consequential it is.
- The paper gives no account of who is affected, why standardization is necessary, or why a library solution would not work.
