Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial but uneven account of why this change belongs in the standard, strongest when explaining the motivating problem and the evolution away from consteval-only types, but much thinner on the external case for standardization. The most serious gaps are the absence of any clear statement of who is affected and the lack of evidence that the problem cannot be addressed outside the standard.

- The paper establishes clearly why the current consteval-only type model causes practical difficulties and why a value-based model is worth pursuing.
- It credibly situates the proposal against prior reflection and consteval-only value work, showing that alternatives have been considered.
- The case for standardization itself remains asserted rather than demonstrated, since the paper does not show that a library or implementation-level solution would be insufficient.
- The paper never identifies the affected users or workloads, leaving the breadth and urgency of the problem unspecified.
