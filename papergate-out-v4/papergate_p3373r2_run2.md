Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper’s strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience, but much of its broader case—who is affected, why it matters, and why a library solution is insufficient—rests on assertions rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest parts are the claims about user impact and the necessity of standardization, which are supported mainly by references to existing practice rather than direct evidence or argument.

- The paper clearly establishes implementation experience through specific references to libunifex and nVidia’s stdexec, showing the proposed behavior is already in use.
- The case for why the standard should change is supported by pointing to existing library implementations and the potential for the standard to codify them.
- The paper claims but does not establish who is affected, relying on unelaborated expressions of concern and a single mention of libunifex’s behavior.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of established justification for why a library will not suffice, since the only evidence offered is that certain libraries already use the strategy, which itself suggests a library-based solution may be viable.
