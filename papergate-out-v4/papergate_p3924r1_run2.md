Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a skeletal case for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on general assertions that some usages are incorrect and that a prior review has found and fixed a few mistakes. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about why the standard is the right vehicle, how the change interacts with other specifications, or whether any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support comes from the cited NB comment and the reference to already-fixed mistakes in N5014, which at least suggest a known review context.
- The paper gestures at affected parties by noting that constructs like `struct S` exist as elaborated-type-specifiers rather than declarations.
- It does not establish why this belongs in the standard rather than being handled editorially or elsewhere.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any demonstrated implementation experience, prior art beyond the cited review, or interoperability consideration.
