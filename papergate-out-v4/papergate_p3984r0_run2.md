Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is uneven: it offers concrete implementation experience, but most of the case for why a standard framework is needed rests on broad assertions rather than demonstrated necessity or comparison with existing practice. The thinnest areas are the absence of an identified affected population and the lack of a sustained argument that a library-based approach cannot suffice.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, credited to a prototype and ongoing work in a major compiler.
- The claim that a library will not do is asserted through general statements about chaos and platform-specific limitations rather than shown through concrete examples or analysis.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the problem, leaving the audience and impact unspecified.
- Prior art and alternatives receive only a summary reference, without enough detail to show that the proposed framework is a necessary or superior path.
