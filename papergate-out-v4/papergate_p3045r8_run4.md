Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper offers substantial support for standardizing physical quantities and units, with particularly strong evidence across motivation, affected users, prior art, and implementation experience. The support is thinnest where it must justify why standardization is preferable to an existing library, since the paper asserts certification and safety advantages without fully demonstrating that a library solution cannot meet those needs.

- The paper convincingly establishes real-world impact through production failures and widespread library adoption, showing both the stakes and the demand for a standardized vocabulary.
- It demonstrates mature implementation experience and community validation through mp-units, including compiler explorer availability and user-reported safety benefits.
- The case for interoperability and coordination is well grounded in concrete examples like the Lockheed Martin and NASA unit mismatch and explicit support from standards bodies like BSI.
- The most glaring omission is the underdeveloped argument for why a library alone cannot suffice, leaving the necessity of standardization resting on claims about certification and unique safety guarantees rather than demonstrated limitations of non-standard approaches.
