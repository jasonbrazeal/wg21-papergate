Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers meaningful support in some areas, particularly in explaining the motivating use cases, surveying alternatives, and pointing to an existing implementation, but the case for standardization is uneven and leaves several essential questions unaddressed. The thinnest parts concern who is actually affected, why a library solution cannot suffice, and how the proposal would coordinate with the standards ecosystem it explicitly leans on.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in Clang, which demonstrates that the core idea is more than speculative.
- The paper also does a credible job explaining the expressive gaps in current macro and control-flow handling that motivate an init-hoist.
- It engages with prior art and alternative spellings, showing awareness of existing extensions and earlier design discussions.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established argument for who is affected or why the need cannot be met outside the standard.
