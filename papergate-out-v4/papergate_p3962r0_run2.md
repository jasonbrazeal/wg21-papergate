Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin foundation for its own standardization, anchored by a credible articulation of why the broader problem matters but leaving nearly every other necessary justification unaddressed. The support is thinnest where a proposal normally needs to be most concrete: prior work, the role of the standard, interoperability, and implementation evidence are all absent.

- The strongest support is the paper’s framing that feature accumulation under resource constraints creates real risks for implementation completeness and portability.
- The paper gestures toward affected parties by reporting attendance at a session, but does not establish who would actually be affected by the proposed standardization or how.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established prior art, alternatives, or implementation experience, leaving no demonstrated basis for standardizing anything specific.
