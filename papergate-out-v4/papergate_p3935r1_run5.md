Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper’s strongest grounding is in its prior art and alternatives, where it clearly identifies C23 and existing implementations as sources, but much of the rest of the case rests on assertions rather than demonstrated need or feasibility. The thinnest support appears around who is affected, why the library itself is inadequate, and whether there is implementation experience beyond borrowing from C.

- The clearest established point is that the proposed functions already exist in C23 or related specifications, giving the proposal a concrete external reference.
- The paper claims but does not establish why these additions matter to C++ users or who specifically would benefit from their standardization.
- The discussion of why a library cannot provide these functions is especially weak, since the paper admits it does not discuss that option.
- The lack of demonstrated implementation experience or independent need beyond C compatibility leaves the standardization rationale largely asserted rather than shown.
