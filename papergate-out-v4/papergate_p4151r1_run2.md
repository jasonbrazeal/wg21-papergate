Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a sliver of support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a claimed naming inconsistency and a remembered remark from discussion. Even that support is thin, since it concerns rationale for a name rather than a demonstrated need for the language or library feature itself. The proposal says nothing about who is affected, what alternatives were seriously weighed, why a library solution is insufficient, or whether anyone has tried implementing the idea.

- The strongest support is the claim that making `affine_on` unary removes the reason for the “on” in its name, which at least gestures at a design inconsistency.
- The paper notes that the author reportedly said during LEWG discussion that a change in interface shape warranted a change in name, though this is presented as recollection rather than established agreement.
- The paper provides no evidence about affected users, existing practice, or implementation experience.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any case for why standardization, rather than a library facility or ordinary naming convention, would be the right remedy.
