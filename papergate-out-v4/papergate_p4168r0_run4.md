Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers credible support for standardization where it documents divergent implementation behavior, defective wording, a concrete existing implementation base, and a clear history of prior standardization attempts. The case is thinnest on demonstrating who is concretely affected and on explaining why a library solution is insufficient, since those points are asserted more than evidenced.

- The strongest support is the established record of implementation divergence and inconsistency with the standard’s wording, which makes the current specification plainly ineffective.
- The paper also establishes solid prior art and alternatives through its references to superseded papers, resolved LWG issues, and existing practice in major standard libraries.
- The most notable gaps are the unestablished claims about the scale of affected users and the lack of a developed argument for why a library cannot adequately address the problem.
