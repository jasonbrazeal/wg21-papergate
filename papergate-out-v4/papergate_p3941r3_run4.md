Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some grounding for revisiting the design of `affine_on`, particularly by pointing to prior discussion and discontinued alternatives, but it leaves much of the standardization case undeveloped. The support is thinnest around who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the design has been meaningfully implemented or coordinated with existing practice.

- The strongest part of the paper is its connection of the proposed change to recorded concerns about `affine_on` and earlier scheduler-based approaches such as `continues_on`.
- The case for coordination and interoperability is asserted through the existence of NB comments, but not substantiated with their content or resolution.
- The claim that a library solution would be inadequate is made only in passing, without explaining why a standard facility is necessary.
- The paper provides no evidence about who would use this facility or what implementation experience supports the specific design being proposed.
