Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow basis for its own standardization, centered on a plausible expression of need and a useful survey of existing naming discussions; beyond that, the case remains largely undeveloped. The thinnest support appears in the areas where a proposal must show why a library solution is insufficient, how the feature would coordinate with existing concepts, and that there is implementation experience behind it.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s citation of prior art and naming alternatives, which grounds the idea in existing practice and discussion.
- The paper asserts, but does not substantiate, that the need arises across several projects and therefore matters to a broader audience.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why the standard library or an existing library mechanism cannot already express the desired semantics.
