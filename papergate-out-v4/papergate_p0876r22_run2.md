Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper’s strongest support is its concrete implementation experience, but much of the rest of the standardization case is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest area is the complete absence of any discussion of who would be affected by adding the facility to the standard.

- The paper does establish implementation experience, with reported work in libstdc++ and existing use of the Boost.Context-based API as evidence.
- The paper claims that the facility cannot be written in portable C++ and that standardization would aid tooling, but these points are not developed with sufficient supporting detail.
- The paper claims prior art and a lineage of superseded proposals, but does not establish how those earlier efforts and their outcomes bear on the current design.
- The paper never establishes who is affected by the proposal, leaving the audience and impact of standardization unaddressed.
