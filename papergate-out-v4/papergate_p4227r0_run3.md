Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gestures toward a standardization need in several directions, but it does not carry any of them through with enough evidence or argument to show that the problem is real, that the affected audience is broad, or that standardization is the right remedy. The thinnest support is around who would actually benefit and why existing library types cannot serve, since those sections lean on assertion rather than demonstrated practice.

- The strongest support is the paper’s recognition that null-terminated string interop with C APIs is the motivating scenario, even though the importance of that scenario is asserted rather than shown.
- The discussion of prior art names related view types and alternatives, but it does not establish how they fall short for the stated purpose.
- The paper does not establish that the affected users or C APIs are common enough to justify a standard type, acknowledging that embedded-NUL C APIs are rare.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of implementation experience or concrete examples showing that a non-standard library type would be insufficient in practice.
