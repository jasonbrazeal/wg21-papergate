Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a clear motivation for exposing a structural-type query, but most of the surrounding case—audience, alternatives, standard-only need, interoperability, and implementation experience—remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest area is the absence of any concrete user or implementer affected by the missing functionality.

- The need to query structural types is grounded in existing standard and library language that already depends on the concept.
- The paper gestures toward prior art in P2996 and alternative reflection-based approaches, but does not develop the comparison enough to establish it.
- The claim that only standardization can solve this because library implementers already have hidden machinery is repeated without supporting evidence.
- The paper never identifies who is affected by the lack of the trait, leaving the practical urgency of the proposal unestablished.
