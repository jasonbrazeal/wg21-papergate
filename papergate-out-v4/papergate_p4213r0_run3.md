Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers real support for the motivating problem and the existence of prior art, but it falls short of showing who specifically is affected or that the library itself has enough implementation experience behind it to justify standardization. The thinness is most visible around the claim that a standard library is the right home for these definitions, since the paper leans on assertions rather than evidence of interoperability failures or practical use.

- The paper clearly establishes why having SI definitions available matters and situates the proposal against existing work like P3045R8 and mp-units.
- Prior art is documented well enough to show the feature has a plausible origin and design lineage.
- The case for standardization specifically, rather than continued use of a library, is asserted but not demonstrated with concrete interoperability or adoption evidence.
- The most glaring omission is any description of who is affected, leaving the proposal without a clear audience or demonstrated need beyond general convenience.
