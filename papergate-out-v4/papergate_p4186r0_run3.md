Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin basis for standardization, resting almost entirely on assertions about confusion and past unsuccessful efforts without substantiating who is affected, what alternatives were actually tried, or why a standard is the necessary remedy. The support is thinnest where the proposal needs to show that the problem belongs in the standard rather than in documentation, libraries, or implementation practice.

- The strongest support is the paper’s recognition that the lack of agreed documentation about profiles creates confusion and blocks meaningful contributions.
- The paper gestures at prior proposals and their failure, but does not establish what those efforts contained or why they fell short.
- The paper never identifies the affected constituencies, the coordination or interoperability concerns, or any implementation experience.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper gives no reason why the problem cannot be addressed outside the C++ standard itself.
