Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers meaningful but uneven support for its standardization case, strongest on implementation experience and weakest on arguments that a library-level fix is insufficient. Its framing of the problem is concrete, but several key justifications are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The implementation experience is the most convincing element, with two independently developed deployments cited and reported as causing no bug reports.
- The paper establishes that the customization mechanism has genuine gaps and that prior work has attempted to address them, giving it a credible foundation.
- The case for who is affected and why standardization is required rests largely on assertion rather than evidence about the population of users or the necessity of a standard change.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution cannot suffice, leaving a central justification unaddressed.
