Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper leans almost entirely on references to other work for its motivation and offers little direct evidence for standardization. The support is thinnest around the actual affected audience, why a library solution would not suffice, and how the feature would coexist with existing practice.

- The clearest statement is that making certain functions `constexpr` has already been identified as a breaking change in some cases, which is at least a stated relevance to current users.
- The discussion of prior art is only a pointer to two other papers rather than an explanation a reader can evaluate on its own.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, leaving the practical scope of the problem unclear.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination concerns, or a reason the standard library cannot address the need.
