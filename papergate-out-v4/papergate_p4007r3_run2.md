Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers some argumentative groundwork for the need to standardize, but much of it remains asserted rather than demonstrated, and several core questions are left entirely unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns the affected audience and implementation experience, which are not established at all.

- The strongest support is the paper’s engagement with prior work, crediting Croydon and earlier proposals with resolving several open issues in this design space.
- The paper gestures at why the standard is necessary by warning that shipping the current split would foreclose environment-based frame allocator propagation without a language change.
- The paper claims a library-only solution is insufficient, but does not actually establish why, resting on the same foreclosure warning without further argument.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of evidence about who is affected or any implementation experience to ground the proposal.
