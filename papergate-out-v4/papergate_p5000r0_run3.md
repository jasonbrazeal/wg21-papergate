Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only scattered assertions in favor of its own standardization, and most of the burden is left for external sources or the reader’s patience. Its case is thinnest around the concrete need for a standard, who would actually be affected, and whether implementation experience shows the direction is viable.

- The clearest, if still underdeveloped, support is that the paper gestures toward a long-standing community desire for networking, though it does not substantiate that waiting or its consequences.
- The paper names other proposals on profiles and safety as prior art, but it only cites them rather than showing how this work relates to or improves on them.
- It almost entirely omits who is affected by the absence of standardization, why a library would not suffice, and what implementation experience exists to ground the proposal.
