Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper’s strongest support comes from its clear framing of prior work, a defined multi-paper plan, and genuine implementation experience through two shipping libraries. The case thins considerably when it relies on asserted industry impact and coordination needs without concrete evidence, and it offers no argument at all for why a library would be insufficient for standardization.

- The paper establishes implementation experience and a concrete roadmap grounded in existing libraries and prior standards work.
- The paper establishes why the problem matters by pointing to two decades of inaction and architectural erosion in the Networking TS.
- The paper claims broad industry impact and existing adoption by named teams but does not substantiate the scale or significance of that adoption.
- The paper entirely fails to address why a library solution would not satisfy the needs it describes.
