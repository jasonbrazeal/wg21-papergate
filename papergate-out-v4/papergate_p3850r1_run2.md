Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for its own standardization, relying heavily on assertions of urgency and importance without substantiating them with concrete evidence. Its strongest moments reference existing wording and implementations, but even those are presented as summary claims rather than demonstrated experience.

- The paper at least gestures toward implementation maturity by noting that most of the proposed extensions have wording and several have working implementations.
- The discussion of prior work points to a roadmap and a comprehensive analysis document, suggesting some continuity with earlier standardization efforts.
- The need for extensions over a library solution is entirely unargued, leaving a central justification for standardization missing.
- The paper repeatedly asserts that the work matters to large codebases and safety-critical systems, but provides no evidence or examples tying those communities to the proposed changes.
