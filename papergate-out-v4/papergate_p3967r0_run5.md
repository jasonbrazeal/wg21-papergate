Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only scattered support for its own standardization, with most of its central claims asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected and the lack of implementation experience, both of which are foundational for a proposal of this kind.

- The strongest support is the coordination and interoperability discussion, particularly the recognition of mangling and alternate-name issues across major object file formats.
- The paper gestures toward prior art and alternatives, but it does not substantiate how its approach compares to or improves upon existing and in-flight proposals.
- The rationale for why a library cannot solve the problem rests on a single asserted benefit about distributing pre-compiled libraries, with no supporting evidence.
- The most glaring omission is the complete failure to identify who is affected by the problem or who would benefit from the proposed change.
