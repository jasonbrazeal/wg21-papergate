Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers solid grounding in prior art and practical implementation, particularly through its explicit derivation from the mp-units library and alignment with the SI Brochure. Its thinnest support lies in the arguments for why standardization, rather than continued use of an external library, is necessary, and in showing how the affected community actually depends on these specific definitions.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, since all synopses are derived from a working open-source library and the chosen constants are described as having proved useful in practice.
- The paper also establishes prior art and alternatives clearly by anchoring its content in the SI Brochure and framing the work as a structured companion to the existing quantities library proposal.
- The case for why the standard itself is the right venue remains largely asserted, with analogies and interoperability claims offered but not substantiated.
- The most glaring omission is evidence about who is affected beyond general statements about SI’s worldwide use, leaving the actual community and its needs underspecified.
