Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper offers substantial support for most of the standardization case, particularly in showing existing practice, affected users, and implementability. The thinnest part is the rationale for why a library solution or mere documentation of existing extensions would be insufficient, which is asserted more than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is the evidence of near-universal compiler acceptance and existing code using `$` in identifiers, showing the feature is already de facto portable.
- The paper also establishes clearly why the standard should acknowledge the construct rather than leave it as an ill-formed extension.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of a developed argument that a library or purely documentary approach cannot handle the need, leaving that part of the case unsupported.
