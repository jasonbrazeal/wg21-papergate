Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some useful groundwork for a naming discussion, but it does not yet make a complete case that the proposed functionality belongs in the standard. Its strongest material concerns why the existing naming landscape is unsatisfactory and what alternatives have already been considered, while the argument thins out considerably around affected users, implementability, and the necessity of standardization itself.

- The paper establishes that the proposed name matters because a `get` returning `optional` would clash with established library conventions and that bad names cause real harm.
- It establishes that prior naming attempts such as `get_optional`, `lookup_optional`, and `lookup` have already been explored without consensus.
- The paper only claims, rather than demonstrates, why this needs to be standardized rather than provided through a library solution.
- It offers no implementation experience and does not identify who would be affected by the change, leaving the practical case for standardization largely unstated.
