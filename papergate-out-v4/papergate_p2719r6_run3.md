Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a mixed account of its own readiness, with the strongest material concentrated in motivation and discussion of prior approaches, while much of the practical case remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any identified affected audience and the lack of substantiated evidence for implementability, interoperability, or why a library-level solution cannot suffice.

- The clearest support is the explanation of why type-specific allocation customization matters and the acknowledgment of limits in current class-scoped operators.
- The paper also usefully engages with prior art and alternatives, including an earlier design and relevant drafting issues.
- What remains most unproven is the claim that standardizing this behavior is necessary, since the paper does not establish who is concretely affected or that existing library mechanisms are inadequate for the problem.
