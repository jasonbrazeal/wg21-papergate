Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a thin account of why this work should be standardized, with most of its case resting on asserted need and design lineage rather than demonstrated demand, affected users, or interoperability. The strongest concrete support is the availability of a GCC fork, but the core justifications—why the standard should provide this, why a library cannot suffice, and how it coordinates with existing or in-flight work—remain essentially unsupported.

- The paper establishes implementation experience through a working GCC fork at the linked repository.
- The paper claims, but does not establish, a meaningful need by framing the work as a response to vaguely referenced existing needs and committee discussions.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives through citations to P3968, P4005, P4009, and P3400, but does not connect them to a demonstrated gap this work fills.
- The paper offers no substantive account of who is affected or how the proposal would coordinate and interoperate with the existing contract and language landscape.
