Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, primarily through implementation observations, while much of the motivating rationale and affected-user analysis remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts concern why the standard itself must change and why a library solution cannot address the problem.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with compiler behavior documented across multiple cases and tool links provided.
- The proposal also establishes prior art and alternatives by tracing intent back to N1821 and connecting the issue to CWG3103.
- The case for who is affected is weaker, resting mainly on repeated claims that implementations agree without explaining what that means for users in practice.
- The most glaring omissions are the absence of any established need for standard wording changes and no argument ruling out a library-level solution.
