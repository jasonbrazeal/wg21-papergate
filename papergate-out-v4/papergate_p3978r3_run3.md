Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a narrow basis for standardization: it points to existing practice and related proposals, but leaves most of the burden of justification unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the basic questions of who would use the feature, why the standard is the right vehicle, and how it fits with the broader library.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with a shipping library implementation and testing on GCC trunk noted in the paper.
- The paper also credibly situates itself against prior art and alternatives, showing continuity with earlier proposals and explaining one deliberate divergence.
- The opening rationale asserts importance by citing language inconsistencies, but does not develop why that inconsistency matters enough to standardize.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, why a library-only solution is insufficient, or how the proposal coordinates with the rest of the standard.
