Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper makes a real start by identifying why current filter views are risky and non-intuitive, but it does not carry that motivation forward into the evidence needed to justify standardization. The support becomes thin very quickly once the discussion moves past the general problem statement and into who is affected, what alternatives exist, and why the standard library is the right place for the solution.

- The strongest established point is that the paper clearly articulates the risk and non-intuitive behavior of current filter views.
- The identification of affected users and basic use cases is asserted but not backed with concrete examples or evidence of prevalence.
- The discussion of alternatives and prior art gestures at related work but does not establish that the proposed approach is the necessary or best option.
- The most glaring omission is any demonstration of implementation experience, coordination with related features, or why a library-only solution would be insufficient.
