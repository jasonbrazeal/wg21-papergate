Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers little direct support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on assertions about naming confusion and a single informal poll rather than a demonstrated need for a standard facility. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about why a library solution is insufficient, how the proposal coordinates with related work, and what implementation experience exists.

- The strongest material is the mention of a Kona poll, though even that is reported without enough context to establish who is affected or how representative the sentiment is.
- The discussion of prior naming attempts and alternatives shows some awareness of history, but does not demonstrate that standardization is the appropriate next step.
- The paper provides no case for why this needs to be in the standard as opposed to a library, nor any evidence of implementation experience.
- The complete silence on interoperability and coordination with existing or planned execution facilities is the most glaring omission.
