Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a clear opening rationale for why its topic matters to latency-sensitive domains, but beyond that initial motivation most of the standardization case rests on assertions rather than demonstrated evidence. The thinnest areas are the lack of concrete implementation experience and the repeated reuse of a few broad statements to cover several distinct burdens of proof.

- The strongest support is the paper’s explanation that P2300-style allocation patterns are incompatible with sub-microsecond, zero-allocation networking requirements.
- The paper asserts interest from financial firms and SG14, but does not show who exactly is blocked today or how widely the need is felt.
- The discussion of alternatives such as P4003 or Asio-style direct models gestures at prior art without comparing trade-offs in enough depth to justify standardization.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of implementation experience, prototype results, or real-world usage data for the proposed direction.
