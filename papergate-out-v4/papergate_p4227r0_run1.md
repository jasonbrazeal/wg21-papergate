Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only a modest amount of support for its own standardization, with the strongest material going to prior art and alternatives. Most of the core argument is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the absence of implementation experience leaves the proposal’s practical viability largely unexamined.

- The paper’s discussion of prior art, including the proposed `cezstring_view` and its relationship to `cstring_view`, is the most fully developed part of the case.
- The motivations involving C APIs and null-terminated strings are repeatedly invoked but not substantiated with concrete evidence of need.
- The paper does not establish who would actually be affected beyond rare and narrow examples, weakening the urgency for standardization.
- No implementation experience is provided, leaving the most basic question about feasibility unanswered.
