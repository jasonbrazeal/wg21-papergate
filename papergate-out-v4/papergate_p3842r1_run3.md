Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only incidental support for its own standardization, relying heavily on references to other documents and recent committee discussion rather than building its own case. The thinnest areas are the absence of any argument for why standards action is required, how the change would interoperate, or what implementation experience exists.

- The paper’s strongest support is its framing of the rollback as a prudent response to a breaking change, though even that is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- It gestures at prior art through P3818 and P3820, but leaves the explanatory burden to those papers.
- It offers no account of why the standard, rather than a library or other mechanism, is the right vehicle.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of implementation experience or coordination evidence for the proposed direction.
