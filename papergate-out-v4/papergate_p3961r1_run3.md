Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case needed for standardization: it demonstrates a real compilation problem and shows a working implementation, but it leaves most of the surrounding justification unstated. The thinnest areas are the lack of any identified audience, any discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient, and any account of how the proposal would interact with existing standard facilities.

- The strongest support comes from a concrete implementation, which at least shows the design is viable in practice.
- The paper also establishes that there is a genuine compile-time gap users can hit today.
- A prior art point is gestured at through a compiler example, but the paper does not develop it into an actual comparison or rationale.
- Most notably, the document never says who is affected, why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, or how it would fit with related standardization work.
