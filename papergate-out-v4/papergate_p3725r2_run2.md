Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a narrow but genuine case that current filter views create practical hazards for ordinary users, yet it stops well short of building a standardization argument around that point. The support is thinnest in the areas that would justify taking this to the committee rather than shipping a library or evolving existing practice: there is no identified affected population, no reason the standard is the right venue, and no evidence of implementation experience beyond a single example.

- The strongest support is the established claim that basic filter-view use cases are broken or risky, which grounds the proposal in real programmer difficulty.
- The paper cites prior discussion and a possible renaming effort, but does not establish enough prior art or alternative designs to show a settled direction.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this cannot be handled outside the standard.
