Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for its own standardization, with solid treatment of prior art and alternatives but little else carried beyond assertion. The support is especially sparse around why a library cannot provide the facility and around concrete implementation experience.

- The strongest part of the paper is its engagement with prior art and alternatives, including P3919R0, P3911, and the deliberate separation from P2900 assertions.
- The paper claims benefits for safety, optimization, and tooling, but does not establish those benefits beyond stating them.
- The discussion of affected users and implementation experience is asserted rather than demonstrated with usable detail.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any case for why a library solution would not suffice.
