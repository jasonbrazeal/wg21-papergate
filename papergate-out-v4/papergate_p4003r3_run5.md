Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers solid grounding for its motivation, prior art, and implementation experience, but its case for why standardization specifically is necessary remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support is in the areas that matter most for a standards-track proposal: why a library cannot suffice, who concretely benefits, and how the feature coordinates with existing standard and non-standard async models.

- The strongest support is the concrete, three-platform implementation experience, which shows the protocol is real and usable.
- The paper also clearly establishes the design context through prior art and a companion rationale document, giving readers a path to the deeper analysis.
- What is most glaringly missing is a demonstrated argument that a library implementation is insufficient, since the paper repeats that claim without showing what breaks or becomes impossible in a library-only form.
