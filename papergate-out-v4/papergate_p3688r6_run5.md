Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper shows real evidence of implementation experience and some awareness of alternatives, but it leaves several central justifications asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts concern the actual need for standardization, the affected audience, and why existing library practice cannot address the problem adequately.

- The strongest support is the concrete prior art and implementation experience, including the µlight implementation and the CompilerExplorer prototype.
- The paper identifies prior approaches such as `std::locale` overloads and clarifies some design choices, though not enough to establish a standardization need.
- The claims about who is affected and why the problem matters do not move much beyond assertion, with little evidence of widespread pain or user demand.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of coordination and interoperability discussion, leaving the proposal disconnected from related standardization efforts.
