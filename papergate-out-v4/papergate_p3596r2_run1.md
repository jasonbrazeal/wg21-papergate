Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper provides only partial support for its own standardization, with its strongest showing in implementation experience and much weaker or absent treatment of who benefits, why the standard is the right venue, and how the work fits with adjacent specifications. The thinnest areas are not minor gaps but missing sections of the case entirely.

- The clearest support comes from collaborative drafting against the working draft, which at least demonstrates the proposed annex wording exists in a standard-like form.
- The paper asserts that cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior is useful and that a categorized structure helps readers, but this remains a claim rather than a demonstrated need.
- It mentions prior attempts and related work but does not show how this proposal improves on or resolves problems with those alternatives.
- The document does not identify who is affected, why a library cannot address the need, or how the proposal coordinates with other standards and implementations.
