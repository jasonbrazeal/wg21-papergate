Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers almost no affirmative case for standardizing what it proposes, resting entirely on the fact that it supplies replacement wording for two earlier papers. That is not enough to show what problem is being solved, who benefits, or why the solution belongs in the standard rather than in a library or separate specification.

- The only thread of support is the identification of two prior papers whose wording this paper would replace, which at least gestures toward continuity with earlier discussion.
- Nothing in the paper establishes why the rename or namespace change matters to users or implementers.
- The paper does not show why the standard is the right venue, or why a library-level or project-level convention would not suffice.
- Most glaringly, there is no evidence of implementation experience, coordination with affected work, or consideration of alternatives beyond the two named papers.
