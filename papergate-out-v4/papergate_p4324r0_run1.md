Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, resting most heavily on its reported GCC fork implementation. Beyond that, the discussion of motivation, affected users, alternatives, and the need for a standard rather than a library remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The document reads more like an exploratory design note than a fully justified standardization case.

- The strongest element is the reported implementation experience in a GCC fork, which shows the design has been put into practice.
- The paper claims connections to existing proposals and committee work, but does not substantiate how those alternatives were weighed or why this approach improves on them.
- The argument for why a library or third-party solution would not suffice is asserted only in passing.
- The most glaring omission is any treatment of coordination and interoperability with the existing contracts work or other standardization efforts.
