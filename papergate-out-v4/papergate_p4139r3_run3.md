Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some useful context about naming and the history of prior discussion, but it leaves most of the case for standardization unexamined. The thinnest parts concern exactly the questions a proposal needs to answer: why this belongs in the standard, what implementation experience exists, and how the feature would interoperate with the rest of the library.

- The strongest support is the discussion of why the name `get` would be inconsistent with existing uses of `get` in the standard library.
- The paper also credibly documents the prior alternatives considered in P3091 and why some of those names failed to gain consensus.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established argument for why this feature requires standardization rather than a library solution.
