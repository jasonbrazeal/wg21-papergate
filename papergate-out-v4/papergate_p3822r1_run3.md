Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a thin case for standardization: its only fully established element is that a Clang fork implements the feature, while the motivation, prior art, and library-workaround claims are asserted rather than demonstrated. The absence of any discussion of who is affected, why a standard change is required, or how the feature interacts with the wider language leaves the standardization argument largely unsupported.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with a concrete Clang fork and linked examples showing the proposed syntax in use.
- The motivation is only claimed, resting on a general statement that conditional non-throwing assertions are sometimes needed and that current workarounds usually require duplication.
- The paper offers no established account of the affected audience, the need for a standard rather than a compiler extension or library technique, or coordination with existing language features.
