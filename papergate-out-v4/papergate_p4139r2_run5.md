Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers some support for its own standardization, chiefly by explaining why the proposed name matters and why earlier naming alternatives were unsatisfactory. The thinnest parts are the absence of evidence about who is affected, why the facility belongs in the standard rather than a library, and whether anyone has implementation experience with the design.

- The paper establishes that the naming choice has real consequences and that prior alternatives from P3091 failed to reach consensus or have since been reconsidered.
- It also establishes a conceptual reason that sequence-container lookup functions would improve consistency with associative containers.
- The paper does not establish who would use the facility or what practical problem they currently face.
- It is silent on why a library solution would be insufficient and on whether the proposed interface has been implemented or exercised.
