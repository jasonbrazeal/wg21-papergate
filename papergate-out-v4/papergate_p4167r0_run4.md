Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some useful groundwork by identifying an alternative to P2822R2 and providing a prototype, but it falls well short of demonstrating that the proposed direction has a clear constituency, a problem the standard must solve, or a plausible path through the standard’s own requirements. The thinnest areas are the almost complete absence of discussion about who is affected and why library-only or existing mechanisms cannot already achieve the stated goals.

- The strongest support is the concrete compiler-explorer prototype, which shows at least preliminary implementation experience with the idea.
- The paper also establishes some prior art and alternative framing by explicitly distinguishing its approach from P2822R2 and from adding CNTTP to ADL.
- A significant omission is that the paper does not establish who is actually affected by the problem or how widespread the need is.
- The most glaring gap is the absence of any case for why the standard, rather than a library or existing annotation practice, is required to address the issue.
