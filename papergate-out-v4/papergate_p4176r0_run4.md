Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin account of its own standardization case, with even the potential motivating language framed as an aim rather than a demonstrated need. The support is thinnest around the basic questions of who is affected, why the standard is the right vehicle, and whether any implementation experience exists.

- The clearest, though still tentative, support is the stated intention to improve the specification by introducing new nonterminals in the relevant subclauses.
- The paper does not establish who would use the proposed change or what practical problem they currently face.
- The paper does not explain why standardization is necessary or why a library-level solution would not suffice.
- The paper gives no implementation experience, coordination considerations, or discussion of alternatives beyond the same brief statement of intent.
