Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is thin, resting mostly on gestures toward implementation flexibility rather than a demonstrated need for a standard feature. The case is weakest where it matters most: there is no discussion of who is affected, why standardization is necessary, how the feature would coordinate with existing specifications, or what implementation experience exists.

- The clearest support comes from the observation that the proposal could coexist with related work such as P2900, allowing implementations to enable the two separately or in combination.
- The motivation section explicitly declines to make the case for why the feature is needed, leaving the paper’s central rationale unestablished.
- The paper gives no account of affected users, required interoperability, existing practice, or why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving most of the standardization case entirely unaddressed.
