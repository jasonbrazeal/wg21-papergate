Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a naming concern that is asserted rather than argued, and it does not address the practical or procedural questions that would justify committee action. The support is thinnest where a proposal normally needs to be strongest: establishing who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the change is implementable or interoperable.

- The strongest support is the assertion that the current “to_input” name is misleading, though even this remains an opinion rather than a demonstrated problem.
- The paper gestures at prior naming conventions like “as_const” or “as_rvalue,” but does not connect that convention to why it must be adopted here.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any stated affected audience or evidence of real-world confusion that standardization would resolve.
