Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper rests almost entirely on repeated assertions about user demand and widespread implementation failure, offering very little concrete evidence for most of the standardization case. The only solid support is the existence of a reference implementation, but the surrounding argument for why that implementation belongs in the standard is largely unsubstantiated.

- Implementation experience is the strongest point, with a complete repository available for review.
- The need for a standard library facility is asserted through anecdotes about common failure but never demonstrated with examples or data.
- Coordination with existing libraries and alternatives receives only a passing dismissal rather than a comparison.
- The most glaring omission is any concrete evidence of who is affected or how often the problem actually arises in practice.
