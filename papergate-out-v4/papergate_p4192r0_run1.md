Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization case: it shows that the relevant subclauses have open issues and that at least one implementation deviates from the current wording. Most of the remaining support is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the case thins almost entirely around library alternatives and implementation experience.

- The strongest support is that the paper identifies multiple open Core issues in the alignment subclauses, which establishes a real specification problem worth attention.
- The paper points to divergent behavior among major compilers, suggesting there is an interoperability concern even if the scope and impact are not fully established.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper does not establish why a library solution cannot address the problem, nor does it show meaningful implementation experience beyond a single compiler example.
