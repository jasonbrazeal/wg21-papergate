Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some grounding for its motivation and acknowledges prior discussion and alternatives, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The support is thinnest where it matters most for a standard library change: demonstrating who is affected, why existing non-standard solutions are insufficient, and whether the design has been exercised in practice.

- The strongest support is the paper’s engagement with prior `affine_on` discussion and its identification of specification concerns from P3796R1.
- The treatment of alternatives is partially developed, noting a discontinued proposal and some subtle behavioral differences from `continues_on`.
- The rationale for standardization rather than a user-level or third-party library solution is asserted but not substantiated.
- The paper offers no established account of the affected audience or of implementation experience, leaving the practical case for standardization largely undemonstrated.
