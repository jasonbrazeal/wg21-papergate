Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with the strongest grounding in its identification of prior art and alternatives, while the rest of its case is largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the failure to establish who is affected, how the feature would coordinate with existing practice, and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The paper gives a usable account of related proposals and prior discussions, including rejected alternatives and disputed performance comparisons.
- The paper asserts that the problems in P3937 matter for standardization, but does not show concretely why the affected audience or use cases are significant enough to warrant the change.
- The paper does not establish coordination and interoperability with existing implementations, tooling, or adjacent language features.
- The paper claims implementation experience with the removal from clang, but offers no evidence that the proposed direction has been implemented or validated in practice.
