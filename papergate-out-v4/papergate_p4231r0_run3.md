Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only scattered claims for its own standardization, with most of the necessary support left asserted rather than demonstrated and no material at all on coordination, interoperability, library alternatives, or implementation experience beyond a hope for a future proof-of-concept.

- The clearest support appears in the link to prior work, since the proposal is described as basically identical to P2746 with an added IEEE conformance constraint.
- The motivation and affected audience are only gestured at, leaving the importance and reach of the proposed facility uncertain.
- The case for standardizing rather than shipping a library is entirely absent, as is any account of how the feature would interact with existing interfaces and implementations.
