Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gives the impression of a useful reference effort but does not, on its own, make much of a case for being adopted as a standard. The thinnest parts are the basic justifications: why this belongs in the standard, who specifically needs it there, and why an external library or document cannot serve the purpose.

- The strongest support is that the proposed annex wording exists as a collaborative branch of the working draft, showing at least some implementation experience.
- The paper points to prior attempts and existing proposals, but it does not show that those efforts failed for reasons the standard can fix.
- It asserts that stable names would help coordinate future mitigation work, without establishing who would rely on that vocabulary or how.
- The paper never establishes who is affected by the absence of this annex or why the standard is the necessary home for the catalog.
