Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper puts real weight behind its motivating claims and shows awareness of relevant prior work, but the case for standardization itself remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of implementation experience, library-only alternatives, or coordination with existing or neighboring standards efforts.

- The paper clearly articulates why profile invalidation matters and what goals it is trying to achieve regarding provable safety and reducing false positives.
- It situates itself credibly against prior work such as P1179 and borrow-checker approaches, and adopts useful definitions from related proposals.
- The claim about implementation experience rests on a passing remark about well-maintained code, with no supporting evidence or detail about actual use.
- The paper is silent on why a library solution would not suffice and offers no discussion of coordination or interoperability with the broader ecosystem.
