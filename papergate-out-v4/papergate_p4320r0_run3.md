Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper leans almost entirely on a single implementation in Nvidia’s stdexec for its evidence, and even that support is only asserted rather than developed into a real case for standardization. The thinnest areas are the basic justifications for standardizing at all: why the facility matters, why it belongs in the standard rather than a library, and what problem the standard would be solving for users.

- The strongest, though still limited, support is the repeated citation of stdexec as an existing provider of the algorithm.
- The paper gestures toward prior art with references to libunifex and P2300, but does not explain how those relate to the need for standardization.
- The most conspicuous gaps are the absence of any established rationale for why the feature matters or why the standard, rather than a library, is the right home for it.
