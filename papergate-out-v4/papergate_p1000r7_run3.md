Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only very limited support for its own standardization, largely through assertions about dissatisfaction with the current release model. Most of the necessary case is absent, with no concrete evidence about affected users, implementation experience, interoperability, or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is a stated rationale for fixed-interval IS releases and a preference against returning to the pre-2012 model, though even this is presented as experience-based assertion rather than evidence.
- The paper gestures at negative consequences of the current process, such as fragmentation and delayed integration testing, but does not substantiate those claims.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any demonstrated implementation experience, leaving the proposal with no practical validation.
