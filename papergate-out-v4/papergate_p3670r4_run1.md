The paper offers only thin support for its own standardization, resting largely on references to related work and an assertion that implementation should be easy. The thinnest areas are the basic justification for standardizing the feature at all, the explanation of who needs it and why, and any demonstration that a library solution would be inadequate.

- The strongest support is the mention of related proposals and CWG3027 as context for the design, though it does not establish that these alternatives were actually compared.
- The paper claims the feature matters because pack indexing in C++26 received positive feedback, but it does not connect that feedback concretely to this proposal’s problem or constituency.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper provides no established reason the feature belongs in the standard rather than in a library or existing language mechanism.
