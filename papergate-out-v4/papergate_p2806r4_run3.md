Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete support for standardization, particularly through implementation experience and acknowledgment of prior art, but its case remains uneven. The thinnest areas are the absence of a direct argument for why a standard mechanism is needed and weak evidence about who would be affected or why a library solution will not suffice.

- The paper points to an existing clang implementation and clearly references prior work in pattern matching and other languages.
- The motivation is grounded in specific use cases such as desugaring control flow operators and supporting pattern matching arms.
- The paper asserts broad usefulness and coordination with other proposals but does not demonstrate the affected user base or interoperability impact.
- The paper never directly explains why this must be addressed in the C++ standard itself rather than through existing or library-level approaches.
