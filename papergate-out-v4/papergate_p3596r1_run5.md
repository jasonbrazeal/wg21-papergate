Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a narrow basis for confidence in its own standardization: its wording has been developed in the working draft, but the surrounding argument for why this catalog belongs in the standard, who it serves, and why other approaches are insufficient remains largely asserted rather than shown.

- The most concrete support is that the proposed annex wording exists as a collaborative branch of the C++ working draft, which at least demonstrates a form of implementation experience.
- The paper gestures toward prior proposals and a hoped-for stable vocabulary, but it does not develop those references into an actual case that standardization is the right home for this information.
- The affected audience is never identified, so the paper does not connect its catalog to any concrete user, implementer, or committee need.
- The paper never addresses why a non-standard library, reference document, or other external resource would not serve the stated purpose just as well.
