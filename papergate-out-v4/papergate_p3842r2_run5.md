Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only the barest scaffolding of a case for standardization, with most of the burden carried by references to other documents rather than by its own argument. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why the standard is the right mechanism, and whether implementation experience exists.

- The paper’s strongest support is its single concrete claim that making certain functions constexpr would be a breaking change in some cases.
- The paper gestures toward prior art and alternatives by citing two other papers that explain the background.
- The paper does not establish who would be affected by the proposed change.
- The paper offers no account of implementation experience, interoperability concerns, or why a library solution would not suffice.
