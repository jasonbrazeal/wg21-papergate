Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case needed for standardization: it convincingly shows a concrete compile failure and points to an implementation, but leaves the affected audience, the standardization rationale, and the interoperability story essentially unaddressed. The thinnest areas are those that would tell the committee who this change serves and why a library solution or existing practice cannot already cover that need.

- The strongest support is the demonstrated implementation experience, with a linked branch showing the proposed change in code.
- The paper establishes a concrete motivating symptom by noting that `r1 = r2` does not compile without the proposal.
- The treatment of prior art and alternatives is asserted rather than shown, with the author asking for real-world evidence instead of providing it.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established account of who is affected, leaving the proposal without a clear constituency or impact statement.
