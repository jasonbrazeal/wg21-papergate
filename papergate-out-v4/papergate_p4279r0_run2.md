Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a narrow but genuine case that the Endian Views direction is inadequate, but it does little to establish that the alternative it gestures toward belongs in the standard. Most of the burden of proof is left unaddressed, with especially thin coverage of affected users, interoperability, implementation experience, and why standardization is needed at all.

- The paper’s strongest support is its argument that serialization typically combines endian adjustment with other transformations, which it treats as a reason to reject the views-only approach.
- Its discussion of prior art and alternatives is only gestured at, since it raises questions about existing library practice and transform-based substitutes without answering them.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any demonstration of implementation experience, leaving the practical viability of the proposed direction entirely unsupported.
