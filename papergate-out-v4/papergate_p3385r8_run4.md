Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a partial case for standardization, but its strongest support is concentrated in implementation experience and connections to existing reflection work, while much of the rationale remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of a defined audience and any argument that the need cannot be met by a library solution.

- The paper’s implementation experience is concrete, with a working prototype on Compiler Explorer and discussion of a real design choice around expression canonicalization.
- Its engagement with prior art is clear, particularly in tying the mechanism to P4033R0’s need to carry attributes through reflection for synthesized enumerators.
- The paper asserts that a uniform attribute vehicle would save committee time across future proposals, but it does not establish who specifically is affected by the current limitation.
- The proposal offers no reasoning for why a library-level approach would be insufficient, leaving a central part of the standardization case unaddressed.
