Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers very little concrete support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on assertions and brief references rather than demonstrated need or evidence. The thinnest areas are the absence of established prior art, implementation experience, and interoperability impact, all of which are reduced to a passing note that Clang’s attribute system diverges in some unspecified way.

- The strongest support is the claim that ABI changes would be required either way, which at least names a standardization-relevant consequence.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, beyond the author’s expectation that use would be “rather limited and specialized.”
- The most glaring omission is implementation experience: a single link to Clang documentation does not show that the proposed direction has been tried, validated, or found workable in practice.
