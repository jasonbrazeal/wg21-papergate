Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a narrow, clear rationale for renaming saturation arithmetic functions, but only weakly connects that rationale to the broader case for standardization. Its strongest material is limited to naming familiarity and consistency, while the questions an adoptable standard would need to answer remain largely unaddressed.

- The paper establishes that the term `saturating` is clearer than `sat` and that the choice matters for future naming policy.
- It claims some external precedent and interoperability benefits, but the evidence is mostly anecdotal and not developed into a standardization argument.
- It does not establish why this belongs in the C++ standard rather than in a library or written guidance.
- It offers no implementation experience to show that the proposed naming is workable inside a standard library specification.
