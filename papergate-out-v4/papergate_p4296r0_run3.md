Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives useful grounding for why the problem matters and shows awareness of related approaches, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who would actually be affected, why a library solution cannot suffice, and evidence of implementation experience.

- The strongest part of the case is the paper’s clear motivation for reducing false positives while pursuing provable safety, including attention to profile invalidation.
- The discussion of prior work is substantive, particularly the relationship to P1179R1’s Lifetime Profile and the claimed advantages over borrow-checker approaches.
- The need for a standard rather than a library is not supported at all, leaving a central justification unexplained.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the proposed change, making the scope and constituency of the proposal unclear.
