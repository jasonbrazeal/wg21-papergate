Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only assertions rather than a developed case for standardization, and the support is thinnest around the questions that matter most: why the feature is needed, what problem it solves for users, and why it belongs in the standard rather than elsewhere. Most of what appears is context about related proposals or confidence about implementability, not evidence that the feature has been demanded, designed against alternatives, or coordinated with the wider language.

- The strongest element is a brief reference to adjacent pack-indexing work having been implemented and well received, though it does not extend to this proposal itself.
- The paper notes compatibility with other in-flight proposals, but this is stated as observation rather than demonstrated coordination.
- The most glaring omission is any substantive motivation for the feature, leaving its importance and intended audience unestablished.
