Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a solid conceptual foundation for why the consteval-only value model is worth pursuing, and it grounds that motivation in prior reflection work and known core-language difficulties. The case is thinnest when it moves from explaining the idea to showing that the idea belongs in the standard, since material on affected users, implementability, and why a library cannot suffice is either asserted or absent.

- The strongest support is the paper’s connection to the reflection design and its documented alternative approaches, which gives readers a clear sense of where the proposal comes from and what it changes.
- The discussion of why the rule matters is also well supported by concrete examples and references to existing core issues and prior models.
- The least developed area is coordination and interoperability, where the paper offers no evidence that the proposed change has been checked against other features or implementations.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this cannot be handled as a library facility or through existing mechanisms.
