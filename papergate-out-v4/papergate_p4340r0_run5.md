Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper provides only a thin evidentiary basis for its own standardization, with real implementation experience but little else beyond assertions about motivation and prior work. Its support is thinnest where a proposal needs to be concrete about the affected audience, interoperability, and why the feature cannot be delivered through a library.

- The clearest strength is implementation experience, demonstrated by a working Clang fork and an accessible Compiler Explorer link.
- The paper gestures at why the feature matters and at prior work, but the relevance and drawbacks of those earlier efforts are asserted rather than shown.
- The discussion of why this belongs in the standard relies on a passing technical remark rather than a developed rationale.
- The paper does not establish who is affected or how the feature would coordinate with existing and adjacent standardization work.
