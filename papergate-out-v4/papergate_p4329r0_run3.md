Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization, with its strongest grounding in existing implementation experience but little direct argument for why this facility belongs in the standard rather than in a library. The thinnest areas are the absence of any case for standardization as such and the lack of coordination or interoperability discussion.

- The paper can point to Nvidia’s stdexec shipping `exec::variant_sender` as concrete implementation experience.
- The paper claims but does not establish who is affected beyond a general appeal to the usefulness of branching constructs.
- The paper gestures at prior art and a library-based obstacle, but does not establish why a library solution is inadequate or what standardizing would add.
