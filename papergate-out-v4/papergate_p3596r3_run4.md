Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some useful context and confirms that a collaborative drafting branch exists, but it leaves the central case for standardization largely implicit. Its thinnest support is in explaining why the standard itself is the right vehicle and why a non-normative library or reference document would not serve the purpose.

- The prior art and alternatives section is the strongest part, showing awareness of earlier efforts and existing categorization work.
- The implementation experience note is concrete, pointing to a collaboratively developed draft branch.
- The claims about usefulness and affected readers rest on general assertions rather than demonstrated need.
- The paper does not establish why standardization is required or why a library or external catalog would be insufficient.
