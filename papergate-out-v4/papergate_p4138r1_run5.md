Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some concrete implementation evidence and a historical trail, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The support is thinnest where the proposal should explain why only a core language change will do, what standardization-level problem exists, and how the affected audience experiences it.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, where the paper cites agreement among implementations on most cases and notes Clang’s existing rejection of the overloads in question.
- The paper gestures at why the change matters through examples and overload-resolution consequences, but it stops short of establishing the practical impact.
- Prior art and alternatives are only sketched through a historical account of ref-qualifiers and `basic.scope.scope`, without weighing other possible directions.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established argument for why the standard is the right venue, or why a library-level or non-standard solution would be insufficient.
