Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gives a serviceable account of why the inconsistency around alias-like types and temporaries is worth addressing, and it points clearly to prior efforts and alternatives. The support thins out considerably, however, when the discussion moves to the specific need for standard wording and to evidence about implementation experience or the limits of a library-only approach.

- The strongest support is the motivation: the paper clearly identifies a real inconsistency in how references and similar alias types are treated with respect to temporaries.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is specific, showing how earlier proposals and existing library components only partially address the problem.
- The case for why this must be solved in the standard, rather than in a library, is asserted but not substantiated.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established information about who is affected or about implementation experience with the proposed direction.
