Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides a thin evidentiary basis for its own standardization, with the strongest material concentrated in implementation experience. Much of the rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the thinnest areas concern why this change belongs in the standard rather than in a library or alternative mechanism, as well as how it would coordinate with existing practice.

- The paper’s strongest support is the existence of a Clang fork implementation and a link to a more complete type-erasure example.
- The motivation and affected audience are only claimed, with brief references to generic programming needs and a compiler diagnostic rather than a developed case.
- The argument for consistency with function declarations and the absence of prior rejection is asserted but not backed by a recorded discussion or alternative analysis.
- The paper does not establish that a library solution is insufficient or that this change requires standardization rather than remaining an implementation extension.
