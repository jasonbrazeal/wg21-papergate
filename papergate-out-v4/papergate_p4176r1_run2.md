Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin rationale for its own standardization, mostly limited to statements of intent about moving grammar definitions and introducing new non-terminals. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, what alternatives exist, why the standard is the right venue, and whether there has been any implementation experience.

- The clearest support is the stated goal of improving the specification by relocating grammar definitions into their relevant subclauses.
- The paper gestures at the intended restructuring of *postfix-expression* grammar, but does not explain how this coordinates with existing wording or affects implementers.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any prior art, alternative design discussion, or rationale for why a non-standard approach would not suffice.
