The paper gives a partial account of why its change is needed, mainly by connecting the removal of `affine_on` to prior discussion and dissatisfaction with the existing specification. Its strongest support concerns motivation and alternatives, while the case for standardization remains thin around affected users, implementability, and the limits of a library-only solution. - The paper establishes a clear motivation by tying the proposed change to documented concerns and prior discussion of `affine_on`.
- It shows familiarity with prior art and alternatives, including the earlier `continues_on` approach and national body comments.
- The paper claims implementation experience only indirectly, without demonstrating meaningful use or validation of the revised design.
- It does not establish why the standard, rather than a library or existing implementation practice, should carry this behavior.
