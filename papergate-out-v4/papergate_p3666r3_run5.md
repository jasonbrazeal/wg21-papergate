Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper offers considerable support for standardization, particularly in explaining why a language feature is needed and how it would interoperate with C23 and existing practice. The evidence is thinnest around who would be affected, where the paper asserts broad relevance and compiler support but does not fully establish the scope or practical uptake.

- The case for why a library type would not suffice is grounded in concrete examples involving C function overloads and bit-fields.
- The proposal connects its design to C23 requirements and demonstrates existing Clang implementation experience.
- Coordination and interoperability concerns are addressed through discussion of ABI portability and the risks of divergent user-defined spellings.
- The paper does not substantiate its claims about the size or needs of the affected user population beyond general statements about compiler extensions.
