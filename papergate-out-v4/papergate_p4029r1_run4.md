Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers some grounding in why this constituency cares about safety without runtime overhead, but it does not establish who is affected, why standardization is needed, or what implementation experience exists. Most of the argument remains at the level of assertion rather than demonstrated case.

- The strongest support is the connection to SG14’s well-defined latency and determinism constraints, which gives the paper a clear reason to exist.
- The discussion of prior work gestures toward alternatives, but it does not actually establish what those earlier proposals did or why they failed.
- The paper claims that a library solution will not do, yet it does not show what architectural requirements a library cannot satisfy.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who specifically is affected and how standardization would change their situation.
