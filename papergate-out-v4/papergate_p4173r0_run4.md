Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only scattered support for its own standardization, with a single concrete implementation reported as the only fully established element. The case is thinnest around who would be affected, why the standard library specifically must act, and what alternatives already satisfy the need.

- The most solid grounding is the author’s reported libstdc++-based implementation, which shows the feature can be realized in practice.
- The paper gestures at motivating use cases and alignment with prior directions, but does not convincingly establish why the problem matters broadly or why existing mechanisms are insufficient.
- It never identifies the affected users or constituencies, leaving the scope and urgency of the need unclear.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not establish why standardization, rather than a library or existing facilities, is necessary at all.
