Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides only a thin evidentiary basis for its own standardization, with much of the case gestured at rather than demonstrated. The most developed claims concern prior art and implementation intent, but even these stop short of showing that the feature is needed in the standard.

- The paper most concretely ties itself to prior work, noting its near-identity to P2746 and its relationship to an already-agreed niche rounding-mode facility.
- Its treatment of why the feature matters is grounded mainly in ambiguity about the current standard’s rounding requirements, but it does not settle whether that ambiguity actually creates a practical problem.
- The paper’s implementation experience amounts to a hope for a future existence-proof, without evidence of an existing implementation to validate the design.
- It offers no meaningful account of who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, how the feature inter-operates with other specifications, or why a library solution would be inadequate.
