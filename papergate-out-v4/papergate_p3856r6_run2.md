Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers solid support for the core need it identifies: the standard already depends on structural types in mandates and non-type template parameters, yet users have no way to query that property. The thinnest parts are the absence of any demonstrated user population and the lack of evidence that a library solution is insufficient, both of which leave the standardization case underdeveloped.

- The strongest point is the clear statement that structural types already matter throughout the standard and library, but no query exists for them.
- The paper claims, without fully establishing, that reflection-based metafunctions are a promising alternative to prior techniques.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper provides no evidence about who is affected by the missing query.
