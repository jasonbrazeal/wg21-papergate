Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some credible motivation and a reasonable survey of existing alternatives, but its case remains largely assertive rather than demonstrated, with much of the standardization need resting on claims that are not backed by concrete evidence. The thinnest areas are implementation experience, which is entirely absent, and the arguments for why a library solution cannot suffice, which are stated rather than shown.

- The strongest support comes from the established prior-art discussion, which situates the proposal against related efforts and current workarounds.
- The motivation is articulated clearly enough to show why the capability could matter in principle.
- The claims about affected users, the need for standard behavior, interoperability, and the inadequacy of library approaches are all asserted without being concretely established.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of implementation experience, leaving the proposal without any practical validation.
