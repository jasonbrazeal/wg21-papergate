Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers meaningful support in a few areas, particularly in articulating the semantic ambiguity that motivates the work and in showing some implementation experience. However, the case for standardization is thin where it matters most: the affected audience, the necessity of a language-level change, and the coordination story are largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The explanation of why transparent wrapper inconsistencies are a real design problem is clear and credible.
- The implementation work and detection logic for `function_ref` unwrapping show that the idea is technically feasible.
- The claim that this must be standardized, rather than handled in a library, is not substantiated beyond stating the ambiguity.
- The paper never establishes who is actually affected by this issue or how existing practice is hindered in a way that standard intervention would resolve.
