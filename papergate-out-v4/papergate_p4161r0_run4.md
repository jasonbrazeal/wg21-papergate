Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper grounds its case in a single, clearly articulated linguistic point, but offers little beyond that assertion to justify a change to the standard library. The strongest material concerns why the issue matters as a matter of English grammar, while the rest of the standardization rationale is largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper’s core claim about `std::less` being grammatically inappropriate for countable integral types is established and gives the proposal its clearest motivation.
- The claim that this affects a majority of production codebases is asserted without evidence, leaving the breadth of impact unproven.
- The argument that the standard is the right place to fix it rests only on the statement that allowing the error to persist is worse, with no deeper rationale for committee action.
- The paper offers no implementation experience, no interoperability discussion, and no explanation of why a library-level remedy would be insufficient.
