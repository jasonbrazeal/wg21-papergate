Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin account of its own need for standardization, with most of the necessary justification left implicit or entirely unaddressed. The strongest material concerns the relationship to prior and concurrent work, while the rationale for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a separate catalogue or library is essentially absent.

- The paper does point to specific prior art and explains a change made to keep an example compatible with other work.
- The claim that the prior catalogue was instantly made incomplete by concurrent papers is asserted but not developed into a broader case.
- The document does not establish whose work is affected by the gap it identifies or why a non-standard remedy would be inadequate.
- It offers no implementation experience, no interoperability analysis, and no explanation of why the standard is the right home for the material.
