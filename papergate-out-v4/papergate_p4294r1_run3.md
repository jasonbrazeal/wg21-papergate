Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers concrete support in a few important places, but it leaves several threshold questions about standardization essentially unargued, particularly around who is affected and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The clearest strength is the identification of a real expressiveness gap in the current range adaptors, with a concrete composition workaround shown.
- The prior-art discussion credibly covers existing practice and correctly notes where a new iterator, rather than a simple composition, would be necessary.
- The thinnest part is the absence of any established case for why the standard library, rather than a third-party or in-house library component, is the right home for this facility.
