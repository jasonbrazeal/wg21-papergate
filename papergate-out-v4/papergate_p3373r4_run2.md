Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers solid evidence of implementation experience and some useful references to prior art, but it mostly asserts rather than demonstrates the broader case for standardization. The argument is thinnest in connecting the lifetime-management problem to a need that cannot be met by existing libraries or by continued evolution outside the standard.

- The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience in libunifex and nVidia’s stdexec, which shows the proposed strategy is already in real use.
- The discussion of prior art is also reasonably established, identifying existing practice in libunifex and a reference implementation extension.
- The paper is much weaker in showing who is affected beyond the cited implementations, since those passages are credited only as claims rather than established impact.
- The most glaring omission is the failure to establish why standardization, rather than a library solution or existing practice, is necessary or how this would coordinate with broader standardization efforts.
