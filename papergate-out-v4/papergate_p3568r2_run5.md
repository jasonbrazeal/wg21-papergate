Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper makes a compelling case in some foundational respects, particularly by rooting the proposal in existing C2y precedent and implementation experience, but it leaves too much of the audience and process argument resting on assertion rather than demonstrated evidence. The thinnest support surrounds the claims about community demand and the impossibility of a library-based workaround.

- The proposal establishes that the feature has clear prior art, an accepted C syntax, and a working GCC implementation, which together form its strongest grounding.
- It establishes that C and C++ coordination is a live concern, with WG14 already committed to the syntax and unwilling to revisit it.
- It does not establish who is concretely affected, since the cited WG21 sentiment, StackOverflow views, and Reddit feedback are presented as evidence without supporting detail.
- Its most glaring omission is a convincing argument for why a library or existing language mechanism cannot adequately address the need.
