Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers substantial support for the core problem and the need for a standard-level fix, particularly through its concrete analysis of the protocol change and its implementation experience. The support becomes noticeably thinner when the paper generalizes about who is affected, how libraries would coordinate, and why the change cannot be handled outside the standard, since those points are asserted rather than demonstrated with evidence.

- The strongest support lies in the established explanation of why the current sender protocol prevents symmetric transfer and why a standard change is required.
- The paper also credibly establishes prior art and a concrete protocol-level alternative through draft wording and references to P2300R10.
- The least supported claims concern the breadth of adoption across major coroutine libraries and the assertion that a library-level remedy is inadequate, both of which are stated without sufficient substantiation.
