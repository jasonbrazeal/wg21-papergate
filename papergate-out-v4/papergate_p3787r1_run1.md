Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin basis for its own standardization, mainly by asserting that this is a small corrective oversight aligned with an already-adopted proposal. The strongest grounding is the claimed continuity with P2248R8, but even that remains an assertion rather than a demonstrated need, and the rest of the necessary case is largely absent.

- The paper’s clearest support is its claim that the proposed change simply extends P2248R8’s adopted design to a family of algorithms that was inadvertently left out.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the omission or what practical problem the missing defaulted template parameter causes for users.
- The paper offers no discussion of why a library-level solution would be inadequate or why standardization is preferable to other remedies.
- The paper provides no implementation experience beyond a vague reference to existing P2248R8 implementations, and no evidence of coordination with implementers or other proposals.
