Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides some useful motivation and prior-art discussion, but it leaves several essential aspects of the standardization case either asserted rather than demonstrated or entirely unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns why library-level solutions are insufficient and why the change belongs in the standard itself.

- The paper’s strongest support is its motivation, particularly the demonstrated problem with `when_all` requesting stops that will never be used.
- Prior art is credited mainly through one implementation’s workaround and observations attributed to Lewis Baker, though this remains narrow.
- Claims about affected users and implementation experience rely on limited references and do not establish general impact or robust real-world validation.
- The case for standardization itself is not established, as the paper does not show why the problem cannot be adequately handled outside the standard or through existing mechanisms.
