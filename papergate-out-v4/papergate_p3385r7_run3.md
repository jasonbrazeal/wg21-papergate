Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a broad but largely rhetorical case for standardization, leaning on general statements about attribute use and the promise of uniform infrastructure rather than concrete evidence or worked examples. The support is thinnest where the paper needs to show why a library cannot address the problem and what real-world implementation experience demonstrates.

- The strongest support is the claim of an experimental implementation available on Compiler Explorer, though the paper does not connect it to a demonstrated need for standardization.
- The discussion of prior work, particularly P2237R0, gestures toward relevant context but does not establish that the proposed approach follows from or improves upon it in a standardizable way.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving a central justification for standardization unaddressed.
