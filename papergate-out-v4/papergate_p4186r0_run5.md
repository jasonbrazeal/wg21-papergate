Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only the beginnings of a case for standardization, mostly through repeated references to missing shared documentation and a short list of prior, unsuccessful profiles-related proposals. The support is thinnest where it matters most: who would be affected, why a library cannot solve the problem, how implementation experience informs the design, and what coordination or interoperability would be required.

- The strongest support, such as it is, lies in the claim that past profiles proposals failed and that WG21 lacks agreed-on documentation about what profiles can and cannot do, though even that is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The paper asserts that profiles are “the best way we know” to achieve its aims, but it does not establish why standardization is the right vehicle for that belief.
- It leaves unshown who the intended users or affected parties are, making the need harder to weigh.
- The most glaring omissions are the absence of any discussion of implementation experience and any explanation of why a library-only approach would be insufficient.
