Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization case: it identifies a real lifetime hazard in a short example, but leaves nearly every other argument about audience, alternatives, standards-level need, interoperability, library feasibility, and implementation experience unaddressed. The support is therefore concentrated in a single motivating failure mode, while the broader justification remains essentially undeveloped.

- The strongest support is the concrete example showing how an exception can leave nested tasks running against out-of-lifetime objects.
- The thinnest areas are the absent accounts of who is affected and what alternatives were considered.
- A particularly glaring omission is the lack of any implementation experience or evidence that a library solution was ruled out.
