Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only thin support for its own standardization: nearly every argument is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the document does not establish who is affected at all. The strongest material is a set of plausible claims about C and C++ differences and underspecification, but those claims remain undeveloped, with no evidence, examples, or analysis to carry them.

- The paper gestures toward a real gap in conversion and floating-point semantics, but does not show how C++ users encounter or suffer from that gap today.
- Its statements about C incompatibility, inadequate `<cfenv>`, and the insufficiency of libraries are bare claims repeated across sections, with little concrete comparison or technical grounding.
- The most glaring omission is that no affected audience is identified, so the paper never connects the proposed standardization to any measurable need or constituency.
