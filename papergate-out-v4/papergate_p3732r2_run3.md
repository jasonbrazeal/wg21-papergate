Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers solid grounding for the motivating problem, prior art, and early implementation work, but its support thins substantially once it turns to the people affected, why the standard is the right home, and how this would coordinate with existing models. The least developed parts are the claims that users cannot get what they need from libraries or existing standard facilities.

- The strongest support is for prior art and alternatives, where the paper situates its design clearly against C++ fold algorithms, P1673, P3351, and oneDPL.
- Implementation experience is credited as established, including the work-in-progress oneDPL deployment and the linked prototype.
- The weakest areas are the interrelated claims about who is affected, why standardization is necessary, and why a library cannot suffice, which remain asserted rather than demonstrated.
