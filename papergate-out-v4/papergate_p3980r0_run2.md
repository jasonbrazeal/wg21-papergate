Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper leans heavily on discussion history and meeting preferences to justify its direction, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The strongest support concerns prior art and the path already favored in committee, while the thinnest areas are the absence of any stated affected audience, implementation experience, or reason the standard library must be the vehicle.

- The paper clearly establishes that committee discussion and NB comment history have already pointed toward the proposed allocator-handling direction.
- The claimed benefits and coordination arguments are asserted but not tied to a concrete user population or demonstrated need in practice.
- The paper offers no implementation experience to show the design is workable or that the change resolves the stated problems.
- It never establishes who is affected, why a library solution would be insufficient, or why standardization is necessary at all.
