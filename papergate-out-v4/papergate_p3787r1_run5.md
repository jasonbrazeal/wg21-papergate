Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin justification for its own standardization, leaning almost entirely on the precedent of a previously adopted proposal while leaving most of the necessary case unstated. The strongest material concerns the existence of a similar prior fix, but the document does not establish that users are affected, that a library solution is insufficient, or that there is implementation experience to draw on.

- The paper’s most concrete support is its reference to P2248R8 and the similar P3217R0, which at least situates the change within an accepted pattern.
- The rationale for the change is asserted as an oversight, but the paper does not demonstrate the practical consequences for programmers.
- The paper makes no attempt to show who is affected by the missing defaulted parameter or why the problem cannot be addressed outside the standard.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence of need beyond the claim of consistency with earlier work.
