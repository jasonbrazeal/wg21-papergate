Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers credible support for the underlying need and some of the relevant prior art, but much of the standardization case rests on claims that are asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are implementation experience, interoperability, and the argument that a library-only solution would be insufficient.

- The paper clearly establishes why by-reference completions matter and identifies the removal of `std::execution::split` as relevant prior art.
- The discussion of alternatives is grounded in cited specification details, showing real engagement with the design space.
- The paper claims but does not establish that a library solution cannot suffice, leaving a central standardization rationale unsupported.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of publicly available implementation experience, which weakens confidence in the proposal’s practical viability.
