Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers meaningful support in its motivation and in connecting the change to existing standardization work, but it leans heavily on informal conversations rather than documented evidence for several required points. The thinnest parts are the claims about affected users, implementability, and why a library-only solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support is the motivation, which ties the missing assignment operators to a concrete limitation in parallel range algorithms and to the earlier P0624R2 direction.
- The prior-art discussion is also grounded, identifying both the current deleted operators and the complementary broader proposal P3960R0.
- The case weakens where vendor interest and implementation feasibility rest on unattributed conversations rather than written position statements or experience.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of established evidence that users cannot reasonably work around the limitation with hand-written callables, which leaves the necessity of standardization undemonstrated.
