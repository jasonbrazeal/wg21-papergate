Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a thin and uneven case for its own standardization. The strongest material supports the general motivation and shows awareness of at least one existing library, but most of the specific burdens—who is affected, why ISO C++ is the right venue, interoperability, the inadequacy of libraries, and implementation experience—are only asserted, not demonstrated.

- The paper does establish a real motivation: C++ lacks a native data frame, pushing AI workflows toward Python/Pandas.
- The citation of an existing DataFrame library gives some grounding in prior art, though it is not connected to implementation experience.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who is affected by the absence of such a facility or how standardizing it would address their needs.
