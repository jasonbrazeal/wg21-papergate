Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers meaningful support in a few narrow areas, particularly in identifying an apparent inconsistency in how `constant_wrapper` behaves for call and subscript operators and in documenting related implementation experience. That support is concentrated mostly in the problem statement and prior work, while the case for who needs the change, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution is insufficient remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The coordination and interoperability dimension is effectively absent.

- The strongest support is the paper’s identification of an operator-level inconsistency and its reliance on concrete prior discussions and implementation testing.
- The treatment of prior art and alternatives is also grounded, with explicit comparisons to earlier proposals and a clear note about a changed wording choice.
- The thinnest support appears in the rationale for standardization itself, where the paper asserts rather than substantiates the need for standard wording over a library remedy.
- Most glaringly, the paper offers nothing on coordination and interoperability with existing or forthcoming library components.
