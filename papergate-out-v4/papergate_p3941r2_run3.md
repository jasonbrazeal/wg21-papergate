Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only scattered, largely self-referential support for its own standardization, and much of what it presents as motivation or evidence is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any identified affected constituency, any rationale for why only the standard can provide the facility, and any implementation or usage experience.

- The paper’s strongest support is a stated motivation tied to prior discussion and a reference to a withdrawn related proposal.
- It gestures at prior art and alternative designs through references to earlier proposals and scheduler adaptation, but does not establish that adequate alternatives were explored.
- It does not identify who would be affected by the proposal, leaving the practical need unclear.
- Its most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, a standard-only rationale, and coordination or interoperability considerations.
