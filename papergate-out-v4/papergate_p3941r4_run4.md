Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case needed for standardization, mainly by connecting its design to prior discussions and replacing an earlier mechanism; beyond that, most of the burden is left unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any demonstrated user need, affected audience, implementation experience, or reason a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support comes from prior art and alternatives, where the paper credibly ties its approach to earlier concerns about `affine_on` and the discontinued P3718R0.
- The motivating problem is only claimed, resting on a reference to internal discussion rather than an independently explained need for the feature.
- No evidence is offered about who would use the facility or whether anyone has implemented it.
- Most strikingly, the paper does not establish why the change belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
