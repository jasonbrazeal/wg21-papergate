Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper has a solid core of implementation experience and some credible prior-art discussion, but much of its standardization rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the proposal needs to connect its feature to user needs, standardizing authority, and limits of non-standard solutions.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with working branches in both GCC and Clang available on Compiler Explorer.
- The prior art and alternatives section adequately grounds the proposal in existing contracts work and related proposals.
- The case for why this needs to be in the standard rather than handled by libraries is only asserted, without concrete demonstration of what libraries cannot do.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of established evidence for who is affected and why the feature matters, leaving the motivating scenarios largely unsubstantiated.
