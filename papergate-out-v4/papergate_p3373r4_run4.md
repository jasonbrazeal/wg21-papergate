The paper’s strongest case rests on concrete implementation experience, but much of the argument for why this change belongs in the standard is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears in the sections on who is affected, why the standard is needed, coordination, and why a library solution is insufficient, where the paper leans heavily on the same recurring claims about libunifex and stdexec without fully developing the broader stakes.

- The paper clearly establishes implementation experience through reference implementations, libunifex, and nVidia’s stdexec.
- Prior art and alternatives are also established, with specific citations to running code and existing practice.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of a developed argument for why a library-level solution would not suffice, since that case is asserted rather than established.
