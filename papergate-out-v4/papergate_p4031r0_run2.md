Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, with most of the required case left unaddressed and only a narrow, contested argument about naming confusion presented as motivation. The thinnest areas are those that would normally anchor a proposal: affected users, implementation experience, interoperability, and why the facility cannot be delivered as a library.

- The only substantive point the paper makes is that pairing a new `system_context_replaceability` name with a nonexistent `system_context` in the current draft invites confusion.
- The paper gestures toward prior art through P3804R1 but does not go beyond noting that its favored names were also poorly received.
- The paper provides no account of who needs this feature, what experience exists with it, or how it coordinates with existing standard or ecosystem facilities.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
