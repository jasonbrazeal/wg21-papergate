Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper’s support for standardization rests almost entirely on its own report of internal implementation and use, without corroborating detail about scope, demand, or the role a standard would play. The thinnest areas are the absence of argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and the lack of coordination or interoperability discussion.

- The strongest support is the claim that saturating addition, subtraction, and casting have been implemented in Intel’s reference implementation and used in software products.
- The paper points to relevant prior art in P0543R3 and notes that hardware instructions and compiler builtins can back these operations.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not explain why a standard is needed, nor why a library solution would be insufficient.
