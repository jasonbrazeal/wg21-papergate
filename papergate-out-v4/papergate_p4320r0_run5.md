Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper’s supporting material is quite thin: it can point to implementation experience in at least two libraries, but it does not connect that experience to a need for standardization, nor does it explain what standardization would change for users or implementers. The most substantial gaps are in the rationale and interoperability sections, where the proposal offers no argument at all.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with the algorithm reportedly available in Nvidia’s stdexec and referenced in libunifex.
- The paper claims some prior art and alternatives by citing those libraries and related proposals, but does not develop the comparison.
- The paper only asserts, without elaboration, who would be affected by standardizing this algorithm.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established case for why standardization matters or why a library would not suffice.
