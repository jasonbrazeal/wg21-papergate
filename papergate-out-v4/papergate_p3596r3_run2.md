Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some groundwork for its proposal by connecting it to earlier efforts and ongoing drafting activity, but it does not convincingly show why this material belongs in the ISO standard itself or how it would coordinate with existing practice. The support is thinnest around the core standardization questions: the need for normative text, interoperability concerns, and why non-normative documentation cannot serve the stated purpose.

- The strongest part of the case is the acknowledgment of prior work in P1705R1, P2234R0, and P3075R0, with existing UB groupings in P3100R6 as a precedent.
- The paper at least gestures at implementation experience by pointing to a collaboratively developed working draft branch, though that alone is not enough to establish real-world use.
- The claim that cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior matters rests on general assertions about programs being “potentially problematic,” without a specific audience or demonstrated demand.
- The paper does not establish why the standard itself is the right vehicle, nor address coordination, interoperability, or why a non-normative library or reference document would be inadequate.
