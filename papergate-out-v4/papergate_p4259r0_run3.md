Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper supports its case primarily on the strength of naming precedent and the weakness of one alternative’s existing names, but it leaves the central standardization questions essentially unargued. The thinnest support is in the areas that matter most for a standard library addition: why this belongs in the standard, why a library cannot suffice, and any implementation experience.

- The paper clearly establishes that the names proposed in the prior work are poor and that floor and ceiling division are the expected terms.
- The paper claims broad cross-language precedent and discoverability under those names, but does not substantiate the affected audience.
- The paper does not establish why standardization is necessary or why a library implementation would be inadequate.
