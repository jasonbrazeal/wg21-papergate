Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper provides only partial support for its own standardization, with the strongest concrete evidence being that the proposed annex wording has actually been developed in the working draft. The rest of the case leans on asserted importance and references to prior work, while several essential justifications—especially who is affected and why this belongs in the standard rather than elsewhere—are effectively absent.

- The most solid support is implementation experience: the annex wording exists as a collaboratively developed branch of the C++ working draft.
- The paper’s motivation rests mainly on general statements that cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior is useful, without showing why this standardization effort matters now.
- The discussion of alternatives gestures at earlier papers and a C comparison, but does not establish that a normative annex is the right vehicle or that those alternatives were insufficient.
- The paper never identifies who is affected or why the standard, rather than a separate reference document, is the necessary home for this enumeration.
