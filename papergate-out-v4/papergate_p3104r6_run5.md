The thinnest support is around why a library cannot address the need and how the proposal fits with existing or near-term standardization efforts.

- The prior art and alternatives section is adequately established through cited algorithmic foundations and acknowledgment of the existing `<bit>` library’s role.
- The justification for why library-level solutions cannot suffice is only claimed, resting on general statements about optimization-pass information without concrete evidence or examples.
- The paper’s assertions about widespread usefulness and affected user populations are supported only by vague statements and a rough code search, leaving the scale of need unestablished.
