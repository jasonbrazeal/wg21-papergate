Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a plausible starting point but leaves much of its standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated, especially around implementation practice and the absence of viable library-level alternatives. The strongest support comes from prior art and precedent, while the thinnest areas concern motivation, affected users, and interoperability.

- The paper establishes that there is relevant precedent and existing practice for the ordering it proposes.
- The paper claims, but does not substantiate, that all major implementations share the described layout behavior and that coordination with the Itanium ABI is meaningful.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving a central justification gap for standardization.
