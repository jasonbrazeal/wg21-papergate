Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper provides solid evidence of implementation experience, but its broader case for standardization rests largely on assertions rather than demonstrated need, with the thinnest support around why existing libraries cannot suffice and how the proposed library would coordinate with the ecosystem.

- The reference implementation and porting work from Boost Graph and NWGraph give the proposal concrete implementation experience to stand on.
- The need for a standard graph library is asserted through general statements about graphs in computer science, but the paper does not establish who specifically is affected or how widespread the unmet need is.
- The discussion of prior art names several libraries but offers little comparative analysis of why they fail as a basis for standardization.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of substantive comparison with alternatives such as LEMON, igraph, or NetworkX to justify why a new standard library, rather than an existing one, is necessary.
