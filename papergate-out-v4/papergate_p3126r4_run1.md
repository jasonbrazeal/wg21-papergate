Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers solid, concrete evidence of implementation experience and a reasonably grounded discussion of prior art, but its broader case for standardization rests on assertions that are often not sufficiently demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the document needs to show that existing libraries cannot suffice and that only a standard library component would meet the need.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with a public reference implementation and ported algorithms from established graph libraries.
- The paper also clearly establishes prior art and alternatives by situating itself against Boost Graph and related efforts.
- The weakest established points are who is affected and why a library will not do, where the paper relies on general claims rather than demonstrated need or interoperability failures.
