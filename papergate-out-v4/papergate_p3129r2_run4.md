Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin account of its own standardization case, leaning heavily on references to companion proposals and a reference implementation rather than developing the rationale directly. The thinnest areas are the core questions of why the standard should contain this facility and why a library would not suffice, which are left effectively unaddressed.

- The most concrete support is the claimed reference implementation, which at least gestures toward something usable and testable.
- The paper relies on prior art and alternatives through pointers to P3130 and P3337, but does not itself establish how those comparisons justify standardization.
- The discussion of why the facility matters is asserted in a single generality rather than demonstrated with concrete impact.
- The paper is silent on who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the proposal would coordinate with the broader C++ ecosystem.
