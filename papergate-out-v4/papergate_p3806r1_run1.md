Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper’s strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience and a clear comparison with range-v3, but much of the broader case for standardization rests on assertion rather than demonstration. The discussion of affected users, the need for a standard facility rather than a library solution, and the practical limitations of existing alternatives is especially thin.

- The implementation link and the explicit differences from range-v3 give the proposal a credible technical starting point.
- The claim that cycling is a common requirement across domains is asserted without examples, usage data, or reports from affected projects.
- The suggestion that existing library approaches are limited and complex is not substantiated, leaving the argument for standardization over a non-standard library weak.
