Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin case for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on assertions about problems with an existing facility rather than demonstrated need, affected users, interoperability, or implementation experience. The support is thinnest where a proposal must show that only a standard can solve the problem and that the design is ready to standardize, and the paper does not address either.

- The strongest support is the articulation of a plausible coupling problem between coroutine frame allocation and sender environments, which the paper says current machinery cannot handle transparently.
- The paper claims prior art and alternatives support its direction, but it does not establish how those alternatives compare or why they are insufficient as a standardization basis.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, leaving no evidence that the proposed approach works in practice or has been validated outside the authors’ own design work.
