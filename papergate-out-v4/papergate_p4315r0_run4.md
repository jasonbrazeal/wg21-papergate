Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a partial rationale for standardization, and much of what it says remains asserted rather than demonstrated. The discussion of why profiles matter gestures at real concerns, but the supporting case is thin almost everywhere else, leaving the proposal without evidence of need, reach, or viability.

- The clearest support comes from the argument that profiles impose mental load and that enabling one broadly can undermine its own goals, though even this is presented more as claim than demonstration.
- The paper gestures at prior art through a brief example about functions reaching completion, but does not develop alternatives or compare approaches.
- Most of the essential groundwork is missing, including who is affected, why a library cannot suffice, and any implementation experience.
- The proposal offers no account of coordination, interoperability, or why the ISO C++ standard is the right venue.
