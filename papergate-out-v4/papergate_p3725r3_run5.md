Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers a clear statement of why safer, more intuitive filtering matters, but much of the surrounding case for standardization is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support lies in the absence of evidence about implementation, interoperability, or why existing library mechanisms cannot address the problem.

- The strongest support is the concrete description of basic filter-view use cases as broken, risky, or non-intuitive for ordinary programmers.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives, but does not develop those comparisons enough to establish how this approach relates to existing or proposed views.
- The argument for why this belongs in the standard, rather than in a library, is left essentially unaddressed.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any implementation experience or demonstrated coordination with related facilities.
