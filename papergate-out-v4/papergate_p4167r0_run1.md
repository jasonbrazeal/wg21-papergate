Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some framing for its design as an alternative to an existing proposal, but it does not make a sustained case for why its own approach needs to be standardized. The discussion relies heavily on one technical observation about ADL and associated entities, while the affected audience, language-level need, and limits of library-only solutions are asserted more than demonstrated. The strongest support is for having considered prior art and having a prototype, but several essential arguments remain thin.

- The paper clearly positions itself against P2822R2 and offers a compiler-explorer prototype, which establishes that the design has been explored in practice.
- The discussion of why the language must change is underdeveloped, with no separate justification offered beyond the stated ADL limitation.
- The paper does not establish who is concretely affected or why a library cannot solve the problem, since those claims rest on a single repeated argument.
