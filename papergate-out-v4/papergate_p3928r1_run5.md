Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a series of assertions about the concept’s usefulness and intended effects, without evidence or worked examples connecting those claims to concrete user needs. The support is thinnest around the core justification for standardizing the facility rather than leaving it to a library, and there is no implementation experience cited at all.

- The paper does gesture at prior art in P2280 and the existing `*tiny-range*` exposition-only concept, though it does not demonstrate how those prior efforts validate this particular design.
- The discussion of affected types is at least specific, naming the standard types the author expects to satisfy the new concept.
- The case for why affected users need this in the standard is not developed beyond general statements about compile-time reasoning and optimization opportunities.
- The paper does not attempt to show why a library solution would be inadequate, leaving the central standardization question unanswered.
