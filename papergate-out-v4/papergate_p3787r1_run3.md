Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper leans on an analogy to an adopted change, but offers almost no direct support for standardizing this particular fix. Its strongest material concerns the existence of a prior, adopted proposal and the fact that another similar follow-up was treated as a hotfix. Beyond that connection, the paper is largely silent about the affected users, the need for a standardese change rather than another solution, implementation experience, or interaction with the broader library ecosystem.

- The paper most clearly establishes that an earlier proposal made analogous changes to other algorithms and was adopted.
- It claims, though without much elaboration, that `uninitialized_fill` was simply overlooked when that earlier change was applied.
- The paper gives no account of who is affected by the current specification or what practical problems arise from the omission.
- It does not establish why this change belongs in the standard rather than being addressable through a library, revision, or non-normative correction.
