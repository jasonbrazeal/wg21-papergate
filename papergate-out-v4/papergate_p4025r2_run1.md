Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization argument: it establishes that AI-related type and dimension confusion is a real problem area, but most of the supporting claims are asserted rather than demonstrated, with the thinnest support around why a library cannot suffice and what implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is the concrete claim that compile-time differentiation and dimension checking address recurring, high-impact errors in AI development.
- The paper gestures at existing work such as the DataFrame library and Python-driven JIT ecosystems, but it does not show how these relate to the proposed design or what they leave unsolved.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any meaningful implementation experience or evidence that a non-standard library approach would fail for the proposed functionality.
