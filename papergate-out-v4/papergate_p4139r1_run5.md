Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers little concrete support for its own standardization, relying mostly on assertions about utility and brief references to an earlier proposal rather than on demonstrated need or evidence. The thinnest areas are those that would normally ground a standardization effort: why this belongs in the standard, why a library solution is insufficient, and whether anyone has actually built or used the proposed facility.

- The paper gestures at the novelty of a fallible, runtime-keyed `get()` but does not establish why that matters enough to warrant standardization.
- It mentions a prior proposal’s lack of enthusiasm without showing how this version improves the case or addresses earlier objections.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination considerations, or a rationale for why an ordinary library cannot provide the same functionality.
