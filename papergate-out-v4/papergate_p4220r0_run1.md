Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a narrow but real justification for further work: it clearly identifies why defining the goal of a `zstring_view`-like type matters, and it situates that work within recent committee direction and existing prior art. The support is thinnest when the paper turns from clarifying intent to arguing for standardization itself, since the need for the standard, interoperability concerns, and why a third-party library cannot fill the role are claimed rather than demonstrated.

- The paper is strongest in establishing why a shared understanding of the type’s contract matters before design proceeds further.
- It credibly points to relevant committee discussion and prior art as the backdrop for its narrower goal-setting contribution.
- Its discussion of implementation experience shows that similar types exist and differ, though this is offered more as motivation than as proof of a standardized solution.
- The most glaring omission is any developed argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than remaining a library-level concern or a specification of requirements for external implementations.
