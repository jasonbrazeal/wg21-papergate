Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives a reasonably clear account of the naming problem it wants to address and of the immediate prior discussion in P3091, but it offers almost no evidence for the broader need to standardize the proposed change. The strongest material concerns motivation and alternatives, while the case for impact, standard-library placement, implementability, and coordination is largely absent.

- The paper’s most solid support is its explanation of why the functionality matters for sequence containers and why the `get` name would be inconsistent with existing library usage.
- It also documents relevant prior art by tracing the evolution of P3091, including names that were considered and rejected.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the change beyond a conceptual appeal to library regularity.
- The paper also provides no implementation experience, no argument that a library solution would be insufficient, and no account of coordination or interoperability concerns.
