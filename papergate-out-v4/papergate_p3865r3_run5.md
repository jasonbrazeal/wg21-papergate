Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers solid support in a few foundational areas, particularly by tying the problem to specific core and library issues and by locating the change within existing deduction design intent and prior proposals. However, the case for standardization is uneven: most of the practical burden falls on assertions that are repeated but not demonstrated, and the paper never identifies who is concretely affected.

- The strongest support comes from linking the proposal directly to CWG 3003 and LWG 4381, including the statement that the library wording has no known fix without core language changes.
- The discussion of prior art and design intent is also well grounded, citing P0091R3, P3863R0, and P0552R0 to show continuity with earlier deduction rules.
- The thinnest support lies in the absence of any established affected-user population, leaving the real-world impact of the current behavior unstated.
- The paper repeatedly claims implementation experience and library necessity, but those claims are asserted rather than established with corroborating detail beyond the paper’s own examples.
