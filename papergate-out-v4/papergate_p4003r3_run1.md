Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper’s strongest support comes from its implementation experience and its framing of the problem, both of which are concrete and directly relevant. Its case is much thinner, however, when it comes to showing who exactly benefits, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the proposal would coordinate with existing async models—these points are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper substantiates its design with a complete implementation across three platforms and points to specific components in Capy and Corosio.
- The rationale and prior art receive solid grounding through references to P4172R0, Boost.Asio, and companion analysis.
- The paper repeatedly claims broad practical rewards and the necessity of language-level support, but it does not establish the affected audience, the limits of a library approach, or the interoperability story.
