Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers meaningful support in a few areas, particularly implementation experience and awareness of prior art, but it does not make a complete case for standardization. The thinnest parts are the absence of any argument for why a library solution is insufficient and only asserted rather than demonstrated claims about who is affected and why this belongs in the standard.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with two independent implementations and no reported bugs.
- Prior art and alternatives are also well established, including links to existing fixes and prior proposals.
- The case weakens around affected users and the need for the standard, where claims are asserted but not backed by evidence.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper never explains why a library will not solve the problem.
