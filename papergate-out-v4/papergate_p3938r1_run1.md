Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers genuine support for its standardization case in the areas of implementation experience and prior art, where the credited passages show broad compiler alignment and a clear intent to match existing behavior. The support is thinnest around the basic justification for standardization: the document does not establish who is affected, why a standard is the right venue, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest evidence is implementation experience, since GCC, Clang, and MSVC are all reported to implement the intended design in a consistent way.
- The paper also establishes prior art by pointing to scattered existing wording and the clearer terminology of the original proposal.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established reason why the standard should address this rather than a library or other mechanism.
