Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some grounding for why the current alignment wording needs attention, chiefly by citing open core issues and known compiler divergence, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The support is thinnest around the intended audience, the limits of non-standard remedies, and the specific role the standard must play in resolving the problem.

- The strongest support is the identification of multiple open core issues tied directly to the subclauses the paper proposes to change.
- Some prior art and implementation experience are gestured at through reported differences among GCC, Clang, EDG, and MSVC, though the comparisons are not developed into a coherent survey of alternatives.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the current wording or how widespread the practical impact is.
- The most glaring omission is any explanation of why a library solution, vendor documentation, or other non-standard guidance would be insufficient.
