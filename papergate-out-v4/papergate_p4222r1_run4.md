Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some useful groundwork by establishing why the initialization problem matters and by surveying prior approaches, but it leaves several central justifications asserted rather than demonstrated. The support is thinnest around the need for a standard-language facility rather than a library, and around evidence of interoperability and implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the clear explanation of the initialization problem’s practical importance and the acknowledgment of known language-level alternatives.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is adequately developed, showing that the design space has been considered.
- The claim that the standard is the right venue rests mostly on asserted fit with the profiles framework rather than on demonstrated need that libraries cannot meet.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established coordination or interoperability analysis, leaving the proposal’s relationship to existing and future standards unclear.
