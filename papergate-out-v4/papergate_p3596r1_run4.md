Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some context and an implementation branch, but it does not substantiate most of the case for why this content belongs in the C++ standard itself. The strongest support is the existence of collaboratively developed wording, while the most visible gaps concern the affected audience and any demonstration that a library or separate reference cannot satisfy the need.

- The only fully established point is that draft wording exists in a public branch, showing at least some collaborative implementation experience.
- The paper gestures at prior efforts and previous calls for standardization, but does not connect those efforts to a demonstrated need or successful outcome.
- It offers no evidence about who is affected by the absence of this annex or why those users need a normative standard annex rather than external documentation.
- Most glaringly, the paper never explains why a non-standard library, database, or maintained reference document would be insufficient for the stated goals.
