Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, mostly rhetorical case for its preferred namespace name, relying on discussion and polling rather than demonstrating a need for standardization. The thinnest support is in the areas that would justify a standards change at all: why the standard must act, why a library cannot, how the feature interoperates, and what implementation experience exists.

- The paper’s strongest support is a Kona poll and discussion of alternatives, but those are presented as claims about preference without establishing who is concretely affected.
- The discussion of why the name matters rests on a prediction of confusion, but the paper does not establish that the confusion would arise in practice or what its cost would be.
- The paper offers no established case for why the standard, rather than a library or existing mechanism, should specify the change, nor for coordination, interoperability, or implementation experience.
