The paper gives a partial account of why the proposed change is needed, with the strongest material concentrated in the discussion of prior concerns and alternative designs. The case becomes noticeably thinner where it needs to connect the feature to a standardization-level problem, show why existing library mechanisms are insufficient, or demonstrate meaningful implementation experience.

- The paper most convincingly establishes that prior discussion of `affine_on` raised real concerns and that the revised design emerged from those objections rather than from speculation.
- The treatment of affected users and interoperability gestures toward relevance but leaves the actual scope and integration burden mostly asserted.
- The paper offers essentially no direct argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, and the implementation-experience claim is little more than a reference to one existing library in a discouraged form.
