Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin account of its own standardization case, resting almost entirely on a national body comment and a related core issue rather than building a direct argument from user impact, alternatives, or implementation practice. The support is least present where a proposal usually needs it most: in explaining what problem is being solved outside committee process and why the standard is the right place to solve it.

- The strongest thread is the explicit connection to NB comment FR-017-155, which at least names a specific affected process and requester.
- Prior discussion is gestured at through CWG 3112, though the paper does not develop what that issue shows about alternatives or expected behavior.
- The most glaring omission is any explanation of who is affected in real code or why the change matters beyond resolving the listed comment.
