Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a reasonably grounded account of why the feature matters and why the standard is the right venue, but several central claims remain asserted rather than demonstrated, especially around affected users, library alternatives, and implementation experience.

- The strongest support is for the need to standardize, with concrete explanation of how language-level enforcement improves diagnostics and enables consteval-only variables.
- The prior art discussion is also well supported, tying the proposal to CWG3150 and already-shipping C++26 features such as std::meta::info.
- The weakest support concerns implementation experience, where the cited examples and talks do not clearly substantiate the specific design being proposed.
