Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow rationale for renaming an existing facility, and that rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, the need for a standard change, interoperability, and implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the observation that the current name no longer matches the algorithm’s changed unary shape.
- The paper points to a prior binary form and earlier names as context, but it does not develop these into a case for standardization.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the rename or what breakage or migration costs might follow.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this renaming belongs in the standard, as opposed to being handled by guidance, aliases, or a library-level decision.
