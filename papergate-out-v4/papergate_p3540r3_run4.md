Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper’s support for standardization rests almost entirely on the assertion that it follows existing `gnu::offset` and `clang::offset` practice, but it does not develop that assertion into a substantive case for most of the questions a proposal needs to answer. The strongest material concerns prior art and alternatives, while the arguments for why the feature matters, who is affected, and why a library cannot provide it remain largely unsupported claims repeated in different forms.

- The paper does establish that the proposed parameter exists in both Clang and GCC and reflects the original author’s intent and passing tests.
- The proposal repeatedly claims popular use and prior approval but provides no evidence of actual usage, demand, or affected users.
- The discussion of implementation experience is asserted rather than demonstrated with concrete examples or references.
- The paper is thinnest on why a standard is needed at all and why a library-level solution would be insufficient, since those points are only restated as goals rather than argued.
