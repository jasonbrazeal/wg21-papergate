Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with its strongest concrete evidence being the existence of collaboratively developed draft wording for the annex. The case is thinnest around who is affected, why a library solution would not suffice, and why the standard itself—rather than another venue—is the right place for this work.

- The paper does establish implementation experience through the collaboratively maintained branch of the C++ working draft containing the proposed annex wording.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives by citing earlier efforts and proposed groupings, but does not establish that the chosen approach is clearly better or adequately explored.
- The paper asserts that stable names could serve as common vocabulary for coordination, but this remains a hope rather than a demonstrated need or commitment from affected parties.
- The paper offers no account of who is affected or why a library would not do, leaving the motivation for standardization dependent on general unease about undefined and IFNDR behavior rather than a demonstrated problem for the standard to solve.
