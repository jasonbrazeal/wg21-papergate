Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some grounding in a real integration effort, but its support is thinnest where standardization specifically must be justified. It gives credible context for why the design question matters to its participants, yet leaves the case for normative action largely implicit.

- The strongest support concerns prior art and the practical setting, since the paper clearly names the existing libraries and the shift from Asio callbacks to coroutine-native I/O.
- The relevance to financial markets infrastructure is asserted rather than demonstrated, because the affected population and the broader need are described mainly through limited interviews and a short integration window.
- The most glaring omission is any established argument for why this belongs in the C++ standard, since the paper does not connect the reported experience to a need for normative specification.
