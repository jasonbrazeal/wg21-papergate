Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers partial support for its own standardization: it demonstrates implementation experience and acknowledges plausible alternatives, but it does not substantively establish who would be affected, why library-level solutions are inadequate, or how the change coordinates with existing standards and implementations. The thinnest parts are the absence of any audience or impact analysis and the unargued assertion that changing the standard is the necessary remedy.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience, with a visible compiler patch and an available prototype.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is credited as established, particularly the comparison with pointer tagging, template costs, inheritance costs, and existing implementation metadata.
- The most notable omission is any identification of who is affected, leaving the motivating user base and practical stakes unspecified.
- The paper also fails to establish why a library-only solution would not suffice or how the proposal coordinates with other standardization efforts.
