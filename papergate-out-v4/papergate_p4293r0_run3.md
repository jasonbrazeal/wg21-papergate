Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization argument, establishing that it responds to prior discussion in P4288 and that the proposed algorithm has a recognizable place in that conversation. Beyond that, the case is largely asserted rather than demonstrated, with the thinnest support around who would actually use the facility and why existing library mechanisms cannot already serve the purpose.

- The strongest support is the paper’s engagement with P4288, which gives the proposal a clear point of origin and a concrete alternative context.
- The paper claims the algorithm matters because it isolates decay-copying as a single purpose, but it does not show who needs that isolation or what problem it solves in practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
