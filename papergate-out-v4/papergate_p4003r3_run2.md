Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper’s strongest support is in the foundational rationale and implementation record: it does establish why the problem matters, how the design relates to prior work, and that a working implementation exists across multiple platforms. The case becomes thinner where the paper asserts but does not demonstrate who specifically is affected, why only the standard can supply the protocol, and how it coordinates with neighboring async models without unstated assumptions.

- The paper establishes its core motivation and the existence of prior art and a cross-platform implementation, which gives the proposal a credible starting point.
- Its weakest established areas concern the necessity of standardization itself: the claims about affected users, standardization-specific value, and interoperability are asserted rather than supported with concrete evidence.
