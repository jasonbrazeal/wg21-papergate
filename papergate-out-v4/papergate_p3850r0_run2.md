Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper’s strongest support comes from its references to prior standardization work, a complete design in P3097R2, and a working GCC implementation, so the existence of a viable route is well grounded. The case is much thinner, however, when it comes to showing who specifically needs the feature, why it belongs in the standard rather than a library, and how it will coordinate with real-world deployment at scale.

- The paper establishes meaningful implementation experience and prior art through the mature P3097R2 design and its working GCC implementation.
- The paper claims, but does not substantiate, that this is a frequently requested extension affecting a clearly identifiable user population.
- The paper claims the feature is easy to teach and has complete wording, but offers no evidence or discussion to support its necessity as a language feature.
- The paper does not address why a library solution would be insufficient.
