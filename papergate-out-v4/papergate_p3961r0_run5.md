Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the necessary justification: it shows a concrete failing in current code and points to an implementation, but leaves almost everything else unexplored. The thinnest support is not in the motivation or feasibility, but in connecting the change to affected users, existing practice, and the boundary between language and library.

- The paper most clearly establishes that the proposed change would address a real compile-time impossibility.
- The existence of a reference implementation gives the proposal some grounding in practice.
- The paper does not identify who would benefit or how widely the problem occurs.
- It offers no comparison with prior art or alternative approaches, and no argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
