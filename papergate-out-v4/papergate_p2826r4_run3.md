Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper establishes a workable motivation and shows credible awareness of existing alternatives, but it leaves several key aspects of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns evidence that the feature is actually implementable and that it coordinates cleanly with existing practice or standard library evolution.

- The clearest support is the demonstration that current workarounds are weak and that related proposals do not address the same overload-set problem.
- The paper also credibly distinguishes its model from prior constexpr-parameter and parametric-expression ideas.
- The case for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library rests mostly on asserted syntax and forwarding benefits, without much substantiation.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, leaving the practical viability of the mechanism entirely unsupported.
