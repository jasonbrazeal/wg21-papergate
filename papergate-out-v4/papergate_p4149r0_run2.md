Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization: it gestures at a consistency problem and a committee request, but leaves most of the burden unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and what implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is the paper’s stated connection to CWG’s desire for consistent treatment of noexcept-specifiers, contract specifiers, default arguments, and annotations.
- The paper claims some prior art by referencing a national-body issue and a working draft, though it does not establish alternatives beyond rejecting the suggestion.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of implementation experience, affected users, or interoperability concerns.
