Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a thin and uneven evidentiary basis for its own standardization, relying heavily on analogy and assertion while leaving several essential categories effectively unaddressed. The strongest material concerns implementation experience, though even that is largely prospective rather than demonstrated.

- The most concrete support is the acknowledgment that some customization point objects are already available in the reference implementation.
- The paper gestures toward prior art and interoperability through references to the STL, `sized_range`, and a companion proposal, but does not develop those into evidence.
- The case for why the standard library, rather than a standalone library, should absorb this work is not made at all.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any account of who is affected by the problem or what real-world need standardizing these facilities would serve.
