Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some grounding for standardization through implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves much of the motivational and necessity case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the paper must explain why the standard, rather than a library, is the right vehicle and how the change coordinates with other specifications or implementations.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with concrete evidence from libunifex and nVidia’s stdexec, including a reference implementation used for validation.
- Prior art and alternatives are also credibly established through existing libunifex practice and references to related proposal context.
- The rationale for why the standard is needed and why a library will not do rests mainly on existing practice claims, without a developed argument for standardization specifically.
- Coordination and interoperability are not established at all, leaving a significant gap in the paper’s case.
