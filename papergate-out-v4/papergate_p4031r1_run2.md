Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidential basis for its own standardization, relying mostly on unsettled preferences and brief allusions rather than concrete justification. The thinnest areas are those where the proposal never moves beyond assertion: it does not show why a library solution would be insufficient, how existing implementations have fared, or how the feature would coordinate with related standard components.

- The strongest support is the discussion of prior naming alternatives in P3804R1, but even that is presented as a set of options the author could accept rather than evidence for a particular choice.
- The paper asserts that adding the proposed namespace name would cause confusion because `system_context` is not in the working draft, yet it does not develop that into a clear case for why the name belongs in the standard now.
- The claimed indication of affected users rests on citing a poll without showing its design, population, or how the results support the proposal.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, which leaves the proposal with no demonstrated viability in practice.
