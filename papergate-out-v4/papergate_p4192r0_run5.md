Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers concrete motivation by identifying specification gaps, core issues, and genuine implementation divergence in alignment rules, but much of its standardization case is asserted through references and examples rather than fully developed. The thinnest support concerns who is affected and why the problem cannot be addressed outside the standard, neither of which is established in the text.

- The strongest support is the demonstration that multiple open core issues and conflicting compiler behavior make the current alignment specification difficult to interpret consistently.
- The paper invokes prior art through existing core issues and compiler divergence, but it does not develop those references into a clear account of alternatives or their tradeoffs.
- The most glaring omission is any explanation of who is affected by the current rules, leaving the practical impact of standardization largely implicit.
