Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization: it names familiar prior art and shows some implementation activity, but it does not convincingly establish who is affected, why the standard library specifically should absorb the feature, or how the proposal fits with existing ranges machinery.

- The strongest point is implementation experience, since the author provides a libstdc++-based implementation and the operations are well established in range-v3 and other languages.
- The rationale for why a library will not do gestures at a real technical gap, but it is only claimed rather than shown through concrete examples or broader evidence.
- The discussion of why the feature matters and what prior art exists is present but thin, offering assertions rather than a developed case.
- The most glaring omissions are the absence of any account of who is affected, why standardization is necessary, and how the proposal coordinates with existing standard components.
