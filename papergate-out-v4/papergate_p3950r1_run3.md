Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper makes only a partial case for itself: it clearly motivates the problem and shows that alternatives and prior work exist, but it leaves several essential parts of the standardization argument asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest area is the lack of evidence about who would be affected and what implementation experience actually shows beyond the author’s own claim.

- The strongest support is the clear explanation of why the current restriction is arbitrary and why the problem matters for coroutine users.
- The paper also establishes that there is relevant prior art and at least one earlier proposal, with new context in `std::execution`.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any account of affected users, use cases, or implementer interest beyond the author’s own work.
