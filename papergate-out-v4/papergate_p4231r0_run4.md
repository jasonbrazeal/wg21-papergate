Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only scattered and mostly unsupported assertions for why its idea belongs in the standard, with no section doing the full work of connecting a real user problem to a necessary language or library change. The strongest material is a gesture at prior art and a vague hope for implementation experience, but the core justifications—who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how this fits with existing practice—are effectively absent.

- The paper's clearest support is its acknowledged continuity with P2746 and an agreed rounding-mode design, though even that is presented without showing the prior work's standardization case was accepted here.
- The case for a standard facility rests partly on a complaint that `from_chars` would be inconvenient, a claim that is neither explained nor connected to actual usage.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who is affected by the current situation, leaving the proposal without an audience or demonstrated need.
