Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a weak, fragmentary case for its own standardization, relying on a contested naming dispute and a single poll rather than a broader justification. Its thinnest support lies in the areas that should anchor a standardization effort, such as the need for standard intervention, implementation experience, and interoperability, where nothing is established.

- The strongest support is the acknowledgment that the proposed namespace name could create confusion because `system_context` does not exist in the working draft.
- The paper records a committee poll and prior discussion about alternative names, though neither the affected audience nor the prior art is actually established as a rationale for standardization.
- The paper offers no support for why the standard should address this rather than a library, nor for how the change would coordinate with existing and related facilities.
- Most glaringly, the paper provides no implementation experience or evidence of a problem that standardization is needed to solve.
