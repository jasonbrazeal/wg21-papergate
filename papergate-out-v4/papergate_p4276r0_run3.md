Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers useful general reasoning about when scalar overloads are warranted, and it credibly establishes that the immediate motivating case is redundant, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support is around implementation experience, which is absent, and around why a library solution cannot capture the intended guidance.

- The strongest support is the principled argument that a scalar overload duplicating broadcast behavior adds API and specification burden without benefit.
- The paper also establishes prior art by showing that existing and pending scalar overloads follow the stated criterion rather than contradicting it.
- It does not establish who is concretely affected by the absent guideline beyond a general claim about expectations.
- Most glaringly, the paper provides no implementation experience or evidence that the proposed standardization would be adopted or useful in practice.
