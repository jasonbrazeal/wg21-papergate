Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers meaningful support in a few areas, particularly prior art, implementation experience, and the general importance of the problem, but it falls short of making a complete case for standardization. The thinnest support is in coordination and interoperability, which is not established at all, and in showing who is concretely affected beyond a claim of widespread confusion.

- The paper is strongest in showing that similar features have been considered before and that a usable reference implementation exists.
- It clearly motivates why integer division rounding is a real source of errors and why ad hoc fixes are unreliable.
- It does not establish who specifically is affected by the current absence of such a facility, relying on anecdotal references rather than demonstrated impact.
- It offers no coordination or interoperability analysis, leaving unexamined how the proposal fits with existing standard library conventions or other languages.
