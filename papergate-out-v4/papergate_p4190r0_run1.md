Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a focused but incomplete case for its own standardization, with useful grounding in prior art and an actual implementation, but little attention to who is affected or why standardization is the necessary path. The strongest material concerns the historical motivation and the concrete library change, while the thinnest areas leave several standard proposal requirements entirely unaddressed.

- The paper clearly situates the proposed change in the history of P2447R6 and P4144R1, explaining the trouble that led to removal and how the new constraint avoids repeating it.
- It points to a real implementation in libc++, which gives some evidence that the design is workable in practice.
- It does not identify who would be affected by the change, nor does it establish why the standard, rather than a library-level solution, is the appropriate venue.
