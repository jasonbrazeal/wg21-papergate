Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only the faintest outline of a case for standardization, and even its strongest points are asserted rather than demonstrated. The support is thinnest where the proposal should be most concrete: there is no account of who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or what implementation experience exists.

- The most developed part of the paper is its gesture toward prior work, pointing to P3818 and P3820 as background for the problem.
- The claim that making certain functions `constexpr` is a breaking change is mentioned, but the paper does not substantiate why that consequence matters enough to justify standardization.
- The paper offers no discussion of who would be affected by the change or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.
