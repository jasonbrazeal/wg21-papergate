Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper offers solid evidence that the proposed taxonomy has been worked through in real implementations, but much of the case for standardization rests on assertions and appeals to committee direction rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest support is around why a library cannot suffice and why the standard itself must act, which weakens the argument for taking this work into the IS.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with concrete validation drawn from both mp-units and Sequoia.
- Prior art and alternatives are clearly established, including the contrast with Au and the convergence of two independent designs.
- The paper claims but does not establish who is affected, leaning on reported usability complaints and committee feedback rather than demonstrated user impact.
- The most glaring omission is a convincing account of why a library will not do, since several central features are admittedly not yet implemented in the reference library and the uniqueness of the design is asserted but not tied to standardization necessity.
