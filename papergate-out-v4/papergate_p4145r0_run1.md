Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only scattered assertions in support of its own standardization, with most of the necessary case left implicit or entirely unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the basic justifications for why this belongs in the standard at all, and whether a library solution or existing practice outside the standard could already serve the need.

- The paper is most concrete about implementation experience, noting that libstdc++ has used the approach for nearly three years.
- It gestures toward coordination by observing that the three major implementations share a particular behavior, though the interoperability claim remains undeveloped.
- It does not establish why the change matters to any identifiable group of users or developers.
- It offers no argument for why the standard is the right venue rather than a library, and no explanation of why a library solution would be insufficient.
