Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its own standardization on the dimensions that matter most: the problem is real and well documented, affected users and production deployment are identified, and the implementation experience is concrete. The case is thinnest where it needs to show why ordinary library mechanisms cannot address the gaps and why standardization is the only route, since those arguments are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from established implementation experience, including production use at Citadel Securities and benchmarking on MSVC, which grounds the paper in actual practice rather than speculation.
- The paper also establishes who is affected and why the issue matters, with evidence drawn from multiple independent sources over several years.
- The most glaring omission is the failure to establish convincingly that a library solution cannot close the identified gaps, especially given that the critique centers on sender-model properties rather than on capabilities unique to language standardization.
