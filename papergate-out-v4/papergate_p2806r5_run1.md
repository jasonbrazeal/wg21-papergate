Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers solid support in several areas, particularly by connecting the proposed feature to real language pain points, existing implementations, and a concrete relationship to pattern matching. The case is thinnest around who is affected and around the negative claim that a library solution cannot suffice, both of which are asserted more than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, with working compiler links and a reference to prior wording evolution.
- The paper also clearly establishes why the feature matters by showing how ordinary control flow becomes awkward without it and by identifying lifetime problems in existing extensions.
- The discussion of prior art and why the standard should address this is well grounded in comparisons to immediately invoked lambdas and to needs arising in the pattern matching proposal.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any concrete account of who is affected, leaving the user population and real-world impact unspecified.
