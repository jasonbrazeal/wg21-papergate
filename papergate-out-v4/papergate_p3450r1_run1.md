Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a sketch of motivation rather than a demonstrated case for standardization, with most of its key claims asserted through brief references or personal experience rather than developed argument. The support is thinnest around why a standard language or library facility is necessary, how it would coexist with existing practice, and whether there is any implementation basis for the proposed direction.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2641R4, which at least situates the idea in a recognizable prior discussion.
- The paper names a specific implementation context, `constexpr std::format`, where the author encountered the issue.
- The case for who is affected rests on a single prior proposal, without showing wider use or demand.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this needs standardization rather than a library-only or non-standard solution.
