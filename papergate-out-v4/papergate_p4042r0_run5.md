Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin, self-referential case for standardization: its sole substantive support is an in-review libstdc++ patch, and even that is asserted rather than demonstrated. Nearly every other question a proposal needs to answer—why the feature matters, who it affects, what alternatives exist, and why it cannot live outside the standard—is left unaddressed.

- The strongest, though still unproven, support is the mention of an implementation and testing effort currently under review for libstdc++.
- The paper claims standardization is warranted because of that patch, but it does not show how the patch resolves a problem general enough to require a standard facility.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any motivating problem, affected audience, or comparison with prior art and alternatives.
