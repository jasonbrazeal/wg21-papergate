Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, conditional rationale for its own standardization, with most of its case resting on the unresolved disagreement surrounding P4288 rather than on independent evidence of need. The support is thinnest where a proposal would need concrete demonstration of affected users, implementation experience, and a reason a library solution cannot suffice.

- The strongest support appears in the paper’s attempt to connect its algorithm to an active dispute over decay-copying in `std::execution`, giving it a plausible hook into current committee discussion.
- The paper gestures at prior art and at a standards-level motivation, but both are framed as contingent on the outcome of P4288 rather than established on their own terms.
- The most glaring omission is any identification of who is affected by the absence of the proposed facility.
