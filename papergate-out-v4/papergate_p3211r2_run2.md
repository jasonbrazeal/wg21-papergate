Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper’s strongest concrete support is its implementation experience, but much of the surrounding case for standardization rests on general assertions rather than demonstrated need, usage, or interoperability. The thinnest areas are the lack of any established coordination story and the largely asserted claims about affected users, prior art, and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The paper establishes implementation experience by pointing to an author implementation based on libstdc++.
- The paper claims, but does not establish, why the feature matters and who is affected beyond a general statement and a tier ranking.
- The paper claims, but does not establish, that a dedicated standard view would meaningfully improve optimization or expressiveness over a library solution.
- The paper does not establish coordination and interoperability with existing or planned range facilities.
