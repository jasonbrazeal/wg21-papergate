Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides solid support in a few areas, particularly by articulating the motivating use cases, acknowledging alternatives, and demonstrating a working implementation. Its case is much thinner, however, when it comes to showing that the problem actually requires a new standardized facility rather than a library solution, or that the affected audience and interoperability concerns have been thought through. The proposal leans heavily on assertion where it most needs evidence.

- The clearest strength is the concrete implementation experience, with a public implementation linked and credited as direct evidence.
- The paper also establishes why the feature matters by describing separated extraction and insertion, key modification, transferability, and the value of isolating source and target containers.
- Prior art and alternatives are addressed credibly, especially through acknowledgment of existing API inconsistency and implementation divergence among major standard libraries.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established discussion of who is affected by the proposal, leaving the user and implementer impact largely unexamined.
