Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers meaningful support for why protocol types address a recurring need and how the approach relates to existing practice, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case undeveloped. The thinnest areas are interoperability, coordination with other facilities, and why a library-only solution would be inadequate.

- The paper clearly establishes that dynamic structural interfaces are a recurring problem and that existing standard facilities do not represent overload sets.
- It shows relevant prior art and positions the proposal against `proxy` and reflection-based alternatives, with a working reference implementation.
- It does not establish who specifically is affected beyond a general restatement that type erasure is common.
- It never addresses interoperability with existing or proposed standard facilities, nor why a library implementation would be insufficient.
