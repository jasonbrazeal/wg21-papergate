Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is uneven: it demonstrates that a draft annex exists in a working branch, but it does not convincingly show who would use the annex, why the Standard is the right home for it, or what alternatives were seriously considered. The thinnest parts concern the absence of an affected audience and the lack of any argument ruling out a non-normative or library-based solution.

- The strongest support is that the wording has been developed as a collaborative branch of the C++ working draft, providing some implementation experience.
- The paper gestures at prior art and hoped-for common vocabulary, but these claims remain undeveloped as justification for standardization.
- It does not establish who is affected by the proposed annex or what problem they would concretely face without it.
- The most glaring omission is the failure to address why a library or separate reference document would not serve the stated purpose.
