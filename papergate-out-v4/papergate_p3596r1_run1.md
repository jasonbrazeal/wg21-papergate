Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some framing for why cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior would be useful and points to prior attempts, but it does little to establish who is actually affected, why the standard is the right home for this information, or why a library or other artifact would be insufficient. The strongest concrete support is the existence of collaboratively developed wording on a draft branch, while the case for standardization itself remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper at least shows implementation experience, since the proposed annex wording has been developed as a branch of the C++ working draft.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives by citing earlier cataloging and formalization efforts.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the absence of this annex.
- The paper offers no substantive argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a separate reference or library.
