Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a narrow but real evidentiary base: it can point to an experimental implementation and some survey of alternative syntaxes, but it does not convincingly establish the affected audience, the necessity of a language change, or why library-level solutions are insufficient. The case is thinnest where it moves from “this is possible” to “this belongs in the standard.”

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, since the author provides a Clang fork demonstrating the feature in practice.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is anchored in concrete proposals and adopted features, giving the reader useful context.
- The paper only asserts, rather than demonstrates, who would benefit from the change and how widely.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library cannot address the problem, leaving the standardization rationale incomplete.
