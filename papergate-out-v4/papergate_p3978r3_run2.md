Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers genuine grounding for its motivation and for the feasibility of the approach, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas concern the affected audience and the explanation of why the required change cannot be delivered through a library.

- The paper most convincingly establishes that the current inconsistency around call and subscript operators is a real language-level problem worth addressing.
- It also shows meaningful implementation experience, including use in a shipping library and testing against current standard library implementations.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is credited as established and gives useful context for how this proposal relates to earlier designs.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who is affected, leaving the scale and practical urgency of the problem unestablished.
