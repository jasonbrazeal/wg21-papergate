Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper offers meaningful support for the need to standardize its layering decision, chiefly by grounding the question in a decade of deployment evidence and by showing that the competing dependency was withdrawn without replacement. The support is thinnest where the paper asserts costs, interoperability consequences, and implementation experience without tying those assertions to the specific wording or architecture under review.

- The paper establishes that the layering question is substantive, contested, and grounded in published prior art and a documented history of committee evolution.
- The paper establishes that the competing mechanism depended on by related wording was explicitly withdrawn, leaving an unresolved coordination gap.
- The paper claims but does not establish that the deployment record at Google and in vendor checkers actually measures the architecture it asks the committee to adopt.
- The paper does not establish why the standardization goal cannot be met by a library or other non-standard mechanism.
