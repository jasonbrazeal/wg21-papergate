Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives concrete support for the existence of a problem and for the viability of its proposed direction, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who is actually affected and whether the proposed behavior is necessary in the standard as opposed to a library or alternative design choice.

- The paper clearly ties its motivation to a specific interaction with P3068 and provides an implemented prototype plus a compiler-explorer demonstration.
- It also documents prior discussion in LEWG and a conservative fallback, showing awareness of alternatives and previous committee reaction.
- It does not establish the affected audience, so the practical scope and urgency of the change remain unclear from the paper itself.
- Its arguments about why only a standard change will do largely restate the desired model and future possibilities without showing that a library or narrower specification could not suffice.
