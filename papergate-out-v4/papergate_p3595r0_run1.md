Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers credible evidence of implementation viability and some awareness of prior work, but it leaves the central rationale for standardization largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts are the failure to show why a library cannot address the need and the repeated reliance on broad, unquantified claims about affected users and use cases.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with working prototypes in both GCC and Clang described as straightforward to produce.
- Prior art and alternatives are meaningfully engaged, including existing Clang attributes and related proposals for source-level control.
- The paper has not established why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving a key question about the necessity of standardizing this feature unanswered.
- The claims about who is affected and why the problem matters rest almost entirely on general statements rather than concrete evidence of demand or impact.
