Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers solid, if narrow, support in the areas where concrete evidence is easiest to supply—prior art and an implementation—but leaves the broader rationale largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts concern who would actually use the facility and why existing patterns are insufficient for them, which makes it hard to judge the urgency of standardization from the paper alone.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with a working libstdc++-based prototype available for inspection.
- The discussion of prior art credibly distinguishes the proposal from range-v3, including its treatment of empty ranges and deliberate avoidance of an optimization.
- The claim that the feature matters rests on a brief list of domains without connecting them to concrete C++ use cases or users.
- The most glaring omission is any identification of who is affected, leaving the audience for the proposal unspecified.
