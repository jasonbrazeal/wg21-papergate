Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a series of assertions about the value of a standard callback shape, with little concrete demonstration of who needs it, how it would work with existing practice, or why standardization rather than a library is required. The support is thinnest wherever the paper gestures toward motivation or prior art but stops short of connecting those observations to evidence, affected users, or implementation experience.

- The strongest support is a repeated claim that the lack of a standard callback shape obstructs generic design.
- The paper also points toward standardization as a possible solution envisioned by its own prior-art discussion.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any identified affected community or concrete need.
