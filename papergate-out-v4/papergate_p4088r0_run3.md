Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper builds a substantial case for standardization across most of the necessary dimensions, with strong grounding in existing practice, production use, and prior art; the support is thinnest where it argues that a library alone cannot deliver the same result, since that claim is asserted more than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience: two shipping libraries with production deployment and scheduled Boost review show the model works outside the committee room.
- The paper also clearly establishes why the problem matters, identifying the twenty-one-year gap in networking and the absence of standard coroutine-native I/O.
- The most glaring omission is the claimed insufficiency of a library solution, which rests on assertions about type erasure and zero-allocation storage without enough evidence to establish why standardization is the only viable path.
