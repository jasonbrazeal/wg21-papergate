Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is uneven: it gestures at relevance and gathers prior published claims, but it never makes the affirmative case that this work belongs in the standard. The strongest material is secondhand, pointing to existing deployment reports and published arguments rather than demonstrating a gap that only a standard can fill. The thinnest areas are the ones that usually carry a standardization proposal: why the standard, what the interoperability story is, and why a library is insufficient.

- The most concrete support is the citation of P2470R0’s reported sender/receiver deployment at Facebook, NVIDIA, and Bloomberg.
- The paper shows care in surveying competing published claims from both sides rather than merely asserting consensus.
- The claimed relevance to billions of monthly users is asserted without a clear evidentiary link to the standardization need itself.
- The paper offers no established case for why this belongs in the standard, how it coordinates with existing specifications, or why a library approach would not suffice.
