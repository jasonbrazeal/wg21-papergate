Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a reasonably grounded account of its core motivation and alternatives, but it leaves several important parts of the case for standardization asserted rather than demonstrated. The clearest support comes from concrete implementation experience and the comparison with prior reflection work, while the weakest areas concern whether a standard change is actually necessary and how the proposed change fits with the broader ecosystem.

- The strongest support is the reported implementation experience, including a Clang prototype and early GCC evidence of compile-time costs in the existing model.
- The discussion of prior reflection work and alternatives is concrete and establishes enough context for the proposal’s direction.
- The paper asserts but does not fully establish that the affected users and standardization path are broad or pressing enough to justify changing the standard.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any case for why the needed change cannot be delivered through a library or other non-standard means.
