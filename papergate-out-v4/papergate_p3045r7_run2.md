Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its own standardization, with particularly strong evidence of real-world impact, domain expertise, and existing implementation experience. The thinnest part of the case is the argument that a third-party library cannot suffice, where the paper asserts constraints from company policy and MISRA certification but does not fully demonstrate why standardization resolves them.

- The paper most convincingly shows the safety-critical consequences of unit errors and that the authors’ existing libraries dominate the field in adoption.
- It also clearly establishes prior art, interoperability concerns, and implementation experience through compiler links and production feedback.
- The weakest point is the claim that a library will not do, which relies on asserted corporate restrictions without showing that standardization would actually overcome those barriers.
