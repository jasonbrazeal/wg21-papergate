Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a partial basis for its own standardization, resting mainly on its acknowledgment of prior work while leaving most core justifications asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who would be affected, where the paper provides nothing at all.

- The paper does give usable context by identifying P3937 as the relevant prior discussion and framing itself as a response to that work.
- It claims, without substantiation, that the committee’s earlier decisions on non-bitwise trivial relocation should be revisited and reversed.
- It fails to establish that the proposed change is necessary for the standard rather than addressable through library design or implementation strategy.
- Most glaringly, the paper never identifies any actual users, codebases, or communities that would benefit from adopting its position.
