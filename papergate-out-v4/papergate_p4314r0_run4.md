Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case needed for standardization, mainly gesturing at conceptual confusion around what profiles do and how they are perceived. Most of the required justification is absent, leaving the reader with little sense of who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how this would work alongside existing practice.

- The strongest support is for the existence of a misunderstanding: the paper claims users confuse profiles with dialecting, epochs, or subset-of-a-superset relationships.
- It at least claims to delimit what profiles can and cannot do, though that claim rests on the authors’ own framing rather than demonstrated prior art or alternatives.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problem or why standardization is needed to address it.
- There is no implementation experience, coordination story, or explanation of why a library solution would be insufficient.
