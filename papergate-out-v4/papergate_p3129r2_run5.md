Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only broad gestures toward its standardization case, relying on references to other work and feature descriptions rather than demonstrating need, impact, or feasibility in the standard itself. The thinnest areas are the absence of any argument for why this must be in the standard rather than a library, and the lack of identified affected users or implementation evidence.

- The clearest support comes from the claim that range-returning view factories integrate with existing pipe syntax, suggesting some attention to interoperability.
- The paper gestures at prior art through references to P3130 and P3337, but does not establish how those compare or what gap remains.
- The most glaring omission is any explanation of why a library cannot provide this functionality, which leaves the standardization rationale essentially unaddressed.
