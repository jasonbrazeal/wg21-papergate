Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some concrete support for its proposal, chiefly through a working implementation, but leaves most of the case for standardization unargued. The thinnest areas are the absence of a demonstrated affected audience, a rationale for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and any discussion of coordination or interoperability.

- The strongest element is implementation experience, since the proposal has been realized in a publicly available branch.
- The paper asserts there is prior art and at least gestures at alternatives, but does not establish that case.
- It does not establish who is affected by the current limitation.
- Most notably, the paper never explains why the standard is the right venue or why a library solution would not suffice.
