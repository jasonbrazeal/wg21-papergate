Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper gives a solid conceptual foundation for why carry-less multiplication matters and what the proposed operation should look like, but it leans heavily on assertion rather than demonstration for the parts that would justify standardization specifically. The thinnest support is around the claim that existing library approaches cannot adequately solve the problem, since the same architectural-dependence argument appears repeatedly without concrete evidence tying it to a standardization need.

- The clearest support is the explanation of the operation’s mathematical role and its importance in cryptographic and related use cases.
- The paper also demonstrates familiarity with relevant prior art by grounding the proposed widening form in an existing standardization style.
- The most persistent gap is the absence of substantiated evidence that the architectural dependence of implementations makes a standard facility necessary rather than merely convenient.
- The paper likewise offers only broad references to hardware support and existing intrinsics without establishing meaningful implementation experience or interoperability outcomes.
