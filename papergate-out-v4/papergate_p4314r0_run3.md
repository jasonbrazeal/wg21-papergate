Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is largely atmospheric: it gestures toward terminological confusion and conversations the authors have had, but it does not turn those gestures into evidence about users, alternatives, or the need for normative action. The thinnest part is the complete absence of any case for why the standard, rather than some other mechanism or none at all, is the right place for this work.

- The strongest support is a vague but plausibly relevant claim that people use conflicting terms and misunderstand what profiles can do.
- The paper asserts that other proposals might conflict with profile-based removal, but it does not establish that this is a live coordination problem requiring standardization.
- The paper offers no implementation experience, no interoperability analysis, and no argument for why a library or non-normative guidance would be insufficient.
