Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some grounding for why a safer, more intuitive filter view matters, but it leaves most of the case for standardization itself unsupported. The thinnest areas concern identifying the affected audience, demonstrating why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and showing coordination with existing facilities.

- The strongest support is the motivation section, which clearly argues that current filter view use is risky and non-intuitive for ordinary programmers.
- Implementation experience is asserted through an example, but not established as evidence that the approach has been tried or validated in practice.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problem or why the standard must address it.
- Most glaringly, there is no demonstrated reason a library solution would be insufficient.
