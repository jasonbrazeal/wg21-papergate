Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for its own standardization, resting largely on assertions about consistency with existing syntax and confidence in implementability. The weakest parts are the absence of any demonstrated user need, lack of standardese-level motivation, and no indication that a library solution or alternate wording approach was seriously considered.

- The strongest support is the appeal to consistency with the existing indexing syntax for packs of types and expressions.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives shows the author checked related proposals, but it does not amount to a demonstrated case for standard action.
- The paper does not establish who is affected or why the feature must be in the standard rather than handled another way.
- There is no implementation experience recorded beyond the author’s stated confidence that Clang could support it.
