Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers concrete implementation experience and a clear account of the operation’s utility, but much of the broader case for standardization rests on assertions rather than developed evidence. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of why a library would not suffice and only passing claims about impact, prior art, and coordination.

- The strongest support is the author’s implementation in beman.scan_view, corroborated by the existence of a similar adaptor in ranges-v3.
- The paper clearly motivates the feature as a lazy, stateful counterpart to existing scan algorithms and as a planned ranges item.
- Claims about who is affected and about the significance of prior art are asserted briefly rather than established with detail.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper never addresses why a library implementation would not be adequate, leaving a central standardization question unanswered.
