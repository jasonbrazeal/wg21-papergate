Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers very little direct support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a brief account of recent work on the annexes and the fact that concurrent changes quickly made them stale. Most of the necessary justification for bringing this before the committee is simply absent, leaving the reader with little sense of who benefits or why the standard is the right venue.

- The strongest support is the stated context that the previously approved annexes became outdated as soon as other papers landed at the same meeting.
- Some basis for relevance appears in the mention of new reviewers raising minor issues about readability and formatting.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the proposed changes or what problem those changes resolve for users.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of implementation experience, prior alternatives, or why the standard rather than a separate document or library is the necessary home for this work.
