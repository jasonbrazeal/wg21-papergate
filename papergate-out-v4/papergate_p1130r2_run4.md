Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some motivation and shows awareness of existing directions, but it leaves the case for standardization largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any account of who is affected and the lack of implementation experience, which leaves the practical need and feasibility under-supported.

- The strongest support is the explanation of why the problem matters, particularly the desire to expose module dependencies to build systems and control access to portions of an SDK.
- The paper also establishes meaningful prior art and alternatives by tying its syntax and timing to `std::embed` and framing itself as a response to that proposal’s translation-unit limitations.
- A notable gap is that the paper never establishes who is affected, so the reader cannot tell how widely the problem is felt or who would benefit from standardization.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of implementation experience, leaving no evidence that the proposed mechanism has been tried, refined, or shown workable in practice.
