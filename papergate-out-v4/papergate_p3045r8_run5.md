Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper offers substantial support for standardization across most of the categories that matter, with particularly strong evidence in implementation experience, affected audiences, and the existence of prior art. The thinnest part of the case is the argument for why a library alone cannot suffice, where the paper asserts rather than demonstrates the necessity of a standard.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, where the paper points to a widely used library with demonstrated code-generation quality and links to inspect the results directly.
- The affected population and prior art are well established, with clear audience sizing and references to existing standards and libraries that ground the problem in real practice.
- The most glaring omission is in showing why a standard library component is required, since the paper claims certification and industry-policy barriers but does not substantiate them as obstacles that libraries cannot overcome.
