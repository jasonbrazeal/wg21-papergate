Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers credible motivation and some useful prior art, but its case for standardization rests heavily on assertion rather than demonstration, particularly around why only a standard facility will do and how affected users would actually adopt it. The thinnest parts are the complete absence of implementation experience and the unestablished claims about affected audiences.

- The paper points to an existing `simd` rebinding trait as meaningful prior art and shows how the pattern could generalize across standard containers.
- It names a possible semantic question for `duration` that a library solution would need to settle, though it does not develop that into a full argument against library approaches.
- The claim that the standard is the right venue is asserted through the `simd` precedent but never demonstrated beyond that single example.
- The paper gives no implementation experience and never identifies who would be affected by the absence of the proposed facility.
