Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper’s strongest support lies in explaining why defaulted postfix operators would be useful and in situating the idea within prior standardization work, but the case that the feature belongs in the standard is much thinner on direct evidence and experience. The argument is largely built on plausible claims rather than demonstrated need or validation.

- The motivation is clearly established through the canonical nature of postfix increment/decrement and the boilerplate and error risks it addresses.
- The prior art and alternatives section is solid, showing continuity with P1046 and the comparison-defaulting approach.
- The paper does not establish that the affected audience is as broad as claimed or that standardization, rather than a library or rewrite rule, is necessary.
- There is no implementation experience offered, which is the most glaring omission given the paper’s own framing around defaulted semantics.
