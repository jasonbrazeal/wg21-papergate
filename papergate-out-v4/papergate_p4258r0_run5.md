Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only scattered gestures toward a standardization case, concentrated in two areas and absent almost everywhere else. Its thinnest support is in the basic work of identifying an audience, reviewing alternatives, and showing why the library or the existing standard cannot address the problem.

- The strongest support is a concrete claim that a provider going away can leave waiting threads blocked forever, at least gesturing at real user harm.
- A secondary point connects a missed optimization in serial iterator advancement to a limitation in current practice, though it stops short of tying that to standardization need.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problems it describes.
- Most glaringly, it offers no prior art, alternatives, interoperability considerations, or argument for why a library solution would be insufficient.
