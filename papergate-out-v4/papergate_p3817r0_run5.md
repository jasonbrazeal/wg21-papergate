Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin foundation for its own standardization, identifying a plausible gap but doing little to substantiate the need, the affected community, or the viability of the proposed remedy. The support is thinnest around the questions that matter most to a committee audience: why the standard must change, why a library cannot address the problem, and whether the approach has any practical validation.

- The clearest contribution is an articulated distinction between structured bindings and `std::tie`, which at least names a possible gap.
- The paper gestures at advantages over existing practice but does not develop who would benefit or why those benefits require language change.
- The argument is weakest on implementation experience, coordination, and the fundamental question of why a library solution is insufficient.
