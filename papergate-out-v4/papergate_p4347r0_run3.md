Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only the faintest outline of a case for standardization, relying on brief, speculative remarks rather than developed evidence or analysis. Its support is thinnest wherever concrete demonstration is expected—coordination, implementation experience, and the question of why a library cannot suffice are left entirely unaddressed.

- The closest thing to a motivating rationale is the observation that implementations might want to enable P2900 and P3100 separately or together, but even that is framed as something one can envision rather than something demonstrated.
- The paper gestures at interest from multiple people and a short path from existing practice to a specification, but gives no substantive account of affected users, alternatives, or why standardization is the right venue.
- It offers no implementation experience, no analysis of coordination with existing features, and no argument that the functionality cannot be provided by a library.
