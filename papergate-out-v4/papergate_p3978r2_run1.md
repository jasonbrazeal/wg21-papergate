Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some support for standardization through its discussion of related proposals and available implementation experience, but most of its central case rests on assertions rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest parts are the failure to show why a library solution is insufficient and the largely first-person framing of who is affected.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete implementation experience in the vir-simd library and testing on current toolchains.
- The paper also establishes familiarity with prior and adjacent proposals, situating the change within an ongoing design conversation.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this cannot be handled outside the standard.
