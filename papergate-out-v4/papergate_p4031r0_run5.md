Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only scattered assertions in favor of its naming change, with most of its standardization rationale left implicit or absent. The thinnest areas are those a proposal most needs to address: why the standard itself must act, why a library solution is insufficient, and whether there is any implementation experience behind the suggested approach.

- The strongest support is for coordination concerns, where the paper argues that a namespace name referencing a nonexistent `system_context` would create confusion.
- Prior art and alternatives receive some attention through a reference to P3804R1 and its favored names, though this remains more a claim than a demonstrated case.
- The paper does not establish why the change belongs in the standard as opposed to a library, nor does it offer evidence from implementation or deployment.
- Most glaringly, the paper offers no direct argument for why standardization is required at all, leaving the core need for a standard mechanism unaddressed.
