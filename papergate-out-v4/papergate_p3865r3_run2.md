Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides meaningful support for the problem it identifies, particularly by connecting the core-language gap to existing issues and prior evolution in template template argument matching. The thinnest parts of the case concern evidence: the affected audience, implementation status, and the claimed necessity of a core-language change are asserted more than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is the concrete linkage to CWG 3003, LWG 4381, and P0552R0, which situates the proposal in an established trail of core-language and library coordination.
- The paper also clearly explains the semantic mismatch introduced by current template template parameter deduction rules.
- Less convincingly, the paper claims all current implementations accept the relevant behavior without providing the kind of detail that would establish implementation experience for the exact semantics proposed.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of substantiation for the repeated assertion that no library-only fix is possible, which underpins much of the standardization rationale.
