Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper’s support for standardization rests chiefly on its account of prior discussion and direction from LEWG and Kona, but it leaves most of the burden of justification unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any identified affected audience, the lack of rationale for why the standard is the right mechanism, and the silence on implementation experience.

- The strongest support comes from the documented LEWG preference and the Kona conclusion, which establish that the proposed direction has committee history and prior art.
- The paper claims but does not establish why the change matters, relying on brief remarks about allocator flexibility and NB comments rather than a developed motivation.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the current specification or who would benefit from the change.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, leaving no evidence that the proposed approach has been tried in practice.
