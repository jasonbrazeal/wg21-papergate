Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper leans heavily on assertions about unresolved design concerns rather than demonstrating a concrete need for standardization, and its support is thinnest in the areas where it matters most: who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the paper’s identification of three components that must align, though even that is only claimed rather than backed by detail.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives gestures at an alternative backend design and namespace renaming, but offers little evidence that these were evaluated seriously.
- The paper provides no implementation experience beyond an unverified mention of a proof-of-concept customization mechanism.
- Most glaringly, the proposal does not establish who is affected, why a library cannot solve the problem, or why the standard itself should address it.
