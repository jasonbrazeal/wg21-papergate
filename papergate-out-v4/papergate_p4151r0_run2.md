Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow naming argument centered on the word “on,” and it does not develop the surrounding case that a standards change is warranted. The thinnest areas are those that would connect the rename to affected users, standardization value, library feasibility, interoperability, or implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the paper’s observation that `affine_on` was redesigned from a binary to a unary interface, making the “on” in its name harder to justify.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives shows some awareness of the design history but relies mainly on assertions rather than a broader comparison of options.
- The paper does not establish why changing this name in the standard matters to users or implementers, nor why a library-level or non-standard remedy would be insufficient.
