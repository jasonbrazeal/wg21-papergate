Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only the beginning of a case for standardization, resting on an intuition about naming rather than evidence or developed argument. The support is thinnest around the very questions that would justify bringing this before the committee: why a library solution cannot address it, how it coordinates with existing specifications, and what implementation experience shows.

- The strongest claim is that the current name conflicts with the usual naming convention for views of this kind, supported only by analogy to names like as_const or as_rvalue.
- The paper asserts that users are affected by the misleading name, but gives no examples of confusion or misuse in practice.
- The paper does not establish why the change requires standardization rather than a library-level or documentation-level response.
- Most glaringly, it offers no implementation experience, coordination plan, or interoperability analysis to ground the proposal in committee concerns.
