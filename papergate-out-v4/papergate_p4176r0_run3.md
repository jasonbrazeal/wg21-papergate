The paper offers only a thin justification for its own standardization, with most of the necessary case left implicit or unaddressed. The strongest material concerns the general motivation and an alternative approach, but even these are asserted rather than developed, and the remaining categories receive no support at all.

- The paper at least gestures toward a motivation and an alternative by saying it aims to improve the situation through new nonterminals placed in their respective subclauses.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the proposed change.
- The paper does not explain why the standard is the right place for this work rather than some other mechanism.
- The paper offers no implementation experience, coordination considerations, or interoperability discussion to ground the proposal.
