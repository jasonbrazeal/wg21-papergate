Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper gives substantial support for the need to standardize a physical quantities and units library, particularly through established evidence of real-world failures, a mature existing implementation, and clear demand from both users and committee review. The thinnest parts of the case concern coordination with existing practice and the argument that a non-standard library cannot adequately serve industry needs, where the paper asserts urgency but does not fully demonstrate why standardization is the only viable path.

- The strongest support comes from concrete safety failures and the popularity and depth of the existing mp-units implementation, which establish both the problem and a plausible foundation for standardization.
- The rationale for why this belongs in the standard rather than remaining a library is asserted through policy and adoption concerns but is not fully established with direct evidence.
- The case for coordination and interoperability with other standards or existing C++ facilities is the most glaring omission, relying on rhetorical examples rather than demonstrated alignment or integration planning.
