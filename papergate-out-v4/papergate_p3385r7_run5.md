Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gestures toward a real gap in attribute handling, but it does not yet carry the burden of showing that standardization is warranted. The support is thinnest around the concrete need for a standard facility, since nearly every essential point is asserted rather than demonstrated with evidence, examples, or community validation.

- The strongest support is the claim of an experimental implementation available on Compiler Explorer, though even that is only reported, not shown through reproducible results or broader usage.
- The paper identifies a plausible interaction with reflection and code generation but relies on a single referenced proposal rather than establishing actual practice or demand.
- The most glaring omission is any substantiated account of who is affected and why existing mechanisms cannot serve their needs beyond a brief assertion about uniform vehicles.
