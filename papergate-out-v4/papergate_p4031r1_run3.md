Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a slim, conversational case for its proposed namespace name, and even that support rests largely on unverified meeting context and personal preference rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest parts are the complete absence of argument for why the standard must address this, why a library solution would not suffice, and whether any implementation experience exists to validate the approach.

- The strongest, though still weak, support is the acknowledgment that prior names like `replacement` and `replacement_functions` were also criticized for being unclear about what is being replaced.
- The claim that the proposal matters rests on an assertion about confusion with `system_context`, but the paper does not establish that `system_context` is actually present or relevant in the current working draft.
- The only evidence of affected parties is an unreproduced Kona poll, so the paper does not show who would benefit or how widely the naming problem is felt.
- The most glaring omissions are the complete silence on why standardization—rather than a library or a different naming convention—is necessary, and the absence of any implementation experience or coordination discussion.
