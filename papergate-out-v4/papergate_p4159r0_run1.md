The paper offers only a narrow procedural justification for its own standardization: it says it is responding to committee direction and supplying wording, but it does not develop the surrounding case for why that wording belongs in the standard. The thinnest support is in the areas that explain the need itself—who is affected, what alternatives exist, why the standard is the right venue, and what implementation experience says—none of which are addressed.

- The strongest support is the paper’s statement that it provides wording in response to LEWG direction on a specific library issue.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by making the concepts exposition-only.
- The paper does not demonstrate why a library-only solution would be insufficient.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, leaving the standardization need asserted rather than shown.
