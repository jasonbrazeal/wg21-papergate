Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a narrow basis for its own standardization: it identifies the design space and some concrete alternatives, but much of the surrounding justification reads more as assertion than demonstration. The thinnest areas are the claimed safety, audience, interoperability, and implementation benefits, where the paper supplies goals rather than evidence that the proposed mechanism is necessary or ready for the standard.

- The strongest support is the discussion of prior art and alternatives, which credibly positions the proposal against P2900 and explains the intended distinctions.
- The paper claims broad safety and certainty benefits for users and tools, but does not establish how the guarantee produces those outcomes in practice.
- The claimed implementation experience is notably limited, since the full proposal is acknowledged as unimplemented and only constituent pieces are cited.
