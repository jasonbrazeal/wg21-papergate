Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers genuine support only for the narrow claim that the continuation framing is a legitimate alternative reading of `execute`, but it makes almost no affirmative case that this framing requires standardization. The strongest material concerns prior art and conceptual reframing; the thinnest concerns who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the approach has been reduced to practice.

- The paper does establish that the work framing is not the only valid interpretation and that the continuation framing changes how the four cited deficiencies are understood.
- It documents relevant prior art and the absence of the continuation framing from the three papers that drove the original pivot.
- It does not establish who would be affected by standardizing the proposed basis operation.
- The paper never makes a case for why this belongs in the C++ standard rather than in a library, and its implementation experience and interoperability claims remain asserted rather than demonstrated.
