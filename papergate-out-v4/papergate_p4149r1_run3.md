Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization, resting on a brief rationale about consistency and a referral from CWG, but leaving most of the necessary case unstated. The thinnest areas concern who would be affected, why standardization is needed, interoperability, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the passage explaining that CWG wants to unify treatment of several constructs to improve language and specification consistency.
- The paper claims some consistency with noexcept-specifiers and records one compiler accepting the behavior, but neither point is developed into a real prior-art or implementation-experience case.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not establish who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how the change interacts with existing practice.
