Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some useful groundwork, but its case for standardization remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The strongest material is the concrete implementation experience and the engagement with prior proposals, while the argument for why this must be a language feature and who specifically needs it is thin.

- The implementation work in a Clang fork and the reference to prior experimentation provide tangible evidence that the mechanism can be built and exercised.
- The paper connects its approach to earlier proposals and examples such as `Fraction` and `SmallString`, grounding it in a recognizable design lineage.
- The discussion of impacts on users is absent, leaving no clear picture of who would adopt this or what practical problem drives them.
- The claims that a library cannot solve the problem and that standardization is the only way are repeated as conclusions rather than supported with an argument or evidence.
