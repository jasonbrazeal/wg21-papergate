Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper provides only a narrow foundation for its own standardization, centered on one concrete piece of implementation experience; most of the surrounding rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears wherever the document needs to connect its catalog to real users, standardization mechanisms, or alternatives beyond brief citations.

- The strongest support is the collaboratively developed wording branch, which shows at least some draft-integrated implementation work exists.
- The paper claims the general usefulness of cataloging UB and IFNDR but does not establish that anyone is concretely affected by the absence of this particular annex.
- The prior-art references are named, but the paper does not show how this proposal advances beyond them or why those earlier efforts failed to resolve the need.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any case for why a standard annex is necessary rather than a separately maintained reference document or library.
