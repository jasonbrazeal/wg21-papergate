Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization, mainly in gesturing at why the topic is worth clarifying and what some alternative framings might be. Beyond that, the case is largely undeveloped: it does not show who is concretely affected, why standardization rather than another venue is needed, how the work would fit with existing or future specifications, or that any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is a plausible motivation: the paper notes that users misunderstand what activating a profile does, which at least hints at a documentation or specification gap worth addressing.
- The discussion of alternatives is thin but present, listing a few terms and observing that other papers could remove features more aggressively than a profile would.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established audience or impact, leaving it unclear whose practice would change or what interoperability problem the paper would solve.
