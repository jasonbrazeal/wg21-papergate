Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper leans heavily on assertions about the needs of low-latency, gaming, and financial constituencies, but it rarely supplies the concrete evidence or named references that would turn those assertions into a case for standardization. The support is thinnest where it matters most: showing that the problem is real, that alternatives are insufficient, and that standardization is the only viable path.

- The strongest material concerns implementation interest, where at least some connection to Bloomberg and existing relocation proposals is named.
- The paper consistently repeats the claim that P2300’s allocation patterns are incompatible with low-latency networking, but never demonstrates that incompatibility or its prevalence.
- Most of the affected-community and prior-art claims are broad endorsements or aspirations rather than documented requirements or evaluated alternatives.
- The absence of any established argument for why a library would not suffice leaves the need for standardization almost entirely unsupported.
