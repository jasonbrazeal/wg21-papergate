Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

Overall, the paper gives substantial support for a standardization effort, particularly by documenting committee interest, real-world use, prior art, and implementation experience. The case is thinnest where it must show why a library alone is insufficient, since the paper asserts the benefits and uniqueness of existing libraries but does not fully demonstrate that standardization is the necessary next step.

- The strongest support is the repeated committee interest and the existing popularity and deployment of the author’s libraries.
- The paper clearly identifies affected groups and gives concrete evidence of widespread need and prior art.
- Implementation experience is well credited through detailed descriptions of design changes informed by production use.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of a convincing demonstration that existing libraries cannot remain non-standard solutions.
