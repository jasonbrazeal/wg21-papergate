Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper leaves much of its standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated, with the most direct support concentrated around consistency arguments and the thinnest support in the areas that usually matter most for a library proposal: implementation experience and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the consistency argument that hidden friend operators already behave this way through ADL, giving the proposal at least a conceptual anchor.
- The paper gestures toward precedent and coordination with `reference_wrapper`, but treats that resemblance as self-evident rather than showing what it implies for standardization.
- The discussion of repairing the language rather than the library acknowledges the obvious alternative but does not establish why the standard library is the right place.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or demonstration that a library-only solution fails to address the need.
