Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers fairly thin support for its own standardization, resting mostly on reported impressions and terminology rather than demonstrated need or technical necessity. Its strongest material concerns the perceived confusion around what profile activation means, but even that remains asserted rather than shown with evidence, while the more concrete questions of standards scope, interoperability, and implementation are left unaddressed.

- The clearest support is the claim that users misunderstand whether proposals in a profile paper take effect only when the profile is active, though no evidence is offered to substantiate that confusion.
- The paper gestures toward alternatives by noting terms like dialecting, epochs, and subset-of-a-superset, but it does not actually compare its approach against those options.
- It does not establish why this work belongs in the standard rather than in guidance, a library, or a separate document.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience or any coordination and interoperability discussion.
