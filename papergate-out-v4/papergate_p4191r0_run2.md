Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a fairly thin evidentiary basis for standardization: every category of support is asserted rather than demonstrated, with repeated reliance on the same short passages. The thinnest areas are those where the paper gestures at existing practice or user need but does not document the scope, prevalence, or consequences of the problem it addresses.

- The strongest support comes from the mention of nVidia’s stdexec and its `__nothrow_connectable` concept, which at least points toward relevant prior art, though it is not developed into an argument.
- The paper repeatedly asserts that users must “roll their own” utility, but provides no examples, prevalence data, or evidence of widespread difficulty.
- The discussion of whom the problem affects is limited to a single sentence about users with either an environment or a receiver, with no supporting detail.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any evidence that a library solution would be insufficient, since the only cited prior art appears to do exactly what the paper proposes.
