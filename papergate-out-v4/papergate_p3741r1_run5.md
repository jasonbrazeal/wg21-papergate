Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with its strongest grounding in implementation experience but little evidence for the broader need, affected audience, or role of the standard. The case is thinnest around motivation, interoperability, and why existing library capabilities are insufficient.

- The clearest support comes from the cited range/v3 implementation and the author’s own libstdc++-based prototype, which show feasibility.
- The prior art discussion gestures toward existing work but mainly asserts a design preference rather than demonstrating why that work is inadequate or what standardization would add.
- The paper does not establish who would benefit or why the standard, rather than a library, is the right venue, leaving the central rationale unsupported.
