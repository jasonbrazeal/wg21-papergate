Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some justification for the problem it addresses and for positioning the change as a workaround, but it leaves several core standardization questions essentially unexamined. The thinnest parts concern who is actually affected, how the feature would coordinate with existing or forthcoming range facilities, and whether a library solution is impossible.

- The strongest support is the paper’s explanation that current filter views are risky and non-intuitive, with broken or surprising behavior in basic use cases.
- The paper also grounds its approach in prior discussion and an SG9 vote, treating the proposal as a deliberate first step rather than a final design.
- However, the paper does not identify the affected user population or demonstrate demand beyond a general appeal to ordinary programmers.
- Most notably, it never establishes why a standard library facility is required rather than a library solution, and it offers only a claimed implementation example without substantive implementation experience.
