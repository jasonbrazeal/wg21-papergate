Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow thread of support for its own standardization, anchored in a single motivating failure scenario, while leaving nearly every other necessary justification unaddressed. The thinnest areas are those that would explain why this belongs in the standard, whom it would affect, and whether the proposed approach is actually viable in practice.

- The strongest support is the concrete lifetime and joining hazard described for `counting_scope`, which at least gives the proposal a recognizable problem to solve.
- The claim that the design responds to LEWG concerns is noted, but the paper does not establish what those concerns were or how this addresses them.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution would be insufficient, which is a central requirement for bringing any facility into the standard.
- Most glaringly, there is no evidence of implementation experience, no discussion of affected users, and no case for why standardization is needed as opposed to a non-standard library.
