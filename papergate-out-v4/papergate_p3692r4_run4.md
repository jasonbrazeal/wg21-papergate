Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers useful groundwork on the out-of-thin-air problem and demonstrates that the standardization gap is real, but it does not build a complete case for the proposed normative change. Its strongest material concerns motivation and prior work, while the argument becomes thin around why a standard change is needed at all and who would actually be served by it.

- The paper clearly establishes that the meaning of “depend on” is under-specified and that invented atomic loads create genuine problems for memory-model work.
- It also provides credible prior art, including GCC’s `-funsigned-char` override and earlier OOTA analyses, showing enough background to frame the question.
- The claim that the change will help researchers focus on advanced tooling is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The paper never establishes who is affected or why a non-standard document, library facility, or existing practice would be insufficient.
