Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper builds a thorough and consistently sourced case that the `Environment` parameter creates structural interoperability problems, and it supports nearly every dimension of its standardization argument with specification analysis, implementation evidence, and ecosystem precedent. The support is broad rather than shallow, though it leans heavily on the same few core examples and reports.

- The strongest support lies in the documented implementation experience and prior art, where the paper cites reference implementations, multiple independent reports, and production precedents that all converge on the same failure mode.
- The case for why a library solution will not suffice is also well established, with clear reasoning that no general type-erasure mechanism exists for the open query protocol.
- The thinnest part of the case is the breadth of affected-party evidence, since the paper relies on a small number of reports and surveys to stand for ecosystem-wide risk.
