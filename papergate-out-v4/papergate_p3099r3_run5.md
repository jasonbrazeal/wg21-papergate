Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper gives a reasonably solid account of prior art and existing implementation experience, particularly around the Clang vendor attribute and available compiler branches, but it leaves some parts of its standardization case more asserted than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience, with the feature already available as a Clang vendor attribute and demonstrated in GCC and Clang branches.
- Coordination and interoperability are also well supported, since the paper describes a shared message layout and compatibility between the two compilers.
- The paper’s argument for why the standard library cannot provide this is essentially absent, making that the most visible gap in the standardization case.
