Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives only a thin account of why this should become a C++ standard rather than remain a library or ecosystem concern. Its strongest moments gesture at a real gap in native data-frame support and the appeal of zero-copy interoperability, but most of the necessary case is asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper establishes that lack of a native data frame pushes AI workflows toward Python/Pandas and that zero-copy exchange with that ecosystem is a motivating goal.
- It claims, but does not substantiate, that standardization is needed to prevent fragmentation, without showing evidence of actual or likely fragmentation.
- It names one similar project as prior art, but does not examine alternatives or explain why they fall short of the proposal’s aims.
- It offers no concrete implementation experience, affected-user analysis, or interoperability detail to support the claim that a library solution would be insufficient.
