Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, resting mostly on claims that its interfaces improve consistency and that related P3130 machinery is available. Nearly all of the required justification is either asserted without evidence or left entirely unaddressed, especially around who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the explicit linkage to P3130’s concepts and utility types, though even that is largely a citation rather than a developed argument.
- The paper asserts practical benefits such as reliable access through data structs and expected values on unordered edges, but does not demonstrate them.
- The reference implementation is mentioned as C++20-compatible, yet no evidence of actual use or implementation experience is provided.
- The paper is silent on affected users, coordination with existing or future work, the need for a standard rather than a library, and prior alternatives.
