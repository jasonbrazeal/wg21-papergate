Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a very narrow justification for the change it wants, and almost all of that justification rests on an appeal to naming convention rather than on demonstrated user harm, clear need, or viable alternatives. The discussion thinnest where the proposal should be strongest: it does not explain why standardization is necessary in the first place, why a library-level solution would be inadequate, or whether the change has been tried anywhere in practice.

- The naming convention argument based on “as_...” examples is at least a concrete consistency claim, though the paper does not show that this convention is actually followed broadly enough to make the rename compelling.
- The claim that the current name is misleading because it sounds active like `std::ranges::to` is asserted, but the paper gives no evidence that users are in fact confused.
- The paper does not establish who specifically is affected by the current name or how severe that effect is.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any case for why the standard must address this, along with no implementation experience or demonstration that a library cannot solve the issue.
