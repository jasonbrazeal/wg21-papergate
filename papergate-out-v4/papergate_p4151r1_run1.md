Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a naming objection that is repeated rather than developed. The argument for a rename is asserted as a matter of rationale, but the proposal never connects that concern to who would be affected, why a library-level workaround would be insufficient, or what implementation evidence exists.

- The strongest point is the claimed inconsistency between making `affine_on` unary and keeping the “on” in its name.
- The paper also gestures at prior LEWG discussion where changing the interface shape was said to warrant changing the name.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the current name or how the rename would improve their experience.
