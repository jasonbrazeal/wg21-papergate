Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper’s support for its own standardization is thin: it asserts a maintenance rationale for updating two newly added annexes, but it does very little to show that the work belongs in the standard or that anyone in particular needs it done there rather than as editorial coordination. The strongest material concerns a narrow inconsistency identified in prior annex-related work, while nearly every other case for action is left to the reader’s inference.

- The paper does give a concrete reason for existence by citing an already-approved annex catalogue that was immediately made incomplete by concurrent changes.
- It identifies one specific defect—pointer difference being made defined without a corresponding definition—where an editorial correction has a plausible target.
- The paper asserts that ongoing annex maintenance should happen in individual papers, but it offers no evidence that the standard currently lacks such a maintenance path or that this document is needed to create one.
- It never establishes who is affected, why the standard rather than an editorial process is the right vehicle, or whether any implementation or user has encountered the described inconsistencies.
