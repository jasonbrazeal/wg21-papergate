Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers solid grounding for its basic motivation and for the existence of prior art, but the case for standardization itself remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who would actually be affected, why a library would not suffice, and any meaningful implementation experience beyond the author’s own project.

- The paper clearly establishes why SI definitions matter for interoperability and readability in quantity expressions.
- It also shows familiarity with the relevant prior work, including the SI Brochure, P3045R8, and the mp-units library.
- What remains unproven is the claimed need for standardization specifically: the interoperability argument is repeated but never developed into a concrete scenario where a widely used library cannot meet that need.
- Most glaringly, the paper gives no substantive evidence of implementation experience or user demand, relying only on straw polls and assertions drawn from a single open-source library.
