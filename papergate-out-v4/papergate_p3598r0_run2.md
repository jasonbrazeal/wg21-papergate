Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only a thin foundation for its own standardization: every point it gestures toward is asserted rather than demonstrated, and two essential questions—coordination and why a library cannot serve—are left entirely unaddressed. The strongest material consists of repeated references to GCC trunk behavior, but even that is stated rather than shown in any detail.

- The clearest support is the repeated claim that GCC trunk already implements the behavior, giving at least a suggestion of existing practice.
- The discussion of prior art leans entirely on another paper without showing how that reasoning transfers to splice-expressions.
- The paper offers nothing to establish how the feature would fit with existing or in-flight standardization efforts.
- Most notably, it never explains why a library solution would be insufficient for the problem it describes.
