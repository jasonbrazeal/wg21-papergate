Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization: nearly all of the required justifications are asserted rather than demonstrated, with the sole exception of implementation experience. The thinnest support lies in the lack of concrete evidence for the claimed prevalence, impact, and impossibility of conforming expression—points that are repeatedly stated but never substantiated with examples, measurements, or references specific enough to support the claims.

- The one clearly established point is that an earlier version has been implemented in libc++ and Clang, with links provided to the implementation and a live compiler explorer instance.
- The paper asserts that pointer tagging is widely used and names several projects, but provides no discussion connecting those uses to a need for standardization or showing how the cited implementations would benefit.
- The claim that the functionality cannot be expressed in a standard-conforming way is repeated in multiple sections but never demonstrated with a concrete example or analysis, leaving the core motivation unsupported.
