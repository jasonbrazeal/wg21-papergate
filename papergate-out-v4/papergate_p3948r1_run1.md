Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some real support on a few points, especially implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves major parts of the standardization case unaddressed. The thinnest areas are showing who is affected, why the standard must change, and whether a library-only solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the implementation work, including a libstdc++ fork and a GCC patch, which demonstrates that the idea can be put into practice.
- The discussion of prior art and the rejection of an alternative approach in LEWG gives the proposal some grounding in existing standardization context.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problem, leaving the audience for the change unclear.
- The justification for why this requires a standard change rather than a library solution is asserted but not substantiated.
