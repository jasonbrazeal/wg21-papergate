Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin argument for standardization, resting almost entirely on an internal naming concern and a passing account of the interface’s redesign. There is no substantive discussion of affected users, standardization need, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving the case largely unbuilt.

- The strongest support is the observation that a unary `affine_on` undermines the “on” in its name and that a change of interface might warrant a change of name.
- The paper also gestures at prior art by noting the redesign from a binary to a unary form accepting only a sender.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any account of who is affected by the proposed change or why the standard should address it rather than a library.
