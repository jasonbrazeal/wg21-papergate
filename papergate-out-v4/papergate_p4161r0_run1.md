Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow justification for its own existence: it establishes that the existing name is grammatically awkward, but little else. Support thins out almost immediately when the paper moves from the linguistic observation to claims about who is affected, what alternatives exist, or why any of this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.

- The strongest support is the paper’s demonstration that `std::less` is grammatically incongruent when applied to integral types.
- The paper asserts, rather than demonstrates, that this affects widespread production code and that no library-level remedy is possible.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or interoperability analysis showing that standardizing `std::fewer` would be feasible and compatible with existing practice.
