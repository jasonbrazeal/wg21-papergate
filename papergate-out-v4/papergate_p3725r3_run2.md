Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only narrow support for its own standardization, centered on a genuine usability problem with current filter views; beyond that, the case is largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any account of who is affected, how the proposed facility would coordinate with existing or in-flight standardization work, and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest part of the paper is its identification of broken, risky, or non-intuitive behavior in existing filter views, which the review credits as an established need.
- The paper asserts that standardization is necessary to give ordinary programmers a safe and self-explanatory workaround, but it does not establish this against plausible alternatives.
- It gestures at prior art and related proposals, but the connections are named rather than shown to support the specific design.
- Most glaringly, the paper never establishes who is affected, how the proposal interoperates with the surrounding standard, or why the problem cannot be solved by a library.
