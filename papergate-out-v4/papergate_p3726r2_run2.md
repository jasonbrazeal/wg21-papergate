Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers the strongest support for its discussion of prior art and alternatives, but most of the case it needs to make—about who is affected, why this matters, consistency with existing language, interoperability, and why a library solution is insufficient—rests on assertion rather than demonstration. The thinnest area is implementation experience, which the paper does not establish at all.

- The paper clearly establishes that prior approaches, including the previous revision and related proposals, were incomplete or limited in ways that motivate further work.
- The paper claims, without adequate support, that the problem affects real users and that the proposed approach is consistent or necessary within the existing language model.
- The paper does not establish any implementation experience, leaving the practical viability of the proposal entirely unshown.
