Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a thin basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on its acknowledged relationship to P2822R2 and a small compiler-explorer prototype. Beyond that recognition of prior art, the document repeatedly cites the same unresolved ADL problem without connecting it to a specific affected audience, practical demand, or evidence that the proposed mechanism is necessary in the standard.

- The strongest support is the paper’s explicit positioning as an exploration distinct from P2822R2, which at least frames the design space it is entering.
- The implementation experience is limited to a single prototype example, so there is no meaningful demonstration of feasibility or use.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established reason why the problem affects real C++ users or why a library-only solution cannot suffice.
