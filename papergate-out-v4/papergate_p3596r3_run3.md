Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers real evidence of implementability and situates its work within an existing lineage of prior efforts, but it is much thinner about why this catalog belongs in the standard, whom it would actually burden or benefit, and what standardization would enable that an external reference could not. The least developed parts are the justifications most specific to ISO standardization itself.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience, with the annex developed against the working draft in a public branch.
- The paper also clearly connects itself to prior cataloging work and to existing category schemes for undefined behavior.
- The discussion of who is affected is only gestural, offering that some totals would be useful without establishing the actual audience or impact.
- The most glaring omission is any case for why this needs to be in the standard, as opposed to a non-normative guide or shared external resource.
