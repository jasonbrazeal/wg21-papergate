Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper offers substantial support for most of its standardization case, with particular strength in implementation experience, prior art, and the reasons a shared vocabulary matters. The support is thinnest where the paper argues that a library alone cannot solve the problem, since that claim is asserted rather than demonstrated with the same concreteness as the others.

- The strongest support comes from the documented, decade-long production use in Boost.Asio and the working implementation in the Capy library.
- The paper clearly establishes why the problem matters across application developers, infrastructure authors, and the broader ecosystem that has failed to produce a shared task type or environment protocol.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of established evidence for the claim that a library solution would not suffice, leaving that section more asserted than proven.
