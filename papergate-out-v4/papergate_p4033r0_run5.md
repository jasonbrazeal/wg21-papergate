Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper’s core argument rests on a plausible motivation and a concrete, if modest, implementation experiment, but it leaves most of the burden of justification unaddressed. The strongest material covers prior art and the acknowledged divergence from existing reflection facilities, while the thinnest areas concern why this belongs in the standard and how it would fit with other standardization work.

- The paper is most convincing when it situates its approach against existing reflection capabilities and notes deliberate design differences from `define_aggregate`.
- Its claim that the problem matters and that real users are affected is asserted largely through one implementation example and an admitted lack of extensive experience.
- The paper offers no established argument for why standardization is necessary, how the feature would coordinate with adjacent proposals, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
