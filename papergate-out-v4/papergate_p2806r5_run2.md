Verdict: Strong (8/14)

The paper gives real but uneven support for its own standardization, grounding the proposal in a concrete implementation and a clear motivating gap, while leaving several other justifications more asserted than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, with working code in Clang and a publicly viewable compiler explorer example.
- The core motivation is established, showing that existing constructs make certain control-flow outcomes needlessly difficult and that block expressions would expand code-injection possibilities.
- Several sections—such as prior art, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library cannot suffice—are claimed but not fully established beyond brief comparisons or statements of limitation.
- The most glaring omission is any established account of who is affected, leaving the intended user base and scale of need unspecified.
