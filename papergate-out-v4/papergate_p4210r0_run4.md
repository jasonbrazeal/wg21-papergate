Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides credible motivation and useful evidence of prior implementation experience, but its case for standardization remains uneven because several arguments are asserted rather than demonstrated, particularly around affected users, the need for a standard component, and why a library cannot suffice. The thinnest areas are coordination with existing standard facilities and the claim that this cannot be delivered outside the standard.

- The strongest support comes from the worked document-model example and the explanation of how `copy_on_write` fills a gap left by `indirect` and `polymorphic`.
- The implementation experience is concrete, with a reference implementation and an existing library cited.
- The paper claims rather than shows who is affected, leaning on a single historical framework without establishing current or broad impact.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of coordination and interoperability discussion with related standard library components and conventions.
