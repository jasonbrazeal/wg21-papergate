The paper offers some context for why the naming question matters and shows that alternatives were considered, but it falls well short of demonstrating that this particular change belongs in the standard. The support is thinnest around the actual need for standardization, the affected audience, and any evidence that the proposed direction works in practice.

- The case for why the inconsistency matters is asserted rather than shown, resting on the claim that a `get` returning `optional` would be unique and confusing.
- The paper does not establish who would be affected beyond noting weak consensus for the original name.
- The paper offers no case for why this requires standardization, how it would coordinate with existing practice, what a library solution would lack, or what implementation experience supports it.
