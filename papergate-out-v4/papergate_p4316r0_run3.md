The paper offers only a skeletal rationale for standardization, and much of that support remains asserted rather than demonstrated. Its thinnest areas concern the absence of any identified affected parties, any argument for why the standard rather than a library is the right vehicle, and any coordination or implementation evidence.

- The paper gestures toward prior art by describing itself as the skeleton for a merged Profiles document, but it does not establish how that intended structure relates to existing alternatives.
- The paper says nothing about who would be affected by standardization, leaving the audience and their needs entirely unaddressed.
- Most glaringly, the paper offers no case for why this work belongs in the standard as opposed to a library, no interoperability considerations, and no implementation experience.
