Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers meaningful support in the areas most anchored in deployment and design history, particularly through its account of production use in Folly since 2018 and its recognition of alternatives such as global cleanup. The case is thinnest where it reaches beyond that experience to justify standardization itself: the claims about who needs the feature, why it cannot remain a library facility, and how it interoperates with the broader ecosystem are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is implementation experience, backed by a concrete open-source facility and years of production use.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is well established, especially in contrasting object cohorts with global cleanup and explaining the performance tradeoff.
- The most glaring omission is a demonstrated argument for why this needs to be in the standard rather than remaining a library capability, despite the identified performance advantage.
