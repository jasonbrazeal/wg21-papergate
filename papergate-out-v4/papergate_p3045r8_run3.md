Verdict: Excellent (12/14)

The paper assembles a substantial and largely persuasive body of support for standardization, with the strongest evidence coming from direct reports of production impact and broad consensus among affected developer communities. Its case is thinnest where it argues that a library cannot suffice, since that argument rests more on general industry practices and asserted limitations than on demonstrated gaps that only a standard could fill.

- The paper most convincingly establishes why the feature matters and who is affected through concrete, high-stakes failure examples and evidence of strong committee and industry consensus.
- It also provides credible prior art and implementation experience by pointing to a widely used, performance-verified library as the basis of the proposal.
- The least developed part of the case is the claim that existing library solutions are inherently inadequate for certification or safety-critical use, which is asserted rather than shown with specific, unaddressable failures of non-standard libraries.
