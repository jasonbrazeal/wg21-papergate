Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some useful groundwork for its standardization discussion, particularly through concrete implementation experience and a clear account of related prior work, but it leaves the central case for standardization largely implicit. The thinnest support is in the areas that would justify committee action: the affected audience, the need for a standard rather than another mechanism, and how the design would interoperate with existing contract facilities.

- The strongest support is a working implementation in a GCC fork, which shows the approach is more than a paper design.
- The paper also situates itself credibly against prior proposals and recent committee discussion, making clear it is engaging with existing alternatives.
- What remains unestablished is why the standard should adopt this direction, who would be affected, and why a library-based solution outside the standard would not suffice.
