Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper gives a reasonably grounded account of the naming problem it is responding to, but it leaves several basic elements of a standardization case—particularly evidence that the library cannot address the need and that implementation experience exists—entirely unaddressed.

- The paper clearly establishes the relevant prior work and the state of naming discussions around P3091, including why the existing `get` name is under reconsideration.
- It offers a plausible conceptual reason that sequence-container lookup would benefit from parity with associative-container functionality.
- The thinnest established support concerns why this belongs in the standard, since the argument rests mainly on consistency concerns for a name not actually being proposed.
- The paper does not establish coordination with existing practice or demonstrate that a library-level solution would be insufficient.
