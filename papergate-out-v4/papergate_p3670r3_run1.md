Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers some tangible grounding in existing committee work through its discussion of prior proposals and CWG3027, but much of the case for action rests on assertion rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest support appears where the proposal should explain why standardization is the right mechanism, how it fits with adjacent work, and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support is the paper’s connection to prior committee work, showing that the proposed syntax parallels existing pack-indexing forms and that related proposals do not conflict with the design.
- The stated feedback and the author’s confidence about implementability are cited as evidence, but neither is substantiated with detail.
- The paper offers no argument for why the standard itself must change rather than addressing the limitation through a library.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of coordination with other active proposals or the broader interoperability consequences.
