Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a clear rationale for the core feature and shows credible prior art, but it leaves several important parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns why the standard must change and whether the feature has been meaningfully implemented or used.

- The proposal firmly establishes why postcondition captures matter by showing they enable otherwise inexpressible assertions, such as the size increase from `push_back`.
- It also establishes prior art and alternatives with references to related proposals and a discussion of rejected capture forms.
- It only claims, without sufficient evidence, that the affected audience and common-library use are as broad as suggested.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not establish why this requires standardization or provide implementation experience to support the design.
