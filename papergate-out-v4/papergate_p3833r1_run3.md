Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete support for its standardization case, primarily in establishing the gap it addresses, presenting prior art and alternatives, and providing implementation experience. The thinnest areas are the absence of evidence about who is affected, how the facility would coordinate or interoperate with existing practice, and why a library solution is insufficient beyond asserted limitations.

- The strongest support is the combination of a clear statement of the missing functionality and a complete available implementation.
- The paper also credibly locates the proposed facility against existing wrappers and alternative approaches.
- The case for standardizing rather than relying on a library is asserted but not meaningfully developed, since the cited drawbacks remain undemonstrated rather than shown to require standardization.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not identify any affected user community or establish coordination and interoperability considerations for the proposal.
