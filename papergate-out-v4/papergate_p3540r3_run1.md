Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper relies almost entirely on the assertion that it standardizes existing `gnu::offset` and `clang::offset` practice, but it does not develop that assertion into an actual case for why the feature needs to enter the C++ standard. The strongest support comes from implementation experience, while the remaining categories are only claimed, often by repeating the same sentence, and the library alternative is left unaddressed entirely.

- The clearest support is that the feature is described as already implemented in Clang and GCC and that standardizing existing practice is the stated motivation.
- The paper repeatedly claims popularity and prior implementation but does not provide evidence of usage, affected users, or why existing practice is insufficient outside the standard.
- The paper does not establish why a library solution would be inadequate, leaving that requirement completely unexamined.
