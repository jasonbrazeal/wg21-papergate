Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper offers only a thin, mostly aspirational case for standardization: it gestures toward a profile’s goals and some general risks, but leaves nearly all of the evidentiary and logistical groundwork unaddressed. The support is thinnest where the proposal needs to show concrete consequences, coordination, and practical feasibility.

- The strongest support is the paper’s beginning of a rationale, explaining why a profile applied everywhere would undermine its own replacement functionality.
- It also at least claims to consider prior art by raising the local-to-global guarantee question, though it does not develop an answer.
- The most glaring omissions are the absence of any affected audience, implementation experience, or interoperability analysis, and no explanation of why the standard rather than a library is the right vehicle.
