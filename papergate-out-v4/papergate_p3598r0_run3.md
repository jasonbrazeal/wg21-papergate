Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper leans heavily on an appeal to coherence and on a single implementation’s behavior, but it does not develop most of the arguments needed to justify standardization on its own. The support is thinnest where the proposal would need to show that the standard must change and that no library-level or other non-core remedy could address the problem.

- The strongest support is the claim that an existing GCC trunk implementation already exhibits the proposed behavior, which at least hints at practical implementation experience.
- The paper also gestures toward prior discussion in P3261R1, but it does not show how those earlier reasons transfer convincingly to the present case.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any reasoning about why a library solution cannot handle the need.
