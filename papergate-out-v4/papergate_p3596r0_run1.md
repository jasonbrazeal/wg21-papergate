Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper provides only a preliminary foundation for the standardization case: it demonstrates that draft wording can be maintained collaboratively, but it leaves the central justification largely implicit. The thinnest support concerns the basic connection to standardization itself—who is concretely affected, why a normative annex is the right vehicle, and why an external catalog or library cannot serve the same purpose.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, since the proposed annex wording already exists as a maintained branch of the C++ working draft.
- The paper gestures at prior attempts and an ecosystem role for stable names, but does not establish that those alternatives were inadequate or that this vocabulary will actually be adopted.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the absence of this annex in the standard, leaving the practical need ungrounded.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any case for why the normative standard, rather than an external reference or library, is required.
