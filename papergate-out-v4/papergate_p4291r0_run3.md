Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of itself, with credible grounding in implementation experience and awareness of comparable prior work, but it leaves central parts of the standardization case unstated or only asserted.

- The strongest support comes from the tested implementation, which demonstrates that the proposed adaptor exists and can be validated in practice.
- The discussion of prior art meaningfully connects the design to a known semantic hazard in filter-view extensions, showing engagement with existing range-adaptor problems.
- The claim that the adaptor fills a notable gap in the Ranges library is presented as a design-philosophy assertion rather than substantiated with concrete use cases or user impact.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, how the feature coordinates with existing components, or why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving the standardization rationale notably thin.
