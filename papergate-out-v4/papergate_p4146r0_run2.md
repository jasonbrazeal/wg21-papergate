Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers real but uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete implementation evidence and relevant prior art, but it leaves several foundational questions about the affected audience, the need for a standard solution, and coordination with existing practice largely unaddressed. The thinnest areas are those that would justify taking the work into the standard rather than leaving it to implementations or libraries.

- The paper’s strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience, including changes already made in libstdc++ and libc++ and work in stdexec.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is credibly grounded in specific proposals and bug fixes.
- The most glaring omission is any clear account of who is affected and why this must be addressed in the standard rather than through existing library mechanisms.
