Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers some grounding for its proposal by connecting it to prior discussion and rejected alternatives, but it leaves several essential justifications largely unaddressed, particularly around who benefits, why this belongs in the standard, and whether existing libraries can already supply the behavior.

- The strongest support is the account of prior debate over `affine_on` and the earlier `continues_on` approach, which shows the issue arose from real specification concerns.
- The paper also points to implementation presence, though it does so only in passing and without meaningful detail.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any explanation of who is affected by the problem or what concrete use cases motivate standardization.
- Equally unaddressed is why the standard, rather than a library, is the necessary venue for this change.
