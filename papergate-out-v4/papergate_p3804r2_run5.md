Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only narrow support for its own standardization, concentrated in its discussion of prior art, while most of the case rests on assertions that are not developed or tied to concrete evidence. The thinnest areas are the absence of any account of who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support is the paper’s engagement with prior art and alternatives, which is credited as established.
- The discussion of coordination and interoperability gestures at real concerns but does not establish them with enough specificity.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problem or why standardization, rather than a library, is necessary.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of established implementation experience or a demonstrated need for the standard itself.
