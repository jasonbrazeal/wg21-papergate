Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only modest support to its own standardization, resting most of its case on broad assertions about usefulness rather than concrete evidence of need. The thinnest areas are the absence of any affected audience, the lack of a justification for why the standard is the right vehicle, and the unexplained dismissal of non-standard alternatives.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, since the proposed annex wording has been developed as a branch of the C++ working draft.
- The paper claims the categorization helps readers and maintainers, but it does not establish why that benefit matters in practice.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives but does not show how this proposal improves on them or why they are insufficient.
- Most glaringly, the paper never identifies who is affected or why a library solution would not suffice, leaving the core standardization case largely assumed.
