Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers solid grounding for the motivation, the need for a standard rather than a vendor-specific solution, and the coordination goals, but its support becomes noticeably thinner when it moves from asserting broad developer need to demonstrating that need or the viability of the approach through implementation experience. The thinnest areas are the unsubstantiated claims about who is affected, why a library-only solution would fail, and what implementation experience actually shows.

- The paper most clearly establishes why the problem matters and why standardization, rather than a coupled or library-only approach, is the intended path.
- It also credibly establishes prior art and the need for cross-implementation coordination among accelerator vendors.
- The weakest support is the claim that C++ developers broadly want this behavior, which is asserted without evidence of affected users or concrete demand.
- The most glaring omission is implementation experience, where a Compiler Explorer link is offered but no results, difficulties, or lessons are described.
