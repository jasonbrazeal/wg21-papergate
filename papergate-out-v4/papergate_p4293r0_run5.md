Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, indirect case for its own standardization: it gestures at a need created by disagreement with another proposal and sketches how an algorithm might address that need, but it does not establish who would use it, why existing facilities cannot serve, or that there is any implementation experience to support it. The thinnest support lies in the absence of any discussion of affected users, the standard’s role, and coordination with related work.

- The clearest support is the identification of a possible need arising from disagreement over P4288’s central thesis.
- The paper claims some prior art and alternatives by referencing P4288 and classifying completion signatures, but it does not show how these relate to a new standardization effort.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about who is affected, why a library will not suffice, or why the standard is the right venue.
