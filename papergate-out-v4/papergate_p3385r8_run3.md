Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some useful grounding through its implementation experience and references to related design work, but it leaves the core rationale for standardization largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any identified affected audience and the lack of a case for why a library-level solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the existence of an experimental implementation available on Compiler Explorer, with specific discussion of equality behavior and profiling.
- The paper also draws on prior art and community feedback, including the connection to P4033R0 and the Wrocław discussion of salient properties.
- The weakest point is that the paper never establishes who is affected by the absence of the proposed facility.
- It likewise offers no argument for why the problem cannot be addressed through a library, leaving the need for a language-level standard unproven.
