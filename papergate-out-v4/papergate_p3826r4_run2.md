Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives solid evidence that the problem is real and that the proposed direction has been implemented in practice, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns why this needs to be in the standard at all and whether a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in two independent codebases without reported regressions.
- The paper also clearly establishes the motivating gap in sender customization and the prior efforts that lead into this fix.
- It claims broad ecosystem impact and interoperability benefits, but does not substantiate who is affected beyond the author’s projects.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library cannot address the problem outside the standard.
