Verdict: Weak (1/14)

The paper rests almost entirely on a citation to prior committee direction, offering little independent justification for why these concepts should become exposition-only in the standard. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, interoperability concerns, and any practical experience with the change.

- The only credited support is the statement that the paper responds to LEWG direction on LWG4361, which at best gestures at prior art and motivation.
- The paper does not establish who would be affected by hiding `receiver_of` and `sender_to` from public use.
- The paper offers no discussion of alternatives beyond the committee direction it cites.
- It is silent on why a library-level approach would be insufficient and provides no implementation experience to ground the wording.
