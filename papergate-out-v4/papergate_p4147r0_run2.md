Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a preliminary, conversational sketch of an idea, and its support for standardization is thin nearly everywhere. The most developed parts gesture at a motivation and a few design options, but even those remain assertions rather than a worked case, while the rest of the required support is absent.

- The clearest support is a stated motivation: there is currently no way to modify or detect a value crossing between constant evaluation and runtime evaluation.
- The paper at least names some design alternatives, such as a member function, a free function, or both, though it does not establish how they compare.
- The case for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library is never made.
- The most glaring omission is any account of who would be affected by the feature or how it would coordinate with existing language and library machinery.
