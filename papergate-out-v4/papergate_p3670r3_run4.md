Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for its own standardization: most of its burden-of-proof categories are either asserted without development or simply unaddressed. The strongest material is the claim that pack indexing is being completed and that related in-flight proposals do not conflict with the design, but even that is presented more as context than as a worked justification.

- The paper's clearest support is its identification of related proposals and a core issue, showing that the design was checked against some ongoing work.
- The rationale for why the feature matters rests on a single unsupported assertion that there is no good reason for the current limitation.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, how the feature would coordinate with existing practice, or what implementation experience exists.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of prior art, alternatives, or why a library-level solution cannot address the need.
