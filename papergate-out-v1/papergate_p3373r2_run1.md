Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper leans heavily on a single piece of evidence—the existing libunifex implementation—to justify standardization, which gives it concrete but narrow support. That support is thinnest when it comes to explaining why the standard itself must change, since the same observation is used to cover several distinct justification categories without additional argument.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in libunifex, which shows the proposed lifetime strategy is already used in practice.
- The paper also offers a specific, runnable example against a reference implementation, grounding the problem in observable behavior.
- The most glaring omission is any direct argument for why a library-level solution would be insufficient, since the paper only asserts this by implication rather than addressing it.
