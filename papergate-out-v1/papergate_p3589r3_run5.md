Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers a reasonably specific case for a common framework, but that case is repeated across several sections rather than developed into distinct arguments, and some key claims are left as bare assertions. The thinnest support concerns why a library solution would be insufficient and what implementation experience actually demonstrates.

- The strongest support is the concrete warning that without a common framework, supplier-specific options will fragment guarantees and portability.
- The paper also positions itself clearly against existing prior art, which helps situate the proposal.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported assertion that a library cannot address the problem, since the same interoperability concern is simply restated without elaboration.
