Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper makes a reasonably concrete case for standardization by pointing to widespread existing practice, real usage data, and relevant prior proposals, but its argument weakens considerably when it reaches the question of why only the standard library can solve the problem. The strongest evidence is external and empirical; the thinnest is the repeated, unsupported assertion that no user-code or third-party solution could suffice.

- The paper’s strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience and usage data, including multiple widely used libraries and GitHub counts showing substantial adoption of the relevant idiom.
- It also grounds the design in prior art by referencing specific proposals that could simplify the implementation if adopted.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any supporting argument for the claim that the solution must come from the standard library and cannot be provided by user code or third-party libraries.
