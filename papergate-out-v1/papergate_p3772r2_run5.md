Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a brief appeal to consistency with other proposals rather than building a case from motivation, impact, or practical experience. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how the feature would work in practice.

- The strongest support is the citation of prior art and the design approval of related proposals, which at least grounds the idea in an ongoing standardization context.
- The paper provides no implementation experience, leaving the feasibility and real-world behavior of the proposed overloads entirely unexamined.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any stated rationale beyond consistency, with no explanation of the problem being solved or the consequences for users.
