The paper provides a reasonable amount of concrete support for its standardization case, particularly through implementation experience and prior-art analysis, but it leaves several important justifications entirely unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient and why standardization specifically is the right venue.

- The strongest support comes from the working GCC implementation and complete wording, which demonstrate technical readiness.
- The prior-art analysis in P3600R0 is cited as comprehensive and directly relevant to the design space.
- The paper asserts that the feature is frequently requested and affects users, but offers no evidence or examples to substantiate that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about why this cannot be done as a library or why the standard itself must change.
