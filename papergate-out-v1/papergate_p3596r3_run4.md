Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some useful context for why a consolidated list of undefined behavior and IFNDR cases would be valuable, but it does not build a complete case for standardizing this material, leaving several key justifications unstated. The strongest support is the reference to prior efforts and existing proposals, while the thinnest areas concern implementation experience and the rationale for placing this content in the standard rather than elsewhere.

- The paper grounds its motivation in the practical risk that IFNDR and undefined behavior pose to programs, and it points to earlier attempts and formal calls for an annex.
- It offers a collaborative drafting branch as evidence of implementation experience, though this is asserted without detail about use or validation.
- The paper does not explain who would be affected by adding such an annex or why the standard is the right home for it.
- It omits any discussion of why a library or separate reference document would not suffice, leaving the standardization rationale incomplete.
