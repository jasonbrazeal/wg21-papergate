Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, resting almost entirely on a brief observation about implementation flexibility while leaving the core rationale unstated. The thinnest support is in the areas that would normally anchor a proposal: motivation, affected users, interoperability, and evidence of implementability.

- The strongest support is the specific note that implementations could enable the proposal separately or in combination with P2900.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or who would be affected by standardizing it.
- It offers no discussion of coordination with existing practice, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- Most glaringly, the paper never addresses why the standard should adopt the proposal at all.
