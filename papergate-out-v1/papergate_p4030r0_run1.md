Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a narrow but concrete rationale for its views by tying them to the existing UTF transcoding adaptors, yet it leaves several parts of the standardization case unstated, particularly around implementation experience and why a library solution would be insufficient. The strongest support is the explicit connection to P2728R7, while the thinnest areas are the unaddressed questions of affected users, library alternatives, and practical implementation.

- The paper’s clearest support comes from linking the proposed views directly to the UTF transcoding range adaptors in P2728R7.
- It also gestures toward broader applicability in network protocols and file formats, though this is asserted rather than developed.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence that the facility works as specified in practice.
- The paper does not address why a library solution would not suffice, leaving a central standardization question unanswered.
