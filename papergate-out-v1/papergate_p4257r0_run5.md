Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides some historical grounding for its motivation and points to a general direction in prior policy discussions, but it leaves most of the case for standardization unstated. The thinnest support concerns who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the approach has any practical implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the specific reference to C++11 LWG behavior and the later convergence of policy proposals around marking wide-contract non-throwing functions `noexcept`.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the proposed change or what costs or benefits they would experience.
- It does not explain why standardization is necessary rather than a library solution or existing practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence that the policy works in real codebases.
