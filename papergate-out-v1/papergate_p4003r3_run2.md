Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper makes a reasonably thorough case for its own standardization, grounding its claims in implementation experience, a companion rationale document, and concrete examples of why the facility cannot be provided as a library. The support is thinnest where it relies on the companion paper for the deeper design justification, leaving this document to assert rather than fully argue some of the central trade-offs.

- The strongest support comes from the cited complete implementation on three platforms, which gives the proposal practical weight.
- The discussion of why a library will not do is specific and tied to a real type-erasure limitation.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper defers much of the design rationale and analysis of alternatives to a separate document, so this proposal alone does not fully carry that burden.
