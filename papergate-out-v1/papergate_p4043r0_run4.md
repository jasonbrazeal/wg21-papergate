Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the Contracts facility is under active scrutiny and who would be affected by changes, but it does not build a complete case for standardizing the direction it describes. The strongest material concerns the existence of recent concerns and NB feedback, while the argument for standardization itself is largely assumed rather than demonstrated.

- The paper grounds its relevance in specific recent papers and more than twenty NB comments related to C++26 Contracts.
- It identifies affected audiences and prior design discussions with enough specificity to show the topic is live and contested.
- It does not address why the standard is the right venue rather than a library or separate specification.
- It offers no implementation experience or interoperability discussion, leaving the practical path to standardization unsupported.
