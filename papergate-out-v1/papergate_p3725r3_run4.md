Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the proposed workaround is useful, but it does not build a case for standardization itself. The strongest material concerns concrete problems and a prior discussion, while the rationale for acting through the standard, rather than through guidance or library code, is absent.

- The paper identifies specific broken or risky use cases and points to SG9 discussion notes, giving the problem some real-world grounding.
- It describes the workaround as simple to teach and follow, which offers a limited pedagogical justification.
- It does not address why the standard is the right venue, how the change would interoperate with existing specifications, or why a library solution would not suffice.
- It provides no implementation experience, leaving the practical viability of the proposed approach unsupported.
