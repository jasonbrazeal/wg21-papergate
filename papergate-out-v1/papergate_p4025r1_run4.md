Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, leaning on broad assertions about C++’s role in AI and the risk of fragmentation rather than concrete evidence or implementation experience. Its strongest material is the identification of prior art and the mention of SG19’s strategic priorities, but even those are presented without detail on how they justify a standard rather than a library. The thinnest support appears in the sections on why the standard is needed and why a library will not suffice, where the same general claims are repeated without elaboration.

- The paper points to an existing DataFrame project as prior art, which at least grounds the discussion in a real alternative.
- It cites SG19 discussions and strategic priorities, offering some indication of community direction, though without supporting specifics.
- The argument for standardization over a library is asserted rather than demonstrated, with no explanation of what a standard would enable that a library cannot.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the proposal, leaving the motivating audience and impact unclear.
