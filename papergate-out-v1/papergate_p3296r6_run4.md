Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow, example-driven justification for its proposal, leaving most of the case for standardization unstated. Its strongest support is a concrete lifetime-safety scenario, but the absence of affected users, alternatives, implementation experience, and standards-committee context leaves the argument largely undeveloped.

- The paper gives a specific, illustrative example of how an exception can lead to use-after-lifetime for a scope and its nested data.
- The same example is reused to argue that a library-only solution would not address the problem.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the issue or how widely it occurs in practice.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternative designs, implementation experience, or coordination with existing standard features.
