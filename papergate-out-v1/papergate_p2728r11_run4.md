Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence in a few areas but little or no justification in several sections that matter for a committee audience. The strongest material concerns implementation experience and precedent, while the case for need, affected users, and interoperability is largely asserted or absent.

- The paper gives specific implementation experience through a reference implementation derived from Jonathan Wakely’s libstdc++ work.
- It supports prior art and the “why the standard” question with concrete examples such as `views::reverse` and the removed `codecvt` facilities.
- It does not address why the feature matters, who is affected beyond a GitHub star count, or how it coordinates with existing library and standard components.
- It offers no discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving a central standardization question unanswered.
