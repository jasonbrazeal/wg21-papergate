The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence limited to implementation experience and terminology history while leaving the central rationale largely unargued. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected, why a library solution would not suffice, or how the feature would coordinate with existing practice.

- The strongest support comes from the generated code example, which at least demonstrates that the proposed facility can be implemented in practice.
- The naming rationale is grounded in prior committee feedback and internal SIMD library vocabulary, giving it some procedural and contextual footing.
- The paper asserts that the feature belongs in the standard because of naming alignment with existing functions, but offers no supporting argument beyond that assertion.
- Most glaringly, the paper never addresses why a library cannot provide this capability or who would be affected by standardizing it.
