Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single unsupported claim about algorithmic efficiency. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The only concrete support is the assertion that merge-based intersection on sorted adjacency lists is more efficient than alternatives for sparse graphs, though no evidence or citation is provided.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the proposed standardization or what problem it solves for them.
- There is no discussion of prior art, implementation experience, or coordination with existing practice.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
