Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers concrete evidence in a few areas, particularly implementation experience and the origin of the current syntax, but it leaves several important parts of the standardization case largely unargued. The thinnest support concerns why the standard is the right venue and why a library solution is inadequate, both of which are asserted without elaboration.

- The strongest support is the availability of a Clang fork implementation, which demonstrates the proposed syntax is technically feasible.
- The paper also grounds its discussion in prior art by tracing the existing unconditional syntax to N3701.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected by the problem or how widespread the need is.
