Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial, concrete support for its standardization case, particularly in identifying the precise scope of the required changes and the convergence of existing practice across major libraries. The support is thinnest where it relies on a single example of implementation experience, leaving the broader ecosystem impact less fully illustrated.

- The strongest support comes from the detailed accounting of affected concept expressions, sender algorithms, and third-party types, which grounds the proposal in a clear and unavoidable standardization need.
- The paper also benefits from citing C++20 symmetric transfer as direct prior art, showing the mechanism already exists in the language and only needs to be extended.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of broader implementation or usage evidence beyond one coroutine-native launcher, which leaves the practical viability across diverse executors and sender pipelines underexplored.
