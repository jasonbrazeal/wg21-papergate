Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper gives concrete evidence that the boilerplate is widespread and that workable implementations exist, but it does not build a clear case for why this needs to be in the standard rather than left to libraries or existing patterns. The strongest support is in the examples and implementation links, while the rationale for standardization itself is mostly asserted.

- The paper points to repeated boilerplate in LLVM and links to a working Clang reflection implementation, showing the problem and a viable technical direction.
- It cites prior art and alternatives with named sources, which helps situate the proposal within existing practice.
- The discussion of why a library solution is insufficient is thin, and the claim that the feature is “sought-after” is not backed by evidence.
- Coordination and interoperability with related standard facilities are not addressed, leaving the standardization case incomplete.
