Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its standardization case by identifying inconsistencies and gaps introduced by related adopted papers, and by pointing to prior art and a rebasing of wording. However, the support is uneven: it does not explain who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, how the feature interoperates, why a library solution is insufficient, or whether there is any implementation experience.

- The strongest support comes from the specific identification of inconsistencies and API gaps introduced by related papers adopted at the same time.
- The paper also situates itself in prior art by referencing P3795R0 and describing the rebasing of wording for `annotations_of` and `data_member_spec`.
- The thinnest area is the absence of any discussion of affected users, motivation beyond avoiding API proliferation, or standardization rationale.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of implementation experience, coordination, or interoperability discussion, leaving the practical path to standardization largely unaddressed.
