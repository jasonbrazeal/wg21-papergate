Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a skeletal case for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single asserted need for a native C++ Data Frame with zero-copy Python interoperability. The strongest support is the identification of a concrete prior art project, but the argument for why this must be standardized rather than remain a library is essentially absent.

- The paper points to an existing DataFrame implementation, giving at least one concrete reference point for prior art.
- The stated motivation around AI workflows and Python/Pandas dominance is specific, if not elaborated.
- The paper asserts that a standard is needed for zero-copy exchange with Python but provides no reasoning for why a library cannot satisfy that need.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by such a standardization or offer any implementation experience to ground the proposal.
