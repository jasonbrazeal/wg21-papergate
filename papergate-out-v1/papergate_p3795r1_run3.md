Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides some grounding for its standardization by pointing to concrete inconsistencies introduced when related proposals were adopted together, but it leaves most of the usual case for a standards change unstated. The thinnest support concerns who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether there is any implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the specific identification of gaps and inconsistent APIs arising from simultaneously adopted papers.
- The paper also situates itself against prior work by noting it rebases wording and fixes missing wording in `annotations_of` and `data_member_spec`.
- It does not address who is affected by the inconsistencies or what practical impact they have.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, coordination considerations, or argument for why the standard rather than a library should address the issue.
