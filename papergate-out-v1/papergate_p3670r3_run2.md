Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers a narrow but concrete rationale for completing pack indexing, grounded in specific prior proposals and a clear gap in P2662R3, but it leaves most of the standardization case unstated. The thinnest support concerns implementation experience, which is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the absence of any discussion of affected users, standard wording needs, or interoperability.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s identification of a specific limitation in P2662R3 and its connection to two other in-flight proposals.
- The proposal frames itself as completing an existing design, which gives it a clear, if brief, justification for standardization.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any implementation evidence beyond the author’s confidence that Clang support would be straightforward.
