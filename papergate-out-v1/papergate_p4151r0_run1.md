The paper offers only a narrow, name-focused rationale for its position, and it does not build a broader case for standardization. The support is thinnest around the practical questions that usually matter most: who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the change has been tried in practice.

- The strongest support is the specific observation that rendering `affine_on` unary would make the “on” in its name semantically puzzling.
- The paper also grounds its naming concern in prior art by listing the existing “on”-named entities in the `std::execution` namespace.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who would be affected by the change or what the real-world impact would be.
- It likewise offers no implementation experience, interoperability analysis, or argument for why a library-level solution would be insufficient.
