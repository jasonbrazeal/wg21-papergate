Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, name-focused argument for its standardization, and that argument is largely negative rather than constructive. The strongest support is a specific objection to the proposed `system_context_replaceability` name, but the paper does not build a positive case for why the feature itself belongs in the standard, nor does it address the practical questions that normally accompany such a proposal.

- The paper gives a concrete reason to avoid the `system_context_replaceability` namespace name by pointing out that `system_context` does not exist in the current working draft.
- It cites prior art in P3804R1 and notes that alternatives such as `replacement` and `replacement_functions` were already favored there.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the proposal or what coordination and interoperability concerns might arise.
- It offers no implementation experience, no discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient, and no supporting argument for why standardization is necessary at all.
