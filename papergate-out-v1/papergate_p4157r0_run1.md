Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, leaning on a single external fact about C23 while leaving most of the case for C++ adoption unstated. The strongest support is the existence of prior implementation experience, but the argument for why this belongs in the C++ standard is largely absent.

- The paper gives concrete implementation evidence by naming GCC and Clang and a maximum supported width.
- The paper identifies C23’s `_BitInt` as relevant prior art with specific document references.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the proposal.
- The paper never explains why a library solution would be insufficient or why standardization is necessary.
