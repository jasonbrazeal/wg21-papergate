Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with its strongest grounding in the discussion of prior art and the rationale for choosing a particular semantic model. Elsewhere, the case is much thinner: several sections that should connect the proposal to real-world needs, users, implementation practice, and interoperability are either silent or rely on bare assertion.

- The clearest support comes from the comparison with the PVI model and the explanation of why the proposed approach is preferred.
- The paper asserts that the changes mostly formalize existing implementations, but it offers no concrete implementation experience to back that claim.
- The discussion of coordination and interoperability gestures toward P2414R3 but does not explain the assumptions or constraints involved.
- The paper never addresses why the changes matter, who is affected, or why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving the motivating case largely unstated.
