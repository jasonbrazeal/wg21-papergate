Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of prior design work and implementation experience, but it leaves several core justifications for standardization largely unstated. The strongest support is concentrated in references to earlier analyses and a working implementation, while the argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library is essentially absent.

- The paper’s most persuasive support comes from its citation of a comprehensive prior design analysis and a mature implementation with complete wording in GCC.
- It also points to a demonstrated need and a straightforward library-only mechanism, though it does not develop those points into a full case for standardization.
- The discussion of who is affected and why the standard is the right venue is missing, leaving the intended audience and standardization rationale unclear.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any explanation for why a library solution will not suffice, especially since the proposal itself is described as library-only.
