Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its concerns, particularly through the poll results and the reference to prior renaming attempts, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The support is thinnest around why this belongs in the standard at all, since the central justification is asserted rather than demonstrated, and several expected sections are entirely absent.

- The strongest support comes from the documented poll and the failed prior attempt to rename the namespace, which show real committee engagement with the naming problem.
- The paper gives a specific reason for concern by pointing out that `system_context` does not exist in the current working draft.
- The paper never addresses coordination, interoperability, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- The core claim that the name will cause confusion is repeated as a conclusion without evidence or examples showing actual misunderstanding.
