Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization, with most of the burden of justification left unaddressed. The strongest material appears in the discussion of prior art, while the case for action by the standard committee is otherwise largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper gives a concrete, specific comparison to existing library practice, showing how a common operation can be expressed without a separate concept.
- The claimed relevance to real-world data such as the IMDB is asserted without any supporting explanation of why the proposed model cannot handle it.
- The paper does not address who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, or why a library would not suffice.
- The implementation experience is presented as an unintegrated note rather than evidence that the design has been tried and validated.
