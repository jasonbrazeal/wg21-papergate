Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper gives substantial, concrete evidence that the proposed design has real-world implementation experience and an active ecosystem, but it leaves the central case for standardization largely implicit. The strongest material concerns prior art, interoperability, and adoption, while the argument for why this must be a standard rather than a library is absent.

- The paper documents shipping libraries, independent adopters, and concrete integration efforts across Redis, MySQL, and Postgres.
- It grounds the design in established prior art and explicitly addresses interoperability between coroutine-native and sender-based code.
- It does not explain why standardization is necessary or what would be lost by remaining a library-based ecosystem.
