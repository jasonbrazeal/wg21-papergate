Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization, grounding its motivation in a specific prior proposal and its acceptance into the C++29 working draft, but leaving most of the surrounding rationale unstated. The thinnest areas are those that would normally anchor a proposal in real-world need and feasibility: who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, coordination concerns, and any implementation experience.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete reference to P3950 and its acceptance, which gives the proposal a clear standards-track context.
- The paper also explains why a library solution will not do, tying that argument directly to the prior proposal’s status in the working draft.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected or what practical problem the change addresses for users.
- Equally missing is any implementation experience or coordination discussion, leaving the proposal’s readiness for standardization largely unsubstantiated.
