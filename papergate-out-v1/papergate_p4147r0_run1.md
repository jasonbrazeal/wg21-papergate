Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin justification for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single asserted gap and an early-stage prototype, with little evidence that the problem is broadly felt or that the proposed direction is necessary. The strongest support is the concrete connection to P3771, but most of the standard rationale sections are either empty or merely restate the same claim without elaboration.

- The paper identifies a specific interaction with P3771 as a motivating use case, which gives the proposal at least one concrete anchor.
- The claim that there is currently no way to detect or modify values crossing constant-evaluation boundaries is asserted rather than demonstrated with examples or analysis.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how the proposal coordinates with existing features.
- Implementation experience is explicitly preliminary and offers no evidence of feasibility or design validation.
