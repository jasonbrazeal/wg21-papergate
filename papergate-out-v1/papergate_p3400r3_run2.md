Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper provides substantial, concrete support for its standardization in most areas, particularly in explaining why the feature is needed, why existing syntax is insufficient, and why a library-only approach would fall short. The support is thinnest when it comes to demonstrating who is actually affected and how widespread the need is, where the paper relies on assertion rather than evidence.

- The strongest support comes in the discussion of why a library will not do, which gives a specific, practical failure mode involving supplementary configuration files and per-build semantics.
- The paper also grounds its case well in prior art and alternatives, explaining concretely why comma and bitwise `and` are unsuitable rather than merely dismissing them.
- The most glaring omission is the claim that the functionality is essential to widespread Contracts adoption, which is stated without any supporting examples, user reports, or domain-specific evidence.
