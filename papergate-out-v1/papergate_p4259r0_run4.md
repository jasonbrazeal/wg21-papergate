Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, name-focused justification for its standardization request, leaning almost entirely on precedent from other languages and the claim that users will search for these names. That support is thinnest where the paper should explain why the standard library is the right venue and why an ordinary library cannot provide the same facility.

- The strongest support is the concrete list of programming languages that already use ceiling and floor division naming, which gives the naming argument some external weight.
- The paper also connects the proposed names to discoverability, arguing that users will look for the operations under those established terms.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, leaving the standardization rationale essentially unstated.
