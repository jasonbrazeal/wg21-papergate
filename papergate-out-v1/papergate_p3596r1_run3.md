Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only partial support for its own standardization, leaning on prior work and a collaborative drafting branch while leaving several core justifications unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any rationale for why this belongs in the standard itself, why a library or separate reference cannot serve the need, and who would actually be affected by the change.

- The strongest support comes from the cited prior attempts and formal proposals, which show that cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior has been a recognized goal over time.
- The existence of a collaboratively developed wording branch offers some evidence of implementation experience, though the paper does not describe what that experience revealed.
- The paper asserts that stable names would be useful as a common vocabulary but offers no supporting argument or example of how they would be used.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about why the standard is the right home for this material rather than a separate document or library.
