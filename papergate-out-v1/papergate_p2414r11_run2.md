Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete historical and cross-committee references but little direct evidence for the prevalence, severity, or practical necessity of the problem it describes. The thinnest support appears wherever the paper asserts importance, affected scope, or the inadequacy of alternatives without accompanying data or examples.

- The strongest support is the specific citation of Treiber’s 1986 implementation and WG14’s N2676, which grounds the discussion in real prior work.
- The paper gives no supporting evidence for its claim that long-standing and heavily used concurrent algorithms depend on the pointer behaviors it wants to standardize.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any demonstrated need for standardization beyond the paper’s own assertions that current standards are problematic and that a library solution will not suffice.
