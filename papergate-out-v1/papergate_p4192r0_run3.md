Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its standardization case, chiefly by pointing to existing open Core issues and observed GCC behavior, but it leaves large parts of the rationale unstated. The thinnest support concerns who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the reference to multiple open Core issues against the relevant alignment clauses, which anchors the proposal in known specification problems.
- The paper also cites specific GCC behavior with a Compiler Explorer link, giving at least one implementation data point.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users or real-world impact.
