Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a general assertion that cataloguing IFNDR and undefined behavior is useful and on the existence of a draft branch. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, interoperability, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the citation of prior related efforts in P1705R1, P2234R0, and P3075R0, which at least establishes that this is a recurring topic of interest.
- The existence of collaboratively developed wording in a draft branch is mentioned, but no actual implementation or usage experience is described.
- The paper never explains why this information belongs in the C++ Standard itself rather than in a separate reference or tool.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who would use the annex, how it would be maintained, or what practical problem it solves for the committee or the community.
