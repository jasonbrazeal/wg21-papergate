Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives concrete evidence for the existence of the gap and for implementation feasibility, but it does not build a case that this gap belongs in the standard rather than in a library or that the proposed direction fits existing standardization priorities. The support is thinnest around motivation beyond assertion, affected users, and coordination with related work.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2996 metafunctions and the Bloomberg Clang implementation, which grounds the proposal in existing practice.
- The paper identifies a real standard/library inconsistency by noting that structural-type mandates exist without a user-facing query.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected or why the standard, rather than a library facility, is the right home for the feature.
