The paper gives a mixed account of its own readiness, offering concrete motivation and some comparison to prior practice, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who would be affected, how the feature fits with the broader standard, and whether the claimed implementation experience is sufficient to reassure reviewers.

- The strongest support is the concrete explanation of why a library-only approach would not meet the performance and initialization needs the paper describes.
- The discussion of prior art in languages such as Ada, C#, and Java gives useful context for the design space.
- The paper does not address coordination or interoperability with existing or planned standard features.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected, despite the claim that the initialization profile would be very widely used.
