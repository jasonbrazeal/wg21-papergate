Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives concrete support for implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves the central standardization rationale largely unargued, so the case for taking this work into the standard is thin where it matters most. The strongest material concerns what has been built and tried, while the weakest concerns why the feature belongs in the standard rather than in a library or vendor configuration mechanism.

- The paper is most convincing when it points to prototype implementations in GCC and Clang that are available on compiler explorer.
- It offers some specific motivation for build-time controls by describing the consumer’s needs as distinct from the author’s.
- It asserts vendor interest in a JSON configuration structure without providing the discussion or reasoning behind that conclusion.
- It does not address why a library solution would be insufficient, nor how the proposal would coordinate with existing or future standardization efforts.
