Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why a guaranteed assertion facility would be valuable, but it does not build a complete case for standardization because several core justifications are asserted rather than demonstrated. The strongest material concerns design rationale and prior art, while the thinnest areas are implementation experience, library alternatives, and the affected audience.

- The paper most concretely supports its design choices by citing prior proposals and explaining how its keyword approach differs from existing contracts work.
- It offers a specific, if brief, technical justification for the proposed mangling as a basis for compiler optimizations.
- It asserts that the main motivation is a guaranteed assertion facility but does not explain why this requires a standard language feature.
- It does not address who would be affected, why a library solution would be insufficient, or provide implementation experience beyond stating that the full proposal has not been implemented.
