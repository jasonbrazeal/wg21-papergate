Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on the assertion that the topic is important and on a collaborative drafting process rather than on evidence of need, impact, or feasibility. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the citation of prior related efforts, which at least situates the paper within an ongoing conversation.
- The implementation experience section asserts collaborative development but provides no details about usage, feedback, or lessons learned.
- The paper never addresses who would benefit from the proposed annex or how it would change their work.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a separate reference document or library.
