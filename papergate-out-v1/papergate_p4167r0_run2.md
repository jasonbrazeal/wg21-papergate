Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow, exploratory basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a comparison with one prior proposal and a passing anecdote about a library presentation. The support is thinnest where a proposal would normally carry the most weight: motivation, affected users, implementation experience, and the case for why the standard—rather than a library—must change.

- The strongest support is the specific contrast with P2822R2, which at least situates the design choice in existing standardization discussion.
- The only other concrete grounding is a reported remark about MP-Units and ADL, but it is asserted without demonstration or analysis.
- The paper does not establish who is affected, why the problem matters, or what practical experience exists with the proposed approach.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why a library solution is insufficient or why standardization is the necessary next step.
