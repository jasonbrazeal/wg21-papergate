Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, fragmentary justification for standardization, anchored in a single allocator-related concern and leaving nearly every other argumentative obligation unaddressed. The support is thinnest where the proposal should establish who is affected, what alternatives exist, and why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper does cite specific NB comments about `task`’s use of allocators, giving at least one concrete reason the topic matters.
- It gestures toward an intended design direction by stating which allocator should be forwarded to child senders.
- It does not address prior art, alternatives, affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- It offers no discussion of coordination or interoperability beyond the single allocator-forwarding remark.
