Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a narrow, mostly rhetorical justification for its proposed rename, leaning on a single naming-consistency argument without developing the broader case for standardization. The strongest material is the specific observation that “to_input” misleadingly suggests active processing, but the paper does not explain who is affected, why a library solution would be insufficient, or whether the change has been tried in practice. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination considerations, and any substantive argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in guidance or a library.

- The clearest support is the concrete claim that “to_input” sounds like an active transformation, unlike the intended passive adaptation.
- The paper also points to existing “as_...” view names as a relevant naming precedent.
- It does not address who would benefit from the change or how widespread the confusion is.
- Most glaringly, it offers no implementation experience, no interoperability discussion, and no argument for why a library-level solution would not suffice.
