Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, leaning on broad claims about C++’s role in AI and the appeal of a native DataFrame without connecting those claims to concrete standardization needs. The strongest support is the citation of prior art, but the paper does not explain why that existing work is insufficient or why a standard library component is required.

- The paper identifies a relevant existing DataFrame implementation, which at least grounds the idea in prior art.
- It asserts that C++ needs native data-frame functionality to avoid fragmentation, but offers no evidence or examples of that fragmentation.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by standardization or how the feature would coordinate with existing libraries and workflows.
- It provides no implementation experience, leaving the feasibility and design constraints of a standardized DataFrame entirely unexamined.
