Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the current draft is contentious and gestures at rejected alternatives, but it does not build a positive case that the proposed facility belongs in the standard. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete mention of OMDB and OMNB objections, which at least grounds the work in a real committee dispute.
- The discussion of prior alternatives is useful but mostly explains what was rejected rather than why this approach is the right one.
- The claim that the semantics are not ephemeral or language magic is asserted without elaboration, leaving the standardization rationale unsupported.
- The paper never addresses implementation experience, affected users, or why a library cannot meet the need, which are major gaps in the case for standardization.
