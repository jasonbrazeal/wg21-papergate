Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a narrow but concrete evidentiary base for standardization, resting almost entirely on comparisons with the PVI model and on the claim that existing implementations already align with the proposed behavior. The support is thinnest where the paper should explain why standardization is necessary at all, who would be affected, and why a library solution cannot suffice.

- The strongest support comes from the specific rejection of the PVI alternative and the explanation of how the proposed model avoids its restrictions on integer conversions.
- The paper also grounds its approach in implementation experience, asserting that the changes formalize rather than alter existing practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why the standard needs this change or what problem it solves for users.
- The paper likewise never addresses who is affected or why a library-level solution would be inadequate.
