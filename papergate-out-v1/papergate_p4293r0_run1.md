Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers a narrow but coherent rationale for its existence by positioning itself as the missing companion to P4288, though it leaves most of the standardization case implicit rather than argued. The strongest support is the direct connection to prior art, while the thinnest areas concern who would use the facility and why it belongs in the standard rather than in a library.

- The paper grounds its motivation in P4288’s specific claim that decay-copying can be isolated into a distinct algorithm, giving it a clear point of departure.
- It does not identify any affected users or use cases that would demonstrate demand for standardizing this algorithm.
- The absence of any discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient leaves the standardization rationale largely unstated.
- There is no implementation experience or interoperability analysis to show how the proposed facility would fit into existing practice.
