Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper gives substantial, concrete support for standardizing its proposed vocabulary types, drawing on production incidents, comparisons with prior art, and a detailed account of implementation experience. The support is thinnest where it leans on rhetorical examples rather than measurable evidence of interoperability or adoption across independent implementations.

- The strongest support comes from specific, real-world failure narratives and production feedback that tie the feature directly to preventing critical bugs in domains like robotics and flight software.
- The paper also grounds its case in a structured audience analysis and a clear comparison with existing libraries, showing where current tools fall short of the safety levels it proposes.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of evidence that the proposed design has been exercised in independent, non-author implementations or standardized interfaces beyond the author’s own library and cited anecdotes.
