Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a narrow, uneven account of why its idea belongs in the standard, with one concrete motivation and some interoperability reasoning but little else to anchor the case. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, prior approaches, implementation experience, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the specific explanation of how the proposal would help build tools track modular dependencies through accumulated file and folder matchers.
- The paper also offers a concrete framing of the problem as unanswered external dependency information in the Modules ecosystem.
- The claim that `#include` is the only current way to declare a dependency is asserted without evidence or acknowledgment of existing tooling conventions.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of implementation experience, prior art, or alternatives, leaving the standardization case largely unexamined.
