Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, providing only a bare assertion of implementation experience while leaving every other justification for a standard library feature unaddressed. The thinnest part is the complete absence of motivation, audience, alternatives, or reasoning about why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.

- The only concrete support offered is the claim that the feature has been implemented and tested in a libstdc++ patch currently under review.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or who would be affected by its adoption.
- It gives no account of prior art, alternatives, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- It says nothing about coordination, interoperability, or the rationale for standardization itself.
