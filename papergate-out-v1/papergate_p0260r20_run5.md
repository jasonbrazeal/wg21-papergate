Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper offers a mixed case for its own standardization: it grounds some claims in concrete references and implementation experience, but several important arguments are asserted without supporting detail. The thinnest support appears around who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the proposed concepts would coordinate with existing practice.

- The strongest support comes from the cited revision history and the availability of a partial implementation, which at least show ongoing work and some practical grounding.
- The discussion of prior art is specific, pointing to P0260R4 and its relationship to P1958.
- The claim that the concepts capture common semantics across implementations is repeated but not substantiated with examples or evidence of that commonality.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of support for why a library-only solution would not suffice, since the paper asserts this without elaboration.
