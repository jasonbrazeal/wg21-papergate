Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on references to two other papers for background while leaving the core rationale, affected audience, and need for a standard mechanism unstated. The thinnest parts are the complete absence of discussion about why the standard is the right venue, why a library solution would not suffice, and whether any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is the citation of P3818 and P3820 as prior art explaining the background problem.
- The paper asserts that making the functions constexpr would be a breaking change, but provides no supporting evidence or examples.
- It does not identify who is affected by the proposed change or why the standard should address it.
- It offers no discussion of implementation experience, coordination, interoperability, or why a library-only approach would be inadequate.
