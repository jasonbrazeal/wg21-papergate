Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its proposal, particularly through references to related standardization directions and a mention of implementation experience, but much of the central rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the paper claims unique value for the standard, interoperability benefits, and why a library solution would be insufficient, since these points are repeated without elaboration or evidence.

- The strongest support comes from the alignment with P3349 and the author’s implementation work based on libstdc++.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is specific enough to situate the proposal within existing standardization trends.
- The claim that a library solution will not suffice is supported only by a generic statement about eliminating reinvented access logic.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected by the proposal or what concrete user or implementer need it addresses.
