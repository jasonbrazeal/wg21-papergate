Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives concrete support for implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves several core justifications for standardization essentially unargued. The thinnest areas are the failure to explain why the standard, rather than a library, is the right home for these functions, and the absence of any discussion of coordination or interoperability.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, which names Intel’s reference implementation and real software products.
- The prior art section is also grounded, citing P0543R3 and its earlier attempt to add saturating operations.
- The paper asserts who is affected by saturating operations but offers no evidence or examples to substantiate that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about why a library solution would not suffice or why standardization is necessary.
