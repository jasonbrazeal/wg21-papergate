Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, resting on bare assertions about common traversal patterns and a reference implementation without evidence of use, motivation, or comparison to existing practice. The thinnest support is in the complete absence of discussion about prior art, affected users, why a library would be insufficient, or why standardization is the right venue.

- The strongest support is the existence of a reference implementation, though even that is only asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The paper does not address prior art or alternatives, leaving no basis for judging whether the proposed views are already covered elsewhere.
- It never explains why a library solution would not suffice or why the standard should adopt the facility.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why the proposal matters, beyond an unsupported claim that these are common traversal methods.
