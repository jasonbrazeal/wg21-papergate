Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single assertion about constness and a reference to a Core Guideline that actually cautions against the very kind of member the proposal appears to involve. Most of the sections that would establish need, audience, feasibility, and design fit are simply absent, leaving the proposal without the evidentiary foundation a committee would expect.

- The strongest support is the citation of C++ Core Guidelines C.12, which at least grounds the discussion in existing community guidance.
- The paper asserts that the feature would reduce code and improve const enforcement, but provides no examples or reasoning to substantiate that claim.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, or whether there is any implementation experience.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any argument for why the standard, rather than existing language or library mechanisms, is the right place for this functionality.
