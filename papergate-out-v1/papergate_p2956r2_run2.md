The paper gives some concrete grounding for its proposal through implementation experience and references to prior work, but it leaves several core standardization questions unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and how it would coordinate with existing or future facilities.

- The strongest support is the reported implementation experience in Intel’s reference implementation and software products.
- The paper also cites prior art in P0543R3 and explains the saturating behavior with a clear, specific definition.
- It asserts the affected operations are common but offers no evidence or examples to support that claim.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why a library solution is insufficient or why standardization is necessary.
