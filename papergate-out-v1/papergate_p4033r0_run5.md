Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a narrow, example-driven justification for its proposal, leaving most of the standardization case unstated. The strongest support appears in the concrete failure mode for index-based switching, but the argument thins considerably when it comes to why a library solution is insufficient or why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper gives a specific, plausible example of silent breakage when variant alternatives are reordered or extended.
- It gestures at limitations in current reflection facilities by citing another proposal, though without developing that connection.
- The paper asserts rather than demonstrates that a library cannot solve the problem, offering no supporting reasoning or implementation evidence.
- It does not address who is affected, coordination with existing features, or any implementation experience, leaving the standardization rationale largely unbuilt.
