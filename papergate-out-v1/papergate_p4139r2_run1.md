The paper gives a partial account of why its proposed operation should be standardized, but it leans heavily on naming consistency and leaves several practical questions about need and viability unexamined. The strongest material concerns prior art and the rationale for avoiding the name `get`, while the case for standardization itself is largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The discussion of prior naming alternatives and their reception in committee provides concrete context for the current proposal.
- The argument that a potentially unbounded lookup should not be called `get` is specific and grounded in existing library conventions.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the change or how it would interoperate with existing code.
- It offers no implementation experience, no explanation of why a library solution is insufficient, and no direct support for why standardization is necessary.
