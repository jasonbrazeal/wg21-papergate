Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, relying almost entirely on a single assertion that the algorithm exists in Nvidia’s stdexec. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of motivation, need for standardization, and any discussion of why a library solution would not suffice.

- The only concrete support is the citation of Nvidia’s stdexec as prior art, which at least grounds the proposal in existing practice.
- The paper asserts implementation experience but provides no details about usage, maturity, or lessons learned from that implementation.
- The paper does not address why the algorithm matters, who is affected, or why it belongs in the standard rather than a library.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any rationale for standardization, including coordination, interoperability, or the limits of a library-only approach.
