Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow, name-focused rationale for its position, leaving the actual case for standardization largely unbuilt. Its support is thinnest where a proposal would normally need to show who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the change fits into the broader standard.

- The strongest support is a specific, concrete argument that the current name becomes misleading once the operation is unary.
- The paper grounds its naming concern in the prior state of the `std::execution` namespace before `std::execution::task` was added.
- It does not address who would be affected by the proposed change or what practical problem it solves for users.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why this belongs in the standard rather than being handled through library design or naming conventions.
