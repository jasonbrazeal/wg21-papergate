Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization, drawing on production use, concrete structural gaps, and prior art to anchor its case. The support is thinnest where it leans on the same production citation for multiple distinct categories, leaving the reader wanting more independent evidence for implementation experience and coordination.

- The strongest support comes from the documented production use at Citadel Securities, which grounds the proposal in real-world demand and interoperability.
- The identification of four specific structural gaps, including the symmetric transfer gap and frame allocator propagation, gives the paper a clear and technically specific rationale.
- The argument that a library solution cannot reach coroutine frames through P2300’s environment propagation is well supported and directly addresses the “why not a library” question.
- The most glaring omission is the repeated reliance on a single production citation across several evidentiary categories, which weakens the breadth of the standardization case.
