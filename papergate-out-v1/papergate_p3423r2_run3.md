Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper gives concrete support for implementation experience and prior art, but it does not build a clear case for why standardization is necessary or why a library solution would be insufficient. The thinnest part is the justification for changing the standard itself, which is asserted rather than argued.

- The strongest support is the experimental Clang implementation, which demonstrates the feature in practice and shows example diagnostics.
- The discussion of N4433 provides useful historical context and shows that the idea has been considered before.
- The paper does not address coordination or interoperability concerns with existing implementations or tooling.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library-based approach cannot achieve the same goal.
