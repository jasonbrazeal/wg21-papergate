Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers almost no substantive support for its own standardization, relying instead on brief assertions about common traversal needs and a reference implementation. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about prior art, affected users, library alternatives, and why standardization is necessary at all.

- The strongest support is the mention of a reference implementation with stated backward compatibility to C++20.
- The paper asserts that the described views provide common ways algorithms traverse graphs, but offers no evidence or examples to substantiate that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of prior art or alternatives, leaving the proposal without context in the existing ecosystem.
