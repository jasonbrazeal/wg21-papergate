Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a partial account of why a uniform attribute-appertainment mechanism belongs in the standard, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case unstated, especially around who is affected and why a library solution is insufficient. The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience and a specific prior-art recommendation, while the motivational and interoperability claims are largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper points to working implementation experience on Compiler Explorer as concrete evidence for the proposed features.
- It identifies a specific prior paper, P3678R0, and an SG7 recommendation to merge it into the current proposal.
- It asserts that a uniform vehicle would save committee time and avoid repeated design debates, but does not substantiate that coordination benefit.
- It does not address why the problem matters to users or who is affected, nor does it explain why a library-only approach would be inadequate.
