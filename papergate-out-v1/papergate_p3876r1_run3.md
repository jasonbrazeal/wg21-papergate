Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper provides a reasonable but uneven case for its own standardization, with the strongest support concentrated in the discussion of prior art and the limitations of a library-only approach. The thinnest areas are coordination and implementation experience, where claims are asserted rather than demonstrated with evidence.

- The paper grounds its motivation and library-only limitations in concrete examples, such as JSON’s need for Unicode encodings and the absence of standard transcoding facilities.
- The discussion of prior art is specific and useful, citing related proposals and showing awareness of the surrounding standardization landscape.
- The paper asserts that existing implementations are numerically equivalent to the proposal but offers no implementation experience or evidence to substantiate that claim.
- Coordination and interoperability are only asserted through a single repeated example, without explaining how the proposal fits with related facilities or existing practice.
