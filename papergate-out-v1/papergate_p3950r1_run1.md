Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a reasonably grounded case for its own standardization, with concrete references to the working draft’s wording and a clear explanation of why the restriction cannot be worked around in library code. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the breadth of implementation experience, which are mentioned only briefly and without detail.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s specific analysis of the current standard wording and why only a compiler can implement the proposed change.
- The discussion of prior art and the failure of library-level alternatives adds meaningful context for why standardization is the appropriate path.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of detail about who is affected beyond the author’s own implementation, leaving the practical impact underexplored.
