Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its proposal, chiefly through an implementation link and a reference to the historical syntax origin, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale unstated. The thinnest support concerns why this cannot be handled by a library and who would actually be affected by the change.

- The strongest support is the availability of a Clang fork implementation on Compiler Explorer, which demonstrates at least preliminary implementability.
- The paper also cites N3701 as the origin of the current unconditional syntax, giving a specific point of prior art.
- It asserts that current workarounds require code duplication but offers no example or elaboration to substantiate that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users, coordination with other features, or why a library solution is insufficient.
