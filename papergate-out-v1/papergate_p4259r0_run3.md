Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides only partial support for its own standardization, concentrating on naming precedent and cross-language usage while leaving the core rationale largely implicit. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of why a library solution would be insufficient, why standardization is necessary, or what implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is the concrete list of other programming languages and their established names for these operations, which grounds the naming argument in prior art.
- The paper also offers some specificity about who is affected, namely users who would search for these operations under the expected names.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any treatment of why a library implementation would not suffice, leaving the case for standardization itself unargued.
