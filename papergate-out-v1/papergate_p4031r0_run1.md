Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, uneven case for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a naming objection while leaving the broader rationale for the feature largely unargued. The thinnest support concerns the absence of any discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete observation that the proposed namespace name would create confusion because `system_context` does not exist in the working draft.
- The paper cites prior art in P3804R1 and a poll showing some preference for the `replacement` namespace, giving the naming discussion a modest evidentiary basis.
- The argument for standardization itself is asserted rather than developed, with no supporting reasoning beyond the naming concern.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of attention to who is affected, implementation experience, or why a library cannot address the need.
