Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, grounding its motivation in the lack of agreed documentation for profiles and citing several unsuccessful prior efforts, but it leaves the central claim that profiles are the best available approach essentially unsupported. The thinnest areas are those that would normally anchor a standardization case: who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, interoperability, feasibility outside the standard, and implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the specific observation that WG21 lacks agreed documentation on what profiles can and cannot do, which gives the paper a concrete problem to address.
- The paper also situates itself against a series of prior profile-related papers, showing awareness of recent unsuccessful attempts.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why profiles are the best approach, beyond a bare assertion.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, affected users, coordination, or why a library solution would be insufficient, leaving the standardization case largely unbuilt.
