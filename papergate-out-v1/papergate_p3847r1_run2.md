Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides solid empirical and ABI-based support for its proposed ordering, but it leaves the normative rationale largely implicit. The strongest evidence is that implementations already agree in practice, while the thinnest area is the absence of a direct argument for why the standard itself must change.

- The paper backs its central claim with implementation testing across all major compilers and a link to Itanium ABI intent.
- It identifies a concrete safety problem and shows that a library-level workaround exists but is unergonomic.
- It does not explain why the current standard wording is insufficient or what specific normative gap the proposal would close.
