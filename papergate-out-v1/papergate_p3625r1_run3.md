Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, relying almost entirely on unsupported assertions and external references rather than evidence or analysis. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about why the standard is the right venue, why a library solution would not suffice, and how the feature would coordinate with existing concepts.

- The strongest support is the acknowledgment of prior usage and discussion on cppreference and Stack Overflow, though even this is presented as a pointer rather than sustained evidence.
- The paper does at least consider alternative names, showing some attention to design surface, but this is a narrow point that does not address the core standardization questions.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, which is essential for a proposal of this kind.
