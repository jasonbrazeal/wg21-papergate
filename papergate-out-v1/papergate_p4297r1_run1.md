Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper gives substantial, concrete support for its standardization case, grounding its claims in specific clause counts, named prior proposals, and a decade of shipping vendor implementations. The support is thinnest where it must connect the proposed layering to actual committee progress, since the EWG review is described as just beginning and the coordination requirement between Labels and Profiles remains an open architectural choice rather than a settled path.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with named vendor checkers shipping since LLVM 3.8 and VS 2017 demonstrating that the core idea is deployable outside the standard.
- The paper also anchors its relevance well by tying the proposal to 77 runtime-checkable undefined-behavior cases and the six foundational clauses now under EWG review.
- The most glaring omission is any evidence that the required interoperation between Labels and the Profiles framework has been accepted by either proposal’s authors or by EWG, leaving the central layering claim unresolved.
