Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives only partial support for its own standardization, concentrating on naming precedent and cross-language usage while leaving the core rationale largely unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of prior art, why a library solution would be insufficient, or whether the feature has been implemented and tested.

- The strongest support is the concrete list of other languages that already use the proposed ceiling and floor division names.
- The paper also explains that users will search for these operations under those established names.
- It does not address prior art or alternatives, leaving the proposal’s novelty and fit with existing practice unclear.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, nor offers any implementation experience.
