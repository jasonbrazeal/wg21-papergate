Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, leaning heavily on assertion and deferral to a companion document rather than making its own case. The strongest support appears in the discussion of why a library-only or iterator-based approach cannot provide the desired interface, but the paper does not substantiate the claimed need, affected users, or implementation maturity.

- The clearest support is the specific explanation that an iterator-based approach cannot offer the proposed property because an iterator must point to an actual object.
- The paper points to a companion proposal for prior art, alternatives, and concrete container examples, though it does not reproduce that support itself.
- The rationale for standardization and the affected audience are asserted without supporting detail.
- The implementation experience section admits that several proposed functions are not yet implemented, leaving the practical basis for the design largely unverified.
