Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its motivation and points to prior art, but it largely asserts rather than demonstrates why standardization is necessary, leaving the core case for a standard Data Frame underdeveloped. The thinnest support appears where the paper should connect its stated goals to evidence of implementation experience, interoperability needs, or why existing libraries cannot suffice.

- The strongest support comes from the specific reference to an existing DataFrame library and the identification of strategic priorities through SG19 discussions.
- The paper asserts that C++ is the engine of AI and that fragmentation must be prevented, but provides no evidence or examples to support that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or concrete demonstration that a library solution would fail where a standard would succeed.
