Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its own standardization, drawing on implementation experience, independent prior art, and direct community feedback to justify the need for a three-way type distinction. The support is thinnest where it relies on future reconciliation between the authors and on a reference implementation that has not yet been completed, leaving the concrete interface details less settled than the conceptual case.

- The strongest support comes from independent confirmation in Oliver Rosten’s Sequoia library and the Au library’s deployed experience, which together demonstrate that the proposed model is both implementable and already tested in practice.
- The paper also grounds its motivation in specific, documented user requests and in feedback from multiple standards bodies, showing that the gap it addresses is not hypothetical.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of a completed, publicly available implementation aligned with the paper’s interface choices, since the authors state that implementation will follow only after favorable SG6 direction.
