Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin, largely asserted case for standardization, resting on a single observation about type repetition in default arguments without developing the surrounding rationale. The support is thinnest where a proposal normally needs to show awareness of its audience, alternatives, and practical path: none of those areas is addressed.

- The strongest support is the concrete observation that default arguments can force repetition of a parameter type, especially with long, explicit, or templated types.
- The paper does not identify who is affected or how common the problem is in real code.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternative syntaxes, or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why the standard should change, beyond asserting that the repetition is noticeable.
