Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its technical motivation and acknowledges a closely related prior proposal, but it leaves large parts of the standardization case unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how the feature would coordinate with existing library and language facilities.

- The strongest support comes from the worked example showing how the proposed behavior would be used in practice.
- The paper also identifies P2746 as prior art and explains the intended difference in conformance constraints.
- Implementation experience is only aspirational, with no completed implementation yet available.
- The paper does not address why a library solution is insufficient beyond a brief remark that `from_chars` would be inconvenient.
