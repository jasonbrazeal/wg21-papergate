The paper offers a mixed case for its own standardization: it grounds several key claims in concrete examples and prior work, but leaves important parts of the argument—especially who is affected and why the standard is the right venue—as bare assertions. The thinnest support appears where the proposal needs to justify standardization rather than merely describe a technical gap.

- The implementation experience claim is supported by reference to a shipping library, though it is not elaborated with usage or adoption details.
