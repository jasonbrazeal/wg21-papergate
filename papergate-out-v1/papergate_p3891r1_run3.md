Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization, grounding its motivation in concrete examples of grammatical bloat and noting one alternative syntax, but it leaves most of the expected justification unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, why a library solution would be insufficient, implementation experience, or how the feature would coordinate with existing standard wording.

- The strongest support is the specific, example-driven motivation showing how missing grouping and repetition lead to repetitive X-seq and X-list rules.
- The paper also briefly documents a considered alternative syntax, which gives some evidence of design deliberation.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any implementation experience or prototype evidence to show the feature works in practice.
- Equally absent is any explanation of why this cannot be done as a library or how it fits with existing standard facilities.
