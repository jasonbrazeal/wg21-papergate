Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a narrow justification for its change, resting almost entirely on consistency with a previously adopted proposal and a passing claim of implementation experience. The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2248R8 and the Tokyo 2024 adoption, but the case thins considerably around motivation, affected users, and why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper clearly ties its proposed change to an oversight in the adoption of P2248R8, giving a specific procedural basis for the fix.
- It asserts that implementations already shipping P2248R8 make the change safer, though it offers no evidence of that experience.
- The paper does not explain who is affected by the omission or what practical problem it causes for users.
- It omits any discussion of why a library-level solution would be insufficient or why standardization is necessary beyond consistency.
