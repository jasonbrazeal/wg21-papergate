Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper gives concrete, useful evidence for several parts of its case, particularly around implementation divergence and the practical consequences for type traits, but it leans on assertion at the exact point where it needs to justify changing the standard.

- The strongest support comes from the table of major standard library implementations, which grounds the discussion in real, observable behavior.
- The paper also clearly identifies a concrete affected population and a specific non-compliant implementation, making the practical stakes easy to see.
- The thinnest part is the claim that the simplified explanation “would be beneficial,” which is stated as a conclusion without argument or evidence connecting that benefit to a need for standardization.
