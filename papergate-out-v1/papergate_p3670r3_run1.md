Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow justification for its proposal, resting almost entirely on the claim that pack indexing is incomplete without template packs. It does not address who would benefit, what alternatives exist, or how the feature would interact with ongoing related work, leaving the standardization case largely unbuilt. The thinnest support is in the absence of implementation experience and any discussion of standard-library or design constraints.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2662R3 and the stated goal of completing pack indexing.
- The paper acknowledges that two related proposals were in flight but does not explain how this proposal relates to them.
- It asserts implementability in Clang without offering any implementation, prototype, or technical analysis.
- It never identifies affected users, prior art, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
