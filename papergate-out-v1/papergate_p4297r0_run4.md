Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper grounds its architectural claims in specific cross-references to P3100R8, P3400R3, and the Profiles work, but it offers almost nothing to justify why any of this belongs in the standard rather than in a library or vendor tool. The support is thinnest where the paper asserts that standardization is necessary and that the foundational clauses are ready to carry the rest of the design.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete mapping between the proposal’s layering claim and published sections of P3100R8 and P3400R3.
- The paper also documents a decade of vendor shipping experience for named check-sets, which anchors the implementation discussion in real practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any audience analysis or statement of who is affected by standardizing this machinery.
- The claim that the standard is the right venue is asserted without supporting argument, leaving the core standardization rationale unexamined.
