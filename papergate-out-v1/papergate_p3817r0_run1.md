Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow, anecdotal basis for its standardization case, focusing on a single motivating example and leaving most of the evidentiary categories entirely unaddressed. The support is thinnest where the proposal needs to justify why the language, rather than a library or existing idiom, should change.

- The paper gives one concrete, real-world usage example from LLVM’s PassBuilder to show who is affected.
- It explains the gap between structured bindings and `std::tie` with a specific, recognizable code pattern.
- It does not discuss prior art, alternative approaches, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- It offers no implementation experience, no interoperability considerations, and no argument for why the standard is the right venue.
