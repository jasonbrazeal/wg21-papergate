Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, resting its case on a single unsupported assertion about profiles as an incremental path to safety. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, prior work, implementation experience, or why the feature belongs in the standard rather than a library.

- The only substantive claim is that profiles provide safety guarantees beyond base-language changes, but no evidence or reasoning backs that assertion.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the proposal or how it would coordinate with existing tooling and code.
- It offers no implementation experience, prior art, or alternatives to demonstrate feasibility or informed design.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why standardization is necessary or why a library-based approach would be insufficient.
