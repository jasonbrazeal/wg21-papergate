The paper gives concrete support for implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated, especially around who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the proposal fits with existing work. The strongest evidence is the reported use in Intel’s SIMD reference implementation and production DSP workloads, while the thinnest areas are the unaddressed questions of coordination, interoperability, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The paper most convincingly supports its case with specific implementation experience in Intel’s SIMD reference implementation deployed in production DSP workloads.
- It also grounds the naming discussion in prior art by citing P3287R2’s alternative unprefixed concept names.
- The claim about who is affected is asserted without supporting detail, making the breadth or severity of the problem hard to judge.
- The paper does not address coordination and interoperability or explain why a library-only solution would be inadequate, leaving a notable gap in the standardization rationale.
