Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow, example-driven justification for its proposal, leaving most of the case for standardization unstated. Its strongest support is a concrete scenario showing how a library-only solution can fail, but it does not explain who is affected, what alternatives exist, or why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper gives a specific code example illustrating a lifetime and exception-safety problem that motivates the need for a language or standard-library change.
- The argument that a library solution is insufficient is tied directly to that same example, showing a real failure mode.
- The paper does not address prior art, affected users, implementation experience, or interoperability, leaving the broader standardization case largely unsupported.
