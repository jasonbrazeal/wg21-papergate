Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization, grounding its motivation in specific objections and prior design discussions but leaving several central justifications as bare assertions. The thinnest areas are the claims about the scale of opposition, the adequacy of deferring configuration, and the absence of implementation experience or interoperability analysis.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete reference to OMDB/OMNB objections and the specific concerns raised about the D3894R0 alternative.
- The claim that opposition is unprecedented in severity is asserted without evidence, weakening the paper’s account of why action is needed now.
- The statement that configurability can be deferred and that affected audiences can proceed today is presented as fact without demonstrating how their needs are met.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, coordination with other features, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
