Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its problem statement and prior art, but it does not build a clear affirmative case for standardization. The strongest support is concentrated in the discussion of existing practice and interoperability, while the rationale for why this belongs in the standard—and why a library solution is insufficient—remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper gives specific examples of WTF-8 use in Rust and Node.js, showing real-world precedent and interoperability concerns.
- It identifies a concrete limitation in current behavior, namely the inability to reliably round-trip paths across platforms.
- The claim that a library cannot solve the problem is repeated but not substantiated with evidence or explanation.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any developed argument for why standardization is necessary, beyond a single unsupported assertion.
