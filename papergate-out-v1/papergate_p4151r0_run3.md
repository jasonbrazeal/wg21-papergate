Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed to support standardization, resting almost entirely on naming consistency while leaving the broader case for the proposed change largely unstated. The thinnest areas are those that would tell a reviewer whether the change is necessary in the standard, whether it fits with existing practice, and whether anyone has actually tried it.

- The strongest support is the specific observation that rendering `affine_on` unary undermines the rationale for the “on” suffix, grounded in the naming of prior `std::execution` entities.
- The paper identifies relevant prior art by listing the earlier “on”-named facilities, but it does not develop that history into a comparison or alternative analysis.
- The document does not address who is affected by the change, which makes it hard to judge the practical impact or urgency.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination, and any argument for why the change belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
