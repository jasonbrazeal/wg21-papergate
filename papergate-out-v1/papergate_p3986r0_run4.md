Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, concentrating on the wording mechanics of a receiver’s lifetime while leaving the affected audience, implementation experience, and standard-library rationale largely unstated. The strongest support is its concrete anchoring in existing execution clauses, but the case thins considerably around who is impacted and whether the change has been tried in practice.

- The paper grounds its central claim in specific standard clauses for `set_value`, `set_error`, and `set_done`, giving the proposal a precise normative anchor.
- It frames the work as a wording strategy for P3425, which at least situates it within an existing change effort.
- The absence of any discussion of affected users or implementation experience leaves the practical case for standardization almost entirely unexamined.
