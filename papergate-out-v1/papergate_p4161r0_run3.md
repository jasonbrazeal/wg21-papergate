Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper gives a light but concrete rationale for renaming the affected member functions, resting almost entirely on a point of English usage rather than on a standardization case. Its support is thinnest where a proposal would normally need to show why the standard is the right venue, how existing code and implementations would be affected, and why a library-level or non-normative remedy would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the specific, well-known grammatical distinction between “fewer” and “less,” backed by a recognizable real-world example.
- The paper acknowledges that the change would affect the majority of ordered containers in production codebases, but does not develop that into an impact or migration discussion.
- It offers no argument for why the standard should address this rather than leaving it to libraries, documentation, or coding guidelines.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, interoperability consideration, or standards-process justification for the proposed change.
