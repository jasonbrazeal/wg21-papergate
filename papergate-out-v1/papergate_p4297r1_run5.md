Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper grounds its standardization argument in a substantial body of cited prior work, committee direction, and deployment evidence, with particularly strong support for the existence and contestability of the layering question it addresses. The support is thinnest where the paper must connect that contested landscape to a concrete path for the committee to act, since much of the evidence establishes that the question is real and unresolved rather than that this document resolves it.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s reconstruction of the proposal’s seven-poll history and its demonstration that no published work contests the characterization of the layering question as substantive and grounded in a decade of deployment.
- The paper also offers specific, sourced coordination evidence by showing how P3100R8’s Section 7.2 requires Labels or Profiles to be specified in terms of the other, with the proposal nominating Labels.
- The most glaring omission is a clear account of how the paper’s own position advances the standardization effort beyond documenting the dispute, leaving the reader with a well-evidenced problem statement but little sense of the proposed next step.
