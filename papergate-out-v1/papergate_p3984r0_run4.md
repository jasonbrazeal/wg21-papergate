Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the work matters and how it fits into ongoing standardization activity, but it does not build a complete case for taking the proposed step in the standard. The thinnest support is around the claim that a library solution cannot suffice and around evidence of implementation experience, both of which are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is the concrete connection to existing Profiles work, including a specification and experimental compiler implementation.
- The motivation is grounded in specific C++ constraints around C compatibility and the zero-overhead principle.
- The paper does not address who is affected, prior alternatives, or why standardization is the right venue.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported assertion that library-based approaches are inherently inadequate, with no examples or analysis to back that claim.
