Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, preliminary case for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single motivating connection to another proposal and a brief statement of a perceived gap. The support is thinnest around the core question of why a language change is necessary rather than a library solution, and there is no discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or coordination with existing features.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P3771, which at least situates the idea in relation to an active standardization effort.
- The paper asserts that constant-evaluation and runtime-evaluation boundaries currently cannot be detected or modified, but it does not demonstrate why this requires core language action.
- The claim that a pure library solution would become possible is stated without explaining what prevents a library solution today.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence that the approach is workable, beyond a note that prototyping has only just begun.
