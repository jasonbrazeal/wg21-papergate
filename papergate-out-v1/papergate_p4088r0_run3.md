Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper offers a reasonably well-supported case for its own standardization, with concrete benchmarks, implementation experience, and clear articulation of why existing mechanisms fall short. The support is thinnest when it comes to who is affected and what the broader ecosystem impact would be, where assertions appear without the same level of evidence or external validation.

- The strongest support comes from the benchmark data and implementation experience, which give measurable evidence for the claimed performance and allocation properties.
- The paper also grounds its argument well in prior art and the limitations of existing standard mechanisms like senders and coroutines.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of supporting evidence for the claimed audience or affected user base, leaving the proposal’s relevance to the wider community asserted rather than demonstrated.
