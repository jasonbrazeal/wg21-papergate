Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper leans heavily on a single piece of evidence—existing practice in libunifex—to support its standardization case, which gives it some grounding but leaves several sections feeling repetitive rather than independently substantiated. The thinnest area is the absence of any discussion of why a library-only solution would be insufficient, which is a notable gap for a proposal aimed at changing the standard.

- The strongest support comes from the claim that libunifex already uses the proposed lifetime management strategy, which the paper cites as evidence of existing practice and prior art.
- The paper also offers a concrete reference implementation output, suggesting at least some implementation experience beyond the libunifex mention.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper does not address why a library will not do, leaving the core justification for standardization incomplete.
