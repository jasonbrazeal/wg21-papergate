Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, technical justification for its change, leaning almost entirely on consistency with a previously adopted proposal rather than building a broader case for standardization. The support is thinnest around the practical stakes: it never explains who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or what alternatives were considered.

- The strongest support is the specific reference to P2248R8 and the Tokyo 2024 adoption, which grounds the change in an established precedent.
- The paper asserts that implementations are already shipping with P2248R8, but provides no evidence or details about that implementation experience.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of affected users, motivation beyond consistency, or why a library-level workaround would be insufficient.
