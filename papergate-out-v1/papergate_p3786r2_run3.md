The paper gives a partial but uneven account of why this feature belongs in the standard, with concrete evidence for implementation feasibility and prior standardization history, but little direct argument for the standard as the right venue or for the scope of the affected audience. The thinnest support concerns the core “why the standard” question, which is asserted rather than explained, and several coordination and library-alternative considerations are left entirely unaddressed.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, with both a Godbolt prototype and a libstdc++ patch cited.
- The paper also grounds the proposal in prior art by noting that P1024 already proposed this feature and was accepted during the C++20 cycle.
- The rationale for standardizing rather than leaving this to a library is not addressed at all.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected or why the standard is the necessary home for the feature.
