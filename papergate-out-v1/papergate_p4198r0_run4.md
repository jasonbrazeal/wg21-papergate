Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only a thin, largely unsupported case for standardization, leaning on a single repeated claim about ABI constraints while leaving most other justification categories unaddressed or asserted without evidence. The strongest material is the existence of a reference implementation, but even that is mentioned only in passing rather than described in enough detail to demonstrate viability.

- The paper’s most concrete support is its reference implementation, which at least suggests the idea is technically explorable.
- The repeated ABI argument gives some motivation, but it is asserted rather than explained or demonstrated.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the proposal or why existing alternatives are insufficient.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, prior art analysis, or interoperability discussion beyond bare assertions.
