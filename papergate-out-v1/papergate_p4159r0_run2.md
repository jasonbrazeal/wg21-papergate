Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization beyond a narrow response to prior committee direction, leaving most of the usual justification unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the specific reference to LEWG direction on LWG4361, which grounds the proposal in an existing committee request.
- The paper does not explain who is affected by the change or what practical problem it solves for users.
- It provides no implementation experience or evidence that the wording has been tried in practice.
- Most notably, it never explains why the standard is the right place for this change rather than a library or other mechanism.
