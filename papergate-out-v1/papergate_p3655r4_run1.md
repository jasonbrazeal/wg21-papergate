Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper grounds its standardization case in concrete evidence of existing use, prior committee history, and a reference implementation, though the support is uneven and sometimes leans on the same examples to serve multiple purposes. The thinnest area is the absence of a direct comparison with alternatives beyond the failed 2019 proposal, leaving the reader to infer why this attempt should succeed where that one did not.

- The strongest support comes from the documented prevalence of similar types across major codebases and the availability of a working reference implementation.
- The paper identifies a clear, practical need for a null-terminated view when interfacing with system calls and third-party libraries.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of what changed since the earlier rejected proposal or how the current design addresses the objections that blocked consensus.
