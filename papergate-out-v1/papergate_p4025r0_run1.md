Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers concrete grounding for its motivation and interoperability goals, but it leaves the central case for standardization largely asserted rather than argued. The thinnest support concerns who is actually affected and whether existing library approaches have been tried seriously enough to justify a standard.

- The strongest support is the specific interoperability requirement for zero-copy exchange with Python, which points to a reason a common container might matter.
- The paper cites a prior DataFrame library, but does not explain what was learned from it or why it is insufficient.
- The claim that C++ needs a standard to prevent fragmentation is repeated without evidence of fragmentation or its costs.
- The paper never identifies the affected users or reports implementation experience, leaving the practical urgency of standardization unestablished.
