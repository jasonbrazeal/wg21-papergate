Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a thin, largely asserted case for standardization, with its strongest concrete support coming from a single reference to the C++ Core Guidelines. Most of the sections that would establish need, affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution is insufficient are either empty or restate the same unsupported claim. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of implementation experience and any discussion of who would be affected by the change.

- The paper’s strongest support is its citation of C++ Core Guidelines rule C.12, which at least grounds the problem in existing community guidance.
- The rationale for why a library cannot address the need is asserted rather than demonstrated, with no examples or comparison to existing library techniques.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the proposal, leaving the scope and audience of the change unclear.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any implementation experience, which leaves the practical viability of the proposal entirely unexamined.
