Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only a narrow, repeated rationale for its proposal, and much of the case for standardization is asserted rather than demonstrated. The strongest support is the reference to prior art in P2830R10, but the discussion of affected users, coordination, and implementation experience is essentially absent.

- The clearest support comes from the citation of P2830R10’s `type_order`, which grounds the idea in existing reflection-related ordering work.
- The paper asserts that direct comparison of `meta::info` would make sorting in metaprogramming more convenient, but it does not develop this into a broader motivation or show who needs it.
- The discussion of why a library solution is insufficient is stated indirectly and never argued with concrete limitations.
- The paper openly reports having no implementation experience for the proposed built-in comparison, leaving the practical case for standardization thin.
