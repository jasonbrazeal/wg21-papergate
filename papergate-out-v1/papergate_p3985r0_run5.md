Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives concrete support for its standardization case mainly through implementation experience and engagement with prior naming work, but several parts of the argument are asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the claims about who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the proposal coordinates with existing or adjacent standardization efforts.

- The strongest support is the reported implementation and production use of the concepts in Intel’s SIMD reference implementation.
- The discussion of P3287R2 provides a specific prior-art comparison, though it does not fully justify the chosen naming approach.
- The paper does not address why a non-standard library would be inadequate for the stated need.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any coordination or interoperability discussion with the existing `std::simd` design or related proposals.
