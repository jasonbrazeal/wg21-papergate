Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a reasonably well-supported case for standardization, with concrete implementation experience and clear reasoning about why a library-only approach would be insufficient. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the broader committee context, which are left largely implicit rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the implementation experience in libc++ and libstdc++, showing the proposal is grounded in practical work rather than pure design.
- The discussion of why a library solution will not do is specific and ties the need for standardization to semantic guarantees that only a core language feature can provide.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any substantive treatment of who is affected, beyond a bare meeting reference that does not establish committee engagement or consensus.
