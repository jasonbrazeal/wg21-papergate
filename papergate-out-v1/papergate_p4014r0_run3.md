Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers meaningful support for its standardization case by grounding its motivation in concrete examples and pointing to existing implementation experience, but it leaves several essential questions about committee fit, interoperability, and why a library solution is insufficient largely unexamined. The thinnest parts of the argument concern the very rationale for standardization rather than library adoption, and the absence of any discussion of affected users or coordination with existing async models weakens the overall case.

- The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience, with iteration and branching equivalents already available in the stdexec reference implementation.
- The motivation section is supported with specifics about which domains the proposal does not serve and why those domains deserve their own model.
- The paper does not address why standardization is necessary rather than continued use as a library.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of coordination and interoperability with existing async models or affected user communities.
