Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives concrete motivation for fixing broken or risky filter-view use cases and points to prior SG9 discussion, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale implicit. The thinnest support is around why this must be a standard facility rather than a library solution, and there is no implementation experience or interoperability discussion to ground the design.

- The strongest support is the specific claim that several basic use cases of the current filter view are broken or risky.
- The paper also cites prior SG9 discussion and a vote as evidence of direction, though the link alone does not show what was actually agreed.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why the standard, rather than a library, is the right place for this work.
