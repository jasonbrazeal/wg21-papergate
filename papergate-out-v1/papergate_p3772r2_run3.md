Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers almost no affirmative case for standardization beyond a bare appeal to consistency, and it leaves most of the questions a reviewer would expect unanswered. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The only concrete support is the reference to prior design-approved work and the stated goal of consistency with another proposal.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change or what problem it solves for them.
- The section on implementation experience is empty, offering no evidence that the feature is implementable or has been tried.
- The rationale for standardization rather than a library solution is never given, leaving the central justification unstated.
