Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence in a few areas but little engagement with the broader case for adding the facility to the standard. The thinnest support concerns the rationale for standardization itself, the affected audience, and the claim that a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, where the paper points to generated code as evidence of the proposed facility’s behavior.
- Prior art and alternatives are addressed with some specificity, tying the proposed name and placement to existing `std::simd` functions.
- The claim that a library will not do is merely asserted as a matter of verbosity, without demonstrating why that verbosity rises to a standardization concern.
- The paper does not address why the feature matters, who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or how it coordinates with existing practice.
