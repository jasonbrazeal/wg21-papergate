Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper provides substantial support for its standardization case, grounding its motivation in concrete examples of current behavior, documented committee sentiment, and broad industry precedent. The thinnest area is the treatment of potential downsides or migration concerns, which are acknowledged only lightly relative to the strength of the affirmative evidence.

- The strongest support comes from the LEWG poll results, which show clear committee interest in the proposed behavior change.
- The paper effectively ties its proposal to established practice in multiple major languages and to a widely used C++ formatting library, giving the design credibility.
- The discussion of why a library-only fix is insufficient is specific and directly addresses a likely objection.
- The most glaring omission is a fuller account of how existing users relying on the current fixed-format behavior would be affected or guided through the transition.
