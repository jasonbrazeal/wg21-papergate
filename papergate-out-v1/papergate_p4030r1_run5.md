Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its proposal through its connection to the UTF transcoding range adaptors and its identification of related use cases, but it leaves several key arguments asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the paper claims broad applicability and argues for standardization as the right venue without evidence or implementation experience.

- The strongest support ties the proposed views directly to the existing P2728R11 UTF transcoding work, giving the proposal a concrete motivating context.
- The paper names additional affected domains such as network protocols and file formats, but does not substantiate how these users would benefit or why existing approaches are insufficient.
- The argument for placing this facility in the standard relies on a design principle rather than evidence that a library solution cannot meet the need.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, leaving the feasibility and practical value of the proposed views unverified.
