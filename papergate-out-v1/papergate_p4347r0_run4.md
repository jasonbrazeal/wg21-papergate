Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper provides only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, with most of the expected rationale left unstated. Its strongest contribution is a brief, concrete observation about how implementations might combine the proposal with related work, but the document otherwise does not build a case for why the feature belongs in the standard or why existing mechanisms are insufficient.

- The clearest support comes from the specific note that implementations could enable this proposal and P2900 separately or in combination.
- The paper does not explain why the problem matters or who would be affected by standardizing it.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, implementation experience, or interoperability concerns.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library solution would not suffice or why the standard is the right venue.
