Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only a skeletal case for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single repeated claim about ABI constraints rather than developing evidence, alternatives, or implementation detail. The strongest support is the concrete reference to an implementation, but even that is asserted without elaboration, and the thinnest areas are the unaddressed audience impact and the unsupported dismissal of library solutions.

- The paper’s most substantive support is the mention of a reference implementation illustrating O(1) dispatch and potential optimizations.
- The central ABI argument is repeated across several sections but never explained or demonstrated with specifics.
- Prior art, alternatives, and the insufficiency of library-only approaches are asserted without any supporting reasoning or examples.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the proposed change or what coordination or interoperability concerns might arise.
