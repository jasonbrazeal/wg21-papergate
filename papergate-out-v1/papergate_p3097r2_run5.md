Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization, grounding its case in prior proposals, implementation experience, and direct feedback from EWG, though the evidence for implementation completeness is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest area is the claim of a complete GCC implementation, which lacks corroborating detail or links.

- The strongest support comes from the EWG poll in St. Louis, showing clear committee interest in merging virtual function contract support into the core contracts proposal.
- The paper cites prior art and alternatives, including C++20 Contracts and GCC implementation history, to establish that this is a known and unresolved problem.
- The rationale for why a library solution is insufficient is tied to concrete repetition of assertion sequences in overriding functions.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported claim of a complete GCC implementation, with no reference, test results, or description of how completeness was verified.
