The paper gives a partial account of why a `lookup` member might be preferable to overloading `get`, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The strongest material concerns naming and prior discussion, while the argument for standardizing this facility specifically is largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper grounds its naming concern in a specific observation about how `get` is used elsewhere in the library.
- It reports concrete polling results and prior proposal history, showing that the design question has been considered by the committee.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, interoperability, or why the functionality cannot be provided adequately by a library.
