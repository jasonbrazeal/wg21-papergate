Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial, concrete support for its standardization case, drawing on real adoption, production evaluation, and a clear articulation of why the standard is the right layer for the vocabulary it proposes. The support is thinnest where it relies on a single institutional evaluation and a forward-looking protocol whose broader ecosystem uptake is still implied rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the documented use by three independent Boost library adopters and a production trading infrastructure evaluation, which grounds the proposal in real-world demand.
- The paper clearly explains why a library-only solution is insufficient, pointing to the IoAwaitable protocol’s frame allocator timing as a problem only a standardized mechanism can solve.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of broader, multi-vendor implementation experience beyond the single production evaluation and the Boost ecosystem, leaving the portability and interoperability claims less fully tested.
