Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a narrow, partially substantiated case for its own standardization, leaning on one specific NB comment and a cross-reference to CWG3132 while leaving most of the standardization rationale unexamined. The support is thinnest around why this belongs in the standard at all, who is actually affected, and whether any non-normative or library-based remedy could suffice.

- The strongest support is the cited NB comment requesting a thorough review of “declaration” usage, which gives the proposal a concrete procedural hook.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is grounded in a specific CWG issue, lending some context to the proposed change.
- The claim that the vast majority of occurrences use the wrong style is asserted without evidence, weakening the sense of real-world impact.
- The paper does not address why the standard is the right venue, how the change coordinates with other work, why a library or editorial fix would not do, or what implementation experience exists.
