Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper grounds its standardization argument most convincingly in concrete implementation experience and specific prior art, but it leaves several key justifications—especially why a library cannot suffice and why the standard is the right venue—as bare assertions. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, coordination, or interoperability, which weakens the case that this belongs in the standard now.

- The strongest support comes from the cited production use at NVIDIA and Citadel Securities and the availability of the stdexec reference implementation.
- The paper identifies P2300R10 as prior art and explains how the proposed facilities extend that foundation.
- The claim that the complexity is necessary and cannot be delivered by a library is asserted without supporting argument or comparison to alternatives.
- The paper does not address who is affected or how the proposal coordinates with existing or adjacent standardization efforts.
