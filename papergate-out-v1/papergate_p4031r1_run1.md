Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with the strongest material being a specific poll result and a brief account of a prior renaming attempt. The argument for changing the namespace rests almost entirely on the claim that the current name is confusing, but that claim is repeated without elaboration, and several standard categories of justification are left entirely unaddressed.

- The paper cites a concrete poll showing weak support for the current name and some openness to the proposed alternative.
- It references a prior attempt to rename the namespace, indicating that the naming problem has been discussed before.
- The central justification that the name will “cause a lot of confusion” is asserted rather than explained or demonstrated.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, coordination with other proposals, or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.
