Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper makes a reasonably grounded case for standardizing the trait, leaning on repeated claims of existing ad-hoc implementations and the inadequacy of common workarounds. The support is thinnest where it treats those claims as self-evident rather than showing concrete code, surveys, or compiler-specific failures that would let a reader verify the breadth of the problem.

- The strongest support is the paper’s consistent appeal to real-world implementation experience, even if that experience is asserted rather than demonstrated in detail.
- The discussion of why simpler library-level approaches fail is specific enough to justify the need for something beyond ordinary user code.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of concrete examples or data showing how widespread the ad-hoc implementations are or how they actually differ in practice.
