Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers almost no direct support for its own standardization, resting instead on brief assertions about perception and terminology without evidence, examples, or engagement with alternatives. The thinnest support is in the complete absence of rationale for why this belongs in the standard, why a library cannot address it, or how it would interoperate with existing practice.

- The strongest element is a passing claim that users misunderstand profile scope, though even this is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The paper gestures at community terminology but provides no documentation or analysis of those interactions.
- It does not address prior art, implementation experience, or coordination with other proposals.
- Most glaringly, it never explains why the standard is the right venue or why a library solution would be insufficient.
