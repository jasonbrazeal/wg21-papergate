Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper provides almost no substantive support for its own standardization, offering only a bare assertion of implementation experience while leaving every other foundational question unaddressed. The thinnest part is the complete absence of motivation, context, or comparison with alternatives, which makes it impossible to judge whether the proposal belongs in the standard at all.

- The only concrete support is the claim that the feature has been implemented and tested in a libstdc++ patch currently under review.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters, who would use it, or what problem it solves.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- It is silent on coordination, interoperability, and the rationale for standardization itself.
