Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its proposal, chiefly through an implementation link and a reference to the historical syntax origin, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of discussion about who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the feature would coordinate with existing standard facilities.

- The strongest support is the availability of a Clang fork implementation on Compiler Explorer, which demonstrates at least preliminary implementability.
- The paper cites N3701 as the origin of the current unconditional syntax, giving a specific point of prior art.
- The claim that current workarounds require code duplication is asserted without examples or elaboration.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or interoperability with related features.
