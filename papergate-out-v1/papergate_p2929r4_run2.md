Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its proposal through implementation details and a nod to related library functions, but it leaves the central case for standardization largely unargued. The thinnest support is around the motivating problem, the affected audience, and why existing library mechanisms cannot already serve the need.

- The strongest support is the inclusion of generated code, which at least shows the proposed mechanism in action.
- The paper also ties the proposed name and placement to existing `std::simd` facilities, giving it some contextual alignment.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected or why the problem matters enough to justify standardization.
- The claim that a library will not do is asserted without supporting reasoning, leaving the necessity of a standard facility unestablished.
