Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin, largely assertive case for standardization: its central claims about legacy compatibility, the inadequacy of existing alternatives, and the suitability of Modules are stated rather than demonstrated. The strongest support appears in the framing of the problem, while the argument becomes thinnest precisely where it needs evidence of feasibility and need for a standard.

- The paper gives a concrete, specific motivation by linking C-compatible behaviors to bugs, complexity, and onboarding difficulties.
- The claim that existing safe-subset efforts either remove necessary power or remain unenforceable is asserted without examples or evidence.
- The paper provides no implementation experience, no interoperability discussion, and no demonstration that a library solution would be insufficient.
