Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper provides substantial support for its standardization case, with concrete implementation experience, compiler availability, and evidence from large codebases. The support is thinnest around coordination with the standard library and the unresolved concerns raised by recent papers and national body comments.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, with two complete compiler implementations publicly available and upstreaming in progress.
- The paper also grounds its case in real-world application, citing large codebases where applying the proposed semantics revealed genuine bugs without reported breakage.
- The most glaring omission is a substantive engagement with the recent papers and national body comments that argue for removal from the C++26 working draft, which the paper lists but does not address in depth.
