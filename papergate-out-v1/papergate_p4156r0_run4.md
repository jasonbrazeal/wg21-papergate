Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper offers almost no affirmative case for standardization, leaving the reader to infer motivation and necessity from the title and a single cross-reference. Its support is thinnest in the areas that usually decide whether a proposal is actionable: rationale, affected users, alternatives, and implementation evidence.

- The only concrete grounding offered is a citation to CWG 3112, which at least gestures toward prior discussion.
- The paper does not explain why the change matters or who would benefit from it.
- It asserts prior art and alternatives without any supporting comparison or analysis.
- It omits implementation experience, library feasibility, and interoperability considerations entirely.
