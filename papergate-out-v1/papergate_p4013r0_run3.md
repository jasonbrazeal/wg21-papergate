Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some concrete implementation evidence and a named prior-art reference, but it leaves most of the case for standardization unstated. The thinnest support is around motivation, affected users, and why the standard library or existing language facilities cannot adequately serve the need.

- The strongest support is the linked compiler implementation and compiler-explorer availability, which shows at least one working prototype.
- The discussion of prior art is specific, citing Microsoft’s STL pointer-tagging practices and relating the approach to P3125.
- The claim that a library solution will not do is asserted without supporting detail, leaving the core rationale for language or standard-library change undeveloped.
- The paper does not address why the problem matters, who is affected, or how the proposal would coordinate with existing standard facilities.
