Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper backs its standardization case with unusually concrete references, from public committee records to a decade of shipping vendor implementations, and it is strongest when tying the proposed layering to specific published proposals and existing tooling. The support thins most where the document gestures at architectural choices—such as the relationship between Labels and Profiles—without showing that the nominated direction has enough working detail to carry the six foundational clauses under review.

- The strongest support is the implementation record, which names specific vendor checkers and release dates spanning roughly a decade.
- The paper also grounds its committee-facing claims in a public issue tracker entry with a recorded straw poll and tally.
- The most glaring omission is that the proposed Labels-first layering is sketched rather than demonstrated, leaving the central coordination mechanism under-specified for the review it claims to support.
