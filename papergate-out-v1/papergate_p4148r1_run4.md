Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers concrete support for its standardization mainly through implementation experience and engagement with prior art, but much of its rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the proposal must explain why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, and how it would coordinate with existing facilities.

- The strongest support is the availability of a reference implementation, which shows the design has been exercised in practice.
- The discussion of prior art is specific and useful, particularly in positioning the proposal relative to `proxy`.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any developed argument for why a library solution would not suffice, given that the paper itself frames the problem as a library extension.
- The sections on who is affected, why the standard, and coordination and interoperability all reuse the same general claim without connecting it to the proposal’s specific design or users.
