Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper grounds several of its central claims in concrete examples and implementation experience, but it leaves important parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns the rationale for changing the standard itself and the absence of any discussion of who would be affected by the change.

- The strongest support comes from the reported GCC implementation experience, which shows the proposal is more than a design sketch.
- The paper also gives specific prior art and explains why library-only solutions cannot address the const-correctness problem it identifies.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any treatment of who is affected, leaving the proposal’s user and ecosystem impact unexamined.
- The case for changing the standard is largely asserted, with little direct argument for why standardization is the necessary path.
