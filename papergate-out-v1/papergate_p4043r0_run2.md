The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of why the feature’s current direction is contested and why deferral has precedent, but it does little to establish that the proposed path is ready for standardization. The strongest material concerns community and committee concern, while the case for a standard rather than a library solution, coordination with other work, and practical implementation experience is essentially absent.

- The paper supports its relevance with specific evidence of widespread national body comments and unresolved design concerns.
- It grounds its argument in a well-known precedent where a major feature was removed and later standardized after further work.
- It does not address why the facility belongs in the standard rather than in a library or separate specification.
- It offers no implementation experience or interoperability analysis to show the proposal is deployable as specified.
