Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a fair amount of concrete support for its standardization case, particularly through references to prior analyses, committee consensus, and implementation work, but several key justifications are asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas concern why a library solution is insufficient, what implementation experience exists beyond a single compiler, and how the feature coordinates with the broader standard.

- The strongest support comes from the cited EWG consensus in Brno, which shows the roadmap has already received formal committee endorsement.
- The reference to P3600R0 offers a substantive prior-art and design-space analysis for the virtual function contract assertions.
- The claim of coordination among SG21, EWG, and CWG is asserted without evidence of what that coordination resolved or how it shaped the proposal.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of why a library-only approach cannot meet the demonstrated need.
