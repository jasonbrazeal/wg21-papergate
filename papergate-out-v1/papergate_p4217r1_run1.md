Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only narrow support for its standardization case, resting on a single motivating claim about generic algorithms and some implementation experience, while leaving most of the evidentiary burden unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, why a library solution would not suffice, or how the feature would coordinate with the broader ecosystem.

- The strongest support is the reported implementation against nVidia’s reference implementation, which at least shows the coalescing option is technically feasible.
- The paper cites a specific request from SG1 to consult named maintainers, indicating some engagement with prior art and external expertise.
- The rationale for standardization is asserted rather than argued, with no elaboration beyond the claim that the status quo creates a generic-algorithm special case.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about who is affected or why a library-level solution would be inadequate.
