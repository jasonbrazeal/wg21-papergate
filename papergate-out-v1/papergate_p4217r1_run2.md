Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives only partial support for its own standardization, with concrete implementation experience and a specific request for external feedback, but it leaves several core justification areas unaddressed. The thinnest support is around why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, who is affected, and how it coordinates with existing practice.

- The strongest support is the reported implementation against nVidia’s reference implementation, which grounds the proposal in real code.
- The paper also cites a concrete prior-art step: SG1 asked the author to consult named maintainers of Intel’s “Bare Metal Senders and Receivers.”
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library solution would not suffice or why standardization is necessary beyond avoiding a special case.
