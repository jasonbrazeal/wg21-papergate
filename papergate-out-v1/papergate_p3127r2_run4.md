Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization: it gestures at one concrete design choice and one piece of prior art, but leaves nearly every other justification unstated. The support is thinnest around the basic questions of who is affected, why the standard should act, and whether existing libraries already suffice.

- The strongest support is the specific comparison showing how `adjacent_vertices` can be expressed through a neighbor projection, which at least grounds one design decision in prior practice.
- The paper does not address why the standard should contain this facility rather than leaving it to libraries.
- The paper does not identify an affected audience or articulate the problem’s significance beyond an unsupported assertion about the IMDB example.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, with the draft itself noting that the referenced code has not yet been brought up to the proposed interface.
