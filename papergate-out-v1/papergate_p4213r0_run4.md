Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of why standardizing these SI definitions would help users and improve interoperability, but it leaves several parts of its standardization argument underdeveloped, particularly around affected users, implementation experience, and why a library alone cannot suffice.

- The strongest support comes from the coordination and interoperability argument, which clearly explains that the framework currently ships without the SI content users would expect.
- The “why the standard” section is also effective, using a concrete analogy to show that a framework without its expected contents feels incomplete.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is partially supported by reference to mp-units, but the claim that the selected constants “proved useful in practice” is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected, which weakens the case that this need is broad enough to justify standardization.
