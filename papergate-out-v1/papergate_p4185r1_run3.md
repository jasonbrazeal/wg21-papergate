Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization case through concrete implementation experience, community feedback, and analysis of alternatives, though the argument is uneven in places. The thinnest support appears where the paper asserts the need for a standard rather than demonstrating it, and where it leans on the absence of prior art as a justification.

- The strongest support comes from the documented real-world experience with the mp-units reference implementation and feedback from multiple standards bodies and the broader C++ community.
- The paper also grounds its case well in the analysis of alternative designs and their specific trade-offs in expressiveness, genericity, and metrological clarity.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any supporting argument for why this work belongs in the standard rather than remaining a library, despite the paper's own observation that no mainstream units library in any language has adopted the proposed three-type model.
