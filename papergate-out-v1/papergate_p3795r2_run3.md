Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a brief account of inconsistencies introduced by earlier related proposals. The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2996R13’s access-checking mechanism, but the document leaves the affected audience, rationale for standardization, and practical experience largely unexamined.

- The paper does identify specific inconsistencies and gaps arising from independently adopted proposals, which gives some concrete motivation for the work.
- It points to a relevant prior mechanism in P2996R13, showing awareness of existing facilities and an indirect precedent.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the proposed changes or why the standard, rather than a library solution, is the right venue.
