Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides concrete motivation and naming rationale for the proposed facility, but it leaves several core standardization questions essentially unaddressed, so the case for adoption rests on a narrow evidentiary base.

- The strongest support is the specific, practical use-case list, including CRC, AES-GCM, parsing, and bit manipulation.
- The naming discussion is grounded in existing practice across Intel, LLVM, and RV64.
- The paper asserts relevance to `simdjson` and string parsing but offers no supporting detail or measured impact.
- The most glaring omissions are the absence of any discussion of why a standard facility is needed, why a library cannot suffice, or how the proposal would coordinate with existing implementations.
