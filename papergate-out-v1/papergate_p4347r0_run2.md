Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed to support standardization, resting almost entirely on a brief remark about how implementations might combine this proposal with P2900. Most of the core questions a reviewer would expect—motivation, affected users, why the standard is the right venue, and evidence of implementation—are left unaddressed. The thinnest support is in the absence of any argument for why the feature belongs in the standard rather than in a library or separate specification.

- The strongest support is a specific statement that implementations could enable this proposal and P2900 separately or together, which at least gestures at coordination.
- The paper does not discuss prior art or alternatives beyond that single implementation-combination remark.
- It offers no implementation experience, leaving the proposal without evidence of practical viability.
- Most glaringly, the paper never explains why the standard should adopt this work or why a library solution would not suffice.
