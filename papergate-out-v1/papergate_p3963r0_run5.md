Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper grounds several of its key arguments in concrete technical context, particularly around P3179R9, P3960R0, and the role of *movable-box* in views, but it leans on unverified conversations for vendor and implementer support. The thinnest parts are the claims about affected parties and implementation feasibility, which are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the technical linkage to accepted and existing proposals, which anchors the problem in ongoing standardization work.
- The discussion of why a library solution is insufficient is specific and tied to a named implementation detail, *movable-box*.
- The claim of NVIDIA’s agreement is reported but not corroborated by any public position or written feedback.
- The most glaring omission is implementation experience, where the paper admits no implementation exists and offers only secondhand assurance that it will not cause problems.
