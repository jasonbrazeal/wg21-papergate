Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a skeletal rationale for standardizing a stable annex of undefined behavior and IFNDR cases, leaning almost entirely on prior proposals and a collaborative drafting branch rather than demonstrating need, impact, or feasibility. The thinnest support is in the areas that would matter most to a standards committee: who is affected, why the standard is the right vehicle, and whether any implementation or user experience validates the approach.

- The strongest support comes from prior art, with specific references to earlier efforts and proposals that establish a lineage for this work.
- The paper asserts that stable names would be useful as common vocabulary, but offers no evidence or examples of how that vocabulary would be used in practice.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of who is affected or why the standard, rather than a separate document or library, is the appropriate home for this information.
