Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, resting entirely on a brief statement of intent and a note that wording was adjusted after feedback. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of motivation beyond assertion, and the lack of any discussion of affected users, alternatives, implementation experience, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The only concrete support is the statement that the paper aims to introduce new non-terminals and place their definitions in the relevant subclauses.
- The paper acknowledges prior feedback from Jens Maurer but does not explain what that feedback was or how the revised wording addresses it.
- The paper provides no evidence that the change is needed, who it affects, or how it would work in practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or consideration of alternatives, leaving the proposal without a demonstrated path to standardization.
