Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a narrow technical justification for its proposed addition, centered on a single efficiency concern, and leaves most of the surrounding case for standardization unstated. The support is thinnest where a reader would expect evidence of need, precedent, or practical viability beyond a brief mailing-list mention.

- The strongest support is the concrete complexity argument showing inefficiency in the current `std::priority_queue` behavior.
- The paper also gestures at avoiding reallocations as a motivation, though it does not develop this into a broader rationale.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of prior art, alternatives, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
