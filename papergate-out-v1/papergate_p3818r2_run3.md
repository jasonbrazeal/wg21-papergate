Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a partial account of why the proposed change is needed and who would be affected, but it leaves several important parts of the standardization case unstated or unsupported. The strongest material concerns the concrete breakage scenario and the availability of an implementation, while the rationale for acting through the standard and the relationship to prior work are largely absent.

- The paper identifies a specific, surprising interaction with P3068 and reports a poll showing mixed but non-negligible committee interest in adopting the solution.
- It points to a working prototype and a Compiler Explorer link, giving readers some confidence that the approach is implementable.
- The discussion of why the standard must change is asserted rather than argued, with no explanation of why other venues or approaches would be insufficient.
- Prior art, alternatives, coordination with related proposals, and implementation experience beyond the prototype are not addressed, leaving the standardization case thin in those areas.
