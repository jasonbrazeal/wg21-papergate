Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its naming concern, including a poll result and reference to prior discussion, but it does not build a broader case for standardization. The argument rests almost entirely on avoiding confusion over a namespace name, while leaving the rationale for standardizing the facility itself largely unsupported.

- The strongest support comes from the recorded poll and the reference to P3804R1, which show that the naming question was actively considered and that alternatives were on the table.
- The paper gives a specific reason for concern by noting that `system_context` does not exist in the current working draft, making the proposed namespace name potentially misleading.
- The case for standardization is thinnest around why the standard is the right venue, since the central claim is asserted without supporting explanation.
- The paper does not address coordination and interoperability, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
