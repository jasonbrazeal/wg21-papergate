Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides substantial support for its standardization case through concrete implementation experience, acknowledgment of prior art, and attention to ABI and interoperability concerns. The support is thinnest in addressing who would be affected by the proposal and in fully distinguishing the standardization rationale from the library-based alternative.

- The strongest support comes from the acknowledgment that most of the proposed core changes have already been implemented in Clang, giving the proposal years of real-world compiler experience.
- The paper also grounds its case well in interoperability and ABI concerns, explaining why a single platform ABI is necessary for cross-compiler and cross-language use.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected by the proposal, leaving the audience and impact unclear.
