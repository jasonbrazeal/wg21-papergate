Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper makes a reasonably well-supported case for its own standardization, with concrete references to committee sentiment, prior art, and domain-specific consequences. The support is thinnest where it relies on assertions about implementation history and correctness without offering evidence or examples to substantiate those claims.

- The strongest support comes from the St. Louis EWG poll, which shows meaningful committee interest in merging virtual function support into the core contracts proposal.
- The paper grounds its relevance in specific affected domains and explains why existing designs in Eiffel, D, and Ada cannot accommodate common C++ usage patterns.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported claim that all previous C++ proposals and the GCC implementation failed to handle assertion inheritance correctly, with no demonstration or citation of that failure.
