Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin rationale for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single historical observation and an unsupported claim about prior discussion. Its strongest material concerns prior art, but the document leaves the actual case for a standard library policy largely unstated.

- The paper’s most concrete support is its reference to prior discussions suggesting wide-contract non-throwing functions should be marked `noexcept`.
- The central assertion that no `noexcept` policy has existed since a certain point is presented without evidence or context.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the proposed policy or why the standard is the right venue.
- It omits any discussion of implementation experience, coordination, interoperability, or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.
