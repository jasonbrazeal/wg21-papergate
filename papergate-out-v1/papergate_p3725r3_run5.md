Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the current filter view is problematic, but it does not build a complete case for standardizing the proposed change. The strongest material concerns concrete use cases and prior discussion, while the argument for why this must be a standard library change rather than guidance or a separate library is largely asserted.

- The paper identifies specific broken, risky, or non-intuitive use cases that motivate the change.
- It points to prior SG9 discussion and a vote as evidence of direction, though the supporting details are only referenced rather than summarized.
- The case for why the standard is the right venue is not addressed at all.
- The claim that ordinary programmers need deep insight to avoid serious mistakes is asserted without examples or evidence, leaving the “why a library will not do” argument thin.
