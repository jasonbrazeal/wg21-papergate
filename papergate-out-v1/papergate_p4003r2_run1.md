Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper grounds its standardization case in concrete implementation experience and a companion rationale document, but it leaves the central claim about language-level necessity largely asserted rather than argued. The strongest support is practical and specific, while the thinnest point is the justification for why the standard itself must act.

- The paper’s most persuasive support comes from complete implementations on three platforms, which lends credibility to the proposed protocol and its real-world constraints.
- The discussion of type erasure and heap allocation offers a specific, technically grounded reason why a library-only solution falls short.
- The claim that “the language provides what a library would reimplement” is asserted without elaboration, leaving the core standardization rationale underdeveloped.
