Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, anecdotal basis for standardization: it names one library that uses the technique and one naming convention, but leaves most of the case for committee action unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of why a standard facility is needed rather than a library, how it would interoperate with existing practice, or what implementation experience beyond a single mention actually shows.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to `simdjson` as a real-world user of the technique.
- The naming rationale is grounded in existing practice across Intel, LLVM, and RV64.
- The paper does not address why standardization is necessary or what problem a standard facility would solve that a library cannot.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion of coordination, interoperability, or broader implementation experience.
