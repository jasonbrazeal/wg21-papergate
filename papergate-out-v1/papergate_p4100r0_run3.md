Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial, concrete support for standardizing its proposed vocabulary, drawing on production use, independent adopters, and an institutional evaluation. The case is strongest where it connects existing practice and interoperability needs to the standard, and thinnest where it relies on a single external evaluation and forward-looking claims about ecosystem adoption.

- The paper grounds its standardization argument in Asio’s twenty-year production history and its role as the foundation of the Networking TS.
- It provides specific evidence from three independent Boost libraries and one institutional trading-infrastructure evaluation.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of broader, independent implementation or deployment experience beyond the single documented evaluation and the author’s own ecosystem.
