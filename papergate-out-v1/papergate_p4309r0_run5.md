Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin, largely asserted case for standardization, with most of its rationale resting on a single repeated annoyance rather than demonstrated need or evidence. The strongest support appears in the discussion of prior art and alternative design approaches, while the thinnest areas concern implementation experience, affected users, and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The paper gives concrete alternatives such as factory functions or additional constructors, showing some engagement with design space.
- The claim that creating a container just to extract one element is “silly” gestures at a usability problem, though without examples or frequency data.
- The paper does not address coordination with other proposals, interoperability concerns, or how this fits into the broader container API evolution.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, user reports, or evidence that the described annoyance has actually blocked real-world code.
