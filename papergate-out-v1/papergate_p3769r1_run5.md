Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a narrow but concrete account of implementation divergence, which is the strongest part of its case, but it leaves most of the surrounding justification for standardization unstated. The support is thinnest around who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether there is any implementation experience beyond the noted divergence.

- The paper identifies two specific cases of implementation divergence in placement new deallocation behavior, grounding the problem in observable practice.
- It points to prior work in P3492R2 as having been forwarded to CWG, giving the proposal a visible path within the committee.
- It does not explain who is affected by the divergence or what practical consequences follow for users.
- It offers no implementation experience, library alternative analysis, or rationale for why a standard change is needed rather than a different remedy.
