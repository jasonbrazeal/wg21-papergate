Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow, example-driven justification for its proposal, leaving most of the argumentative burden unaddressed. Its strongest support is a concrete scenario showing why a library-only solution would fail, but it does not connect that scenario to broader user impact, prior work, or the need for standardization specifically.

- The paper gives a specific code example illustrating a lifetime and exception-safety problem that a library solution cannot handle.
- The same example is reused as the only evidence for why the standard should address the issue, without additional cases or elaboration.
- The paper does not discuss who is affected, prior art, alternatives, coordination with existing features, or implementation experience.
- The absence of any implementation experience or interoperability discussion leaves the practical path to standardization largely unsupported.
