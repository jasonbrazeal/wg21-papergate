Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a reasonably grounded case for standardization, drawing on concrete compiler divergence, existing issue links, and implementer reasoning. The support is thinnest when it comes to showing who is affected and how widespread the practical consequences are, since that part is asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from tying the proposal to an active core issue and a national body comment, which anchors it in the committee’s existing problem tracking.
- The discussion of compiler behavior and the notion of separately instantiated entities gives the proposal credible implementation grounding.
- The paper does not address the affected user population or the real-world impact of the current ambiguity, leaving the urgency of standardization under-supported.
