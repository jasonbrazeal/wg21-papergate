Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of its motivation, prior work, implementation status, and the language-level necessity of the feature, but it leaves the affected audience and some practical consequences largely implicit. The strongest support is concentrated in the technical rationale and the availability of an implementation, while the thinnest part is the absence of any discussion about who would use the feature, how migration or diagnostics would feel, or what costs and interactions might arise.

- The paper grounds its proposal in specific prior proposals and explains why the property cannot be achieved through a library solution.
- It provides a working implementation and a publicly accessible demonstration, which substantiates feasibility.
- The motivation is tied to a forward-looking goal for containers and constant template parameters, giving the proposal a clear direction.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change or what the practical impact on existing code and users would be.
