Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization by grounding its proposal in existing practice and concrete specification references, though the case is uneven because the same implementation-experience claim is reused across several categories rather than developed into distinct evidence. The thinnest area is the affected-audience discussion, which is not addressed at all, leaving the proposal’s impact on users and implementers largely implicit.

- The strongest support comes from the repeated citation of libunifex’s existing `let_*` implementation, which anchors the proposal in real-world usage and suggests standardizing established behavior.
- The paper also provides specific cross-references to the Networking TS and the current `std::execution` specification, giving the lifetime-management argument a concrete standards context.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of who is affected by the change, making it hard to assess the proposal’s practical reach or migration burden.
