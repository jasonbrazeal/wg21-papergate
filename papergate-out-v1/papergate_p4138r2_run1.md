Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides a reasonably concrete case for the problem it identifies, but its support is uneven: the strongest material concerns language behavior and implementation observations, while the argument for standardization itself is largely implicit. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, the standards process rationale, or how the proposed change would interact with existing practice.

- The paper gives specific, technically grounded examples of how adding a `(this)` overload can silently change or break existing calls.
- It cites prior art and shows implementation agreement on most tested cases, which lends some empirical weight to the discussion.
- It does not address who would be affected by the change or why the standard is the right venue.
- It offers no coordination or interoperability analysis, leaving the practical path to adoption unclear.
