Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a sliver of support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a linguistic observation while leaving the actual case for a standard change largely unbuilt. The thinnest areas are those that would normally carry the weight in a proposal: why the standard is the right venue, what alternatives exist, and whether anyone has tried this in practice.

- The strongest support is the specific distinction drawn between *fewer* and *less*, which at least grounds the paper in a recognizable semantic problem.
- The claim that this affects a majority of ordered containers in production codebases is asserted without evidence, so it cannot carry much persuasive weight.
- The paper does not address why a library solution would be insufficient or why standardization is necessary.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination considerations, or any discussion of how such a change would interoperate with existing code.
