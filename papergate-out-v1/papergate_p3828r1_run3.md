Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a narrow but concrete rationale for the rename, leaning almost entirely on naming consistency and a single misleading connotation of the current name. Its support is thinnest where a proposal normally needs to show that the change matters beyond terminology: there is no discussion of affected users, standardization necessity, or practical experience.

- The strongest support is the specific argument that “to_input” misleadingly suggests active processing, unlike established “as_” views such as `as_const` or `as_rvalue`.
- The paper also grounds its alternative in existing naming precedent within the standard library.
- A notable omission is any discussion of who is affected by the current name or how the rename would help them.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any case for why this requires a standard change rather than being handled through documentation, guidance, or a library-level alias.
