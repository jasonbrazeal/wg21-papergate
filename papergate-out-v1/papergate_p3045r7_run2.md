Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper backs its standardization case with unusually concrete evidence, from production bug reports to adoption metrics and interoperability examples, though the repetition of the same production-feedback quote suggests the authors lean on a narrow set of testimonials rather than broadening the evidentiary base.

- The strongest support comes from the authors’ demonstrated domain credibility, including libraries that dominate GitHub engagement for C++ physical-units work.
- The paper also makes a persuasive interoperability argument by naming specific existing libraries and showing how each mishandles the same example.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of compile-time cost, binary impact, or migration burden for existing codebases that would adopt the proposed vocabulary types.
