The strongest material concerns real-world usage and implementation experience, while the thinnest concerns the absence of prior-art discussion and a clear non-library rationale.

- It explains why compiler extensions can become compliance issues in some embedded environments.
- It does not address prior art or alternatives, leaving the standardization landscape unclear.
