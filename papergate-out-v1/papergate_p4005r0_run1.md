Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives only partial support for its own standardization, with concrete discussion of prior art and mangling interoperability but little evidence that the proposed facility is necessary or implementable in practice. The thinnest areas are the absence of any motivation tied to real users or problems, and the lack of implementation experience beyond an assertion that the full form has not been built.

- The strongest support comes from the specific comparison to P3919R0 and P3911, which at least situates the proposal among existing design guidance and alternatives.
- The discussion of mangling and its effect on compiler optimizations offers a concrete interoperability rationale, though it is narrow in scope.
- The paper asserts that a standard guarantee would provide memory and UB safety, but it does not explain why that safety is needed or who would benefit.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any implementation experience, with the paper itself admitting the full proposal has not been implemented.
