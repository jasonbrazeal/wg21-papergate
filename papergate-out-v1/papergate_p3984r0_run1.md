Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a mixed account of its own readiness, offering concrete reasoning for why standardization is needed and why non-standard solutions fall short, but leaving several important parts of the case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who is actually affected, what alternatives were seriously considered, and whether the described work has been meaningfully implemented.

- The strongest support is the specific argument that divergent invocation styles and tool-chain-specific profiles would create chaos and excessive boilerplate without a standard.
- The paper also explains concretely why library-only approaches tend to be limited, non-portable, and dependent on coding guidelines and build-system details.
- It asserts implementation experience through prototypes and guideline use, but provides no supporting detail about scope, maturity, or lessons learned.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected, leaving the intended audience and real-world impact of the proposal unclear.
