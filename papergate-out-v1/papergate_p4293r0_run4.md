The support is thinnest where the paper should explain why the standard is the right venue and what problem would remain unsolved by a library solution.

- The paper does not identify who is affected by the current behavior or what practical need the change addresses.
- It offers no implementation experience, no interoperability considerations, and no explanation of why a library cannot provide the same capability.
