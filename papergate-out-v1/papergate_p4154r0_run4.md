Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on the fact that it resolves a national body comment and reduces merge conflicts among in-flight papers. Beyond those assertions, the proposal does not explain who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, or whether the change has been implemented or validated in practice.

- The strongest support is the concrete identification of the national body comment and the two prior papers whose wording it replaces.
- The paper asserts that replacement wording will minimize merge conflicts but provides no specifics about the conflicting papers or the nature of the conflicts.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, leaving no evidence that the proposed renames are workable in real code.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users or why the standard should adopt the change rather than leaving it to a library or other mechanism.
