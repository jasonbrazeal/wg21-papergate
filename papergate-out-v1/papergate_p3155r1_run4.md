The paper offers substantial support for its standardization case in the areas of motivation, prior art, and implementation experience, but it leaves important gaps around who would be affected and how the proposal would coordinate with existing practice. The thinnest parts are the absence of any discussion of affected users or interoperability concerns, which weakens the overall completeness of the case.

- The paper grounds its motivation in specific engineering reasons and a survey of the current working draft, giving the proposal a concrete evidentiary base.
- It identifies prior work and explains why a library-only approach is insufficient, which strengthens the argument for a standard change.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected by the proposed change or how it coordinates with existing code and implementations.
