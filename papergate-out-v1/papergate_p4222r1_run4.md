Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers a mixed case for standardization: it grounds several important points in concrete examples and prior art, but leaves some of its most foundational claims asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the paper argues that the initialization profile is essential to other profiles and that an implementation already exists, since neither claim is backed by specifics.

- The strongest support comes from the discussion of prior art and alternatives, which cites definite assignment in Ada and C# and default initialization in Java.
- The paper also gives specific, practical reasons why a library-only solution would fall short, such as run-time and memory costs.
- The most glaring omission is the claim that the initialization profile is foundational to nearly every other profile, which is asserted without explanation or evidence.
