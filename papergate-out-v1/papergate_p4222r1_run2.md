Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper gives a mixed account of its own readiness, offering concrete reasoning in several areas but leaving key parts of the standardization case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears around why the standard is the right venue and what practical experience backs the proposal.

- The strongest support comes from the discussion of why a library solution is insufficient, with a clear performance-sensitive example.
- The paper also grounds its treatment of prior art and interoperability in specific tradeoffs and common allocator patterns.
- The rationale for standardizing rather than using another mechanism is merely asserted, with no supporting argument.
- Implementation experience is claimed but not substantiated, leaving the maturity of the proposal unclear.
