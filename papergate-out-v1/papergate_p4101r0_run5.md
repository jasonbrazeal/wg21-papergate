Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper grounds its core rationale in concrete friction with the current reflection model and explains why the proposed consteval-only value rule is a simpler enforcement mechanism, but it leaves several standard proposal expectations largely unaddressed. The strongest support is for the problem statement and the need for a core-language rule, while the thinnest areas concern who is affected, implementation experience, and coordination with related work.

- The paper gives specific examples of limitations in the existing consteval-only type approach and explains how the proposed rule avoids them.
- It makes a clear case that a library-only solution cannot diagnose the problematic declaration because the issue is not visible in the type system.
- The paper does not identify the affected user communities or the practical impact on existing code and teaching.
- It offers no implementation experience or evidence of prototype validation for the proposed rule.
