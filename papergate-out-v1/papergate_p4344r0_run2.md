Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a narrow, fragmentary rationale for standardization, with a few concrete references to prior work but little evidence that the problem is widely felt or that the proposed direction is implementable. The support is thinnest around the affected audience, the need for a standard-language solution rather than a library one, and any practical experience with the idea.

- The strongest support is the specific connection to P2266R3 and the observation that current handling of temporaries and aliases is inconsistent.
- The claim that a library solution is insufficient is asserted without examples or explanation of why existing aliasing techniques fail.
- The paper does not identify who is affected or how common the motivating problem is in real code.
- There is no implementation experience, wording guidance, or coordination discussion to show the proposal is ready for standardization.
