Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers a modest but uneven case for standardization, grounding its motivation in a real gap in the Ranges library and pointing to existing implementation experience, while leaving several important questions about affected users, standardization rationale, and interoperability largely unexamined.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete identification of a missing capability in constrained algorithms and the citation of an existing range/v3 implementation.
- The discussion of prior art is useful in explaining a deliberate design choice to omit projections, even if it does not fully justify that limitation.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the proposal or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of coordination with other proposals or interoperability concerns.
