Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete polling data and prior-art references doing much of the work, while the central rationale is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of implementation experience, coordination, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support comes from the cited poll showing clear committee sentiment against the current namespace name and in favor of alternatives.
- The discussion of prior art usefully points to P3804R1’s preference for `replacement` and `replacement_functions`.
- The paper asserts that the proposed name will cause confusion but offers no supporting reasoning or examples beyond the bare claim.
- The proposal does not address implementation experience, interoperability, or why the functionality cannot be delivered as a library.
