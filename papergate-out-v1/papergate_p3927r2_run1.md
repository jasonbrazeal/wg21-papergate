Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper offers almost no affirmative case for standardization, resting its entire argument on a single implementation claim while leaving the motivating problem, audience, alternatives, and standards rationale unexamined. The support is thinnest where a reader would expect the core justification: why this belongs in the standard, why existing library mechanisms cannot suffice, and how it fits with related facilities.

- The strongest support is the assertion that the solution has been implemented in the `stdexec` reference implementation, which at least suggests feasibility.
- The paper does not explain why the described problem matters or who is concretely affected beyond an unsupported claim.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, or why a library-only solution would be inadequate.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any rationale for standardization itself, including coordination and interoperability considerations.
