Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only a narrow, example-driven rationale for its proposed operations, leaving most of the standardization case unstated. Its support is thinnest around who would be affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and whether any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support is the concrete contrast with existing `compare_exchange` operations and the claim that dedicated operations would express intent more clearly.
- The paper asserts that a library approach is fragile under refactoring, but offers no evidence or elaboration to support that assertion.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or coordination with related standardization efforts.
