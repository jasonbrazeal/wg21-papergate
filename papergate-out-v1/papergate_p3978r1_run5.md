Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, mostly anecdotal case for standardization: it identifies a real language inconsistency and points to one implementation, but it does not develop the broader need, alternatives, or interoperability concerns that would justify committee action. The thinnest support is around why a library solution is insufficient and why the proposed change belongs in the standard rather than in user code.

- The strongest support is the concrete description of the call and subscript operator inconsistency, tied to an existing implementation in the vir-simd library.
- The paper does not address prior art or alternative approaches, leaving the design space largely unexplored.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library-level solution cannot adequately serve users.
