Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper offers a reasonably well-supported case for its own standardization, with concrete implementation experience and specific discussion of prior art, interoperability, and library limitations. The support is thinnest when it comes to motivating the affected audience, where broad claims about popularity are asserted without evidence.

- The strongest support comes from the author’s Clang implementation, which demonstrates feasibility and grounds the proposal in real experience.
- The discussion of P3412R3 and the risk of foreclosing future language changes gives a clear, specific reason for acting in the standard rather than in a library.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any substantiation for the claim that string interpolation is “wildly popular” or that the affected user base is large enough to justify standardization.
