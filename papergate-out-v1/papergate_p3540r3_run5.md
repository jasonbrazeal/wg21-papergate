Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper leans almost entirely on the existence of `gnu::offset` and `clang::offset` as its justification, but it does not develop that fact into a persuasive case for standardization. The thinnest areas are the absence of any stated user need, rationale for ISO adoption, or discussion of why a library solution would be inadequate.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to existing implementations in GNU and Clang, which at least grounds the proposal in real-world practice.
- The paper asserts that the feature is “extremely-popular” but offers no evidence, examples, or affected-user context to support that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about why standardization is necessary or what problem it solves for the C++ community beyond matching existing vendor extensions.
