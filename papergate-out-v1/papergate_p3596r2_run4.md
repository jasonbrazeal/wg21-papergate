Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardizing its proposed annex, relying heavily on assertion rather than demonstration. Its strongest support is the concrete identification of prior art and the collaborative drafting branch, but it leaves the affected audience, rationale for standardization, and practical experience largely unexamined.

- The paper gives a specific, verifiable account of prior art and the structural inconsistency it aims to resolve.
- The existence of a collaboratively developed wording branch provides at least a tangible starting point for implementation.
- The claim that cataloging undefined and IFNDR behavior is useful is asserted without evidence of demand, use, or consequence.
- The paper never addresses who is affected, why the standard is the right vehicle, or what implementation experience has shown.
