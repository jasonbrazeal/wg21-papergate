Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow, name-focused rationale for its proposal, leaving most of the evidentiary burden for standardization unaddressed. Its support is thinnest in areas that typically matter most to committee review, such as affected users, implementation experience, and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The paper grounds its naming argument in specific prior art from the `std::execution` namespace and the recent addition of `std::execution::task`.
- It offers no discussion of who would be affected by the change or what the practical impact on existing code would be.
- It does not explain why the standard is the right venue rather than a library-level solution.
- It provides no implementation experience or evidence of real-world use to support the proposed change.
