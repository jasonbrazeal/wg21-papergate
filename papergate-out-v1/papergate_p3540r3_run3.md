Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a thin, mostly asserted case for standardization, with its strongest support resting on existing implementation practice and prior art rather than on demonstrated need or user impact. The thinnest areas are the absence of any evidence for the claimed popularity and the lack of discussion about why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The paper is most concrete when describing alignment with existing `gnu::offset` and `clang::offset` behavior and passing implementation tests.
- It asserts extreme popularity and existing implementation as the core rationale, but provides no usage data, user reports, or ecosystem evidence to support that claim.
- The paper does not address why a library-based approach would not satisfy the need, leaving a key standardization question unanswered.
