Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow foundation for its standardization case, resting almost entirely on a reference to existing wording and an implementation link, while leaving most of the persuasive burden unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns the core rationale: why the standard itself needs this change and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support is the existence of prior wording in N5032 and an implementation in the Beman Project, which at least grounds the proposal in concrete artifacts.
- The paper asserts that the current inconsistency forces users out of the Ranges world, but offers no example, user impact, or interoperability analysis to substantiate that claim.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why the standard should adopt this, leaving the proposal without a stated motivation tied to the standard’s goals or evolution.
