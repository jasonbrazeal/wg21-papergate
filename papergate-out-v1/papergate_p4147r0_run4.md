Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, preliminary case for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single motivating gap and a pointer to related work, while leaving most of the evidentiary burden unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution cannot suffice, which makes the proposal feel more like an early request for direction than a developed standardization argument.

- The strongest support is the concrete connection to P3771, which gives at least one plausible downstream use case for the proposed capability.
- The paper identifies a specific missing ability—detecting or modifying values across constant-evaluation boundaries—as its central motivation.
- The claim that a library solution will not work is asserted without supporting reasoning or examples.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, coordination with other work, or any implementation experience.
