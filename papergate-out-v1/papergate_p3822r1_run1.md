Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its proposal, chiefly through implementation experience and a pointer to prior syntax origins, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, why a library solution is insufficient, or how the feature fits with the broader standard.

- The strongest support is the existence of a Clang fork implementation, which demonstrates at least preliminary feasibility.
- The paper cites N3701 as the origin of the current unconditional syntax, giving a minimal anchor in prior art.
- The claim that code duplication is currently required is asserted without examples or elaboration, weakening the case for a language change over a library approach.
- The paper never addresses who is affected or why the standard is the right venue, leaving the motivating audience and standardization context unclear.
