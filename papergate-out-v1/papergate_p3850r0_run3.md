Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its standardization case, chiefly through references to prior analyses, existing wording, and implementation experience, but it leaves several important justifications unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected, why a library solution would be insufficient, and why the standard is the right venue.

- The strongest support comes from the cited prior work and a working GCC implementation, which give the proposal a tangible design and implementation foundation.
- The paper points to a comprehensive analysis of virtual function contract designs, lending some weight to the surrounding design space.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this cannot be achieved as a library outside the standard.
