Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, with only a single specific reference to prior art and no discussion of the motivating problem, affected users, implementation experience, or why the standard is the right venue. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of rationale for changing the standard and the lack of any evidence that the change is needed or feasible in practice.

- The only substantive support is a pointer to CWG 3112 as prior art.
- The paper does not explain why the issue matters or who is affected by it.
- It offers no implementation experience or evidence of real-world use.
- It never addresses why the standard, rather than a library or other mechanism, is the appropriate place for the change.
