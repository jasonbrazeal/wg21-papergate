Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization case, particularly through concrete implementation comparisons and a clear argument that existing latitude serves no legitimate purpose. The support is thinnest around the absence of any discussion of why a library-level solution would be insufficient, which leaves a noticeable gap in the rationale for changing the standard itself.

- The strongest support comes from the specific table showing divergent major standard library implementations, grounding the problem in observable practice rather than hypothetical concern.
- The paper also makes a pointed case that the remaining implementation freedom can only be used for user-hostile behavior, which directly supports the need for normative tightening.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any consideration of non-standard alternatives, such as a library workaround or guidance, leaving the necessity of a core language or standard library change underexplored.
