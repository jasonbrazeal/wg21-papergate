The paper offers some grounding for its proposal through implementation experience and an explanation of the API inconsistency it wants to fix, but it leaves several central questions about users, standardization need, and interoperability largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who is actually affected and why existing library mechanisms cannot address the problem.

- The most glaring omission is any account of who is affected by the current API inconsistency, leaving the motivating user need abstract.
