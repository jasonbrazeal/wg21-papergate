Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin, mostly definitional basis for standardization, and its support is concentrated in a few asserted motivations rather than demonstrated need. The thinnest areas are the absence of any affected-user analysis, any reason the standard is the right venue, any interoperability or coordination discussion, and any implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the paper’s stated motivation that users misunderstand what profile activation can and cannot do.
- The paper gestures at prior art and alternatives by naming dialecting, epochs, and subset-of-a-superset, but does not establish how this proposal relates to or improves on them.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the problem or why the standard, rather than a library or other mechanism, is needed.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of implementation experience, coordination, or interoperability evidence for standardizing the proposed framework.
