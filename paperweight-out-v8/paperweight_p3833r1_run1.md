The paper offers meaningful support in the areas of motivation, prior art, and implementation experience, but its case is uneven: it does not establish who is affected, how the facility would coordinate with existing practice, or why the functionality cannot be adequately provided by a library. The thinnest parts concern the necessity of standardization itself and the absence of evidence about users or interoperability.

- The discussion of prior art and alternatives credibly shows that existing standard facilities and proposals do not combine multi-mutex support with the full flexibility of `unique_lock`.
- The paper claims, but does not establish, that the variadic template design and interface consistency justify standardization rather than a library solution.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any established audience, use case, or affected-user evidence, alongside silence on coordination and interoperability.
