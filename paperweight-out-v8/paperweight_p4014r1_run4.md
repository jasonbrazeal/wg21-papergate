The paper offers meaningful support for its standardization in the areas of motivation, prior art, and implementation experience, but it leaves several essential parts of the case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support concerns who is affected, while the arguments for why the standard, why a library will not do, and coordination and interoperability are present only as claims.
