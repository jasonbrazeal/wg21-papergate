Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers solid grounding in prior work, implementation experience, and the coordination story for integrating `assert` with a shared contract-violation handler, but its case thins considerably when it comes to showing who is concretely affected and why only a standard can provide the proposed hooks.

- The strongest support comes from the demonstrated implementations in both libc++ and libstdc++, including publicly available Compiler Explorer links.
- The paper also establishes meaningful prior art and alternatives, particularly by distinguishing its ABI choice from P3311R0 and acknowledging the semantic differences among legacy assertion facilities.
- The weakest part of the case is the absence of any established description of who is affected, leaving the motivating audience for the migration path largely implicit.
- The claims that standardization is necessary and that a library solution will not suffice are asserted with only a single sentence about “associated drawbacks,” without enough detail to establish the point.
