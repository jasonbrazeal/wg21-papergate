Verdict: Strong to Excellent (10/14)

The paper gives a reasonably solid account of existing practice and real-world demand, but its argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than remaining a widely shared library type is noticeably thinner. The strongest material concerns implementation experience and interoperability, while the weakest concerns the necessity of standardization itself.

- The paper convincingly shows widespread, active use of cstring_view and zstring_view across major and minor codebases, including independent implementations with nearly identical features.
- It establishes clear prior art and a concrete interoperability need through frequent interaction with C-style null-terminated APIs and standard library calls.
- It does not adequately establish why a standard type is needed when the proposal itself acknowledges that many developers already use custom types successfully.
- The claim that the type is a “lingua franca” and therefore belongs in the standard is asserted rather than supported with evidence of problems that only standardization would solve.
