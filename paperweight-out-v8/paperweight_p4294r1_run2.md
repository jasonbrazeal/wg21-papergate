Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some support for its proposal, mainly through a concrete implementation and a clear statement of the gap it aims to fill, but it leaves the central question of why standardization is necessary almost entirely unaddressed. The thinnest parts are the absence of any argument that a library solution would be insufficient, and the lack of discussion about how the proposed views would coordinate with existing range machinery.

- The strongest support is the author’s implementation of `views::take_last` and `views::drop_last`, which demonstrates that the design is at least workable in practice.
- The paper clearly explains why the operation matters: there is no direct suffix-oriented adaptor, and input-only non-sized ranges cannot support it without buffering.
- The discussion of prior art is only partially convincing, since it points to `take`/`drop` as a model and lists other languages but does not establish what alternatives were seriously considered.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of a case for why this cannot be done as a library, which leaves the need for standardization unsupported.
