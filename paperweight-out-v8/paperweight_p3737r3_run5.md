The paper offers solid support in a few narrow areas—particularly implementation experience and the existence of divergent prior art—but it leaves several core justifications for standardization asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest parts concern who is actually affected and why the change cannot be handled outside the standard.

- The case for why this belongs in the standard, rather than remaining an implementation detail or being addressed by a library, is asserted but not backed by argument or evidence.
