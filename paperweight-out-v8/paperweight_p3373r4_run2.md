Verdict: Adequate to Strong (8/14)

The paper offers solid evidence of implementation experience and some useful engagement with prior art, but it leaves several central justifications—especially why the problem matters, who is affected, and why a library solution is insufficient—more asserted than demonstrated. The strongest support is practical, while the thinnest is the argument that standardization is necessary at all.

- The paper clearly documents working implementations in libunifex and stdexec, which grounds the proposal in real experience.
- It identifies relevant prior art and alternatives, including reference-implementation behavior and existing library practice.
- It does not convincingly establish who is affected beyond the authors of those libraries, nor why users cannot address the issue through a library.
- The most glaring omission is a clear, substantiated explanation of why the lifetime-model problem matters enough to require a standard change.
