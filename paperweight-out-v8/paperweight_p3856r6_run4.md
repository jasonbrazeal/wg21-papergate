Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers a clear motivation for exposing a structural-type query, but its case for standardization remains thin in most other respects. The strongest support is concentrated in the opening rationale, while the arguments about prior art, the need for a standard facility, and implementation experience are asserted rather than demonstrated, and several essential areas are simply not addressed.

- The paper establishes why a structural-type query matters by pointing to a concrete gap in NTTP and constant-evaluation use cases.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives gestures toward a sample implementation and the promise of richer reflection APIs, but does not actually establish that existing techniques are insufficient.
- The claim that library mandates imply the functionality must exist but is hidden from users is stated without evidence connecting that hidden capability to the proposed query.
- The paper offers no assessment of who is affected, no coordination or interoperability analysis, and no argument for why a library solution would not suffice.
