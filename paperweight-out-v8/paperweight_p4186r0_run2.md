Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a general assertion that profiles are currently underdocumented and that past efforts have not succeeded. Beyond that, it does not establish who is affected, why standardization is the right venue, how the work would coordinate with existing features, why a library solution is insufficient, or whether there is any implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the observation that past profile-related proposals have appeared without success, which at least gestures toward prior art.
- The paper asserts that a lack of agreed documentation is causing confusion, but it does not demonstrate that this confusion is widespread or consequential.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence about how the proposed standardization would work in practice.
