Verdict: Adequate (4/14)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization case, centered on the problems identified with `affine_on` and the existence of prior discussion and related proposals. Beyond that, the argument is largely undeveloped: the affected audience, the need for a standard rather than a library solution, coordination with existing specifications, and implementation experience are all left unaddressed or merely asserted.

- The strongest support is the documented record of concerns and NB comments about `affine_on`, which shows the current design is problematic and motivates a change.
- The paper also points to prior art in P3718R0 and the original `task` proposal, indicating the direction has been considered before.
- The thinnest part of the case is the absence of any established implementation experience, with the only relevant statement being a vague claim about one existing library.
- Most glaringly, the paper does not establish who is affected, why a library cannot provide the functionality, or how the proposal coordinates with the broader standard.
