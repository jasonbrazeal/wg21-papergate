Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers only a thin, preliminary rationale for standardization, mostly gesturing at confusion around profile terminology and the authors’ own framing of what profiles can and cannot do. The support is thinnest where a proposal would normally need concrete evidence: affected users, prior practice, implementation experience, and a clear reason the work belongs in the standard rather than in guidance or library form.

- The strongest support is the observation that users commonly misunderstand what activating a profile can do, which at least suggests a documentation or specification gap.
- The paper claims to define profiles’ limits, but it does not establish that this definition needs to be normative or standardized.
- The discussion of alternatives is mostly a list of terms and possibilities, without showing why this particular approach is the right one.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience or evidence about who is affected, leaving the standardization need largely asserted rather than demonstrated.
