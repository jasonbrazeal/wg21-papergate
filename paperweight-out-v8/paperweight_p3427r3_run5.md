Verdict: Adequate to Strong (8/14)

The paper offers solid grounding for its core motivation and for the existence of a production-tested implementation, but it leaves several parts of the standardization argument asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the positive case for putting this in the standard, how it would coexist with related facilities, and why existing library-level approaches cannot remain sufficient.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with Folly’s `hazptr_obj_cohort` in heavy production use since 2018.
- The paper also establishes why the feature matters by connecting synchronous reclamation to general-purpose usability and performance-sensitive cases.
- Prior art and alternatives are adequately established through the Folly reference and the comparison with the C++26 hazard pointer interface.
- The most glaring omission is coordination and interoperability, for which the paper provides no substantive discussion.
