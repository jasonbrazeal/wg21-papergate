Verdict: Adequate to Strong (8/14)

The paper offers meaningful support for standardizing object cohorts through its record of production use in Folly and its explanation of why the feature matters for performance-sensitive code, but the case is uneven: several essential arguments are asserted rather than demonstrated, and one is absent entirely.

- The strongest support is the implementation experience, with object cohorts in production use under the name `hazptr_obj_cohort` since 2018.
- The paper also establishes why the feature matters by explaining how cohorts avoid the impractical overhead of global cleanup in performance-sensitive cases.
- The arguments for why a library will not do and who is affected are only claimed, resting on assertions about efficiency and production use without further substantiation.
- The most glaring omission is coordination and interoperability, for which the paper offers no discussion at all.
