Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a clear reason why a general `mdspan` copy operation would be useful, but beyond that motivation it mostly asserts rather than demonstrates the need for standardization. The thinnest parts are the absence of any identified audience and the lack of concrete evidence for alternatives, implementation experience, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the established motivation that copying between `mdspan`s with complex layouts is difficult for users without standard library help.
- The paper claims prior art by noting that `std::linalg::copy` is limited to rank two or lower, but it does not establish how that limitation actually fails in practice.
- The paper repeatedly asserts that existing standard library facilities are insufficient, but it does not show why a non-standard library could not fill the gap.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper never identifies who is affected by the problem.
