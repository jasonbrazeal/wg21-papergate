The paper’s strongest, most concrete support comes from implementation experience, where it can point to working code in libunifex, stdexec, and a reference implementation. Beyond that, the case for standardization rests heavily on repeated assertions about existing practice rather than demonstrated need, impact, or interoperability. The thinnest areas are the arguments that a library solution is insufficient and that the standard itself must change.

- The paper offers no substantive argument for why a library cannot address the issue, making that a glaring omission in the standardization case.
