Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a narrow but genuine reason to consider standardization: it establishes that efficient copying between `mdspan`s with complex layouts is a real challenge for users. Beyond that single point, however, the case is largely asserted rather than demonstrated, with the thinnest support around who is affected and what alternatives exist.

- The strongest support is the established claim that users face difficulty copying efficiently between `mdspan`s with mixed complex layouts without standard library help.
- The paper claims relevance to HPC, image processing, and graphics, but does not establish who specifically is affected or how widespread the need is.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is only claimed, leaving unclear how the proposed facility meaningfully differs from or improves upon existing options like `std::linalg::copy`.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established implementation experience or evidence that a library-only solution would be insufficient.
