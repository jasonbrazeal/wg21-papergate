Verdict: Adequate (7/14)

The paper gives a reasonably clear account of the functional gap it wants to fill and points to a concrete implementation, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case largely implicit, especially around the affected audience and how the feature would fit with existing practice.

- The strongest support is the explanation of why the missing combination of multi-mutex ownership with `unique_lock`-style flexibility matters in practice.
- The paper also establishes relevant prior art and alternatives, including the relationship to `std::scoped_lock` and the proposed free functions in P3832.
- The thinnest parts are the absence of any identified user population and the lack of discussion about coordination or interoperability with related library components.
- The argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library is asserted mainly through type-safety and optimization claims, but not developed into a compelling standardization rationale.
