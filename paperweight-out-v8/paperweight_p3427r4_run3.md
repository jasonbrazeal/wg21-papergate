The thinnest support is around why standardization is necessary rather than a library solution, and around who specifically benefits beyond a general performance claim.

- The strongest support is the established implementation experience, with Folly’s `hazptr_obj_cohort` in heavy production use since 2018.
- The paper also establishes prior art and alternatives by showing concrete code comparisons with the P2530R3 interface.
- A notable gap is that the affected user population is only claimed through the same production-use sentence and a broad performance assertion, without further evidence of who needs this in the standard.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of an established case for why a library cannot provide this, since the efficiency and overhead arguments are stated but not substantiated.
