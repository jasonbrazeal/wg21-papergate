Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers meaningful support in a few narrow areas, chiefly by identifying a real gap in error handling and by situating its approach against prior work, but it leaves large parts of the standardization case unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the proposal would coordinate with existing facilities.

- The strongest support is the identification of a genuine deficiency in error propagation under the work framing, where behavior is implementation-defined and no channel exists.
- The paper also establishes that coroutine-native I/O and `std::execution` are complementary, and it grounds its alternative in a documented prior-art trail.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any established account of who is affected by the problem or why standardization, rather than a library solution, is necessary.
