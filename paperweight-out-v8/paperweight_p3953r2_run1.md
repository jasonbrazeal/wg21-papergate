Verdict: Weak (3/14)

The paper offers a narrow but genuine foundation for its case, centered on the semantic mismatch introduced by `constexpr` evaluation and the precedent of existing terminology. Beyond that, however, the support is largely absent: it does not identify affected users, explain why the standard is the right venue, address coordination or interoperability, rule out a library-level solution, or report any implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the established rationale that `std::runtime_format` has become misleading now that it can be evaluated at compile time.
- The paper also establishes relevant prior art by connecting the proposed name to existing terminology such as dynamic format specifiers.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected by the current name or the proposed change.
