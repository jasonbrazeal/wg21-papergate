The paper offers only a narrow basis for its own standardization: it can point to consistency with prior design-approved work and to an existing analogous operation, but it does not develop a case around user impact, the need for a standard facility, or practical experience. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The paper also establishes that `std::simd::bit_repeat` is analogous to `std::simd::rotl`, giving it a recognizable place in the existing interface.
- The paper does not establish who is affected by the proposed overloads or what problem they would solve for those users.
- Most glaringly, the paper never explains why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library, nor does it offer any implementation experience to support standardization.
