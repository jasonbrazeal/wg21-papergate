The paper offers only a thin, largely asserted case for its own standardization, with most of the burden carried by a single design rationale and little evidence about users, alternatives, or implementability. The support is thinnest where the proposal needs to show that the problem is real for a definable audience and that the standard is the only viable place to solve it.

- The paper does not identify who is affected by the current design or how widespread the concern is.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, coordination discussion, or argument for why a library solution would not suffice.
