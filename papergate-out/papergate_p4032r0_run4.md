Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow, convenience-based rationale for standardizing direct comparison of `meta::info`, and much of its argument is asserted rather than demonstrated. The strongest support appears in the discussion of prior art, while the sections on affected users, standardization need, and implementation experience are essentially undeveloped.

- The paper grounds its proposal in existing work by citing P2830R10’s `type_order` as a related mechanism.
- The motivating example of sorting reflection values with standard algorithms is concrete but repeated without elaboration across several required justification areas.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the change or what real-world code would benefit.
- The author states there is no implementation experience for the proposed built-in comparison, leaving the feasibility and design unverified.
