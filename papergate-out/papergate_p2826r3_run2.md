The thinnest support concerns the core motivation and the claimed implementation experience, both of which are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the discussion of why a library solution is insufficient, which is tied to a specific technical consequence about expression aliases and instantiation.
- Coordination with existing practice is also grounded, particularly through the example of the standard library’s change to `operator>>` for character arrays.
- Prior art is acknowledged with a named alternative and a brief explanation of its limitation.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any substantive account of why the problem matters or who is affected, leaving the proposal’s urgency and audience unclear.
