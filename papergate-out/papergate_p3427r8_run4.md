Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper leans heavily on one piece of evidence—the production use of object cohorts in Folly since 2018—to support nearly every aspect of its case, which gives it real-world credibility but leaves the argument for standardization itself largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest part is the direct justification for why this belongs in the C++ standard rather than remaining a widely used library facility.

- The strongest support is the concrete, dated production deployment in Folly, which substantiates implementation experience, prior art, and real-world impact.
- The paper also offers a specific technical motivation by connecting object cohorts to more generally usable concurrent hash maps with arbitrary key and value types.
- The most glaring omission is any substantive reasoning for standardization itself, beyond a bare recommendation with no supporting argument.
