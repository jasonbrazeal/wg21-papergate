Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper grounds its most concrete claims in implementation experience and prior art, but it leaves the central rationale for standardization largely asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears wherever the document says that standard library types “would benefit” without naming them, quantifying the benefit, or explaining why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from the author’s deployed implementation and the availability of the functionality through beman.emplace_from, which shows practical viability.
- The discussion of prior art is specific enough to situate the proposal against an earlier core-language approach.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why a library-only solution cannot address the stated problem, leaving the need for standardization unestablished.
