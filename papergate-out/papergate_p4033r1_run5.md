Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives concrete motivation for the problem and shows some awareness of related work, but it does not build a complete case for standardization because several core sections are left unaddressed or merely asserted. The strongest material concerns real-world breakage and interoperability, while the weakest concerns why a library solution is insufficient and who would actually be affected.

- The clearest support comes from the concrete example of silent breakage when variant alternatives are inserted or reordered.
- The interoperability discussion is grounded in a specific industry format and its versioning behavior.
- The paper asserts that reflection facilities are too limited for a library solution but offers no supporting detail or demonstration.
- The affected audience, implementation experience, and rationale for standardization rather than a library are entirely absent.
