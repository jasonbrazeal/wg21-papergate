Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper provides a reasonably well-supported case for standardization, with concrete implementation experience and specific discussion of interoperability and prior art. The thinnest support is in motivating the affected audience, where broad claims about the popularity of string interpolation are asserted without evidence.

- The strongest support is the reported Clang implementation, which demonstrates that the design has been worked through in practice rather than only sketched.
- The discussion of prior art and coordination with P3412R3 is specific about trade-offs and why this approach avoids foreclosing future language changes.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported claim that string interpolation is “wildly popular,” leaving the affected-user case asserted rather than demonstrated.
