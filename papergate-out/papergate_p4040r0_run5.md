Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of existing practice and the portability benefits of standardizing case ranges, but it leans heavily on the same implementation-history point and never engages with the possibility of a non-language solution. The support is thinnest where the proposal should justify why this must be a core language feature rather than something expressible through existing or library mechanisms.

- The strongest support is the documented, long-standing implementation experience in GCC and Clang, which grounds the proposal in real-world usage.
- The paper also makes a clear interoperability argument, noting that standardization would ease porting between C and C++.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion of why a library-based or alternative language approach would be insufficient.
