Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers a narrow but concrete case for standardization, grounding its motivation in observable behavioral differences and citing at least one real-world implementation workaround. The support is thinnest where it relies on the same single example to carry multiple distinct argumentative burdens, leaving the reader to infer broader relevance.

- The strongest support is the specific, observable side effect of creating an `inplace_stop_source`, which gives the proposal a clear behavioral anchor.
- The reference to an implementation in the wild that treats `when_all(s)` as equivalent to `s` provides useful evidence of practical friction.
- The mention of implementation against nVidia’s reference implementation suggests at least preliminary validation of feasibility.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of distinct evidence for standardization need, alternatives, and coordination beyond the same repeated example, making the case feel underdeveloped in scope.
