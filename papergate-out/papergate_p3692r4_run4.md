Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a partial but uneven account of why its requested normative change should be adopted, with the strongest material going to prior art and the minimal burden on implementers, while several standard review dimensions are left entirely unaddressed. The thinnest parts concern evidence of real-world implementation experience and any discussion of affected users, library alternatives, or interoperability.

- The paper grounds its core claim in specific prior work and explains why the change would require no implementation or user-code action.
- The discussion of implementation experience asserts that compiler-based implementations freeze nondeterministic choices into executables, but offers no supporting evidence or examples.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change or how it coordinates with existing practice and adjacent standards.
- It also omits any explanation of why a library-only solution would be insufficient.
