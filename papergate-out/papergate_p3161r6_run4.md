Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper makes a reasonably concrete case that the proposed functionality is difficult to express portably and efficiently in today’s C++, and it points to accepted prior work as evidence that the general area is already moving through the committee. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the precise shape of the proposed facility, leaving the reader to infer much of the motivation and scope.

- The strongest support comes from the discussion of prior art and implementation experience, which ties the proposal to an accepted paper and to widely available hardware support.
- The paper also explains clearly why a portable third-party library would struggle to achieve the same efficiency as a standardized feature.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any description of who is affected or what concrete use cases would benefit, beyond a general mention of wide integer arithmetic.
