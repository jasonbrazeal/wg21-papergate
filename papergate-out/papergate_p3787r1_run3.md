Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides a narrow but concrete rationale for its change, anchored in a specific prior standards decision, but it leaves most of the case for standardization implicit rather than demonstrated. The strongest support is the direct link to P2248R8 and the claim of existing implementations, while the thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, alternatives, or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The paper clearly identifies the omission relative to the adopted P2248R8 and explains why consistency with that decision matters.
- The mention of implementations already shipping with P2248R8 offers some, though unsubstantiated, evidence of practical uptake.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change or what the practical consequences of the oversight are.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of why this cannot be handled outside the standard or what alternatives were considered.
