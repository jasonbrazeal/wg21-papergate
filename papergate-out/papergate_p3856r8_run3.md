Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives concrete evidence for implementation feasibility and notes a real gap in existing reflection facilities, but it does not build a full case for standardization because it leaves the affected audience, coordination concerns, and the necessity of a standard solution largely unexamined.

- The strongest support is the specific implementation experience using Bloomberg’s Clang fork, which shows the proposed functionality is technically achievable.
- The paper also grounds its motivation in the existing precedent of P2996’s reflection metafunctions, giving readers a clear point of reference.
- The thinnest support is the claim that library mandates imply the functionality must already exist, which is asserted rather than demonstrated as a reason users need a standardized query.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of who is affected or how this would coordinate with related standardization efforts.
