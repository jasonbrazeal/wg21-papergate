Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper leans heavily on the author’s own judgment and a single expression of mailing-list support, but it does not build a case that the proposed restriction is necessary or ready for standardization. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, implementation experience, or why a library-level solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from a concrete example intended to show that exposing certain constructors provides no observable value.
- The paper cites one supportive comment from an SG9 mailing list participant as evidence of direction and momentum.
- The argument for changing the standard rests on assertion rather than demonstrated need or benefit.
- The paper does not address who would be affected, how implementations would handle the change, or whether a non-standard solution could achieve the same goal.
