Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a reasonably concrete case for its own standardization, with specific references to the working draft’s wording, compiler-only enforceability, and the author’s implementation experience. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the broader design context, leaving the reader to infer who benefits and how this fits with existing practice.

- The strongest support comes from the precise identification of the normative restriction and why only a compiler can address it.
- The author’s implementation experience and the worked example of promise-type mapping give the proposal practical grounding.
- Prior art is acknowledged through a reference to an earlier paper with the same goal, though without much comparison.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the current restriction or how widespread the need is.
