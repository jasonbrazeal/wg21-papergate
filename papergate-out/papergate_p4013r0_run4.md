Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, resting almost entirely on the existence of an implementation while leaving the motivating problem, affected users, alternatives, and standardization rationale unexamined. The thinnest support is the unsupported assertion that a library facility cannot suffice, which is especially conspicuous given the paper’s own reference to `std::any`.

- The strongest support is the availability of concrete implementation experience, with code linked for inspection.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or who would be affected by its absence.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, or coordination and interoperability concerns.
- The most glaring omission is the claim that a library solution will not do, which is asserted without any supporting argument.
