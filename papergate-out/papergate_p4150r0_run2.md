Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper backs its standardization case with concrete examples from existing practice, affected users, and implementation experience, though the support is uneven in places where it gestures at problems rather than demonstrating them. The thinnest area is the argument that a library solution cannot suffice, which relies on a general claim about lost multidimensional information without showing how that loss blocks realistic use cases.

- The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience in CUB and Kokkos, showing that the proposed facility already exists in recognizable form.
- The paper gives specific, credible examples of affected users and interoperability limits in current practice.
- The most glaring omission is a detailed demonstration of why a library-only approach would fail, beyond the general statement that ranges make views opaque.
