Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with concrete comparisons to existing practice but little evidence that the proposed facility belongs in the standard rather than in a library. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of affected users, interoperability, or implementation experience beyond a bare link.

- The strongest support comes from the specific contrast with `views::drop` and `views::take`, which grounds the motivation in a recognizable readability problem.
- The mention of range/v3’s `views::slice` provides at least one concrete point of prior art, though it is not developed into a case for standardization.
- The claim that `views::slice` aligns C++ with other languages is asserted without examples or explanation, leaving the “why the standard” argument unsupported.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of who is affected or how the proposal coordinates with existing range facilities, leaving the standardization rationale largely unexamined.
