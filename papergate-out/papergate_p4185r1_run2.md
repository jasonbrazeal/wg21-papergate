Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper gives a reasonably grounded account of the problem and of implementation experience, but its case for standardization leans heavily on the reference implementation and community feedback rather than on a demonstrated need for a standard beyond what a library can already provide. The thinnest support appears where the paper asserts uniqueness or necessity without connecting that claim to a concrete standardization rationale.

- The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience in mp-units and Au, with specific features identified as already deployed or design-complete.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is specific about trade-offs, which helps frame the design space.
- The claim that no mainstream units library distinguishes absolute quantities, deltas, and points is repeated in two different sections without being tied to why standardization is required.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any supported argument for why a library solution is insufficient, since the same uniqueness claim is asserted rather than demonstrated.
