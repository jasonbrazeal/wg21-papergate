Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper makes a strong, concrete case for standardization by tying the feature to real-world safety failures, production feedback, and a clear gap in existing vocabulary types. The support is thinnest where it relies on anecdotal or external claims rather than showing how the proposed design itself directly addresses those failures in standardese or committee-facing detail.

- The strongest support comes from specific, named prior art and its divergent or failing behavior on the same example, which makes the need for a single standard answer vivid.
- The audience analysis and implementation experience give the proposal a practical grounding that many library papers lack.
- The most glaring omission is any substantive discussion of how the proposed facility would coordinate with existing or forthcoming standard library components beyond a brief critique of `std::ratio`.
