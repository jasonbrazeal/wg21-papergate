Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides only a narrow foundation for its standardization, anchored in a concrete implementation divergence and a reference to one related proposal, but it leaves most of the case unstated. The support is thinnest around motivation beyond defect resolution, affected users, and why a standards change is the necessary remedy.

- The clearest support comes from the cited national body issue and the observed GCC-versus-Clang/MSVC split, which grounds the need in real divergence.
- The discussion of prior art is limited but at least engages with a directly relevant paper on default function arguments and immediate context.
- The paper does not explain who is affected by the lack of a definition or what practical consequences follow for ordinary C++ users.
- It omits any argument for why the standard is the right place to address this, or why a library-level or non-normative clarification would be insufficient.
