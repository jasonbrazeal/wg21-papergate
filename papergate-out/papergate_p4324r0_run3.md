Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization: it grounds the design in prior proposals and provides a concrete implementation, but it does not explain why the feature belongs in the standard, who would be affected, or how it would interoperate with existing practice. The thinnest support is around motivation and necessity, where the paper asserts committee interest and implementation-defined possibilities without building a case for normative action.

- The strongest support is the cited implementation experience in a GCC fork, which at least shows the approach is technically realizable.
- The paper also engages with prior art and review feedback, giving the proposal some continuity with existing committee work.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why a library solution would not suffice or why the standard is the right venue.
