Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper provides almost no substantive support for its own standardization, offering only a brief motivation and an unsupported claim of implementability. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, prior art, coordination with related proposals, or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the paper’s clear statement that it aims to complete the pack indexing design begun in P2662R3.
- The paper acknowledges related proposals but does not analyze how they interact with or affect this work.
- The claim of implementation confidence is asserted without any supporting evidence or experience.
- The paper entirely omits discussion of who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the feature interoperates with existing or in-flight language features.
