Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a reasonably grounded case for its own standardization, with the strongest support concentrated in its discussion of prior art, implementation experience, and the need for an orthogonal language feature rather than a library solution. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected and a somewhat narrow framing of coordination and interoperability that mostly restates the primary motivation rather than exploring broader ecosystem impacts.

- The paper gives concrete implementation and wording history through references to P2806R3, showing the feature has been refined in practice.
- The argument for a language feature over a library is supported by specific shortcomings identified in existing extensions.
- The motivation is tied to concrete use cases such as macro definitions and desugaring the control flow operator from P2561R2.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the proposal, leaving the audience and impact scope unclear.
