Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, relying heavily on assertion rather than demonstration of need, impact, or practical viability. Its strongest support lies in the concrete reference to collaborative drafting and the specific discussion of prior structural alternatives, but the absence of any treatment of affected users, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be inadequate leaves the case largely unbuilt.

- The paper gives a specific, grounded account of prior annex-structure approaches and their maintenance tradeoffs.
- It points to a concrete collaborative drafting branch, which at least indicates some implementation-adjacent activity.
- It never addresses who is affected by the proposed change or what problem they concretely face.
- It offers no argument for why standardization, rather than a library or separate guidance document, is the right vehicle.
