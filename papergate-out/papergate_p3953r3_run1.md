Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization, grounding its motivation in a genuine semantic mismatch but leaving most of the evidentiary burden unaddressed. The support is thinnest around who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether the change is implementable or already proven in practice.

- The strongest support is the concrete identification of a naming and conceptual mismatch between `std::runtime_format` and its compile-time usability.
- The paper situates the issue within recent standard evolution by citing P2918 and P3391, showing awareness of the surrounding design history.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of discussion about affected users, implementation experience, or why a library-level solution would not suffice.
