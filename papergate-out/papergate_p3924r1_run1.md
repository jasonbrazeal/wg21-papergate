Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, grounding its relevance in a specific NB comment but leaving most of the surrounding case unstated. The thinnest support is in the areas that would show the proposal is ready for the working draft: affected users, alternatives, implementation experience, and why a library cannot solve the problem.

- The paper’s strongest support is its citation of NB comment US 11-400, which gives a concrete, standards-process reason for the work.
- The paper points to CWG3132 as the separate venue for prior art and alternatives, but does not itself summarize that context for a reader.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the issue or what practical impact the change would have.
- The paper offers no implementation experience, no discussion of coordination or interoperability, and no explanation of why a library solution would be insufficient.
