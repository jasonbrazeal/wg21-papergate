Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper offers a reasonably specific case for standardization, particularly by quantifying the prevalence of the affected idiom and by demonstrating that a library-only solution cannot cover expression-only contexts. Its support is thinnest around prior art and coordination, where the discussion remains largely unaddressed rather than merely underdeveloped.

- The strongest support comes from concrete usage data and a proof-of-concept implementation verified across GCC, Clang, and MSVC.
- The paper clearly explains why the standard library, rather than user code or third-party libraries, must provide the extension mechanism.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any treatment of prior art and alternative approaches.
