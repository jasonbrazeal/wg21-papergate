Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on prior art and a collaborative drafting branch while leaving the motivating problem, affected audience, and rationale for a standardese annex largely unargued. The thinnest support is in the sections that would normally justify why the standard is the right vehicle and why a library cannot suffice, both of which are absent.

- The strongest support is the citation of earlier related efforts and prior committee interest in formalizing undefined behavior and IFNDR data.
- The claim that the annex wording was developed collaboratively as a branch of the working draft is asserted without evidence of broader implementation experience or review.
- The paper does not address who is affected or why the standard is the appropriate place for this information.
- It also omits any discussion of why a library or non-normative reference would not meet the stated need.
