Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives a partial account of why these saturating operations belong in the standard, but it leaves several core justifications as bare assertions rather than argued positions. The strongest material concerns prior work and implementation experience, while the case for standardization itself is notably underdeveloped.

- The paper grounds its proposal in prior art by citing P0543R3 and confirms practical viability through implementation in Intel’s reference implementation and software products.
- The explanation of saturating arithmetic semantics is concrete and clearly tied to the proposed behavior.
- The claim that these operations are the most common saturating operations is asserted without evidence or usage context.
- The paper never explains why a library solution would be insufficient or how the proposal would coordinate with existing or planned interfaces.
