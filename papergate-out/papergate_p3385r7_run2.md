Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with a few concrete references but little evidence that the proposal has been tested, coordinated, or justified against non-standard alternatives. The thinnest areas are those that would normally establish readiness for the committee: implementation experience, affected users, and the rationale for standardizing rather than using a library.

- The strongest support is the specific prior art, including the recommendation to merge P3678R0 following SG7 guidance.
- The claim that the features are available on Compiler Explorer is asserted without details about usage, maturity, or feedback.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why a library solution would not suffice, or how the feature would coordinate with existing practice.
- It also omits any discussion of why standardization is the right venue, leaving the core motivation incomplete.
