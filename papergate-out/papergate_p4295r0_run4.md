Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only partial support for its own standardization, with a useful historical reference and a link to an implementation but little else to establish the need for a standard facility. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the proposal would coordinate with existing or planned concurrency features.

- The strongest support is the citation of prior art showing that single-ended concurrent queue interfaces were considered in an earlier proposal and deliberately separated by SG1.
- The paper offers an asserted partial implementation, though it provides no details about completeness, usage, or lessons learned.
- The paper does not address why a standard library facility is needed rather than a user-space or third-party library.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of affected users, coordination with other standardization efforts, or implementation experience beyond a bare link.
