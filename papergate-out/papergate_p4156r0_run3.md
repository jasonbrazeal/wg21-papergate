Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper provides almost no affirmative case for standardization, leaving most of the questions a reviewer would ask entirely unaddressed. Its support is thinnest around motivation, affected users, and any evidence that the proposed change is needed or viable in practice.

- The only concrete reference offered is a link to CWG 3112, but the paper does not explain what that issue shows or how it supports the proposal.
- The paper asserts prior art and alternatives exist without describing them or comparing them to the proposed approach.
- It does not address why a library solution would be insufficient, nor does it mention any implementation experience.
- The paper never explains why the change matters, who is affected, or why it belongs in the standard rather than elsewhere.
