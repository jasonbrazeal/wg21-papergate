Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a skeletal justification for its own standardization, leaning on prior work and a collaborative drafting branch while leaving most of the rationale for standardizing this material unstated. The thinnest support is in the areas that would normally persuade a committee to act: who is affected, why the standard is the right home, and what practical experience shows.

- The strongest support is the citation of earlier related efforts, which at least situates the proposal within an ongoing conversation.
- The claim that stable names would aid future mitigation work is plausible but asserted without examples or evidence.
- The paper does not explain why the standard, rather than a separate reference or library, is the necessary vehicle.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users or implementation experience beyond a link to a draft branch.
