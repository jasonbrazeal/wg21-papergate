Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow basis for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on the observation that profiles lack agreed documentation and that prior attempts have failed. The thinnest support appears where the paper asserts profiles are “the best way” without argument, and where it leaves affected users, implementation experience, and interoperability entirely unexamined.

- The paper gives a specific, useful reason for action by pointing to the absence of agreed documentation about what profiles can and cannot do.
- It situates itself against several recent unsuccessful profile-related papers, showing awareness of prior attempts.
- The central claim that profiles are the best known approach is asserted rather than supported.
- The paper does not address who is affected, how profiles would coordinate with existing practice, or whether any implementation experience exists.
