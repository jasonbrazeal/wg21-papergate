Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow slice of the case for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single motivating example while leaving most evaluative dimensions unexamined. The support is thinnest around the practical and procedural questions that typically determine whether a proposal is ready for the committee.

- The strongest support is a concrete scenario showing how a library-only solution can fail when an exception allows nested tasks to outlive the scope and its data.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the problem or how broadly it arises in real code.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternative designs, or implementation experience.
- It does not explain why a language or standard-library change is necessary beyond the single example, nor how the proposal would coordinate with existing features.
