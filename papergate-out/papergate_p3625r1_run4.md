Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, relying on unsupported assertions of need and a handful of external links rather than sustained data, implementation experience, or a clear argument for why a library solution would be insufficient. The strongest material is its brief discussion of naming alternatives, but even that is limited to terminology rather than motivation.

- The paper gives specific reasons for rejecting alternative names such as `oneof` and `noneof`.
- It gestures toward prior usage through cppreference and Stack Overflow links, though without analyzing that evidence.
- It asserts the need has arisen across several projects but provides no details, examples, or measurable frequency.
- It does not address why the standard is the right venue, how the feature would interoperate with existing concepts, or why a library implementation would not suffice.
