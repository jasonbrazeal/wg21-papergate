Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper grounds its standardization case in concrete interactions with the C++26 contracts facility and ABI concerns, but it leaves several foundational justifications unstated. The strongest support appears in the discussion of prior art and the specific technical problem that a library solution cannot solve, while the thinnest areas concern who is affected and why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper offers specific reasoning about how `quick` `enforce` creates ABI constraints that only a standard mechanism can address.
- It situates the proposal within the existing contracts framework, showing coordination with current standardization direction.
- It does not explain who would be affected by the change or what practical problem they face.
- It never articulates why standardization, rather than another path, is necessary beyond the narrow ABI point.
