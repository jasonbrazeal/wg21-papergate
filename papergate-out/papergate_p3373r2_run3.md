Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization by grounding its proposed lifetime rule in existing practice, particularly the libunifex implementation, and by connecting the change to constraints already present in the Networking TS model. The support is thinnest when it comes to broader ecosystem impact and independent implementation experience beyond libunifex, leaving the case somewhat reliant on a single codebase.

- The strongest support comes from the repeated citation of libunifex’s existing use of the proposed lifetime management strategy, which suggests the change standardizes proven practice.
- The paper also ties the proposal to explicit requirements in the Networking TS, showing coordination with an established asynchronous model.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of evidence from implementations other than libunifex, which limits confidence that the rule reflects widespread existing practice.
