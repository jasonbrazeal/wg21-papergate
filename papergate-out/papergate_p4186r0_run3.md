Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its motivation and acknowledges prior unsuccessful attempts, but it does not build a case that standardization is the right next step. The thinnest support is around the central claim that profiles are the best available mechanism, which is asserted rather than argued, and around practical considerations like implementation experience or why a library solution would not suffice.

- The paper identifies a real documentation gap and supports that concern with specific references to recent profile-related proposals.
- It situates itself honestly within a history of unsuccessful efforts, which helps explain why the topic remains unresolved.
- It asserts that profiles are the best known approach without offering evidence or comparison to alternatives.
- It does not address implementation experience, coordination with other features, or why the work cannot be done outside the standard.
