Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper provides a reasonably well-supported case for standardization, with concrete implementation experience and prior art anchoring several of its central claims. The support is thinnest when it comes to demonstrating who is actually affected by the absence of a standard facility, since that section asserts rather than substantiates the breadth of the user base.

- The strongest support comes from the documented Boost.Context implementation experience and the list of higher-level libraries built on the proposed API.
- The discussion of why a library alone will not suffice is grounded in a specific, observable ABI and exception-handling discrepancy.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported claim about who is affected, which offers no evidence of demand or usage beyond the assertion itself.
