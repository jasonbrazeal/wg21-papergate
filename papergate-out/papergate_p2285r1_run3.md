Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper makes a reasonably specific case for standardization by tying its motivation to compiler divergence, an open core issue, and the stated purpose of SFINAE. The support is thinnest around the affected user population and the practical consequences of leaving the status quo unchanged, which are asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from linking the proposal to CWG2296 and a national body comment, grounding the change in existing standardization discussion.
- The paper also offers concrete implementation context, including vendor feedback and the connection between immediate context and separately instantiated entities.
- The most glaring omission is any real discussion of who is affected or what user-facing problems currently arise from the disagreement.
