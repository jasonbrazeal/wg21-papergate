Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper grounds its motivation in a concrete set of recently adopted reflection-related proposals and points to specific inconsistencies, gaps, and API mismatches that arose from their simultaneous adoption. Beyond that, however, it offers almost no supporting material for standardization: it does not identify affected users, discuss alternatives, explain why a library solution is insufficient, or report any implementation experience.

- The strongest support is the specific identification of adopted papers and the resulting inconsistencies the proposal aims to address.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the current inconsistencies or what practical problems they cause.
- Prior art and alternatives are mentioned only as the adopted papers themselves, with no discussion of other ways to resolve the inconsistencies.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, coordination considerations, or a rationale for why the fix belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
