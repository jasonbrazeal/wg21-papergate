Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides almost no direct support for its own standardization, offering only a brief theoretical framing and one concrete comparison to existing practice. The case is thinnest where it should be strongest: there is no discussion of why the standard is the right venue, why a library would not suffice, or whether anyone has actually implemented the design.

- The strongest support is the specific observation that `adjacent_vertices` from other libraries can be expressed through the proposed neighbor projection, which at least grounds one part of the design in known prior art.
- The paper asserts that it provides motivation and theoretical background, but offers nothing to substantiate that claim beyond the assertion itself.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience, with even the referenced code flagged as needing to be brought up to the proposal’s own specification.
