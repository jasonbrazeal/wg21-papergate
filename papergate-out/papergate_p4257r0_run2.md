Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case for standardization, grounding its relevance in a specific historical gap but leaving most of the evidentiary burden unaddressed. The strongest material concerns prior policy discussions, while the rationale for acting through the standard and the practical consequences for users remain largely absent.

- The paper provides a concrete account of the missing post-C++11 `noexcept` policy and the apparent consensus around marking wide-contract non-throwing functions.
- It does not identify who is affected by the absence of a policy or what problems they currently face.
- The paper offers no discussion of why this belongs in the standard rather than in library guidance or implementation practice.
- It contains no implementation experience, coordination considerations, or interoperability analysis to support adoption.
