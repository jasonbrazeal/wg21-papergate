Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its proposal through an implementation and a brief account of why existing range libraries lack the feature, but it leaves most of the standardization rationale unstated. The thinnest support is around the core questions of who benefits, why the standard is the right venue, and how the change fits with existing practice beyond the author’s own work.

- The strongest support is the existence of an implementation in the Beman Project, which at least shows the proposed interface is buildable.
- The paper identifies a plausible historical reason for the omission of `Searcher` overloads from major range libraries.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of why the feature matters or who is affected by its absence.
- The paper also does not explain why a library solution would be insufficient or why standardization is necessary.
