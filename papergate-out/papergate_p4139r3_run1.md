Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a mixed case for its own standardization, with concrete polling data and discussion of prior naming alternatives, but it leaves several important evidentiary gaps. The thinnest support concerns why a library-level solution would be insufficient and whether the proposed facility has any implementation experience.

- The strongest support comes from the recorded committee poll, which gives a tangible sense of where sentiment currently stands.
- The discussion of prior art in P3091 and the fate of names like `lookup` and `get_optional` grounds the proposal in real committee history.
- The paper asserts that a `get` returning `optional` would be inconsistent, but does not develop that claim with examples or analysis.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any treatment of implementation experience or why a library-only solution would not suffice.
