Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization: it grounds a few key claims in concrete technical reasoning, but leaves the motivating problem and affected audience largely implicit. The thinnest support is around why the feature belongs in the standard at all, since that argument is asserted rather than developed.

- The strongest support is the specific explanation of why a library solution would not suffice, particularly the point about avoiding separate function instantiations for each format string.
- The discussion of prior art is usefully concrete, noting how Parametric Expressions failed to interact well with overload sets.
- The paper asserts that wrapping C APIs would become much easier but does not explain why that matters or who would benefit.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any real implementation experience beyond an acknowledgment, leaving the feasibility claim unsubstantiated.
