Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence in a few areas but large sections of the case left entirely unaddressed. The strongest material concerns implementation experience and technical context, while the argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library is essentially absent.

- The paper gives specific implementation grounding by naming Capy and beman::execution and including a complete implementation in an appendix.
- It supports coordination and interoperability with a concrete detail about the receiver’s environment carrying the I/O execution context.
- It does not address who is affected, prior art, or alternatives, leaving the proposal’s audience and design rationale unclear.
- It never explains why a library will not do, which is the most glaring omission for a standardization proposal.
