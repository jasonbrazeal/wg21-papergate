Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its standardization case, but much of its argument rests on unsupported assertions about widespread use and the necessity of standardization. The thinnest support appears where the paper claims broad relevance and interoperability benefits without offering evidence or examples to substantiate those claims.

- The strongest support comes from the implementation experience, with a partial implementation available for review.
- The paper also grounds its motivation in a specific limitation of the existing `deque` and cites prior work through P0260R4 and P1958.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of evidence for the claimed widespread use and affected audience, which weakens the case for standardization priority.
