Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the case needed for standardization, mostly tying its wording strategy to an existing proposal and citing the completion-function requirements. The support is thinnest around the practical and ecosystem questions: who is affected, how this coordinates with other work, whether implementation experience exists, and why the standard is the right venue.

- The strongest support connects the proposed wording to the receiver completion functions in the existing execution clauses and to P3425.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the change or what implementation experience exists.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why the standard should adopt this rather than a library solution or a different specification path.
