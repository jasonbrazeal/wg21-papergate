Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its own standardization, drawing on implementation experience, community feedback, and direct engagement with national bodies and working groups. The thinnest area is the gap between design completeness and actual implementation for two of its central features, which leaves the strongest architectural claims less directly validated than the rest.

- The paper grounds its case in concrete, real-world feedback from the mp-units reference implementation and multiple standards bodies, showing that the current model fails common engineering patterns.
- It identifies a clear standardization need by pointing to the absence of any mainstream units library that distinguishes absolute quantities, deltas, and points as separate types.
- The most glaring omission is that the two most novel features—the three-way split and affine space annotations—are described as design-complete but not yet implemented, weakening the implementation-experience claim where it matters most.
