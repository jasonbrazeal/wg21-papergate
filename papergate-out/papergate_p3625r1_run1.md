Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, resting largely on the authors’ own project experience and a pair of external links rather than sustained data or a clear argument for why a library solution is insufficient. The strongest support is the acknowledgment of prior art and the reasoning behind rejecting alternative names, but the absence of any discussion about why the standard is the right venue leaves the case incomplete.

- The paper gives a concrete, specific reason for rejecting alternative names such as `oneof` and `noneof`.
- The authors point to cppreference and Stack Overflow as evidence of prior interest, though these are asserted rather than analyzed.
- The paper does not address why a library implementation would be inadequate.
- The paper never explains why standardization is necessary or how the feature would coordinate with existing concepts and future language work.
