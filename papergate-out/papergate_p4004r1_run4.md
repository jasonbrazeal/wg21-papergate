Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper leans heavily on a single piece of evidence—the divergence between EDG and the other major implementations over the past decade—to justify its case, but it does not develop that evidence into a broader argument about why the proposed direction is the right one. The support is therefore real but narrow, with the thinnest coverage around how the change would interact with existing code, other standardese, or future evolution of the feature.

- The strongest support is the concrete, repeated observation that only EDG implements the current specification and that this has produced real-world bug reports.
- The paper also grounds its relevance by naming GCC, Clang, and MSVC as agreeing on the alternative behavior, which gives the interoperability concern some weight.
- The most glaring omission is any substantive discussion of alternatives beyond noting the current divergence, leaving the reader without a clear sense of what was considered and rejected.
