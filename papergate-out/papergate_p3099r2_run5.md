Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper grounds its standardization case in concrete implementation and deployment experience, and it identifies the affected users and alternative syntaxes clearly. The support is thinnest where it needs to explain why a library-based or existing workaround cannot adequately serve the same purpose, since that claim is asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the reported vendor implementation and deployment experience in libc++ and the LLVM codebase.
- The discussion of possible syntaxes and their relationship to existing practice and other proposals gives the standardization question useful context.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported dismissal of library-level or workaround approaches, leaving a gap in the argument for why a core language feature is necessary.
