- The paper also records a specific request from SG1 to consult named external maintainers, showing some responsiveness to review process expectations.
- The rationale for standardization is asserted only through a single sentence about generic algorithms, with no elaboration or supporting examples.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of discussion about affected users, coordination with existing facilities, or why a library-level solution cannot address the problem.
