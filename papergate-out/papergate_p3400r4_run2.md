The paper grounds several of its key claims in concrete implementation experience and specific technical scenarios, but the overall case for standardization rests on a narrow evidentiary base. The strongest support appears where the paper connects its design choices to real implementation constraints and prior art, while the thinnest support is the unsupported assertion about the proposal’s importance to widespread Contracts adoption.

- The discussion of prior art and the inadequacy of library-only solutions is tied to specific technical mechanisms and build-configuration burdens.
- The claim that the functionality is essential to unhindered and widespread adoption across many domains is asserted without any supporting evidence or examples.
