Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a concrete reason for exposing structural-type queries and shows an implementation path, but it does not build a full case for standardization because several key arguments are asserted rather than explained. The thinnest support is around why a library solution is insufficient and who would actually be affected by the missing facility.

- The strongest support is the sample implementation using existing reflection facilities, which demonstrates feasibility and gives the proposal practical grounding.
- The paper identifies a real gap between library mandates and user-accessible functionality, though it does not develop that point into a full standardization argument.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any discussion of affected users or use cases beyond library implementers.
