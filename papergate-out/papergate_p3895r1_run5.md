Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives a concrete, well-supported reason for standardization by pointing to widespread user demand and the poor quality of ad hoc implementations, but it leaves several important parts of the case largely unargued. The strongest support is the existence of a complete implementation and the specific claim that most hand-rolled attempts are wrong, while the thinnest areas are the lack of engagement with prior art, interoperability, and the assertion that a library solution is inadequate.

- The paper’s strongest support is its reference to a full implementation and its specific claim that most user attempts on StackOverflow and blogs fail.
- The argument that users need this functionality constantly is asserted rather than demonstrated with examples or evidence.
- The paper does not discuss prior art, alternative approaches, or how the feature would coordinate with existing standard library facilities.
- The dismissal of a third-party library as “silly” is stated without explaining why a small dependency is unreasonable in this case.
