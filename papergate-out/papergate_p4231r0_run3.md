Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why its approach might be preferable, but it leaves several core standardization questions unanswered, so the case feels more like a sketch than a complete justification. The strongest material concerns the relationship to existing practice and prior proposals, while the rationale for changing the standard itself is largely absent.

- The paper grounds its design in a concrete comparison with P2746 and ties its constraints to `is_iec_559`, which gives the proposal a clear technical anchor.
- It identifies a specific ambiguity in the current standard around rounding of constants, suggesting at least one place where normative clarification may be needed.
- The discussion of implementation experience is promising but still prospective, with no working implementation yet available to support the claims.
- The paper does not address who would be affected by the change, why a library solution is insufficient, or how the proposal would coordinate with existing standard guarantees.
