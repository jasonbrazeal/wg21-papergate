The paper offers a reasonably grounded case for standardization, drawing on direct implementation experience and a clear precedent from the executors work, but it leans heavily on a single project and repeats a few justifications across categories rather than broadening the evidence. The thinnest support is around why a library solution would be insufficient, where the explanation is more conceptual than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in kokkos-kernels, showing the need arose in real generic algorithm development.
- The paper cites a specific, adopted precedent from P2855R1/P2300R10, which anchors its design choice in established committee practice.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of broader ecosystem evidence beyond Kokkos, leaving open whether the need is widespread enough to justify standardization.
