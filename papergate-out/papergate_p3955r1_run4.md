The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete reasoning in some areas but little more than assertion in others. The thinnest support appears where the paper relies on quoted suggestions or broad ecosystem goals rather than demonstrated user need or implementation evidence.

- The strongest support comes from the explanation of how the utility improves on a common `execution::just(...) | execution::let_value(...)` idiom and from the acknowledgment that C++26 async scopes provide a justification.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is weakened by repeating the same quotation about users forgetting `noexcept` rather than offering independent analysis.
- The claims about who is affected and why the standard is needed are asserted without supporting specifics, leaving the audience to take the urgency on faith.
