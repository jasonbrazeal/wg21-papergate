Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper gives substantial, concrete support for standardizing its quantity and units vocabulary, drawing on production incidents, implementation experience, and interoperability failures to justify the work. The support is thinnest where it leans on anecdotal or hypothetical examples rather than demonstrating how the proposed design would be adopted across existing codebases or committees.

- The strongest support comes from specific, real-world failure modes—such as unit mismatches in robotics and aerospace—that directly motivate a safer, standardized approach.
- The discussion of prior art and library limitations is well grounded, showing why existing solutions fall short and why a standard type would help.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of evidence about how the proposal would integrate with existing standards or widely used interfaces beyond the cited historical examples.
