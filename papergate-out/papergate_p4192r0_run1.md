Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper grounds its standardization case mainly in existing implementation behavior and a handful of open core issues, but it leaves large parts of the usual proposal rationale unstated. The thinnest support concerns who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and how the change would coordinate with existing practice beyond a single compiler.

- The strongest support comes from concrete references to open Core issues and a compiler explorer link showing GCC’s current behavior.
- The paper identifies prior art in GCC’s non-conforming acceptance of matching alignment without a repeated specifier.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of affected users or real-world code that would benefit from the change.
