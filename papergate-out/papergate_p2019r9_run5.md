Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its own standardization, grounding its case in concrete user impact, prior art, and implementation experience. The support is thinnest where it relies on anecdotal reports from industry rather than broader, verifiable evidence of demand or portability concerns across platforms.

- The strongest support comes from the cited prototype implementation, which demonstrates that the design has been validated in at least one standard library on POSIX systems.
- The paper also benefits from clear prior art in the form of two earlier proposals, showing continuity and refinement of the idea within the committee.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of detail on how the proposed attributes behave on non-POSIX platforms, leaving portability and feasibility questions largely unaddressed.
