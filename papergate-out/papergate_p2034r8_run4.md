Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of why the language change is needed and how it would fit into the existing lambda model, but its support is uneven: the technical and implementation arguments are well documented, while the claim about community sentiment and the broader motivation remain largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support comes from the implementation experience, where a concrete compiler proof-of-concept and a specific, small change to closure member types make the proposal feel tractable.
- The paper also grounds its case in prior art and standards history, showing how the current capture rules arose and why a targeted adjustment is plausible.
- The thinnest support is the appeal to a “commonly shared feeling” about a simpler language, which is offered without evidence or examples of that sentiment.
