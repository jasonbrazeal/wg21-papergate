Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper gives a narrow but concrete rationale for its syntax, mainly by showing how the absence of grouping and repetition creates grammatical bloat, but it leaves most of the standardization case unstated. The strongest material concerns motivation and prior art, while the discussion of affected users, implementation experience, and why a library solution is insufficient is entirely absent.

- The clearest support comes from the specific examples of low expressiveness and grammatical duplication that the proposed features would address.
- The paper also briefly documents an alternative syntax considered, which gives some sense of deliberate design.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of implementation experience or feasibility, leaving the proposal without evidence that it can be realized in practice.
- The paper also never explains why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library or separate tooling.
