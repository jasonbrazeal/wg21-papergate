Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers a mixed case for its own standardization, with concrete implementation experience and engagement with prior work, but it leaves several key justifications largely unstated. The thinnest support concerns why the standard is the right venue and why a library solution would not suffice, as well as who would be affected by the proposed facilities.

- The strongest support comes from the existence of a GCC fork implementing the approach, which demonstrates at least exploratory feasibility.
- The paper also grounds itself in specific prior proposals and review feedback, showing awareness of the surrounding design space.
- The rationale for standardizing these facilities is asserted rather than argued, with no clear explanation of why the standard should adopt them.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why a library cannot meet the need, or how the proposal coordinates with existing or adjacent standardization work.
