The paper offers only a narrow justification for its proposal, resting almost entirely on a grammatical distinction while leaving the standardization rationale largely unstated. The support is thinnest in the areas that would matter most to a committee: why the standard is the right venue, how the change would interoperate with existing code, and whether anyone has actually tried implementing it.

- The strongest support is the specific linguistic observation about *fewer* versus *less*, which at least grounds the proposal in a recognizable distinction.
- The paper acknowledges that the change would affect most ordered containers in production, but does not explore the consequences for those users.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why a library solution would not suffice or why the standard itself must change.
