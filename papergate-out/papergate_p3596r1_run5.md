Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, resting almost entirely on the general claim that undefined behavior and IFNDR are worth cataloguing and on the existence of prior related papers. It does not explain who would use the annex, why the standard is the right home for it, or how it would interact with existing practice, and the only implementation experience cited is the drafting branch itself.

- The strongest support is the reference to prior efforts such as P1705R1, P2234R0, and P3075R0, which shows the topic has a history and some community interest.
- The paper asserts that the wording was developed collaboratively in a working draft branch, but offers no evidence of use, review, or implementation beyond that branch.
- The rationale for why the information belongs in the standard, rather than in a separate document or library, is not addressed at all.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected or what problem the annex would solve for them in practice.
