Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper provides substantial support for its own standardization, with concrete examples across implementation experience, prior art, and tooling integration. The thinnest area is the repetition of the same rationale for both “why the standard” and “coordination and interoperability,” which suggests the interoperability argument may not be as fully developed as the others.

- The strongest support comes from the implementation experience section, which identifies a concrete ABI-level divergence that a library-only approach cannot fully resolve.
- The paper also makes a compelling case for standardization through the debugger and performance analyzer awareness that only a standard facility can reliably enable.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of distinct reasoning for coordination and interoperability, since it reuses the same sentence already offered for why the standard is needed.
