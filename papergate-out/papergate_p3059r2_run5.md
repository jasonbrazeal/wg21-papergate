Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives some concrete backing for its claims about low user value and likely low breakage, but it leans heavily on mailing-list sentiment and assertion rather than building a complete standardization case. The thinnest areas are the absence of implementation experience, no discussion of why a library solution is insufficient, and no treatment of coordination or interoperability.

- The strongest support comes from quoted mailing-list discussion suggesting real-world breakage would be rare and, if found, probably indicative of questionable code.
- The paper offers specific reasoning that the affected constructors provide no observable value to users.
- The argument for changing the standard rests mostly on the author’s assertion that implementation-only constructors need not be exposed.
- The paper does not address implementation experience, library alternatives, or coordination and interoperability concerns.
