Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper grounds its motivation in concrete broken or risky use cases and points to prior SG9 discussion, but it does not build a complete case for standardization because it leaves the necessity of a standard solution, library alternatives, and implementation experience unexamined.

- The strongest support comes from the specific, practical failures of the current filter view that the paper identifies as affecting ordinary programmers.
- The reference to SG9 discussion and a recorded vote provides some evidence of community engagement and prior direction.
- The thinnest part is the assertion that the proposal is “what is necessary” without explaining why a library cannot provide the workaround or how the change fits with existing components.
