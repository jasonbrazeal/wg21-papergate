The paper offers only a sliver of contextual support for its standardization, leaning on references to prior discussions and related papers while leaving the core rationale largely unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any explanation for why the standard is the right venue, why a library solution would not suffice, or whether anyone has actually tried implementing the idea.

- The strongest support comes from the specific references to P3818 and P3820, which at least anchor the problem in prior work.
- The paper asserts that making the functions constexpr would be a breaking change, but provides no examples or reasoning to substantiate that claim.
- It does not identify who would be affected by the change or what the practical consequences would be.
- Most glaringly, it never addresses why standardization is necessary or why a library-level approach cannot solve the problem.
