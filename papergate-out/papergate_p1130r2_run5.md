Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a partial account of why its proposed mechanism might be useful, but it does not build a complete case for standardization, leaving several foundational questions unexamined. The strongest material concerns concrete build-tool and module-visibility scenarios, while the argument for acting at the standard level remains largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper offers specific examples of how external dependency information and file matchers could improve rebuild behavior in a Modules ecosystem.
- It gives a concrete illustration of why a library-only solution would be insufficient for controlling visibility into portions of an SDK.
- It does not address prior art, alternative approaches, or implementation experience, leaving the proposal without meaningful comparison to existing practice.
- It asserts that standardization is warranted by referring to exploration requests from EWG and SG15, but provides no supporting rationale for why a standard is the right venue.
