Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, resting on bare assertions rather than evidence or analysis. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about prior art, affected users, interoperability, and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the mention of a reference implementation, though even that is only asserted without details about usage or completeness.
- The paper asserts that the described views are common traversal methods, but provides no examples, citations, or data to substantiate the claim.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a standalone library.
