Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single implementation claim while leaving the motivating rationale and standards-specific reasoning unstated. The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in the `stdexec` reference implementation, but the case thins quickly around why the standard should adopt this, what problem it solves for users, and how it fits with existing or planned facilities.

- The paper’s strongest support is its citation of a working implementation in `stdexec`, the `std::execution` reference implementation, as of 2026-01-22.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or what problem it addresses for affected users.
- The paper does not discuss why a library solution would be insufficient or why standardization is the right venue.
- The paper is silent on coordination and interoperability with related proposals or existing standard facilities.
