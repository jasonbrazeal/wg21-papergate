Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides some useful context for why a consolidated list of undefined behavior and IFNDR cases would be valuable, and it points to prior standardization efforts, but it does not build a complete case for adopting the proposed annex. The thinnest support concerns the rationale for putting this material in the standard itself, the affected audience, and any evidence that the approach has been validated in practice.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to earlier papers and the collaborative drafting branch, which shows the idea has some history and a working text.
- The paper explains the general danger of IFNDR and undefined behavior, giving a reason to care about cataloging them.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected or why the standard, rather than a separate document or library, is the right home for this annex.
