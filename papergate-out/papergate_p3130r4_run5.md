Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper gives uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete discussion of customization point objects and some interoperability rationale, but it leaves key parts of the case asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected, why standardization is necessary, and meaningful implementation experience beyond noting that some functions are missing.

- The strongest support comes from the explanation that iterator-based approaches cannot provide the desired property because iterators must point to actual objects.
- The paper points to a companion proposal for prior art, alternatives, and worked examples, which at least directs readers to more detailed supporting material.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper never addresses who is affected by the proposal or what problem it solves for them.
