Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow sliver of support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on the existence of a similar algorithm in Nvidia’s stdexec while leaving the broader case essentially unmade. The argument is thinnest where it matters most: motivation, standardization rationale, and evidence of real-world use are all absent.

- The strongest support is the concrete mention of Nvidia’s stdexec as an existing implementation.
- The paper does not explain why the feature belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, or interoperability concerns.
- Most glaringly, it never addresses why the algorithm matters or who would benefit from standardizing it.
