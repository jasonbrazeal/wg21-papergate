Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why indexing packs of templates should be standardized, but it leans heavily on the momentum of a related feature rather than building a complete case for this extension. The strongest material concerns prior art and the apparent gap in P2662R3, while the justification for standardization itself is largely implicit.

- The paper identifies a concrete limitation in P2662R3 and connects it to the stated intent that all packs should be indexable.
- It situates the proposal against related in-flight papers, showing awareness of the surrounding design space.
- It offers no implementation experience beyond an unsupported assertion of confidence.
- It does not address who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, or why the standard is the right venue.
