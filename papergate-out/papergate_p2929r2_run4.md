The paper offers some concrete grounding in implementation experience and naming alignment with existing facilities, but it largely asserts rather than argues the case for standardization. The thinnest support is around motivation, affected users, and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support is the specific generated code shown as implementation experience.
- The naming rationale is tied to existing `std::simd` functions such as `chunk` and `cat`.
- The paper does not address why the feature matters or who is affected.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any supported argument for why this cannot be done as a library.
