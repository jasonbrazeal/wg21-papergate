Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a reasonably well-supported case for its own standardization, with concrete implementation experience, clear motivation, and useful engagement with prior art. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the broader design consequences, leaving some practical context underdeveloped.

- The strongest support comes from the existence of a working Clang implementation and a public Compiler Explorer demonstration.
- The paper also grounds its motivation in specific limitations of immediately invoked lambdas and in coordination with the pattern matching proposal.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the problem or how widely the need arises in real code.
