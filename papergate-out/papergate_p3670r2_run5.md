Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow justification for its proposal, resting almost entirely on the assertion that there is “no good reason” for the existing gap, while leaving most of the evidentiary burden unaddressed. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, prior art, alternatives, standardization rationale, interoperability, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2662R3 and the claim that pack indexing for types and expressions is already implemented in Clang and GCC with positive feedback.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the change or what problems they currently face.
- The treatment of related in-flight proposals is limited to a statement of uncertainty rather than an analysis of how they interact with this idea.
- The implementation experience section is an unsupported assertion of confidence, with no evidence of prototyping or compiler investigation.
