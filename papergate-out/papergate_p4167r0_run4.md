Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper provides only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, resting almost entirely on a comparison with one prior proposal while leaving most of the expected rationale unstated. The thinnest areas are the absence of any motivation tied to user impact or standardizing need, and the lack of evidence beyond an asserted prototype.

- The strongest support is the specific contrast with P2822R2, which at least situates the design relative to existing work.
- The discussion of why a library approach fails is brief but concrete, pointing to ADL limitations with the current value representation.
- The most glaring omission is that the paper never explains why the feature belongs in the standard or who would benefit from it.
