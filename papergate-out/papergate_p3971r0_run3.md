Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow foundation for its standardization case: it identifies a real gap and points to one concrete precedent, but it does not develop the argument that the standard is the right venue or that the proposed facility would work beyond the motivating example. The support is thinnest around the absence of any discussion of who is affected, implementation experience, or why a library solution cannot suffice.

- The strongest support comes from the specific reference to `std::simd`’s `rebind_t`, which grounds the problem in existing standardization work.
- The paper asserts coordination with recent simd casting discussions, but provides no detail on how this proposal would interoperate with or generalize those efforts.
- The claim that no other current container can do the same is left unsupported, leaving the uniqueness of the problem unestablished.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of implementation experience or any evidence that the proposed facility has been tried in practice.
