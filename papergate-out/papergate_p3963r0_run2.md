Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper provides a reasonably well-supported case for standardization, with concrete references to related proposals, vendor discussions, and technical obstacles that motivate the work. The support is thinnest around implementation experience, where the claim rests on informal conversations rather than any demonstrated or documented result.

- The strongest support comes from the coordination and interoperability section, which cites direct discussions with NVIDIA as a parallel algorithms vendor who agrees the problem is important.
- The paper also grounds its motivation and prior art in specific proposals like P3179R9 and P3960R0, showing awareness of the surrounding standardization landscape.
- The most glaring omission is implementation experience, which is asserted without any completed implementation, prototype, or written confirmation from compiler implementors.
