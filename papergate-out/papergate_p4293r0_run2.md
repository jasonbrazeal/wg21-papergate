Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only a narrow slice of the justification needed for standardization, centering its argument on a specific relationship to P4288 rather than building an independent case. Its support is thinnest where a proposal normally needs to be strongest: explaining who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the feature would work in practice.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s concrete engagement with P4288’s claim that decay-copying can be encapsulated in a distinct algorithm.
- The paper does not address who would use the proposed facility or what problem it solves for them beyond the internal debate with P4288.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, implementation experience, or interoperability concerns that would ground the proposal in existing practice.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than in a library.
