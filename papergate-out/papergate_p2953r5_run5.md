Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the current situation is undesirable and shows that a concrete wording change has been implemented and tested, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case largely unargued. The strongest support is practical, while the rationale for acting through the standard and for choosing this design over alternatives remains thin.

- The paper offers concrete implementation experience, including compiling LLVM/Clang/libc++ and another large codebase with the proposed wording.
- It identifies a specific comprehensibility problem and notes that related proposals would otherwise have to preserve the same oddity.
- It does not explain who is affected by the issue or why the standard, rather than another mechanism, is the right place to address it.
- It gives no discussion of coordination or interoperability concerns that a language change of this kind might raise.
