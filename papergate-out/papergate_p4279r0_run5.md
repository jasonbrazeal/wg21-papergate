Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, technical rationale for its proposal, and it leaves most of the case for standardization unstated. Its support is thinnest around the questions a committee would most need answered: who is affected, what alternatives exist, and whether any implementation experience validates the design.

- The strongest support is the specific claim that the proposed facility would cover a pipeline step that existing endian views do not.
- The paper also argues, with some specificity, that the existing facility is low-value because it merely wraps a trivial transform.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of prior art, alternatives, or implementation experience, leaving no evidence that the design has been tested or compared against other approaches.
