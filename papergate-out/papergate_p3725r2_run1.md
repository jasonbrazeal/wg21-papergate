Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a concrete account of why the current filter view is risky in basic use, but it offers almost no evidence that the proposed change belongs in the standard rather than in a library or implementation. The strongest material concerns the problem itself and a prior SG9 discussion, while the case for standardization remains largely unstated.

- The paper supports its motivation with specific examples of broken or risky behavior in current filter view use cases.
- It points to prior SG9 discussion and a vote as evidence of direction, though the supporting details are only linked rather than summarized.
- It does not address why the standard is the right venue, why a library solution would not suffice, or whether there is any implementation experience.
