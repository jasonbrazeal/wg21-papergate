Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers only a thin basis for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single motivating example and an early-stage prototype. Its strongest support is the concrete connection to P3771, but the argument for why the standard must change is asserted rather than demonstrated, and several sections that would normally carry the proposal are empty or unsupported.

- The paper identifies a real gap in detecting or modifying values across constant-evaluation and runtime-evaluation boundaries, and ties that gap to a specific existing proposal.
- The reference to P3771 provides a plausible library-solution motivation, though it is repeated without elaboration.
- The claim that a library solution will not suffice is stated without evidence or comparison to alternative approaches.
- The paper offers no implementation experience beyond an admitted early prototype and explicitly defers the question of usefulness to EWG, leaving coordination, affected users, and standardization rationale unaddressed.
