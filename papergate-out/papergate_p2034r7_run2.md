Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper grounds its case in concrete language history and practical use cases, but its support is uneven: the core rationale is well documented, while evidence of real-world validation is essentially absent. The thinnest part is the claim of implementation experience, which is asserted without any supporting detail, leaving the proposal’s readiness for standardization largely unproven.

- The strongest support comes from the historical account of lambda capture evolution, which ties the proposal to a clear, documented gap in the language’s existing model.
- The paper also makes a specific, credible case for why library workarounds like `std::cref` are insufficient for the intended use.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of implementation experience or usage evidence, despite the heading indicating such experience was discussed but not substantiated.
