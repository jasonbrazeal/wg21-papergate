Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of why the naming choice matters and how other ecosystems have handled it, but it leaves several parts of the standardization case unstated, particularly around affected users, implementation experience, and why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from the comparison with Rust, Java, C#, and LLVM, which grounds the naming discussion in established practice.
- The argument that this decision sets policy for future arithmetic variations gives the proposal a clear standardization rationale.
- The paper does not identify who is affected by the current naming or how widespread the confusion is.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of implementation experience or why a library-only approach would not suffice.
