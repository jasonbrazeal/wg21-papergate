Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives only partial support for its own standardization, with some concrete discussion of trade-offs and prior art but little engagement with the broader case for changing the standard. The thinnest areas are the absence of any account of who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, or whether any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support comes from the paper’s specific acknowledgment of added complexity and its reference to divergent implementation attribute systems.
- The claim about linking incompatibility with older compilers is asserted rather than demonstrated with examples or evidence.
- The paper does not explain why this belongs in the standard rather than remaining a vendor extension or library facility.
- It never addresses who would be affected by the change or reports any implementation experience.
