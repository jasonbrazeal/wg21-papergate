- The strongest support is the concrete implementation experience in the stdexec reference implementation, with a specific date.
- Prior art and alternatives are acknowledged only through that same implementation, offering little comparative context.
- The paper does not address why the problem matters or who is affected by the described scheduling behavior.
- It also leaves unaddressed why the standard is the right venue and why a library would not suffice.
