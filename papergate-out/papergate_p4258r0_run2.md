The paper offers only narrow, uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete references to prior work and implementation behavior but little explanation of the problem’s significance, affected users, or why a library solution would be insufficient. The thinnest areas are the absence of motivation and the lack of any discussion about coordination with existing standard components.

- The paper does not explain why the issue matters or who is affected by the current specification.
- It also fails to address why the standard, rather than a library or user-level workaround, is the right place for the change.
