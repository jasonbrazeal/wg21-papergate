Verdict: Excellent (13/14)

The paper provides a reasonably well-supported case for standardizing these division functions, with concrete evidence drawn from prior standardization efforts, existing practice, and implementation difficulties. The support is thinnest where claims about cross-language team habits and interoperability are asserted without examples or evidence.

- The strongest support comes from the documented history in P0105R1 and the Numerics TS, showing this feature has already been through standardization-adjacent work.
- The survey of existing practice and the difficulty of correct user-level implementation give the proposal a solid practical foundation.
- The most glaring omission is the unsupported claim about Python-oriented teams benefiting from explicit rounding modes, which reads as speculation rather than demonstrated need.
