Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a narrow sliver of justification for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single readability observation about default arguments. Beyond that one point, the supporting material is largely absent: the affected audience, prior art, alternatives, library feasibility, and implementation experience are all unaddressed, and the stated goal of improving readability and conciseness is asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The strongest support is a concrete example showing that default function arguments currently force repetition of the parameter type.
- The paper asserts that the proposal is intended to improve readability and conciseness, but provides no evidence or examples that it would do so.
- The proposal does not discuss prior art, alternative approaches, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of implementation experience or any indication that the feature is practical for compilers to adopt.
