Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper grounds its standardization case in concrete compiler divergence and ties its motivation to an open core issue, which gives the proposal a clear reason to exist. The support is thinnest around the affected audience and the practical consequences for users who are not compiler or library implementers.

- The strongest support comes from specific references to CWG2296 and a national body comment, anchoring the proposal in known standardization gaps.
- The paper also cites implementation experience from Clang and EDG, showing that the proposed direction reflects existing practice rather than untested theory.
- A notable omission is any discussion of who is affected by the current inconsistency, leaving the real-world impact on ordinary C++ developers largely unstated.
