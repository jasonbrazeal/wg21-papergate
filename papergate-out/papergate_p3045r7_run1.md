Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper makes a strong, example-driven case for standardizing its proposed vocabulary types, with concrete production failures and prior-art comparisons that tie the motivation directly to safety and interoperability. The support is thinnest where it leans on rhetorical “maybe” scenarios rather than demonstrated standardization benefit, but the overall argument is coherent and well-sourced.

- The strongest support comes from concrete production feedback and historical failures that show real-world consequences of unit confusion.
- The comparison with existing libraries and `std::ratio` limitations gives a clear technical rationale for why a standard facility is needed.
- The most glaring omission is a lack of direct evidence that the proposed design, rather than an improved library or guideline, is the necessary next step for the standard.
