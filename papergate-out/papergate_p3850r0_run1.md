Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its motivation and prior work, but it largely assumes rather than argues for the need to standardize the proposed library hook. The thinnest support appears where the paper should explain why this belongs in the standard, why a library outside the standard cannot suffice, and what implementation or usage experience actually demonstrates.

- The paper points to a prior analysis and a mature design with wording and a GCC implementation as its strongest evidence of readiness.
- It gives specific reasons why extending C++26 contract assertions matters for usability at scale.
- It asserts that the proposal is small, straightforward, and library-only without showing why standardization is necessary.
- It does not address why a non-standard library would be insufficient or who the affected users are.
