The paper provides concrete implementation evidence and some motivation for addressing the oddity it identifies, but it leaves several important standardization questions largely unexamined. - The paper’s implementation experience is its most compelling support, since the proposed wording has been tested against large real-world codebases.
- The motivation is grounded in a specific, permitted-but-implausible declaration and notes a consistency concern for related proposals.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change or what the practical impact would be for existing code.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of why the standard, rather than a library or tooling solution, is the appropriate place to address the issue.
