Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper provides concrete support for its proposal through implementation experience, prior art, and interoperability considerations, but it leaves the core standardization rationale largely asserted rather than argued. The thinnest support appears where the paper should connect the change to affected users and to the standards process itself.

- The strongest support comes from implementation experience, with both the reference implementation and libc++ already behaving as the proposal specifies.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is well grounded in specific external protocols and formats.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of who is affected, despite the paper’s own framing around Python-driven workflows.
- The rationale for standardization itself is asserted without supporting reasoning, leaving the case for committee action underdeveloped.
