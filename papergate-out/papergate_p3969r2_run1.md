Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of the problem, the affected audience, and the implementation landscape, but it leans on assertion at the point where it needs to justify standardization rather than another remedy. The strongest material concerns practical evidence and prior discussion, while the weakest is the absence of any treatment of coordination or interoperability.

- The paper supports its motivation with a specific, recognizable failure mode and a clear contrast with `std::unreachable`.
- It grounds the affected audience in a recorded LEWG poll with concrete numbers and attendance.
- It offers useful prior art and implementation experience, including an active Clang warning that matches the proposed ill-formed cases.
- The case for changing the standard is asserted rather than argued, and the paper does not address coordination or interoperability at all.
