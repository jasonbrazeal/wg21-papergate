Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives only partial support for its own standardization, with a few concrete references but little evidence that the problem is widespread or that the proposed approach is the right vehicle. The thinnest parts are the absence of any discussion of library alternatives or interoperability, and the reliance on assertions where specifics would be expected.

- The strongest support comes from the cited prior art and the SG7 recommendation to merge P3678R0, which at least grounds the direction in committee discussion.
- The claim that attributes are used heavily and growing is asserted without data, leaving the affected audience and urgency unclear.
- The motivation that a “uniform vehicle” is needed is stated as a conclusion rather than argued from demonstrated portability or usability problems.
- The paper never addresses why a library solution would not suffice or how the feature would coordinate with existing attribute handling, and the implementation experience is reduced to an unsupported mention of compiler explorer.
