Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper gives a mixed account of its own case, offering concrete motivation and some evidence of committee interest, but it leaves important parts of the standardization argument asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest areas are the absence of implementation experience and the lack of a developed rationale for why this change belongs in the standard rather than in guidance or library evolution.

- The strongest support comes from the specific identification of known design issues, including the constness bug, which grounds the motivation in documented problems.
- The inclusion of a poll with clear numerical support shows at least some committee appetite for eventual deprecation.
- The claim that deprecation would unify the standard library and guide users is asserted without supporting examples or analysis of migration costs.
- The paper does not address implementation experience at all, leaving the practical path to deprecation and replacement unexamined.
