Verdict: Strong (11/14, close to Excellent)

The paper gives a reasonably concrete account of the problem and of prior work, but it does not directly argue why this belongs in the standard library rather than in a standalone library or a future numerics extension. The strongest material concerns feasibility and existing practice, while the case for standardization itself is largely implicit.

- The paper supports implementation feasibility with a concrete example and a link to a fuller implementation.
- It grounds the need in real-world demand by citing a common Stack Overflow question and the difficulty of correct user implementations.
- It shows prior standardization history through references to P0105R1 and P1889R1.
- It leaves the core justification for standardization unstated, offering no argument for why a standard facility is preferable to a library solution.
