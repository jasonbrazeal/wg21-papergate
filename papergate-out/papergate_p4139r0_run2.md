Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, mostly asserted case for standardization, with its strongest support resting on a single observation about the novelty of a fallible `get()` and a brief mention of prior art. Beyond that, the document leaves the central questions of why standardization is necessary, how the feature would interoperate, and whether it has been implemented almost entirely unexamined.

- The paper grounds its motivation in the specific claim that this would be the first fallible `get()` in the library.
- It acknowledges prior art in P3091 and explains why those earlier names failed to gain traction.
- It does not address why the standard is the right venue rather than a library solution.
- It offers no implementation experience, coordination strategy, or interoperability discussion to support the proposal.
