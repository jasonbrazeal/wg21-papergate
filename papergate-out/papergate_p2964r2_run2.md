Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper provides concrete implementation experience and performance evidence for its approach, but it does not make a direct argument for why standardization is necessary or why the work cannot remain a library solution. The strongest support is practical and technical, while the thinnest areas concern the standard’s role and interoperability with existing or future specifications.

- The paper offers specific implementation and testing details across multiple Intel architectures and user-defined types, which grounds its feasibility claims.
- It identifies affected users and use cases, such as strong typedefs, enumerations, and DSP types, with concrete examples.
- The rationale for standardization is asserted rather than argued, with no clear explanation of why a library cannot suffice.
- Coordination and interoperability with related standards or existing practice are not addressed at all.
