Verdict: Weak (3/14, close to Adequate)

The paper leans heavily on external references and a prior committee discussion, but it does not itself assemble a case for standardization. The thinnest areas are the absence of any explanation of who is affected, why a library solution is insufficient, or what implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support comes from the specific references to P3818 and P3820, which at least point readers to the background and alternatives.
- The paper cites an LEWG teleconference outcome, giving some procedural grounding for the proposed direction.
- It asserts that making the functions constexpr is a breaking change, but offers no examples or evidence of the breakage.
- The paper does not address why the standard is the right venue, how the change coordinates with existing practice, or whether any implementation experience exists.
