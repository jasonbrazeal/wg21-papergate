Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, resting almost entirely on the assertion that C++ needs a native data frame to compete with Python/Pandas. The strongest support is the concrete reference to an existing implementation and the interoperability argument around zero-copy exchange with the Python ecosystem, but the paper never explains why a standard is required rather than a library, nor does it demonstrate implementation experience or address who would be affected.

- The clearest support is the specific prior art reference to an existing DataFrame library, which at least grounds the idea in something real.
- The interoperability point about zero-copy data exchange with Python is the most substantive reason offered for why such a container might matter.
- The paper asserts rather than argues that a library will not suffice, leaving the central standardization question unanswered.
- The most glaring omission is the complete absence of any discussion of who is affected or what implementation experience exists, leaving the proposal without a demonstrated constituency or feasibility basis.
