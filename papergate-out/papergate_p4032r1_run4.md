Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single convenience argument that is repeated without elaboration. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and why a library solution would not suffice.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to P2830R10’s `type_order`, which at least grounds the idea in existing standardization work.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the proposed change or what the practical impact would be.
- The rationale for standardization, as opposed to a library solution, is asserted but never explained.
- The author explicitly states there is no implementation experience for the proposed built-in comparison, leaving the feasibility and design entirely unvalidated.
