Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a thin, anecdotal basis for standardization: it names a real need and gestures toward prior discussion, but does not explain why a library solution is insufficient, how the feature would interact with the rest of the standard, or whether anyone has actually implemented it. The strongest material is the concrete motivation and the considered naming alternatives, while the case for standardization itself is largely absent.

- The paper gives a specific, credible motivation by pointing to repeated project needs and external discussions of the same pattern.
- It shows some care in design by discussing and rejecting alternative names such as `oneof` and `noneof`.
- It does not address why a library-only approach would be inadequate, leaving the threshold question for standardization unanswered.
- It provides no implementation experience or coordination analysis, so the proposal rests on assertion rather than demonstrated practice.
