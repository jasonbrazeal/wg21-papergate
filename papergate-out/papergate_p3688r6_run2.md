Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper provides only a thin evidentiary basis for standardization, resting almost entirely on a single implementation example and a general assertion about the prevalence of ASCII work. Its strongest concrete support is the linked Compiler Explorer implementation, but the argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library is essentially unsupported, and several key sections are missing altogether.

- The paper offers a concrete implementation link, which at least demonstrates feasibility of the proposed functions.
- The claim that ASCII handling is common enough to justify standardization is asserted without data, examples, or user impact.
- The paper does not address prior art, alternatives, coordination, interoperability, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
