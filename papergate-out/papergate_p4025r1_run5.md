Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a thin case for standardization, leaning heavily on a single asserted motivation about Python/Pandas and AI workflows while leaving several key justifications unsupported. The strongest material is the reference to prior art and the mention of ongoing work in study groups, but the argument for why a standard is needed—rather than a library—is essentially absent.

- The paper identifies a concrete prior implementation, showing at least some awareness of existing alternatives.
- The claim that C++ lacks a native Data Frame and frictionless data ingestion is repeated as the central justification, but it is asserted rather than demonstrated.
- The paper provides no implementation experience or evidence that the proposed features have been tried in practice.
- The most glaring omission is the complete lack of argument for why a library would not suffice, which is central to any standardization proposal.
