Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides some grounding for its proposed wording by citing the relevant completion operations and linking the receiver concept to synchronous function returns, but it leaves much of the standardization rationale unstated. The thinnest support concerns who would be affected, why the standard is the right venue, and whether there is any implementation experience behind the approach.

- The strongest support is the concrete reference to the completion functions that must receive the receiver object.
- The paper also situates itself as a wording strategy for P3425, giving it a clear relationship to existing work.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any discussion of affected users or implementation experience.
