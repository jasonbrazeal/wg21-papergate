Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its standardization case by identifying implementation divergence in placement new deallocation behavior and citing prior art, but it leaves several important evidentiary areas entirely unaddressed. The thinnest support concerns who is actually affected, why a library solution is insufficient, and whether any implementation experience exists.

- The strongest support comes from the specific identification of two divergent implementation behaviors and the acknowledgment of a related prior fix in earlier work.
- The paper does not explain who is affected by the divergence, leaving the practical impact of standardization unclear.
- It offers no discussion of why a library-level solution would be inadequate, which is a standard expectation for language-change proposals.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, making the proposal feel speculative rather than grounded in practice.
