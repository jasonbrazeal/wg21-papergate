Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers some concrete evidence that the problem is real and that implementers have already explored a fix, but it does not build a broader case for why the standard should change. The support is thinnest around the intended audience, the need for normative action, and how the change would interact with existing specifications.

- The strongest support is the citation of partial implementations in all three major standard libraries, which shows practical interest and feasibility.
- The paper gives a specific SFINAE example illustrating the hard-error failure mode it wants to address.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected or how widespread the problem is in real code.
- The paper also does not explain why a library-level solution is insufficient or why standardization is the right venue.
