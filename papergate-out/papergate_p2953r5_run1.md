Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper provides concrete implementation evidence and a specific rationale for addressing a narrow language oddity, but it leaves several important standardization questions largely unexamined. The strongest support is practical, while the thinnest areas concern who is affected and why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper substantiates its concern with a concrete example and notes real implementation experience in Clang forks used on large codebases.
- It connects the issue to related proposals, showing awareness of broader consistency concerns in the language.
- It does not address who is affected by the current behavior or the proposed change.
- It offers no discussion of why the standard, rather than a library or other mechanism, is the appropriate place for the fix.
