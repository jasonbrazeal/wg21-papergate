Verdict: Strong (8/14, close to Adequate)

The paper offers only a thin, mostly asserted case for standardization, with its strongest moments coming from pointing to related work and a general claim about C++’s role in AI. The support is thinnest where it matters most: why a standard is needed rather than a library, what implementation experience exists, and who would actually be affected.

- The clearest concrete support is the reference to an existing DataFrame project, which at least grounds the idea in prior art.
- The paper asserts that C++ lacks a native DataFrame and frictionless ingestion, but it does not develop this into a case for standardization.
- The claim that work began in SG19, SG14, and SG6 in 2018 is offered as implementation experience, but no details or outcomes are provided.
- The paper never addresses who is affected, why a library would not suffice, or how the proposal would coordinate with existing ecosystems.
