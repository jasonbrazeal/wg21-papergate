The paper gives a partial account of why this facility belongs in the standard, with concrete implementation experience and some engagement with adjacent work, but it leaves several core justifications largely asserted rather than argued. The thinnest areas are the failure to explain why a library solution would not suffice and the lack of evidence for coordination with existing or forthcoming standard facilities.

- The strongest support is the availability of a reference implementation, which at least demonstrates the design can be realized in practice.
- The discussion of `proxy` as prior art is specific enough to help locate the proposal in the existing design space.
- The claim that the need is “real and recurring” is repeated rather than supported with concrete use cases or user demand.
- The paper never addresses why a library implementation would be inadequate, which is a central question for any proposal targeting the standard.
