Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers a substantial amount of concrete, field-specific support for its standardization, drawing on real implementation experience, cross-industry demand, and clear failures of existing workarounds. The support is thinnest where it gestures at prior art and mailing-list sentiment without naming or analyzing those sources in enough depth to establish a durable precedent.

- The strongest support comes from the detailed account of compiler and build-system strain caused by current array-literal and script-generation approaches, including a named production tool from MongoDB.
- The paper also makes a persuasive coordination argument by listing several distinct industries and noting that the problem would otherwise be solved repeatedly by every implementation.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of a substantive survey of existing embed or resource-compiler mechanisms in other languages and toolchains, which weakens the claim that standardization is the necessary next step.
