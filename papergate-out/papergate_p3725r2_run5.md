Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper gives a partial account of why the current filter view is problematic and points to SG9 discussion as a source of direction, but it does not build a complete case for standardization. The thinnest areas are the absence of any argument for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library, and the lack of implementation experience or interoperability considerations.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete claim that several basic filter view use cases are broken or risky, tied to an SG9 discussion and vote.
- The paper asserts that ordinary programmers need deep insight to avoid serious mistakes, but offers no supporting explanation for why a library solution would be insufficient.
- The paper does not address why the standard is the right venue at all.
- Implementation experience and coordination or interoperability concerns are entirely absent.
