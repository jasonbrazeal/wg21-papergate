Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper offers only a narrow rebuttal to another proposal, and it does not build an affirmative case that its own position should be adopted by the committee. The support is thinnest around the core standardization questions: why the standard should change, who would be affected, and why a library solution is insufficient.

- The strongest support comes from concrete implementation experience, noting that trivial relocation was previously implemented and then removed from clang.
- Prior art and alternatives are grounded in specific committee discussions about type-erased containers and non-bitwise copying semantics.
- The paper asserts rather than argues its central claims about type safety and the impossibility of a library solution.
- It never addresses who is affected or how the proposed direction would coordinate or interoperate with existing standard features.
