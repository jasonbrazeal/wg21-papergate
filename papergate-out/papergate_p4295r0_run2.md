Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single piece of historical context and an unverified implementation link. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of any discussion of affected users, why a library solution would be insufficient, or what coordination with the rest of the standard would require.

- The strongest support is the specific reference to N3353, which shows the single-ended interface was previously considered and deliberately separated by SG1.
- The implementation experience claim is weakened by being only a bare link with no description of completeness, usage, or lessons learned.
- The paper never addresses why this cannot remain a library facility, which is a central question for any standard library addition.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any motivation beyond an unsupported assertion about unidirectional communication, leaving the actual need for standardization unestablished.
