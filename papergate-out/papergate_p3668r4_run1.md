Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides substantial support for its standardization case by grounding the motivation in concrete boilerplate reduction, identifying a broad set of affected operations, and engaging with prior art and library-based alternatives. The support is thinnest where the document would normally demonstrate feasibility and confidence in the design, since the implementation experience section is present but empty.

- The strongest support comes from the concrete identification of 53 candidate operations and the linked companion paper exploring their defaulting.
- The paper also makes a clear standards-specific argument by showing how defaulted postfix operations could shrink the library specification.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any implementation experience, leaving the practical viability of the proposal unsubstantiated.
