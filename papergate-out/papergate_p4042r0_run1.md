Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper provides almost no support for its own standardization, offering only a bare assertion of implementation experience while leaving every other rationale for a standard change unaddressed. The thinnest part is the complete absence of motivation, audience impact, alternatives, or justification for why this belongs in the standard rather than a library.

- The only concrete support is a single statement that the feature has been implemented and tested in a libstdc++ patch currently under review.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or who would be affected by standardizing it.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- Most glaringly, it never articulates why the standard should adopt the proposal at all.
