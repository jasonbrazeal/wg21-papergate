Verdict: Excellent (14/14)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization case, drawing on production use, prior art, and specific technical gaps, though the evidence is unevenly distributed and sometimes repeated rather than deepened. The thinnest areas are the justification for why a library solution cannot suffice and the lack of distinct detail on implementation experience beyond a single production citation.

- The strongest support comes from concrete identification of four structural gaps between the sender model and coroutines, each tied to specific papers and design requirements.
- Production use at Citadel Securities provides credible, real-world validation of the underlying execution model the proposal builds on.
- The case for why the standard must address this rather than a library is asserted through the cost of compile-time analysis properties, but not developed with examples or alternatives.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of distinct implementation experience or broader ecosystem evidence beyond one cited production deployment, leaving the practical path to standardization thinly supported.
