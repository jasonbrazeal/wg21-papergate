Verdict: Adequate (7/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers some concrete grounding for its naming concern, particularly through the poll results and reference to P3804R1, but it does not build a broader case for standardization. The strongest material is narrowly about namespace naming, while the rationale for why this belongs in the standard is asserted rather than demonstrated. Several areas that would normally support a standardization proposal are entirely absent.

- The paper supports its naming objection with a specific poll showing weak support for `system_context_replaceability` and a prior proposal favoring `replacement`.
- The paper identifies a real consistency problem by noting that `system_context` does not exist in the current working draft.
- The paper asserts that the chosen name will cause confusion but offers no supporting reasoning or examples beyond the missing `system_context` reference.
- The paper does not address coordination, interoperability, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
