Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper provides a reasonably specific case for standardizing its proposed feature, with the strongest support concentrated in its discussion of prior art, implementation experience, and the limitations of existing extensions. The thinnest areas are the absence of any discussion of who is affected by the proposal and a somewhat narrow articulation of why the standard is the right venue beyond the technical gaps it identifies.

- The paper grounds its motivation in concrete use cases, particularly macro definitions and the desugaring of a control flow operator from another proposal.
- It cites prior work and an existing implementation in Clang, which gives the proposal a tangible basis for further standardization.
- The discussion of why a library solution will not suffice is tied to specific shortcomings in an existing extension rather than stated as a general claim.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the change, leaving the audience and impact of the proposal unclear.
