Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers uneven support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence in a few areas but little or no discussion of several questions that would normally matter to a committee audience. The strongest material concerns implementation experience and prior art, while the case for why the feature belongs in the standard, how it coordinates with existing facilities, and why a library solution is insufficient is largely absent.

- The paper gives specific implementation experience through the beman.utf_view reference implementation and its connection to libstdc++ work.
- It identifies relevant prior art in the `views::reverse` CPO and positions the proposal against the removed `codecvt` facets.
- It does not address coordination and interoperability with other standard or proposed facilities.
- It does not explain why a library would not be sufficient, leaving a central standardization question unanswered.
