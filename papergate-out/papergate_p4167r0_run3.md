Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper gives only a narrow, technically specific justification for its approach, leaving most of the case for standardization unstated. Its strongest support appears in the comparison with prior art and the identification of an ADL-related problem, but the discussion of affected users, standardization rationale, interoperability, and implementation experience is essentially absent.

- The paper grounds its motivation in a concrete ADL limitation of the `quantity` template and contrasts its design with P2822R2.
- It notes that only a Godbolt prototype exists, with no broader implementation experience reported.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the problem or why a library-only solution would be insufficient.
- It offers no discussion of why the standard is the right venue or how the proposal would coordinate with existing and related work.
