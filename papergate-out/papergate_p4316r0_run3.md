Verdict: Weak (1/14, close to None)

The paper offers almost no support for its own standardization, resting on a single unsupported assertion about profiles while leaving every practical question about scope, feasibility, and necessity unexamined. The thinnest part is the complete absence of any argument connecting the proposed direction to a standards process, an affected audience, or prior work.

- The only substantive claim is that profiles are an incremental path to safety guarantees, but even that is asserted without evidence or elaboration.
- The paper does not identify who would be affected by the proposal or what problem it solves for them.
- It offers no discussion of prior art, alternatives, implementation experience, or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- Most glaringly, the paper never explains why this work belongs in the C++ standard at all.
