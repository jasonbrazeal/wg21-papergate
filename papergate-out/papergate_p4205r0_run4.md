Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers some concrete support for standardization through its implementation experience and its discussion of prior art, but it leaves several core parts of the case unstated, particularly around why the standard is the right venue and why a library solution is insufficient. The thinnest support concerns the motivating problem and the affected audience, which are never directly explained.

- The strongest support is the author’s implementation in the Beman Project, which shows the feature can be built and tested in practice.
- The paper also grounds its proposal in prior art by explaining the absence of searcher overloads in Boost.Ranges and range-v3.
- A notable omission is any discussion of who is affected by the current inconsistency or why their needs justify standardization.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of a supported argument for why this cannot be handled by a library rather than a change to the standard.
