Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper gives a concrete reason for its own existence by pointing to how quickly the prior cataloguing work became stale, but it leaves most of the surrounding case for standardization unstated. The strongest support is the specific reference to P3596R3 and the concurrent papers that made it out of date, while the thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about affected users, implementation experience, or why the standard is the right venue.

- The paper grounds its motivation in a specific, verifiable gap created when P3596R3 was approved alongside other papers at the same meeting.
- It identifies prior art directly, showing awareness of the existing annexes and their intended scope.
- The paper does not address who is affected by the proposed change or what practical experience exists with the approach.
- It offers no discussion of why the standard, rather than a library or other mechanism, is the appropriate place for this work.
