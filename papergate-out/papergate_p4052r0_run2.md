Verdict: Adequate (6/14)

The paper makes a reasonably concrete case for its preferred naming direction by citing existing practice in other languages and libraries, but it leaves several important standardization questions essentially unexamined. The strongest material concerns naming precedent and the policy implications of that choice, while the thinnest support appears around implementation experience, library-only alternatives, and who would actually be affected by the change.

- The paper grounds its naming argument in specific, searchable evidence from Rust, Java, C#, and LLVM, as well as GitHub usage counts.
- It identifies a plausible standards-level consequence: the chosen name may set policy for future arithmetic variations.
- It does not address implementation experience or why a library solution would be insufficient.
- It does not discuss who is affected by the naming decision beyond a general reference to novices and code search results.
