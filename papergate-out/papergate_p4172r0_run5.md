Verdict: Excellent (12/14, close to Strong)

The paper offers substantial support for its standardization case through concrete historical evidence, implementation experience, and a clear articulation of the interoperability problem, though its argument is thinnest when it comes to identifying who specifically is affected by the absence of this standard.

- The strongest support comes from the twenty-year history of Boost.Asio and the observed failure of the ecosystem to converge on shared async primitives without standardization.
- The implementation experience with Capy and Corosio provides credible evidence that the proposed foundation is practical and maintainable.
- The argument that a library alone cannot solve the frame allocator propagation problem is specific and well-supported.
- The most glaring omission is any discussion of who is affected by the proposal, leaving the audience and impact of the standardization unclear.
