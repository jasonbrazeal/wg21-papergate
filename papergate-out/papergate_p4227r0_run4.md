Verdict: Weak (2/14)

The paper offers very little support for its own standardization, resting almost entirely on a single reference to prior art while leaving the motivating problem, affected users, and rationale for standardization unstated. The thinnest parts are the complete absence of any argument for why the standard should adopt the facility or why a library solution would be insufficient.

- The only substantive support is the citation of P3655 as prior art for a null-terminated string-view type.
- The paper does not explain why the feature matters or who would benefit from it.
- The paper gives no reason for pursuing standardization rather than a library implementation.
- There is no implementation experience or interoperability discussion to ground the proposal in practice.
