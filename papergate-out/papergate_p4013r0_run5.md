Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper offers only fragmentary support for its own standardization, with concrete evidence limited to implementation experience and a brief comparison of alternatives. Most of the contextual argument—why the problem matters, who is affected, why the standard is the right venue, and how the feature would interoperate—is absent, leaving the proposal’s rationale largely unstated.

- The strongest support is the linked compiler implementation and its availability on Compiler Explorer, which demonstrates at least some practical experimentation.
- The discussion of prior art and alternatives is specific, noting tradeoffs such as template instantiation costs and virtual table overhead.
- The most glaring omission is the absence of any motivation, affected audience, or justification for standardization rather than a library solution.
