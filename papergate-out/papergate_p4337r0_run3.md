Verdict: Strong (10/14)

The paper provides some concrete grounding for its proposal through named prior art, an implementation reference, and a connection to std::execution’s requirements, but much of its central justification is asserted rather than demonstrated. The thinnest support appears where the paper claims broad user need, increasing future demand, and the necessity of standardization rather than a library solution, without offering evidence or elaboration.

- The strongest support is the identification of existing implementations and alternative names, which shows the idea has circulated in practice.
- The link to std::execution’s immovable operation states gives at least one specific context where the facility would be needed.
- The most glaring omission is the lack of any supporting detail for the claim that users will increasingly need this functionality, beyond the single std::execution example.
- The paper also asserts that a library solution will not suffice without explaining what standardization specifically enables that a library cannot.
