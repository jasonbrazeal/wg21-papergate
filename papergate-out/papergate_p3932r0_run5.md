Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a narrow, issue-resolution rationale for its changes, tying them to existing LWG issues but offering little broader justification for standardization. The support is thinnest around the practical and ecosystem questions that would normally accompany a proposal of this kind.

- The paper clearly identifies the specific LWG issues it resolves and explains the drafting overlap that motivates bundling them.
- It does not address who is affected by the change or what impact it would have on users and implementers.
- It offers no implementation experience, leaving the feasibility and real-world consequences unexamined.
- It does not explain why the standard is the right place for this change rather than a library solution.
