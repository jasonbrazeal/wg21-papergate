Verdict: Adequate (4/14, close to Weak)

The paper provides only a thin rationale for standardization, resting almost entirely on a general assertion that IFNDR and undefined behavior are worth cataloguing, while leaving most of the standard proposal justification sections unaddressed. The strongest support comes from its citation of prior efforts and a collaborative drafting branch, but even that implementation experience is asserted rather than demonstrated with evidence of use or validation.

- The paper’s most concrete support is its reference to earlier related proposals and a public working-draft branch where the annex wording was developed.
- The core motivation is stated as a broad principle about IFNDR and undefined behavior being “potentially problematic,” without explaining why this particular annex is the right response.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why the standard is the appropriate vehicle, or why a library or separate reference document would not suffice.
- The implementation experience section offers only a link to a draft branch, with no discussion of actual use, review, or lessons learned from that work.
