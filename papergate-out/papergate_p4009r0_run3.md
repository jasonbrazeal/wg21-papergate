Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper gives a partial account of why the proposed semantics might be preferable to the current draft, but it does not build a complete case for standardization on its own. The strongest material concerns objections to the existing approach and prior alternatives, while the argument for putting this design into the standard is largely asserted rather than demonstrated.

- The paper grounds its motivation in specific objections to the current draft, which at least explains why the work is happening.
- It identifies prior design alternatives and gives concrete reasons for concern about them, showing awareness of the surrounding design space.
- It asserts that function calls provide a familiar extension mechanism, but offers no supporting argument for why this belongs in the standard.
- The paper does not address who is affected, why a library solution would be insufficient, or whether there is any implementation experience to support the proposal.
