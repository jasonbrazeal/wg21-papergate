Verdict: Strong (9/14)

The paper offers only a narrow, example-driven justification for its standardization, leaning heavily on a single implementation’s existence while leaving the broader case largely unstated. The thinnest areas are the complete absence of discussion about why the standard should address this, how it would coordinate with related facilities, and who beyond one vendor is actually affected.

- The strongest support is the concrete code example showing that current return type deduction rules reject the intended pattern.
- The paper points to Nvidia’s stdexec as evidence of implementation experience and prior art, though it does not elaborate on that experience.
- The most glaring omission is any explanation of why the standard, rather than a library or vendor extension, is the right venue for the change.
