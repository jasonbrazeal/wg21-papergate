Verdict: Adequate (5/14)

The paper offers only partial support for its own standardization, with its strongest grounding in the observation that profiles lack agreed documentation and that prior attempts have failed to advance. The case is thinnest where it asserts profiles are the best path forward without explaining why, and where it leaves entirely unaddressed who is affected, how the standard is the right venue, and whether implementation experience exists.

- The paper gives specific, cited evidence that profiles currently lack sufficient documentation for WG21 to evaluate them meaningfully.
- It situates the proposal within a concrete history of unsuccessful prior papers, showing awareness of the surrounding standardization landscape.
- It asserts that profiles are the best known approach to making C++ safer, but offers no supporting reasoning or evidence for that claim.
- It does not address implementation experience, affected users, interoperability, or why a library solution would be inadequate.
